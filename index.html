How this record is made

  1. From the sourceMinutes and agendas from the Town's Agenda Center, checked for new agendas every day.
  2. Read and checkedOutcomes and questions are read with AI assistance, and every figure is checked against the minutes it came from.
  3. SourcedEach case links to the minutes, agenda documents and, where recorded, the meeting video. The minutes remain authoritative.
  4. IndependentNot affiliated with the Town of Mount Pleasant. Not legal advice. Corrections are welcome and published.
