6.   Business
     A. PUBLIC HEARING, Towne Centre, TMS No. 558-00-00-096, -100, -102, -340,
         -348, -387, -399, Request to amend Wilson Tract PD and approval of Impact
         Assessment. R-15-25 Impact Assessment         Staff Report

         Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

        Mr. David Donato, representative of the owner, and Ms. Morgan Bentivegna,
     The Beach Company, reviewed the request with the Commission.

          Ms. Denise Fugo, Housing for All, stated that she is in support of the
     affordable housing component for this project. She stated that it is needed for
     the Town. She suggested that there are current residents losing housing in the
     Town because of not being able to continue to afford their homes. She suggested
     that there should be flexibility in this plan to allow for retail units to be converted
     to residential units. She suggested that this would allow better work/live areas
     without longer commutes.

         There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed public comment.

         Mr. Donato provided final comments, stating that the residential component
     would be an important part of the project. He suggested that this project would
     incorporate smart growth as an infill project and does have the infrastructure
     already completed in the area.

         Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

          Mr. Lacy moved for approval of the PD amendment with the following
     stipulations:
         ➢ Reduction of proposed development height to a maximum of 55 feet
         ➢ A minimum of 60,000 square feet of family entertainment uses
         ➢ A 6/2 density split, requiring a mandatory two attainable housing units for
            every six market-rate residential units constructed
         ➢ Minimum of 14% green space
         ➢ Short-term incubator program space to have a minimum of two units
     Mr. Ferrara seconded the motion.

    Mr. Lacy moved to amend the motion to include that if a short-term incubator
program is desired, it should have a minimum of two units. Mr. Ferrara seconded

Smyth, Mr. Bennett, Ms. Sanford, Mr. Lacy, Mr. Davis, Mr. Berry and Ms. Mansour
in favor; Ms. Ireland opposed.

    Mr. Bennett called for a vote on the motion as amended. Motion passed on
an 8 to 1 vote with Mr. Ferrara, Mr. Smyth, Mr. Bennett, Ms. Sanford, Mr. Lacy,
Mr. Davis, Mr. Berry and Ms. Mansour in favor; Ms. Ireland opposed.

    Mr. Lacy moved for approval of the conceptual plan. Mr. Ferrara seconded
the motion. Motion passed on an 8 to 1 vote with Mr. Ferrara, Mr. Smyth, Mr.
Bennett, Ms. Sanford, Mr. Lacy, Mr. Davis, Mr. Berry and Ms. Mansour in favor;
Ms. Ireland opposed.

     Mr. Davis moved for approval of the impact assessment. Ms. Mansour
seconded the motion. Motion passed on an 8 to 1 vote with Mr. Ferrara, Mr.
Smyth, Mr. Bennett, Ms. Sanford, Mr. Lacy, Mr. Davis, Mr. Berry and Ms. Mansour
in favor; Ms. Ireland opposed.
