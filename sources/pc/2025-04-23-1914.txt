5.   General Public Comment

All correspondence was linked to the agenda.

    Ms. Myra Richardson, 2755 Earl Johnson Lane, President of Hamlin HOA, spoke
regarding annexation of the community property. She expressed concern with
properties in the neighborhood being able to annex into the Town and circumvent
the moratorium currently in place with Charleston County. She suggested that the
community is historic and should not be “forced” to annexing into the Town to
receive water and sewer. She expressed concern that this could mean that
properties could be developed that would not be in keeping with the historic
character of the neighborhood. She asked for consideration of the moratorium.

6.   Business
     A. PUBLIC HEARING, 3638 Zacoma Drive, TMS No. 614-09-00-014, R-08-25,
         Request to Annex and Zone R-3. Staff Report

         Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

         Mr. Samuel Morris, attorney for the applicant and owner, reviewed the
     request with the Commission.

          Ms. Sherry Fortner, 3642 Zacoma Road, suggested that the proposed
     zoning is not in keeping with the existing development of the neighborhood.
     She expressed concern with development affecting the Lieben canal and
     increase stormwater runoff. She also expressed concern with child safety with
     increasing the amount of traffic on the roadway. She suggested that the
     proposed zoning would be too intense and that the R-2 zoning would be more
     appropriate. She stated that she has a petition from neighbors in opposition
     to the request.

       Mr. Robert Chestnut, owner, stated that the property is currently zoning
     MHS in Charleston County that would allow the subdivision of the property.
                                                            Planning Commission

He stated that because the property is contiguous, annexation into the Town
must be requested. He stated that because of Town requirements, the R-3
zoning was requested in order to be able to subdivide.

    Mr. Chris Fortner, 3642 Zacoma Rd, stated that currently there are no
properties on Zacoma have been subdivided, but there are lots on other
property in the neighborhood that would be subdivided.

    Ms. Marilyn Simmons, Summerville, stated that she was the previous
owner of 3638 Zacoma Drive. She stated that previously she would have been
able to subdivide the property and remain in the county. She suggested that
the property should not have to be annexed in order to receive water and
sewer.

    Ms. Kimberly Hudson, 3649 Zacoma Drive, stated that she is opposed to
the request. She expressed concern with increased traffic.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing
and moved to Commission discussion and deliberation.

     Ms. Ireland moved for denial. Mr. Davis seconded the motion. All in favor.

E.    PUBLIC HEARING, Dunes West PD, TMS No. 594-05-00-219, R-12-25,
      Amendment to Dunes West PD to allow for Self-Service Storage Facility.
      Staff Report

     Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     Mr. Brett Skinner, developer, reviewed the request with the Commission.

     Mr. George Hynd, 1691 Heather Grove Lane, asked the request to be
denied. He suggested that there are 1535 storage units available in the near
vicinity and suggested that additional storage units are not needed. He also
expressed concern with the proposed height and suggested that other
commercial areas along Hwy 41 are mainly single-story buildings.

    Ms. Valerie Johnson, 2338 Kingsgate Lane, expressed concern with this
intensive use in close proximity to a residential neighborhood. She expressed
concern with light pollution, late night noise, devaluation of property values,
and concerns of not being aesthetically pleasing. She asked for the request to
be denied.

    Ms. Carol Montgomery, 226 Fair Sailing Rd, stated that she is opposed to
the request. She suggested that this proposed development would not be in
keeping with the surrounding neighborhood or Hwy 41.

F.    PUBLIC HEARING, Seaside Farms PD, TMS No. 561-00-00-158, R-13-25,
      Amendment to Seaside Farms PD, proposal to add one additional Hotel
      in Neighborhood Retail Tract. Staff Report

     Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     Mr. Scott Benedict, applicant, reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Ms. Kathy McKee, 1247 Palmetto Peninsula Drive, expressed concern with
the request and suggested that an additional hotel would not be the best use
of this property and not an appropriate location. She suggested that the
proposed use would not benefit or provide improvements to the community.
She expressed concern with the removal of trees on the property. She asked
that the request be denied.

    Ms. Laura Del Duca, 1272 Wild Olive Drive, expressed concern with the
request and that another hotel would increase traffic in the area.

   Ms. MaryAnn Becker, 1900 Grandview Court, expressed concern with the
proposal increasing traffic and that it is not appropriate for the neighborhood.

     There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

     Mr. Ferrara moved for approval. Mr. Lacy seconded the motion.

    Ms. Ireland moved to amend the motion to include the condition that the
proposed use meets the current boutique hotel requirements. Ms. Mansour
seconded the amendment. All in favor.

   Mr. Bennett called for a vote on the motion as amended. Motion passed
on a 6 to 1 vote with Mr. Ferrara, Mr. Smyth, Mr. Bennett, Mr. Lacy, Ms.
Mansour, and Ms. Ireland in favor; Mr. Davis opposed.
