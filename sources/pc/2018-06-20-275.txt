6. Requests
   A. R-12-18, Request to amend the Pepper Tract PD, Planned Development
      District Ordinance (Ordinance No. 11048, as amended), by removing the
      square footage limitations on buildings associated with specific uses as
      identified in “Attachment E: Permitted uses for RC and I Zoning
      Classifications”, located along Darrell Creek Trail/US Highway 17 N. and
      Chandler Road / known as the Pepper Tract Development. TMS No.: 615-
      00-00-044 and 615-00-00-173

         Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment
   1).

   Mr. Wren asked if there is a height restriction. Mr. Rutherford answered that
   he is not sure if there is a different height restriction from the Town’s 35 feet.

   Mr. Tim Cook, representative for the applicant, reviewed the request with the
   Commission.

   Mr. Gates asked the reason for waiting two years to amend the PD. Mr. Cook
   answered that after trying to market the property, it was evident that this would
   be needed and the better approach would be to amend the PD as opposed to
   requesting variances for each individual parcel.

   Mr. Neal asked how many potential sales were lost. Mr. Cook answered that
   there is currently no commercial for the requested area. Mr. Neal asked what

types of uses are being considered. Mr. Cook answered that they have had
inquiries for various uses including a professional swim facility that would need
an approximately 10,000 square foot building.
Mr. Wren asked the height restriction. Mr. Cook answered that he is not
positive, but suggested that it is 45 feet.

Mr. James Lawrenceson, 46 State Street, stated that they have had several
inquiries, but once the information is provided, they do not follow up. He stated
that as currently zoned, most businesses wanting to locate on the property
would have to request a variance.

Mr. Neal asked if a commercial use would require DRB approval. Mr. Rutherford
answered in the affirmative.

Mr. Steven Cadelono expressed concern with what types of businesses would
locate on the property.

Mr. Cook stated that there are no definitive types of uses proposed for the
property at this time, but that the allowed uses are not proposed to change. He
stated that the request is to only allow the additional square footage.

There being no further comments, Mr. Wren closed public comment.

Ms. Smith expressed concern with removing the square footage requirements
for all use types.

Mr. Neal suggested that much of the property is restricted. He stated that the
property would still be capped at 195,000 square feet of commercial use.

Mr. Gates asked if the current infrastructure supports commercial uses. Mr.
Rutherford answered that the entire PD is served by Mount Pleasant
Waterworks (MPW).

Ms. Gregg asked if the PD would have to be further amended if more square
footage of commercial was desired. Mr. Rutherford answered in the
affirmative.

C. R-13-18. Request to amend the Dunes West PD, Planned Development
   District Ordinance (Ordinance adopted 9-4-90, as amended), by rezoning
   33 parcels from R-1, Low Density Residential District, to Dunes West PD,
   located off Bessemer Road/Known as Covington at Park West. TMS No.:
   583-00-00-003, 583-03-00-254 thru -281, and 583-03-00-286

   Ms. Canon reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).
                                                             Planning Commission
                                                                    June 20, 2018

Mr. Gates asked about the setbacks. Ms. Canon clarified that the side setback
is measured between the building lines and not from the property line.

Ms. Smith asked the reason for the request. Ms. Canon answered that it would
provide more flexibility on the placement of the structure on the lot.
Mr. Chris Donato, representative for the applicant, reviewed the request with
the Commission. He stated that the setback change would allow for a different
building form and would be consistent with the other lots in the PD. Ms. Smith
asked if it would provide footprint flexibility. Mr. Donato answered in the
affirmative and stated that the lots would be consistent with the remainder of
the property in the PD.

Ms. Gregg asked the reason for requesting a lesser setback than the Town’s R-3
zoning. Mr. Donato answered that the setbacks requested would be consistent
with the remainder of the R-3 lots in the PD.

Mr. Wren asked the number of R-3 units available. Ms. Canon answered that
there are approximately 300 units remaining in the DW-PD.

There being no comments, Mr. Wren closed the public hearing.

Ms. Gregg asked how long the units have been constructed on Brightwood. Ms.
Canon answered that it has been approximately eighteen months.

Mr. Wren expressed concern with adding units to an existing PD and the
potential ramifications from this. He stated that as previously seen, this has
been a concern, particularly for this area.

   Mr. Neal moved for approval of the request. Ms. Smith seconded the motion.

Mr. Wren stated that he is not in favor of the request because of a concern with
making a change to the PD.

Ms. Smith stated that she is not in favor of increasing land in this area and
suggested that the desired outcome can be accomplished with the existing
zoning.

E. Proposal to amend Chapter 156, Zoning Code, pertaining to Signs by adding
   political signs to Section 156.151, Definition of Sign and adding a new
   paragraph (Q) to section 156.162 prohibiting signs located within the public
   right-of-way and providing for their removal by authorized officials.
                                                               Planning Commission
                                                                      June 20, 2018
                                                                         Page 7 of 8



   Mr. Prause reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

Mr. Neal asked how this would be enforced, particularly during election time.
Mr. Prause answered that political signs are referenced in another section of the
Code of Ordinances. He stated that the amendment would allow the code
enforcement officer jurisdiction.

Mr. Lehman asked if there is a fine for a violation. Mr. Prause answered in the
affirmative.

Mr. Cuttino asked if there is a fine for not picking up political signs. Mr. Prause
answered in the negative.

Mr. Wren asked if there has been consideration of imposing a fine for not
removing a political sign. Mr. Prause answered in the negative. He stated that
the Town is working with the County on an intergovernmental agreement for
policing political signs that are on county property.

Mr. Pagliarini stated that this would allow for better enforcement of the sign
regulations.

There being no comments, Mr. Wren closed the public hearing.

     Ms. Smith moved for approval of the request. Mr. Neal seconded the motion.
All in favor.

F. Proposal to amend Chapter 156, Zoning Code, pertaining to
   Telecommunications Towers by adding a new section 156.121 regulating
   Small Wireless Facilities.

    Mr. Prause reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).
He stated that after consulting with legal counsel, it was suggested that more
work should be done prior to implementation. He reviewed the options
available to the Commission regarding with the public hearing.

Ms. Smith suggested that the public hearing should remain open.
                                                               Planning Commission
                                                                      June 20, 2018
                                                                         Page 8 of 8

   Mr. Jack Mitchell, AT&T, stated that they desire the amendment to allow the
   small cell communication towers. He suggested that the model ordinance has
   been vetted by several cell companies and the Municipal Association of South
   Carolina (MASC). He stated that the small cell towers would allow better
   capacity and faster service for mobile devices. He stated that he would be
   willing to work with staff on this issue.

   Mr. Gates asked if the Town has the right to deny a tower. Mr. Prause answered
   that it is a matter of permitted use and a cell tower can co-locate on existing
   towers and structures. Mr. Gates asked if the Town can deny co-location. Mr.
   Prause answered that the requirements are currently outlined in our existing
   ordinances.

   Mr. Wren suggested that the public hearing be kept open.

   Mr. Neal asked if a motion was needed to keep the public hearing open. Mr.
   Pagliarini answered in the negative.
