C. Zoning Case R-03-21: Request to amend the I’On PD, Planned Development
   District Ordinance, by increasing the allowable residential unit allocation
   by 13 units, to increase the total allowable residential units for I’On from
   762 to 775 units with all unutilized units being allocated to the property
   located at Jane Jacobs Street, bearing TMS No. 535-06-00-652. Further,
   there is a request included to relocate Civic Lot CV-8 and to restrict its use
   so that it may be used only by the I’On Assembly and its members for I’On
   sponsored activities and that no more than (1) building or other structure
   can be constructed on Lot CV-8, which single building or other structure
   shall not exceed 1,000 square feet in total floor area. TMS No’s. 535-06- 00-
   652 and 535-06-00-877

This item was withdrawn.

D. Rezoning Case R-04-21: Request to annex and zone R-2, Low Density
   Residential District, an approximately 11.54-acre tract of land located at
   1240 Six Mile Road and known as Watts Mobile Home Park. Parcel is not
   proposed to be included in the SB-OD, Sweetgrass Basket Overlay District.
   The applicant proposes redevelopment of the site with approximately 32
   single family detached residences subject to the Town’s R-2 standards
   (which allows for 8,000 sf lots). TMS No. 558-00-00-130

   Ms. Reed reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

   Mr. Chris Donato reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Mr. George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Road, stated that the existing
development has been in place for over 40 years. He suggested that changing
the zoning would change the character of the development and the
neighborhood. He expressed concern that there was no interconnectivity to the
adjacent lots. He asked that the community character be maintained.

   Mr. Donato clarified that the proposed development would remain at 3 units
per acre and would have interconnectivity.
