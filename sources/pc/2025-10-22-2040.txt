B.    PUBLIC HEARING, Zoning Code Text Amendment Amendment to
      Mount Pleasant Zoning Code, modifying development standards for
      accessory structures and Neighborhood Preservation Overlay District
      Draft Text Staff Report

     Ms. Reed reviewed the draft ordinance with the Commission.

    Mr. Ryan Castle, Association of Realtors, expressed concern with some
aspects of the draft ordinance. He suggested that the height restrictions of
the accessory structure to no higher than the principal structure could mean
more tear down of existing homes in order to have the additional height. He
also suggested that this could reduce the amount of affordable housing
because of limiting the number of accessory structures. He suggested
another aspect is the new tree protection ordinance that would further limit
the size o homes. He suggested that the BAR would more affect those that
have not renovated their homes due to the additional restrictions.

     There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

     Ms. Ireland moved for approval with the recommendation to adjust the
BAR for R-3 and R-4 zoning as discussed. Mr. Davis seconded the motion. All
in favor.

    D.   PRESENTATION, Engineering and Development Services Department
         Continuing to highlight topics for Planning Commission discussion--
         Commercial Development Process and Density Standards for Residential
         Subdivisions Staff Presentation

       Mr. Mitchell gave a presentation on the commercial development process
    and residential density standards for subdivisions.

    The Commission discussed density and what changes in the ordinance should
    be considered to have better deliberate, smart development and
    redevelopment.

    The Commission agreed that subdivision from an access easement should not
    be allowed. They also agreed that a subdivision should only be allowed for
    property meeting a one-acre threshold.

The Commission agreed to hold a special meeting to discuss and review a draft
zoning ordinance changing the density allowed within Rural Residential from 3 units
per acre to 1 unit per acre on Monday, November 17th.
