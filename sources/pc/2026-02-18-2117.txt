B.    PUBLIC HEARING, R-09-26, Sweetgrass HOA Space, TMS Nos. 558-15-00-
      260, 558-00-00-401, Request to amend Wilson Tract PD, for additional
      uses within Sweetgrass Amenity Area. Staff Report

     Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

    Mr. Elliot Hood, president of Sweetgrass HOA, reviewed the request with
the Commission.

     Ms. Pam Leonard, 1838 Falling Creek Circle, stated that her home is
adjacent to the proposed property. She expressed concern that the removal
of the trees would cause more flooding.

    Ms. Linda Choate, 1888 Falling Creek Circle, expressed concern with the
wildlife that would be displaced if the request is approved. She also expressed
concern with the increased noise level that could occur with this property
being developed. She expressed concern with more flooding.

    Ms. Holly Giles-Regan, 1842 Falling Creek Circle, stated that she is opposed
to the request and suggested that the HOA members have not voted for this
request. She suggested that a number of trees were removed with the pond
that was installed. She suggested that the removal of the trees would cause
additional noise, removal of wildlife, etc.

    Ms. Jessica Maginsky, 1834 Falling Creek Circle, stated that she is opposed
to the request and agrees with the previous comments.

    Mr. Stan Sutton, 1846 Falling Creek Circle, stated that he supports the
request for the additional amenities.

    Ms. Claudia McNeil, 1307 Horseshoe Bend, suggested that the required
buffer should not be removed. She also suggested that the amenities would
displace the existing wildlife. She suggested that the noise would increase
without having the buffer and the trees.

    Ms. Aimee Ratner, 1307 Horseshoe Bend, suggested that the sign was not
placed in front of the neighborhood and was only put up last night. She
suggested that more notification would have meant more attendance from the
neighborhood.

    Ms. Jenna Nelson, 1850 Falling Creek Circle, expressed concern with the
wildlife going away if the property is developed.

   Ms. Ashley Pelletier, 1312 Old Mill, stated she supports the request. She
suggested that without the amendment there is no means to add a trail or
other amenities that could benefit the neighborhood.

     There being no further comments, Ms. Ireland closed the public hearing.

     Mr. Davis moved for denial. Ms. Mansour seconded the motion. Motion
passed on a 4 to 2 vote with Mr. Smyth, Mr. Davis, Ms. Mansour, and Ms.
Ireland in favor; Mr. Barber and Ms. Sanford opposed.
