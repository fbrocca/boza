6.   Requests
     A. Proposal to amend Sections 156.340 - 156.345 of Chapter 156, Zoning
         Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances, pertaining to Short
         Term Rentals. Proposed is to amend the entire ordinance to address
         several concerns, including but not limited to such things as increasing
         the parking requirements for STR properties, implementing minimum
         night rental requirements, increasing the application fees for whole-
         house rentals, including civil penalties for violations, and limiting the
         number of permits allowed per property owner.

        Ms. Reed reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

         Mr. David O’Reilly, 1368 Founders Way, expressed concern with there
     being a cap on the number of permits allowed to be issued as it deprives
     owners from being able to have a short-term rental and benefit from that
     income. He suggested that the number of permits issued to an individual
     should be limited. He stated that he is in favor of having minimum rental
     requirements for STR permit holders. He suggested that having more permits
     available would bring in more revenue for the Town.

         Ms. Lori Bauer, 506 Live Oak Dr, stated that she has been a STR operator
     since 2016. She suggested that the transfer of a permit should not be
     restricted to immediate family members, but should be allowed to be
     transferred to a qualified person as deemed by the owner.

         Mr. David Vernon, 2108 St. James Drive, agreed that there should be a
     minimum rental period. He suggested that the limiting the number of permits
     per individual might not be enforceable and should be reviewed. He agreed
     that there should be civil penalties as well as allowing a permit to be
     transferred to a family member.

         Mr. Eric Vincent, 242 6th Avenue, stated that his concerns have been
     addressed through the proposed changes. He expressed concern that limiting
     the number of permits issued to an individual might be too restrictive. He
     thanked the Commission for their work.

         Mr. Bill Macchio, 1319 Lake Mallard Blvd, suggested that if a transfer fee
     is assessed, it should be a nominal fee. He agrees with the ability to transfer
     the permit to family members.

     E.    Further discussion on the proposed Mixed Neighborhood Floating
           Zoning as previously presented at the January 18th Special Planning
           Commission Meeting

          Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Commission.

        The Commission discussed some of the concerns with having a floating
     zone.

         The Commission suggested that the requirements within table 7-2 of the
     Comprehensive Plan should be used for the floating zone; there should be
     distance between the hubs and the NM district boundaries; there should be a
     maximum number of acres for the entire site, a minimum amount of
     commercial use required, and there should be a maximum number of units
     allowed in addition to a maximum number of bedrooms allowed.

         The Commission asked that information on the zoning code rewrite be
     provided in advance of the meeting so it can be thoroughly reviewed.
