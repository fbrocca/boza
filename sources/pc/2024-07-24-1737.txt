6.   Requests
     A. Text Amendment Proposal to amend Section 156.110 of Chapter 156,
         Zoning Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances, pertaining to
         Accessory Uses. Proposed is to amend the ordinance to expand Home-
         Based Businesses to allow certain outdoor instructional activities,
         including but not limited to swimming lessons.

         Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Commission.

        Ms. JoAnn Gore, 683 Kent St, expressed concern with the amount of noise
     from those taking the swim lessons. She also expressed concern with the
     additional traffic because of the lessons. She stated that another concern is
     the flooding of the roadway and that the increased traffic could cause
     additional flooding due to the wear on the roadway.

         Mr. Eugene Slater, Greenhill resident, suggested that the increased noise
     and traffic from the swim lessons has impacted the quality of life for the
     neighborhood. He suggested that there should be some type of noise
     cancellation required such as the pool being domed.

        Ms. Judy Tolly, 746 Spark St., expressed concern with the tranquility of the
     neighborhood being negatively impacted with the home-based business
     expansion to allow swimming lesson.

    Ms. Gale Lesesne, 721 York St, stated that she was “shocked” to hear about
this use being proposed for the neighborhood. She suggested that there are
plenty of pools around Mount Pleasant that can be used for swim lessons. She
expressed concern with child safety and the possibility of children getting into
the pool unsupervised.

    Mr. Ryan Sacko, Johns Island, stated that children are naturally noisy and
rambunctious. He stated that the swim lessons would be a life-saving measure
that is a great community need.

   Ms. Shannon Smalls, 683 Kent St, suggested that having the swim lessons
within a residential neighborhood is not appropriate. She suggested that the
amount of noise is very disruptive for the neighborhood on a daily basis.

    Mr. Larry Keys, 3101 Yachtsman Drive, stated that his grandchild has taken
swim lessons at this location and it has been very quiet. He suggested that the
noise might be associated with some other activity rather than the swim
lessons taking place.

    Mr. Steve Manti, 675 Kent St, stated the pool is fenced in by two fences
and that they have taken extra precaution in maintaining safety. He stated
that they have children as well that enjoy the pool during the day and can at
times be very rowdy and loud.

   Mr. Nick Scover, 850 Pine Shadow, stated that they have toddlers and have
had a hard time finding swim lessons. He suggested that having more options
should be allowed.

    Ms. Frances Laughton, McClellanville, expressed concern with the danger
of children that cannot swim and that for children between the ages of 1-4
years old, drowning is a major cause of death. She stated that there is a need
for additional swim lessons in the area. She suggested that the lessons are
more survival lessons than swim lessons for these toddlers.

    Ms. Sara Goldstein, James Island, stated that her children take private
swim lessons. She stated that more options for swim lessons is necessary for
better safety. She stated that there is a lengthy wait list for lessons.

    Ms. Julie Fowler, 2317 Darts Cove, stated that she is a swim instructor, and
there is a need for additional swim lessons. She stated that children with
water trauma need additional care when teaching them. She stated that
drowning is a real concern, especially in this area. She suggested that
additional swim lessons should be allowed and is an important and vital
service.

    Ms. Jody Manti, 675 Kent St, stated that they want to offer swim lessons
for the residents in Mount Pleasant. She stated that it is a necessary service.
She stated that she tries to be cognizant and respectful of her neighbors.

    Ms. Claire Keys, 3101 Dawson Drive, stated that her experience with swim
lessons has been that the swim lessons are more one-on-one. She suggested
that there is a way to allow these lessons that does not impact the
neighborhood.

    Ms. Caitlynn Rainey, 423 Ansley Court, stated that her child has taken swim
lessons and it has been a valuable resource. She stated that her toddler can
now swim on his own and it is a relief. She suggested that it would be a
disservice if these small home-based businesses were not allowed.

     There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

     Ms. Ireland moved for approval to allow expansion of the uses for home-
based businesses, with the provision that there is a time limitation of operation
from 10am-4 pm, with no more than two patrons at a time, with parking
located on site and to the rear of structure. Mr. Smyth seconded the motion.
All in favor with Mr. Davis abstaining.

D. Sketch Plan: SP-02-24 Review of Sketch Plan for 2.89-acre parcel located
   in Hibben Phase 7 of the Belle Hall Subdivision. Proposed is to create nine
   Single-Family lots on current TMS Nos. 537-08-00-423, 537-08-00-357,
   property is currently zoned PD, Planned Development.

   Ms. Noble and Mr. Aton reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

    Mr. William O’Neal, civil engineer, and Mr. Alan Coleman, Plantation
Partners, reviewed the request with the Commission. Mr. O’Neal stated that
they are amenable to all staff comments with the exception of extending
Cobblestone Way as the property between Cobblestone and this property is
owned by the HOA and would require a majority vote of the HOA in order to
construct and connect the roadway to this proposed phase.

   Mr. Ferrara moved for approval as presented including all staff comments
with exception of extending the roadway connection to Cobblestone Way. Ms.
Mansour seconded the motion.

    Ms. Ireland moved to amend the motion to include installation of a
hammerhead termination of Cobblestone Way and installation of pedestrian
and bike connection as recommended by staff at this meeting. Mr. Bennett
seconded the motion. All in favor.

Mr. Bennett called for a vote on the motion as amended. All in favor.
