     located at 3745 North Highway 17 (Town Property at Lieben Road). TMS
     No.: 614-00-00-033

        Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission
  (attachments 1 & 2).

  Mr. Jody Peele stated that the Town is in the process of securing a consultant to
  complete a Public Services master plan.

  Ms. Smith suggested that it would be beneficial for the Town to reach out to the
  community to provide more information on what is proposed in the future,
  transportation issues, etc. Mr. Peele answered that a better idea of how the
  property would be utilized would be known once the master plan was
  completed. He stated that there are no plans to use this property as a transfer
  yard.

  Mr. Neal asked if it is beneficial to defer annexation and zoning until the master

  be better to have it annexed and zoned within the Town.

  Mr. Peele answered that there are five divisions working out of the Six Mile
  Facility: stormwater, waste management, facilities, grounds, and infrastructure.
  Ms. Gregg asked what a transfer yard entails. Mr. Peele answered that all
  garbage goes to a landfill or commercial drop-off site. He stated that any
  vegetative material is transported to the Six Mile Facility, reloaded and then
  transported to the Bees Ferry landfill. He stated that by doing this, it keeps as
  many vehicles in Mount Pleasant working throughout the Town.

  Mr. Darien Mackey, 3605 Purple Martin, asked if the requested property would
  be used as a transfer station. Mr. Wren answered that it was indicated that it
  would not be used as a transfer yard.
