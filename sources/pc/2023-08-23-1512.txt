6.   Requests
     A. R-07-23 Proposal to amend the Central Mt. Pleasant PD, Planned
         Development. Requested amendment specifically applies to a 22.69-
                                                            Planning Commission

      acre tract of land made up of two parcels in the Neighborhood Tract,
      identified by TMS# 560-00-00-068 and 560-00-00-066. Proposed
      amendment would conditionally allow up to 109 CCRC, Continuing Care
      Retirement Community units within the existing CCRC.

     Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

    Mr. James Wilson, representative for the applicant, and Mr. Patrick Allen,
Liberty Senior Living, reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Mr. Peter Berotti, 1361 Heron Bay Loop, expressed concern that the
proposed street is more of an alley than an actual street.

    Mr. Emory Haseldon, Darrell Creek subdivision, stated that he was a
previous resident of South Bay and is currently on the waiting list. He
suggested that South Bay is the only facility that provides four areas of care.

    Mr. Don Wineheart, 1571 Heron Bay Loop, expressed the same concern as
Mr. Berotti regarding the extension of the street as it currently is more of an
alley than a road. He expressed concern with increased traffic along the
“alley” and there could be traffic accidents.

   Ms. Roberta Berotti, 1361 Heron Bay Loop, expressed concern with parking
and that there is not sufficient parking for the residents. She asked that this
be addressed.

    Ms. Pat Goodman, 1400 Liberty Midtown Drive, agreed that parking is an
issue. She expressed concern that there is not sufficient staffing for the
amenities that are offered and suggested that this would add to the parking
issue as well.

     There being no further comments, Ms. Smith closed the public hearing.

   Mr. Lacy moved for approval. Ms. Ireland seconded the motion. Motion
passed on a 7 to 1 vote with Mr. Smyth, Mr. Bennett, Mr. Koedding, Mr. Lacy,
Ms. Smith, Ms. Mansour and Ms. Ireland in favor; Mr. Davis opposed.

     Mr. Jonathan Ford, 2906 Canyon Lane, stated that this is a part of the
Phillips historical community and suggested that the rezoning could affect the
