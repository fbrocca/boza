c.   Rezoning Case: R-28-20, Request to annex and zone an approximately
     1.00-acre tract of land located in the Ten Mile Community to R-4,
     Medium Density Residential District, located at 3804 Garden Hill Road.
     TMS No. 614-00-00-041

     Ms. Reed reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

    Ms. Pat Eardley and Mr. Link Eardley, applicant, reviewed the request with
the Commission.

    Mr. Cory Austin, 3784 Garden Hill Road expressed concern with the
rezoning request. He stated that although Habitat for Humanity is a good non-
profit group, the request would change the character of the surrounding area
as there are only single-family homes. He also expressed concern with the
additional homes causing more traffic and damage to the already
deteriorating roadway. He stated that he is opposed to the request.

    Mr. Waverly Pickney, 991 Gadsdenville Road, expressed concern with the
amount of increased development in the Ten Mile Community and
surrounding areas, particularly with the amount of increased traffic. He stated
that he is opposed to the request as it will disrupt and destroy the historical
and cultural character of the settlement community. He suggested that the
same respect should be given for the settlement communities as is given for

    Mr. Timothy Ascue, 977 Gadsdenville Road, expressed concerns with the

    Ms. Abrea Pryor, 3819 Garden Hill Road expressed concern with the
