4.   New Business
     A. Review of outstanding questions related to drafts of Subchapters A:
        Introduction, B: Zoning Districts, and C: Use Regulations of the Town’s
        Zoning Code Rewrite.

        Ms. Cousino reviewed the changes to the zoning code with the
     Commission.

     Mr. Smyth entered at this time (3:47 pm).

    The Commission agreed that the NP-OD district should be expanded to
other neighborhoods that were built before 1979 to help maintain the
character of those neighborhoods.

      The Commission discussed the uses allowed in the new Mixed
Neighborhood District (NM). The Commission agreed that professional office
uses such as CPA offices, attorneys, etc. should be included as allowed uses.
The Commission agreed that the recommendation of the Comprehensive Plan
to limit the rezoning of commercial property to a residential zoning should be
kept in mind. Mixed use buildings will be allowed but not required within the
NM zoning.

    The Commission discussed the definition for a family and maximum
occupancy. The Commission agreed to eliminate the definition of a family and
to use the number of bedrooms for maximum occupancy requirements.

    The Commission discussed duplexes and where they should be allowed as
a conditional use. The Commission agreed to the proposed requirements for
duplexes.

   The Commission discussed triplex and quadplexes as a special exception.
The Commission agreed that triplex and quadplex should not be allowed.

    Ms. Cousino reviewed the potential for a new zoning district with the
Commission. The Commission agreed to the new neighborhood zoning and
excluding driveways. They also agreed that the new zoning district should
have similar requirements as the NP-OD and buildings should be limited to
3,000 square feet.

B.   Preliminary discussion on Subchapter D: Trees, Buffers, and Screening

    Ms. Cousino reviewed the proposed changes to the critical line buffer and
setbacks. The Commission agreed with the changes.

The Commission agreed that the critical buffer requirements should apply to
each lot and be required to come into compliance if the improvements are
50% or more. The Commission agreed that existing non-conforming structures
should be removed if improved 50% or more.

       Ms. Cousino reviewed the special right-of-way buffer with the
    Commission. The Commission agreed to a 50-foot buffer requirement.

        Ms. Cousino reviewed some of the proposed changes to the tree
    protection ordinance and stated that more information on potential changes
    would be forthcoming from the consultants.

         The Commission agreed that the majority of the tree protection
    requirements should be moved to the Land Development Regulations section
    of the ordinance.

         Mr. McCormick entered at this time (4:47 pm).

        The Commission agreed that protected tree size and species and the
    mitigation required for tree removal should be closely reviewed and changes
    considered to simplify and improve the process.

    C.   Next Steps

    Ms. Cousino stated that the changes discussed would be completed and would
    then come back to the Commission for a public hearing.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:52 pm.
