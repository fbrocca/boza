        Ms. Smith moved for approval of the request. Ms. Ireland seconded the
     motion. All in favor.

     B. Sketch Plan SP-08-20, Sketch Plan approval for 753 Winthrop Street, a
        proposal to subdivide one lot into two (2) buildable lots. Located at 753
        Winthrop Street. TMS No. 532-06-00-252

           Mr. Mitchell reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment
     1).

        Mr. Wofford Stribling, Patriots Civil Engineering, reviewed the request with
     the Commission.

         Ms. Smith moved for approval of the request. Mr. Wren seconded the
     motion. Motion passed on a 8 to 1 vote with Mr. Koedding, Mr. Campbell, Mr.
     Davis, Mr. Gates, Mr. Wren, Ms. Smith, Mr. McCormick, and Ms. Ireland in
     favor; Mr. Bennett opposed.

     C. Continued discussion on implementing an Environmental Policy Program
        for the Town. Staff will introduce an initiative to create a homeowner’s
        guide for better environmental living with a focus on providing common
        sense information to help detached single family homeowners live more
        environmentally friendly.

           Ms. Campbell reviewed the program with the Commission (attachment 1).

          The Commission discussed how the importance of environmentally
     friendly community can be accomplished. Mr. Mitchell and Ms. Campbell
     stated that staff would finalize the homeowner’s guide and bring back to the
     Commission.

     The Commission received this as information.

     D. Planning Commission requested discussion to address stormwater
        reviews and a possible procedural change particularly involving new
        construction in Old Mount Pleasant. Staff will be discussing the Special
        Stormwater Management Area Ordinance, the Impervious Surface
        Overlay District, and the internal procedure for reviewing stormwater
        management plans for new development inside this area.

          Mr. Mitchell reviewed the proposed changes with the Commission
     (attachment 1).

     The Commission agreed that staff would bring back a recommendation of
     amendments to Zoning Code Chapter 152, Flood Damage Prevention, and any
     associated amendments for consistency.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 6:10 pm.
