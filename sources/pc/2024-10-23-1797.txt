2.   Correspondence
3.   General Public Comment

    Mr. James McKee, 1247 Palmetto Peninsula Drive, spoke regarding the Town’s
tree protection ordinance and the Community Forestry Master Plan (CFMP). He
stated that he has read both documents and suggested the following
considerations:
   ➢ Are the CMFP recommendations incorporated into the ordinance?
   ➢ Designation of a commission for direct oversight of tree protection and
      removal.
   ➢ Alignment of enforcement of tree protection and removal for the Town.

    Ms. Kerry Fink, 1772 Journey Lane, suggested that comments should not be
allowed until after the draft plan has been reviewed. She suggested that there
should be a senior community center near her neighborhood that would service
that area of the Town. She expressed concern with the amount of traffic on Faison
Road and suggested that Faison Road is not equipped to handle the amount of
traffic occurring on a daily basis.

     Ms. Diedre Kennedy, 971 Cliffwood Drive, spoke regarding the tree ordinance.
She suggested that the current tree ordinance places more emphasis on mitigation
rather than planting. She suggested that more planting of replacement trees
should be considered, and the mitigation fee-in-lieu of planting should be
increased. She also suggested that more enforcement is necessary to prevent
illegal removal of trees. She suggested that there should be a task force established
to review the draft ordinance.

Ms. Lisa Schniptke, 185 Mossy Oak Way, suggested that regarding enforcement of
the tree removals, there should be one person or entity to oversee the tree removal
requests. She suggested that there should be a higher percentage of replacement
required. She also suggested that there should be certified arborists reviewing
requests and tree removals should not fall under the Engineering Division and
certified arborists should be reviewing tree removal requests. She suggested that
historic pine trees should be protected as well. She suggested that the fee-in-lieu
of planting fee should be specifically allocated for replanting trees throughout the
Town.

    Mr. Jim Scarfo, 1772 Journey Lane, expressed concern with the current
development in Carolina Park and suggested that there should be a forum for such
comments.

   Ms. Michele Strauss, 545 Veranda View, suggested that there should be trained
and certified staff reviewing the tree removal requests. She suggested that the
GREEN Commission should be given oversight over tree removals as well.

     Mr. David Quick, Chair of GREEN Commission, spoke regarding tree removal.
He suggested that trees should be protected within the Town. He suggested a task
force should be implemented to determine a solution for tree removals within the
Town. He suggested that a tree commission would be a better option rather than
the GREEN Commission as it would have a single focus. He suggested that the Town
is “losing control of tree preservation” and this should be a main focus.

    Ms. Shannon Harris, 1277 Calais Drive, stated that she is a certified arborist.
She suggested that the Town’s tree canopy is diminishing. She suggested that the
tree mitigation amount is reasonable, but there should be an emphasis on
replanting rather than paying for replacement trees. She suggested that lowering
the size required for replanting trees would make replanting more feasible. She
suggested that replacement trees should be allowed to be planted on neighboring

3.   Requests
     A. Public Hearing and Review of Chapter 156, Zoning Code of the Town of
         Mount Pleasant, Draft Subchapters A: Introduction B: Zoning Districts,
         C: Use Regulations, D: Buffers and Screening, E: Tree Protection, F:
         Parking and Loading, G: Signs, H: Building and Site Design, I: Procedures,
         J: Reviewing and Decision-Making Bodies, K: Nonconformities, L:
         Enforcement, M: Rules of Interpretation and Measurement, N:
         Definitions and Acronyms, O: Building Permit Allocation System.

        Ms. Reed reviewed the Community Forestry Master Plan (CFMP) with the
     Commission and how it pertains to the current and draft zoning code.

         Mr. Andrus suggested that smaller trees have better sustainability than a
     larger tree. Additionally, the smaller trees grow at a faster rate than the larger
     tree and therefore, would be larger and healthier than the larger tree. He also
     expressed concern that there are properties that have the trees removed as
     well as grass, replacing the grass with artificial grass that then causes runoff
     issues.

         Mr. Ryan Zimmer, 1123 Old Course Lane, thanked the Commission for this
     discussion. He expressed concern with commercial tree removal and
     suggested the tree removal requirements for commercial development should
     be reviewed and possible stricter requirements considered.

        Mr. Mike Jacob, 1017 N. Shem Drive, referred to §156.424 pertaining to
     accessory dwelling units height being increased to 25 feet. He stated that he
     supports the changes proposed to this section.

    Mr. McKee stated that as he reads the draft zoning code, it does not
require replacement trees for historic trees to be the same category and/or
species and suggested that this should be considered.

Ms. Reed noted that this is included in the draft zoning code but could be
better clarified within the code.

    Ms. Kennedy suggested that tree protection zones should be required for
any work done on the property including removal of another tree, even if the
tree being removed is not a protected tree.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

     Ms. Ireland moved to recommend consideration of increasing the street
frontage or lot width for properties within the Neighborhood Character
Overlay District to prevent more subdivisions resulting in narrow cookie cutter
lots. Ms. Mansour seconded the motion. All in favor.

   Ms. Ireland moved to recommend increasing the open space requirement
from 5% to 10% in the Redevelopment Center Overlay District. Mr. Ferrara
seconded the motion. All in favor.

    Ms. Ireland moved to recommend that the accessory structure height be
maintained at a 20-foot maximum and to prevent the height of the accessory
structure to be higher than the main structure. Mr. Lacy seconded the motion.
Motion passed on a 7 to 2 vote with Mr. Smyth, Mr. Bennett, Mr. Lacy, Mr.
Davis, Ms. Mansour, Mr. Tinkey and Ms. Ireland in favor; Mr. Ferrar and Ms.
Sanford opposed.

    Ms. Ireland moved to recommend consideration of increasing the parking
ratios especially residential allowances and to require the excess or portion of
the excess to be pervious. Motion failed due to the lack of a second.

   Ms. Ireland moved to recommend reviewing the residential parking ratios
and consider increasing the requirement with a portion of the increase to be
pervious. Mr. Ferrara seconded the motion. All in favor.

     Ms. Ireland moved to recommend changing the setback description
outlined on page 370 from the fifth story to the fourth story. Mr. Tinkey
seconded the motion. All in favor.

    Ms. Ireland moved to recommend prohibiting murals as the content cannot
be restricted. Mr. Lacy seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 6 to 3 vote
with Mr. Smyth, Mr. Bennett, Ms. Sanford, Mr. Lacy, Ms. Mansour and Ms.
Ireland in favor; Mr. Ferrara, Mr. Davis, and Mr. Tinkey opposed.

    Mr. Tinkey moved to recommend including the requirement of planting
groups of trees in parking islands as recommended in Community Forestry
Master Plan (CFMP) and where applicable there are flush mounted standing
curb. Mr. Davis seconded the motion. All in favor.

    Mr. Ireland moved for approval of the draft zoning code as amended and
to recommend the establishment of a task force for trees to include tree
experts, Town Council, Planning Commission member, and GREEN Commission
member to review the tree ordinance section and recommend any additional
improvements or changes. Mr. Tinkey seconded the motion. All in favor.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:57 pm.
