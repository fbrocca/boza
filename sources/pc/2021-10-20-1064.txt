6.   Requests
     A. An ordinance to amend sections 156.007, 156.171, 156.304, 156.318,
         156.325 of the Town of Mount Pleasant Zoning Code related to Hotels,
         Inns, Boutique Hotels, and Parking Requirements for the same.
         Proposed amendments pertain to the following:
         ➢ Amend section 156.007 to add definitions for Bedroom, Extended
            Stay Hotel, Guest Suite and Guest Room and to amend definitions for
            Hotel and Boutique Hotel.
         ➢ Amend section 156.171, Schedule for Off-Street Parking Space
            Requirements for certain accommodations and lodging.
         ➢ Amend section 156.304, Nonresidential Use Districts, related to
            Maximum Building Sizes.
         ➢ Amend section 156.318, Boulevard Overlay District, related to NC
            uses and Maximum Building Size.
         ➢ Amend section 156.325, Principal Use Table, to remove Boutique
            Hotels as a Permitted Use in the NC District.

        Ms. Reed reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     Ms. Ireland moved for approval. Mr. Gates seconded the motion.
