D.    SP-04-22, Proposal for Sketch Plan approval for a subdivision to create
      nine lots on property zoned R-1, Low Density Residential District.
      Property is located on Hale Road, consists of approximately 5.41-acres,
      and is identified by TMS No. 560-12-00-070.

     Ms. Moeser reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

Mr. Ike Boatwright, representative for the applicant, stated that they are
available for questions.

    Ms. Smith moved for deferral in order for the applicant to include a tree
study and mitigation plan, additional information on the stormwater
management plan, and possible solutions to address Back Bay Village’s
concerns regarding buffers. Mr. Gates seconded the motion.

    Mr. Ike Boatwright stated that they have a tree study completed as well
as a full grading plan that shows all stormwater being contained on the site.
He stated that they are working with staff to save as many trees as possible,
but the majority of the trees on the lot are pine trees.

     Ms. Smith called for a vote on the motion. All in favor.

    F.   A request at the March 2022 Planning Commission to discuss the current
         usage of abbreviated minutes, to document meetings, in comparison to
         the previously applied verbatim minutes.

         Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.
