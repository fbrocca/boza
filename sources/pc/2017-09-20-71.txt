5. Staff Update on the 2018 Comprehensive Plan Process

Ms. Boyles updated the Commission on the progress of the update of the 2018
Comprehensive Plan (attachment 2). She stated that the forum held their first
meeting with the consultants and it was very productive and successful. She
stated that an open house would be held on Tuesday, October 17th at 6 pm.

Mr. Cuttino asked if all the members were in attendance. Ms. Boyles answered
that there were a few of the forum members were not able to attend. Mr.
Cuttino asked how the format of the meeting was determined. Ms. Boyles
answered that it was a collaboration between staff and the consultants.

Mr. Wren asked how often the forum would meet. Ms. Boyles answered that
they will be meeting on a monthly basis.

Mr. Millet asked for some background information on the consultants. Ms.
Boyles answered that there are two consulting firms: McBride, Dale, Clarion and
CDM Smith. She stated that the Long-range Transportation Plan (LRTP) would
be updated during this process as well.

Mr. McNeill asked what the process would be for the forum’s final
recommendations. Ms. Boyles answered that the final recommendations would
be forwarded to the Commission and the Commission would then make a
recommendation to Town Council.

Ms. Gregg asked if a flier would be generated for the open house. Ms. Boyles
answered in the affirmative.

that all aspects of social media available to the Town would be utilized.

Ms. Pat Sullivan, 1002 Plantation Court, stated that as a participant of the forum,
she was impressed with the members and the diversity represented both
culturally and geographically. She stated that it was very invigorating to see the

 RELATIONSHIP TO THE COMPREHENSIVE PLAN
The Comprehensive Plan 2009-2019 (2014 Update) Future Land Use Map recommends Urban Corridor
land uses for the subject property (see map below). The Urban Corridor land use is intended to foster
an active, pedestrian-oriented commercial environment with a variety of uses, activities, and scales.
Form is as important as use within Urban Corridors, with buildings and primary entrances near the public
realm and high quality pedestrian streetscapes in front. There should be a high degree of design quality
for the building façade and a strong interaction between buildings and the public realm. Public spaces
such as plazas should be integrated with surrounding development.
Mixed use, multistory development is encouraged, generally with the most public uses, such as retail,
on the first floor, and more private uses, such as residences or offices, on upper floors.
The following list of applicable Comprehensive Plan goals and strategies is provided so that they may be
considered along with this zoning request:
    Promote appropriate infill and redevelopment while protecting the context of existing
        neighborhoods. [TARGET STRATEGY]
    Encourage development of infill properties with appropriately scaled redevelopment to utilize
        infrastructure already in place.
    Encourage appropriate commercial development to increase the property tax base and minimize
        the tax burden on homeowners.

 STAFF COMMENTS
1. Request is to rezone the subject parcel from NC, Neighborhood Commercial District, to AB,
   Areawide Business District. The property is to remain in the Urban Corridor Overlay District.
2. The Comprehensive Plan Future Land Use Map recommends Urban Corridor land uses for this area.
   The proposed AB zoning is an appropriate underlying zoning district classification for the land use
   recommendation.
3. The Urban Corridor Overlay District expands permitted uses on commercially-zoned properties
   (regardless of the underlying commercial zoning district) to include all uses permitted in the OP,
   NC, AB, CO, and PI-1 zoning districts.
4. The application does not indicate a proposed use; however, four adjacent parcels are under the
   same ownership so the request to rezone is presumably to aggregate parcels and/or adjust lot lines
   for redevelopment.
5. Development/redevelopment of the parcel is subject to the Design Review Process.
