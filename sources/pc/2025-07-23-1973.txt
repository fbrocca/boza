         Mr. Lacy moved for approval of the street name request. Ms. Ireland
     seconded the motion. All in favor.

     B.   Discussion, Staff Presentation and Commission Discussion regarding the
          heights of detached accessory structures.

          Ms. Reed and Mr. Mitchell reviewed the request with the Commission.

         The Commission discussed how to best address this issue and what
     changes should be considered.

     The Commission agreed for the historic district to allow detached accessory
     structures and accessory dwellings to have a maximum height to 20 feet with
     a ten-foot rear and side setback, with remainder of the town allowed a
     maximum of 25 feet height for an accessory dwelling unit or accessory
     structure with any portion over 18 feet required to have a 10 ft setback, with a
     maximum of 850 square feet and no more than 40% of the principal structure.

     C.   Discussion, Staff Presentation and Commission Discussion regarding
          regulations for demolitions and rebuilds/major additions in older
          neighborhoods.

          Ms. Reed and Mr. Mitchell reviewed the request with the Commission.

          The Commission discussed demolition, rebuilds, additions, and
     renovations and how this can be better addressed, particularly with regard to
     drainage.

     The Commission agreed that the building area ratio (BAR) should be applied to
     the Old Mount Pleasant area south of Coleman Boulevard from Shem Creek to
     the Intracoastal Waterway.
