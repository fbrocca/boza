6. Requests
   A. Rezoning Case: R-14-21 Summary: Proposal to amend Chapter 156, Zoning
      Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances, by adding new sections
      Chapter 156.306, 156.307, 156.308, 156.304(E)(3), 156.110(F), establishing
      new Shem Creek Waterfront District and design standards. Amendments

   pertain to provision of permitted uses and development standards for this
   District. Affected code sections include 156.007, 156.325, 156.326, 156.310,
   156.318, 156.314, 156.110, 156.304.

   Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

   Mr. Bennett moved for approval. Mr. Davis seconded the motion.

    M. Lisa Jones, 930 Live Oak, expressed concern with the parking for the
issue, particularly if there is a storm and they have more than 50% in
renovations. She stated that there is currently not sufficient parking for their
business. She stated that she likes the direction the changes are going, but
Coastal Expeditions would not be able to meet the proposed parking changes.

    Ms. Ashley Woody, 110 Friend Street, agreed with Ms. Jones regarding the
parking in the area. She suggested that meeting the parking standards would
be a hardship.

     Ms. Caroline Johnson, 112 Beach Street, expressed concern with the
Simmons Seafood area and what the proposed plans are for the site. Mr. Wren
stated that this could be addressed during discussion.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Wren closed public comment.

   Mr. Wren called for a vote on the motion. All in favor.
