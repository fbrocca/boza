6.   Old Business
     A. Proposal to amend Sections 156.340 - 156.345 of Chapter 156, Zoning
         Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances, pertaining to Short
         Term Rentals. Proposed is to amend the entire ordinance to address
         several concerns, including but not limited to such things as lowering
         the maximum number of allowed short term rentals, annual renewal
         requirements and submittal processes, and violations and penalties for
         noncompliance.

          Ms. Reed reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

         Ms. Laurie Bixler, 144 3rd Ave, thanked staff for their work on this issue.
     She stated that this is a complex issue. She expressed concern with the
     changes to the ordinance and that more work is needed to provide better
     enforcement for the Town. She suggested that enforcement is the main issue
     that needs to be addressed.

          There being no further comments, Mr. Wren closed public comment.

         Mr. Gates moved for denial of the request in order for staff to re-work the
     issues identified. Mr. Wren seconded the motion. All in favor.

7.   Requests
     A. Request to annex and zone Pepper Tract PD-CD, Planned Development-
         Conservation Design District, an approximately 4.93-acre tract of land
         comprised of two parcels located on Chandler Road. Proposed is to
         designate both parcels as LDSF, Low Density Single Family within the PD.

          This item was withdrawn by the applicant.

     B.    Zoning Case R-06-21, Request to annex and zone Dunes West PD,
           Planned Development District, an approximately 9.05-acre tract of land
           comprised of two parcels located in Covington Subdivision off Bessemer
           Road. Proposed is to designate the parcels, like Covington Phase 1 and
           2, as DW-PD R-3 within the PD, but to utilize the DW-PD R-1 minimum
           lot size and lot coverage.

          Ms. Reed reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

   Mr. Ellis Lesemann, attorney for the applicant, asked for a deferral to allow
time to speak with some of the stakeholders.

    Mr. James Coaxum, 1570 Joe Rouse Rd, expressed concern on how the
front properties would be affected by the proposed request.

   Ms. Yvonne Gilbert, 2844 Wagoner Way, stated that she is opposed to the
request. She suggested that the applicant has not met with the existing
property owners in Covington regarding the development plans. She
suggested that there has been misleading information from Crescent Homes
regarding this project. She also suggested that if they move forward with this
project there could be legal issues surrounding the development of the
property. She expressed concern with the safety of the existing community.

    Mr. Aaron Ober, 1486 Brightwood, stated that he is opposed to the
request. He stated that he agrees with the previous comments made. He
suggested that the addition of these phases would impact the neighborhood
character and the expansion of Covington in this manner was not previously
known.

      Ms. Raquel Deschler, 2867 Wagner Way, agreed with the previous
comments. She suggested that there are other options for access without
utilizing the roadways within Covington. She expressed concern with child
safety.

    Mr. Mark Slater, 1483 Brightwood Dr, stated that he agrees with
comments that the impression given was that no other phases would be
considered. He suggested that this would adversely affect the neighborhood.

     Mr. Daniel Shaw, 2836 Wagoner Way, suggested that this request is
“changing the rules”. He expressed concern with these phases increasing
traffic and adversely affecting the safety of the neighborhood. He expressed
concern with current construction traffic and that the addition of these phases
would exacerbate the issue. He asked that the request be denied.

     Ms. Jennie Holmes, 2804 Wagoner Way, expressed concern that this
request would adversely impact the existing neighborhood. She suggested
that there is currently only one way in and out to the neighborhood and that
this request would increase traffic.

    Mr. Clark Dank, 2808 Wagoner Way, expressed concern with child safety
and that this request would increase traffic in the neighborhood. He
expressed concern with the development standards being changed from what
currently exists. He stated that he is opposed to the request.

    Mr. Jeff Risser, 1511 Hopkins Lane, stated that he is currently building a
home in the neighborhood. He expressed concern that the roadway is narrow
and that emergency vehicles would not be able to access homes because of
on-street parking. He expressed concern with the number of homes that are
being built and that the addition of more homes would adversely affect the
neighborhood.

    Ms. Lisa Homes, 2828 Wagoner Way, agreed with all comments. She
expressed concern with the request and that it would adversely affect the
neighborhood.

     There being no further comments, Mr. Wren closed public comment.

     Ms. Ireland moved for denial. Mr. Gates seconded the motion. All in favor.
