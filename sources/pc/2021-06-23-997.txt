5.    General Public Comment

    Mr. George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Road, spoke regarding item 7D, Watts
Park Sketch Plan and expressed concern that this is being reviewed by the Planning
Commission as the zoning was denied by Town Council. He suggested that his
understanding was that when the zoning was denied, the property would come in

as community conservation zoning and in the Sweetgrass Overlay District. He
suggested that this request should be deferred in order to have a legal
determination on how to proceed.

     Ms. Pat Sullivan, 1002 Plantation Court, spoke regarding the Phillips
Community and expressed concern that the requested rezoning would be out of
character with the community and the community standards. She also stated that
the property is in the process of being designated historic through Charleston
County. She also expressed concern with drainage and that there is currently
flooding issues and bringing in more development would exacerbate this issue.

    Mr. Richard Habersham, Phillips Community, spoke regarding the rezoning of
the Phillips Community and that the proposed rezoning would be out of character
with the current historic settlement community. He suggested that the historic
designation being established through Charleston County should be upheld by the
Town and be applied to future properties being annexed and rezoned. He also
expressed concern with the size of the lots that would be allowed if the requested
zoning were approved.

Mr. Davis entered at this time (5:12 pm).

    Ms. Cashion Drolet, Historic Charleston Foundation, spoke regarding the
rezoning of the Phillips Community and stated that she is opposed to approval of
the rezoning request. She suggested that the current Charleston County zoning
and historic designation would be more appropriate for the community. She
suggested that approval of the request would not be consistent with the
community. She asked that the request be denied.

There being no further comments, Mr. Wren continued with the agenda.
