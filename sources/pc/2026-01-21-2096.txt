C.    PUBLIC HEARING, R-03-26, 0 May Lane, TMS No. 532-01-00-133, Request
      to Rezone R-2, parcel is currently zoned AB. Staff Report

     Mr. Lubert reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     Mr. John Rice, applicant, reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Ms. Jeri Eddings, 130 Rose Lane, suggested that the property should be
zoned residential and asked if the requested R-2 zoning would only allow for a
single residential dwelling.

    Mr. Barry Newton, current owner, stated that they envision only having a
single-family residence on the property.

    Mr. Frederick Gathers, May Lane, suggested that the area is becoming
crowded. He suggested that there has been some talk about developing
condominiums in the area.

    Ms. Velma Wright-Dickerson, May Lane, suggested that the property
should only allow a single residential home. She suggested that having
multiple dwellings would further congest the area.

   Mr. Dwayne Gathers, 109 May Lane, stated that the roadway is congested
and narrow, only allowing one car at a time. He expressed concern with
emergency vehicles being able to access the roadway.

     There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

    Mr. Ferrara moved for approval. Mr. Smyth seconded the motion. All in
favor.

G.    PUBLIC HEARING, R-07-26, 0 George Browder Boulevard, TMS No. 598-
      00-00-437, Request to amend Dunes West PD, incorporating 7.05-acres.
      Staff Report

     Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

   Ms. Jenny Palmer, Seamon, Whitesides, and Associates, and Turner
Rhodes, reviewed the request with the Commission.

   Mr. David Seay, 3404 Salterbeck, suggested that the price point for the
homes would be achievable for Mount Pleasant, but not qualify as affordable
housing. He suggested that this is a good infill project that would benefit the
Town and the owners.

    Mr. Jeff Johnston, 681 McCants, representative for First Baptist Church,
stated that he supports the request as it provides a benefit for the community
and utilizing the property for that benefit.

     Ms. Michelle Stress, 545 Veranda View, suggested that there are already
traffic issues in the area and suggested that the proposed use might not be the
most appropriate use for the community. She asked that the request be
denied.

     There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

   Mr. Davis moved for denial. Ms. Ireland seconded the motion. Motion failed
on a 4 to 4 vote with Mr. Barber, Mr. Davis, Ms. Mansour, and Ms. Ireland in
favor; Mr. Ferrara, Mr. Smyth, Mr. Bennett, and Mr. Berry opposed.

     Mr. Berry moved for approval. Mr. Ferrara seconded the motion.
