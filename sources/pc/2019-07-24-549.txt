6. Requests
   A. R-17-19 Request to rezone the Mount Pleasant Regional Airport from R-1,
      Low Density Residential District, to LI, Light Industrial District, located at
      700 Faison Road/Mount Pleasant Regional Airport Parcel ID (TMS No.):
      540-00-00-021.

   This item was withdrawn by the applicant.

   B. R-18-19. Request to 1. Rezone from PD, Planned Development District
      Ordinance, to WG-C, Cooper River Waterfront Gateway District; 2. Amend
      the official Building Height Plan Map to allow up to 80 feet in building
      height; and 3. Approval of an Impact Assessment and Conceptual Plan,
      located at Patriots Point Road. TMS No.: 517-00-00-001 (portion thereof)

       Ms. Canon and Mr. Morrison reviewed staff comments with the Commission
   (attachment 1).

   Mr. Larry Murray, executive director for Patriots Point Development Authority,
   James Wilson, attorney, and Gary Collins, Seamon, Whiteside, and Associates,
   reviewed the request with the Commission.

   Ms. Pam Ireland, 820 Fiddler’s Lane, suggested that the plan is a good concept,
   but expressed concern with the request for increased height to 80 feet. She
   stated that a majority of respondents of a survey during the Comprehensive
   Plan update process were against additional height along the gateways.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Wren closed the public hearing.

       Ms. Smith moved for approval of the rezoning request. Mr. Wren seconded
   the motion. All in favor.

      Mr. Gates moved for denial of the increased height request of 80 feet. Mr.
   McNeill seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 6 to 1 vote with Ms. Gregg,
   Mr. Gates, Mr. McNeill, Mr. Wren, Ms. Smith and Mr. Bennett in favor; Mr.
   Campbell opposed.

      Mr. McNeill moved for approval of the impact assessment including all staff
   comments. Mr. Wren seconded the motion. All in favor.

   C. R-19-19. Request to rezone one parcel from R-1, Low Density Residential
      District, to OP, Office Professional District. Also request to include subject
      parcel in the BOD, Boulevard Overlay District, located at 1228 Melvin
      Bennett Road. TMS No.: 560-02-00-020

       Ms. Canon reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

   Mr. Scott Shellhaas, owner and applicant, reviewed the request with the
   Commission. He showed pictures of the surrounding property for the
   Commission.

   Mr. Paul Taylor, 1336 Bluebird Drive, expressed concern with the rezoning
   request and that it is located in a residential neighborhood. He suggested that
   the commercial use is not appropriate adjacent to residential. He asked that
   the rezoning request be denied.

   Mr. Christian Ensminger, 1244 Melvin Bennett Road, suggested that the
   property should remain residential.

   Mr. Doug Sadler, 1224 Melvin Bennett Road, suggested that the request would
   have an adverse effect on the surrounding property and would affect residential
   property values.

   Ms. Sybil Noll, 1330 Bluebird Drive, suggested that the property should remain
   residential. She suggested that the rezoning request would adversely affect the
   neighborhood. She expressed concern with child and pedestrian safety. She
   expressed concern with traffic in the area and that this request would increase
   traffic. She asked that the request be denied.

   Mr. Wesley Davis, 423 Springfield Road, suggested that the request could be
   beneficial for the neighborhood. He suggested that the office use would not
   adversely affect the neighborhood.

Ms. Alex Coleman, 1495 Green Shade Way, expressed concern with the request.
She suggested that the request would be a “tragedy”. She asked that the
request be denied.

Ms. Denise May, 1324 Bluebird Drive, suggested that the request would

D. R-20-19. Request to rezone one parcel from NC, Neighborhood Commercial
   District, to R-1, Low Density Residential District. Subject parcel is proposed
   to remain in the BOD, Boulevard Overlay District, located at 1218-1220
   Schirmer Avenue. TMS No.: 532-07-00-142

   Mr. Gates brought a point of order that the address on the application does
not exist. Ms. Canon answered that the TMS was correct and the property was
appropriately posted.

Ms. Canon reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

Mr. Tim Askins, owner, reviewed the request with the Commission.

There being no comments, Mr. Wren closed the public hearing.

    Ms. Smith moved for denial of the rezoning request. Mr. McNeill seconded
the motion. Motion passed on a 5 to 2 vote with Ms. Gregg, Mr. Campbell, Mr.
Gates, Mr. McNeill, and Ms. Smith in favor; Mr. Wren and Mr. Bennett opposed.
