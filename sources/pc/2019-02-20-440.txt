6. Requests
   A. R-04-19, Request to annex and zone PI-2, Public Institutional-2 District, one
      parcel in its entirety, located on Labor Camp Road. TMS #: 558-00-00-944

      Ms. Canon reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

   Ms. Singleton stated that she had questions about the zoning requested, what
   the property would be used for and what affect the intended would use have
   on the neighboring properties.

   Ms. Smith asked why the property is not already located in the Town. Ms. Canon
   answered that the property was recently purchased by Mount Pleasant
   Waterworks (MPW) and would be aggregated with the neighboring MPW
   property and zoned PI-2.

   Mr. Wren asked what the buffer requirements are for PI-2 zoning when next to
   residential. Mr. Prause answered that because it is PI-2 zoning, it would not
   require DRB approval, but would be reviewed by staff.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Wren closed the public hearing.

        Mr. Neal moved for approval of the request. Ms. Smith seconded the motion.
   All in favor.
