6.   Requests
     A. Zoning Case: R-14-24 Request to establish a new PD, Planned
         Development District Ordinance, an approximately 30.32-acre portion
         of a larger 162.86-acre tract of land located off Patriots Point Road,
         property is currently zoned WG-C, Cooper River Waterfront Gateway
         District identified by TMS No. 517-00-00-001 (portion thereof) and
         depicted on a plat recorded by the Charleston County ROD Office in Plat
         Book W, Page 025. The newly established district is proposed to be
         called Patriots Annex PD, Planned Development.

        Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

        Mr. James Wilson, attorney for the applicant, Mr. Gary Collins, SWA, and
     Mr. Michael Bennett reviewed the request with the Commission.

     Mr. Ferrara left at this time (5:55 pm). Ms. Sanford left at this time (5:57 pm).

          Ms. Tori Sanders, Coastal Conservation League, expressed concern with
     the environmental implications of the proposed plan, particularly moving the
     grand lawn to the rear of the buildings rather than closer to the waterfront as
     it reduces the connectivity to the waterfront. She expressed concern that
     having the open space closer to the waterfront provides crucial ecosystem
     support and allows for better stormwater infiltration, reduces runoff, and
     protects water quality in the harbor and marsh. She suggested that it is a
     critical area for wildlife, provides bio-diversity, and provides a better cohesive
     ecological and environmental asset. She expressed concern with having large
     parking areas near the water and suggested that multi-modal uses and other
     parking strategies should be considered.

    Ms. Katie Zimmerman, Executive Director for Charleston Moves, thanked
the applicant for inclusion of bike and pedestrian access and suggested that
conversations for further connectivity for those biking, walking, and using
transit should be considered as it is important for the community. She
suggested that more detailed streetscapes, trailscapes, and connections to
those resources as the project moves forward.

   Mr. Ian Duncan, 1427 Topaz, stated that reason for moving the grand lawn
was to allow for a more active waterfront village. He also noted that the
property naturally drains toward the golf course.

    Mr. David Quick 1502 Village Square, suggested that there is an
opportunity for more green space, as well as biking and pedestrian
connectivity. He stated that he is comfortable with the new layout with the
open space relocated. He suggested that an elevated waterfront walkway
should be considered and brought to fruition. He suggested that native
plantings should be considered during landscaping.

    Ms. Michele Stress, 545 Veranda View, agreed that this project is a great
opportunity to showcase different environmental designs and green
technologies that would better preserve the natural spaces, wildlife, and
native trees within the Town.

     Mr. Wilson rebutted that the parking would be based on the ULI
regulations. He stated that the PD includes a plan that would exceed the
minimum of 10% open space required. He stated that the PD language would
include the requirements within the current WG-OD. He stated that they
would like to have the walkway continue along the entire waterfront.

There being no further comments, Mr. Lacy closed public comment.

     Ms. Ireland moved for approval of the PD and conceptual plan with the
following conditions:
    ➢ A broader visual corridor of green space from the hotel to the
        waterfront, which will necessitate minimizing the mixed-use buildings
        at waterfront;
    ➢ Prohibit residential uses on the ground floor with the residential use

       ratio consistent with the current code of four residential units per acre
       with allowing an additional four units per acre if those additional units
       are for attainable housing;
  ➢ Maximize boardwalk and walkways for maximum connectivity for
       biking, pedestrian, and other access;
  ➢ Under the building design elements, change the use of stucco to be

     Mr. Lacy called for a vote on the motion as amended. All in favor.

    Ms. Theresa Roland, 2023 Welsh Pony, suggested that there has been an

     Ms. Pam Ireland moved for denial of the impact assessment with the
findings that it is a less passive use, has more potential physical traffic and
safety issues, and more potential noise. Mr. Smyth seconded the motion. All
in favor.

    Ms. Ireland moved for denial of the conceptual plan with the findings that
there are traffic and safety issues, it is a more active warehouse use rather than
being a more passive use. Mr. Lacy seconded the motion. All in favor.

    Ms. Ireland moved for denial of the PD amendment with the findings that
there are traffic and safety concerns that will impact the residents. Ms.
Mansour seconded the motion. All in favor.
