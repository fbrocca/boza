4.   General Public Comment

    Ms. Susan Porter, SCDNR, spoke regarding the zoning request. She stated that
she supports the Medal of Honor project but does not support the location of it as
it would be located on the approximate 23.7-acre conservation easement. She

stated that the conservation easement is a binding, voluntary legal agreement. She
stated that SCDNR will enforce the conservation easement agreement.

     Ms. Cheryll Woods-Flowers, 1529 Old Village Drive, stated that she was an ex-
officio member of the Patriots Point Board when the conservation easement was
established. She suggested that the conservation easement is important to the
Patriots Point area and the Town. She suggested that if this agreement is breached
it could set a precedent for breach of other easement agreements throughout the
state. She expressed concern with the approval process for this PD amendment.

    Ms. Blake Keppler, Deputy Director of SCDNR Marine Resource Center, stated
that the conservation easement is a valuable resource for several migratory and
wintering birds and butterflies. She stated that over 125 bird species have been
documented using the easement in addition monarch butterflies, which are close
to being classified as an endangered species. She suggested that these stop over
areas are becoming rare

    Mr. Noah Moore, 1309 Founders Way, asked that the Commission deny the
request. He stated that the nature trail in the conservation easement was named
for his deceased son and asked that the easement be maintained and preserved.

    Mr. David Quick, 1502 Village Square, stated that he was a reporter at the time
the conservation easement was established. He stated that the easement is a
valuable migratory stop for birds. He suggested that the nature trail has been
neglected and should be cleaned up. He suggested that natural resources should
be preserved in the Town and suggested that conservation easement is a valuable
environmental resource.

    Ms. Alys Campaigne, 1314 Wayne St, stated that she has enjoyed the trail and
the easement. She also would like to see the easement preserved and not utilized
as a parking lot. She stated that the Comprehensive Plan calls for the preservation
of natural assets and trails throughout the Town.

     There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed public comment.

5.    Requests
      A. Zoning Case: R-13-24      Request to Amend the Patriots Point PDD,
          Planned Development District Ordinance, pertaining to an aggregate of

    nine separate parcels totaling approximately 74 acres. Proposed
    amendment establishes development standards and permitted uses for
    the project area to include a comprehensive development plan that
    provides a mix of uses to include sports and entertainment, civic uses to
    include museums and conference centers, retail/restaurant, hotel and
    tourism-related uses, trails, parks and opens spaces, and associated
    parking, including structured parking.

   Mr. DeMoura and Ms. Farrell reviewed the request with the Commission.
He stated that Town Council recommended approval of the request. He
provided a video on the proposed project.

     Mr. Robert Boyles, Director of SCDNR, stated that the conservation
easement is owned by the State of SC, with SCDNR being tasked with
overseeing the easement. He stated that SCDNR is currently not in any
negotiations with the other stakeholders of this project. He stated that while
this project will be a great asset for the Town, the conservation easement
should remain.

     Ms. Susan Porter stated that SCDNR agreed to swap 4.69 acres with
Patriots Point for a drainage ditch, parking, and the Vietnam display. In
exchange, the easement was extended to 2048. She expressed concern that
this project would not be an enhancement or preservation of the easement,
but would destroy the habitat and negatively impact the migratory birds and
butterflies. She stated that the exchange was allowed with the intent to
continue to protect and preserve this area within the easement.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

    Mr. Lacy moved for denial of the request. Ms. Ireland seconded the motion.
Motion failed on a 1 to 6 vote with Mr. Lacy in favor; Mr. Ferrara, Mr. Smyth,
Mr. Bennett, Ms. Mansour, Mr. Tinkey and Ms. Ireland opposed and Mr. Davis
ineligible to vote.

   Mr. Tinkey moved for approval of the PD amendment with the following
conditions:
   ➢ No net loss of public green space
   ➢ No net loss of bird habitat for migratory birds as are currently protected
