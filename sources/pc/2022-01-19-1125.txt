     A. Zoning Case R-01-22, Request to Annex and zone TH, Townhouse
         District, A tract of land located at 1131 Louise Terrace. Subject parcel is
         approximately 0.34-acres, identified by TMS Nos. 559-14-00-015. A

    request to Annex and zone TH, Townhouse District, the adjacent tract
    of land located at 1137 Louise Terrace, approximately 0.58-acres,
    identified by TMS Nos. 559-14-00-013 is forthcoming, yet not
    incorporated in this request. Ownership and Property Identification:
    1131 Louise Terrace, TMS No. 559-14-00-015 Correspondence

   Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

   Mr. Stuart Whiteside reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Mr. Chad McPeters, 1147 Carter Ave, expressed concern with traffic,
drainage, and impact to greenspace. He suggested that the rezoning request
would be a detriment to the neighborhood and should not be approved.

Mr. Paul Pruitt, 1135 Carter Ave, stated that the neighborhood would like to
remain in the county jurisdiction. He stated that the rezoning would be a
detriment to the neighborhood and would increase traffic. He suggested that
the request would increase the amount of noise in the neighborhood. He
asked that the request be denied.

     Mr. Craig Kroll, 1133 Carter Ave, stated that he likes the peace and
quaintness of the neighborhood. He suggested that the request would be a
detriment to the neighborhood and would change the country feel of the
neighborhood. He suggested that if approved, the neighborhood would be
negatively impacted. He suggested that the neighborhood should remain as it
currently exists.

    Mr. Kim McGraw, 1459 Cambridge Lakes Drive, stated that growth in the
area would be a natural progression. He suggested that development is
inevitable, and that the property is already a high-density area with over 100
existing units and commercial. He suggested that nice townhomes would be
a better fit than having single family residential units.

    Mr. Richard Taylor, 1137 Louise Terrace, stated that he would like to have
his property annexed into the Town. He suggested that the proposed
development would clean up the neighborhood and annexation would provide
better police and fire protection.

    Ms. Allison McAdams, 1125 Louise Terrace, asked that the property not be
annexed, and the zoning denied. She suggested that the neighborhood is
