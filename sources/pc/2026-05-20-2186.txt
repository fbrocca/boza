7.   Continued Discussion on The Comprehensive Plan’s Recommended Future
     Land Use as it relates to Zoning.

    Ms. Reed reviewed this item with the Commission. She stated that at the May
Planning Committee meeting, the Planning Committee recommended that this
issue be sent to this Commission to consider rezoning the school properties to

Public Institutional (PI) zoning. She reviewed the school properties through a
PowerPoint presentation.

    Mr. Bennett asked when the next update of the Comprehensive Plan would be
completed. Ms. Reed answered that the Comprehensive Plan was last updated in
November 2025; the next update would be in five years. Mr. Bennett asked if a
vote is needed at this time. Ms. Reed answered in the negative. Mr. Bennett
suggested that zoning the properties according to the future land use designation
might be done, if appropriate. He expressed concern with zoning property to PI as
it might allow a more intensive uses that would not be appropriate for the
neighborhood or surrounding properties. He suggested that moving the density
back into the Comprehensive Plan is also not the best option.

     Mr. Davis asked about Trident Academy and if a sports field could be
constructed. Ms. Reed answered that prior to zoning code change in May 2025,
because PI zoning did not have a density assigned to it, an owner with an existing
residentially zoned property would have to request a Comprehensive Plan
amendment. If that amendment was denied, the property would not be able to be
developed as it was currently zoned, which could be considered arbitrary and
capricious and possibly a taking of property.

    The Commission discussed the pros and cons of rezoning the school properties
to PI zoning as recommended in the Comprehensive Plan.

    Mr. Bennett suggested that not zoning the properties to the recommended PI
zoning would be a more conservative option as the uses of right would be less
intensive. Ms. Reed agreed that PI zoning allows more intensive uses than a
residential use.

   Mr. Berry expressed concern with zoning all of the properties to PI zoning.

    Mr. Davis suggested that fewer residential homes would be desired. Mr. Barber
stated that it depends on the use because residential homes might be more desired
in an established neighborhood than having a lay down yard in the middle of the
neighborhood.

    Mr. Berry suggested that keeping the current zoning would be best as it would
give the best oversight and review over future development of that property.
