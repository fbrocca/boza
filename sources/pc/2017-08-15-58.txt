                  TOWN OF MOUNT PLEASANT, SOUTH CAROLINA
                       SPECIAL PLANNING COMMISSION
                              AUGUST 15, 2017
                                  MINUTES


Present:       Roy Neal, Alys Campaigne, DeAudre Gregg, John McNeill, Peter Lehman,
               Joseph Wren, Tripp Cuttino, Kathy Smith, Rodly Millet.
Staff:         Christiane Farrell, Kent Prause, Michele Canon, Susan Bettelli, Liz Boyles

         Mr. Neal called the meeting to order at 12:00 pm.

1. Correspondence and Public Statements

There being no comments, Mr. Neal continued with the agenda.

2. Selection of Comp Plan Forum Members

Ms. Farrell introduced staff to the Commission.

Ms. Boyles reviewed the procedures with the Commission (attachment 1). She
stated that over 275 applications were received. She stated that the Town planning
area was divided into 9 neighborhoods.

Ms. Smith asked if the neighborhoods are similar in population. Ms. Boyles
answered in the affirmative.

Mr. Wren asked if there is any concern with determining members by ethnicity,
age, etc. Mr. Neal suggested that the criteria should be determined before they
begin selection.

Ms. Gregg stated that there were some of the ID numbers that could not be seen.
Ms. Boyles answered that they may be overlapping and suggested that this be
further discussed as the Commission comes to them.

Mr. Neal suggested that the Commission weigh in on those that they would like to
consider in each neighborhood and then a final selection would be made from
those candidates. Ms. Boyles stated that this could be done. Mr. Neal suggested
that the census data could be utilized as a framework.

Ms. Smith suggested that this could be done, but suggested that it is critically
important that we have at least three candidates from each neighborhood for fair
distribution of representation. Mr. Neal agreed.

Mr. McNeill suggested that the applicants should not be discounted because of a
minor detail. He suggested that the only qualification suggested was that they be
a resident of the Town.

Mr. Millet agreed that decisions should be based on each neighborhood with the
end result being a qualified, diverse group. He agreed that being a resident makes
one qualified.

Mr. Neal suggested that vetting initially through the different neighborhoods would
be a way to reduce the number of applicants that would be further considered.

Mr. Millet stated that he has not had an opportunity to fully review each of the
applicants because the information was distributed yesterday. He suggested that
time should be taken to make an educated decision.

Ms. Campaigne stated that it was previously discussed that some outside expertise
should be taken into consideration as well. She stated that mirroring the census
data in regard to ethnicity could be difficult as the number of applicants would not
support those ratios.

Mr. Wren agreed that some “at large” members might be needed.

Ms. Gregg agreed with narrowing down the applicants within each neighborhood
and then making final selections.

The Commission reviewed each neighborhood and selected those applicants that
that they would like considered.

Ms. Boyles reviewed the census data with the Commission for those applicants
selected.

Ms. Campaigne asked which neighborhoods fall under 29429 zip code. Ms. Bettelli
answered that it is mainly neighborhoods six and nine.

Mr. Neal suggested that they try to determine three representatives from each of
the eight neighborhoods and then consider “at large” members.

Mr. Lehman asked if a larger neighborhood should have more representation. Mr.
Neal answered that this could be considered. Ms. Smith noted that each
neighborhood was determined so that they were approximately the same in
population.

Ms. Gregg suggested that the top three should be determined by each
Commissioner and then determine if additional conversation is needed.

The Commission agreed that a vote should be taken.

Mr. Neal suggested to take a ten minute break to review the candidates and then
come back and vote.

The Committee convened for a short break at 1:25 pm and reconvened at 1:40 pm.

The Commission voted on the members for each neighborhood through a ballot.

Staff tallied the results of the votes for the Commission.

Based on the votes, 33 people received the highest votes. The Commission
discussed how to further decide on how to determine the final members.

Mr. McNeill suggested that the 13 applicants with the highest votes should be
finalized and then determine which other members should be considered.

Ms. Campaigne moved for approval of the 13 candidates with the majority votes as
forum members. Ms. Smith seconded the motion. All in favor.

The Commission discussed Area 1 applicants.

The Commission discussed including the original 33 members. Mr. Neal suggested
that candidate 3028 should be included as a member as they have lived in both

areas of the Town and have an interest in having adequate infrastructure, senior
housing, and preserving the quality of life.

Ms. Campaigne moved for approval of the additional 20 applicants as forum
members with the addition of applicant 3028 for a total of 21 additional forum
members with a grand total of 34 members of the forum. Mr. Cuttino seconded the
motion.

Ms. Smith suggested that this was a good process that ended with both geographic,
ethnic, and gender diversity.

Mr. Wren asked how those not selected would be notified. Ms. Boyles answered
that they would be notified that they were not selected and asked to sign up for
notifications from the website on the Comprehensive Plan process and would have
an opportunity to participate as general public citizens going forward.

Mr. McNeill asked if someone declines participation, would there be a process to
appoint a different person. Ms. Boyles answered that because of the number of
members it should not be necessary. However, if the Commission wanted to they
would need to appoint any additional members they would like as alternates.

Ms. Campaigne thanked staff for their hard work.

Mr. Neal called for a vote on the motion. All in favor.

Mr. Lehman asked if alternates would need to be considered. Ms. Boyles answered
that it would be difficult for staff to determine what alternate member should be
considered unless clear direction was provided by the Commission.

Ms. Farrell suggested that the learning curve for any alternate could be
burdensome. The Commission agreed that no alternates would be considered.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 2:58 pm.
