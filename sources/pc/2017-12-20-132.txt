1. Approval of Minutes from November 15, 2017 Meeting

     Mr. Neal moved for approval of the minutes. Mr. Millet seconded the motion.
All in favor.

2. Update on Planning Commission Recommendations

Mr. Rutherford reviewed Town Council decisions with the Commission.

3. Correspondence and Public Comments

There being no comments, Mr. Wren continued with the agenda.

4. Requests
   A. Rezone from CC, Community Conservation District, to R-2, Low Density
      Residential District. Location: 1843 Rifle Range Road Parcel ID (TMS No.):
      561-00-00-012

         Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission
   (attachments 1 & 2).

   Mr. Millet asked about CC zoning. Mr. Rutherford answered that the
   Comprehensive Plan does not recommend CC zoning.

   Mr. Neal asked if both CC and R-2 zonings allow three units to the acre. Mr.
   Rutherford answered in the affirmative. Mr. Neal asked if the only difference is
   the lot size requirement. Mr. Rutherford answered in the affirmative.
                                                             Planning Commission
                                                               December 20, 2017
                                                                       Page 2 of 8

Ms. Erin Stevens reviewed the request with the Commission.

Mr. Neal asked if there are grand trees on the property. Ms. Stevens answered
that the tree survey has not yet been conducted but there are a significant
number of trees on the property.

Mr. Wren asked if there was access other than Rifle Range Road. Ms. Stevens
answered in the negative.

Mr. Neal asked about development plans. Ms. Stevens answered that a finalized
plan has not been completed to date.

Mr. Millet asked the reason for the rezoning. Ms. Stevens answered that the
difference between the zonings would allow more flexibility with the lot sizes.
She stated that being able to have the smaller lots would allow more green
space within the development and more of a buffer between the main roadway
and the development. Mr. Millet asked about the nearby roadway and if this
could be the main access for the development. Ms. Stevens answered that the
adjacent property is located in the County and is not a dedicated right-of-way
for the requested property.

Ms. Gregg asked if R-1 zoning was considered. Ms. Stevens answered in the
negative and stated that R-2 was considered to provide more flexibility with the
lot sizes.

Ms. Campaigne noted that R-2 would allow smaller lot sizes and potentially
more lots could be created as opposed to R-1 zoning. She suggested that the
applicant work on providing future interconnectivity to the adjacent lots.

There being no comments, Mr. Wren closed the public hearing.

Mr. Wren asked about the Foxhorn property and if the adjacent property was
located in the Town. Mr. Rutherford answered in the affirmative and pointed
out those properties not in the Town for the Commission.

    Mr. Neal moved for approval of the request including all staff comments.
Mr. Wren seconded the motion.
                                                             Planning Commission
                                                               December 20, 2017
                                                                       Page 3 of 8

Mr. Neal stated that the property would be sold in the near future and
suggested that the rezoning would allow flexibility and provide the opportunity
for a better development.

Mr. McNeill agreed and expressed concern with access from Rifle Range Road
and the proximity to Magnolia Woods Drive. He suggested that this should be
considered going forward.

Mr. Neal agreed and stated that the Commission would have the opportunity to
review and approve the sketch plan for this development.

Ms. Gregg expressed concern with increased traffic on Rifle Range Road and
suggested that R-1 might be more appropriate.

Mr. Wren agreed and stated that the adjacent properties are zoned R-1 and
there is not any R-2 zoned property in proximity to this property.

Mr. Millet suggested that the zoning should not be changed as the same number
of units are allowed.

   Mr. Wren called for a vote on the motion. Motion failed on a 3 to 3 vote with
Ms. Campaigne, Mr. McNeill, and Mr. Neal in favor; Ms. Gregg, Mr. Wren, and
Mr. Millet opposed.

Mr. Wren stated that the item would move forward with no recommendation.

B. Amend the Dunes West PD, Planned Development District Ordinance, by
   rezoning from R-1, Low Density Residential District, to DW-C-R-1, Dunes
   West Covington R-1. Location: Bessemer Road Parcel ID (TMS No.): 583-00-
   00-003 and 583-03-00-254 thru -261, -263, -265 thru -281

      Mr. Prause reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachments 1&
2).

Mr. Neal asked if this is part of the recent “clean-up” of zonings for the Dunes
West properties. Mr. Prause answered that a portion of the property was
separately purchased. Mr. Neal asked about restriction for accessory dwelling
                                                              Planning Commission
                                                                December 20, 2017
                                                                        Page 4 of 8

units (ADUs). Mr. Prause answered that this restriction was offered by the
applicant.

Mr. Wren asked why this property was not be recommended to be zoned R-3.
Mr. Prause answered that the community preferred the larger lots as opposed
to being allowed the smaller lot size allowed with R-3 zoning.

Mr. Millet commended the applicant for including the community. He asked the
reason for the request. Mr. Prause answered that they are requesting reduced
setbacks.

Ms. Campaigne asked about ADUs. Mr. Prause answered that ADUs are allowed
in R-1 zoning. He stated that as a planned development, they are requesting
that ADUs not be allowed for the development.

Mr. McNeill asked if an ADU could not be constructed because of the setbacks.
Mr. Prause answered that the current setbacks are for the main structure. He
stated that an ADU could be constructed within six feet of the property line. Mr.
McNeill asked if the requested setbacks could mean that the main structures
could be constructed with only a ten foot separation. Mr. Prause answered in
the affirmative, but suggested that the setback could be staggered so that there
would be more distance between the main structures if desired by the
developer.

Mr. Chris Donato, representative for the applicant, reviewed the request with
the Commission.

Mr. Wren asked if ADUs are allowed in the Dunes West PD. Mr. Donato
answered that he does not believe that the Dunes West PD specifically
addresses ADUs. He stated that the requested PD would specifically restrict the
construction of ADUs.

Ms. Campaigne suggested that restricting construction of ADUs would limit the
rights of the property owner. Mr. Donato stated that the setbacks are being
requested in order to have more flexibility in product and as another
contingency, the developer included the restriction of construction of ADUs.
Mr. Rutherford stated that there are other PDs that restrict construction of
ADUs.
                                                             Planning Commission
                                                               December 20, 2017
                                                                       Page 5 of 8



Mr. McNeill suggested that a new zoning would be created with the restriction
of the ADUs.

Mr. Neal asked the number of existing lots. Mr. Donato answered that there
would be 25. Mr. Neal asked how many different product variations could be
utilized if rezoned. Mr. Donato answered that the different product type
variations would increase from four to approximately seven.

Mr. Millet asked if a special exception would be needed under the R-1 zoning to
have the different product types. Mr. Donato answered in the negative and
stated that a special exception would require Board of Zoning Appeals (BOZA)
approval. Mr. Millet asked if this could be included in the Dunes West PD. Mr.
Donato answered in the negative as they would like different setbacks in order
to have the flexibility with product types. He suggested that this would allow
for a better overall development. Mr. Millet expressed concern with the
request as there was recently a large concerted effort with changes to the Dunes
West PD. Mr. Donato suggested that the request would further “clean up” the
PD.

Mr. McNeill asked if the number of lots would change. Mr. Donato answered in
the negative.

Mr. Kevin Cunnane, 3032 River Vista Way, suggested that the request should be
carefully considered before the acreage for Dunes West is increased. He
suggested that the amenities within Dunes West are already to capacity.

Mr. Danny Cignanovic, 3216 Carmel Bay Drive, asked for clarification on how the
existing vacant land would be developed and which PD the property is located
in.

There being no further comments, Mr. Wren closed the public hearing.

Mr. Wren asked about the maximum number of units allowed under the Dunes
West PD and if property could be added to the PD and therefore have a new
maximum number of units allowed. Mr. Prause answered that there is not a
request to change the maximum number of units. Mr. Pagliarini agreed and
stated that this would be an amendment to the PD. Mr. Prause concurred and
                                                              Planning Commission
                                                                December 20, 2017
                                                                        Page 6 of 8

stated that a request to change the maximum number of units allowed is not
included with this request.

   Mr. Neal moved for approval including all staff comments. Ms. Campaigne
seconded the motion.

Mr. Neal suggested that this was continuation of the “clean up” of the Dunes
West area. He suggested that while expanding the footprint of the Dunes West
subdivision, it would be developed within the Town and the Town would have
some control on how it is developed.

Mr. Millet commended the applicant for their work on this plan and for including
the surrounding community.

Ms. Campaigne stated that she is in favor of the request and that it is sensitive
to the surrounding communities. She took exception to restricting construction
of ADUs.

Mr. Millet agreed with Ms. Campaigne’s comment regarding restriction of ADUs
and suggested that this could be omitted. Mr. Neal suggested that this should
be the decision of the applicant. Mr. Pagliarini stated that if desired, the
recommendation could be made to not restrict the ADUs.

Mr. Wren suggested that this would be setting up another PD and could set a
precedent.

    Mr. Wren called for a vote on the motion. Motion passed on a 4 to 2 vote
with Ms. Campaigne, Ms. Gregg, Mr. McNeill, and Mr. Neal in favor; Mr. Wren
and Mr. Millet opposed.
