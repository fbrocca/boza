      Office to NC, Neighborhood Commercial District. Located at 763 and 767

      parcels are 0.64-acres in aggregate. Parcels are located within the HC-

     Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     Mr. Jay Satterfield, owner, reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Ms. Marylee Waters, 771 Long Point Road, expressed concern with the
request and that it would be detrimental to the settlement community. She
stated that the property has been residential and suggested that if rezoned it
would affect her quality of life and cause further gentrification. She suggested
that the request is out of character with the Comprehensive Plan and asked
that the request be denied.

    Mr. Michael Jordan, 1707 Snowden Rd, expressed concern with the
request and suggested that the area has been residential since the 1800s. He
suggested that if approved, the rezoning would be detrimental to the
community. He asked that the request be denied.

    Ms. Vera German, 368 Greer St, asked that the request be denied. She
suggested that the current businesses that were allowed because the
neighborhood did not speak up against those requests. She suggested that

the community is being slowly degraded parcel by parcel. She suggested that

H.    Proposal to amend Sections 156.340 - 156.345 of Chapter 156, Zoning
      Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances, pertaining to Short
      Term Rentals. Proposed is to amend the entire ordinance to clean up
      outdated language, and to the clarify language related to the cap and
      how it is administered based on those already operating a STR.

     Ms. Reed reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

   Ms. Laurie Bixler, 144 3rd Avenue, expressed concern with the software,
days of use is arbitrary, and should be allowed to apply through Jan 31st. She
expressed concern that it would allow an “overrun” of STRs.

   Ms. Michele Whitbeck, 712 Hibbens Grant Blvd, suggested that existing STR
renters should be able to renew through Jan 31st. She suggested that there
should not be a minimum number of days of usage. She suggested that
emergency contact information should be provided during the application
process and not required each time the owner leaves town.

    Ms. Rachel Isaac, 178 Hobcaw Drive, spoke concerning the violations. She
suggested that there are several illegal renters because of the cap. She
suggested that allowing more permits would mean additional revenue for the
Town. She also suggested that the penalties for violations or illegal STRs should
be higher.

    Mr. Alex Catsman, 656 Coleman Blvd, expressed concern with not knowing
whether the current permits would be renewed. He asked that current permit
renters should be able to renew and receive a permit because of being an
existing legal STR operator. He agreed with the application time limit.

    Mr. John Clark, 174 Sixth Avenue, stated that they have a STR accessory

    Ms. Polly Cillpam, 620 Bonneau Lane, stated that she is concerned with
