5.   General Public Comment

There being no comments, Mr. Wren continued with the agenda.

6.    Requests
     A. Discussion of Town’s permitted residential density in commercial zoning
         districts. Town Council approved placing this item on the July Planning
         Commission agenda. The discussion pertains to the Town’s permitted
         residential density in commercial zoning districts, which is currently
         12units/acre.

     Ms. Reed reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     The Commission discussed the density within the hubs and throughout the
     Town and if the density for areas outside the hubs should be reduced.

     The Commission agreed that six units/acre may be appropriate, as well as
     adding a condition requiring it to be workforce housing only but would like staff
     to review and bring back more data to the commission to determine if this
     density would be appropriate. The Commission also would like to see staff’s
     recommendation regarding missing middle housing.

     The Commission agreed to further discuss this issue at their August meeting.

     B. Presentation by Engineering and Development Services staff, detailing
        the Land Development approval process, as administered by the
        Development Review Team. Presentation will cover purview of both
        Town staff and Planning Commission in vesting Sketch Plans.

     Mr. Mitchell and Ms. Wright reviewed the Development Review Team
     approval process with the Commission.

     The Commission discussed approval of sketch plans and agreed that the
     application should be complete with all information required on the checklist
     is included with the application. They discussed if conditional approvals
     should be given or if the request should be denied because the information
     was not included.
