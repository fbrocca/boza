3.   General Public Comment

There being no comments, Ms. Smith continued with the agenda.

4.   Items from March Meeting
     A. Rezoning Case: R-21-20, Request to amend the Long Point PD, Planned
        Development District Ordinance, specifically pertaining to a 5.98-acre
        parcel designated as Tract 12 of the PD located near the intersection of
        Needlerush Parkway and Mount Royall Drive. The proposed amendment
        would allow the erection, operation and maintenance of a wireless
        communications facility including a tower not to exceed 140 feet in height.
        The property is currently improved with the neighborhood amenity center
        and soccer fields.

           Ms. Reed reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

   Mr. Jonathan Yates, Mr. Nick Wyndham, Mr. Murphy Duffy, and Mr. Jody
McInerny reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Ms. Linda Seaton, 561 Galero Lane, suggested that the Commission is
elected and that this should be reconsidered if the desires of the citizens are
not heard for this request. She recited names of experts in cell service, NSA,
UN, etc. She suggested that these individuals are experts on 5G and that this
request is in direct response to the recent technology.

    Mr. Rob Turquoise, attorney with Rice Motley, stated that cell towers have
non-ionized radiation and early studies did not provide sufficient information
on the effects of cell tower radiation on health. He stated that studies within
the last ten years indicate that the radiation causes significant health issues.

   Mr. Bob Hamis, Longpoint resident, stated that he is opposed to the request.
He expressed concern with child health issues associated with cell towers. He
asked that this radiation be kept from the children and residents.

    Ms. Amy McCloud, 125 King St, stated that it is the FCC that regulates cell
towers and does not prohibit decisions made based on health concerns. She
expressed concern with decrease in property values. She expressed concern
that the cell tower would cause health issues for the surrounding residents.

    Ms. Michele DeMarco, 1854 Peaceful Way, expressed similar concerns with
the cell tower that have already been addressed. She questioned whether the
Longpoint residents were surveyed on what kind of service they currently
receive.

      Mr. Nathan Bowers, 192 Oak Landing Drive, expressed concern with child
safety and the unknown of the effects the cell tower may have. He suggested
that the reason for the proposed location was to be farther away from residents
because of the effects of the cell tower, the affect to property values, and that
it is unsightly.

    Mr. Matt Everson, 324 Wild Horse Lane, suggested that there is a decrease
of Verizon coverage at his home. He stated that access to first responders is
important as well as being able to contact family members. He expressed

concern with decrease in property values due to poor service in the area. He
stated that he has started a petition allow the cell tower.

   Ms. Cindy Campson, 794 Needlerush Parkway, asked how the request met

would be impacted by the cell tower that were not heard.

   Mr. Brian Wilson, 1976 Long Circle, suggested that there was not a proper

   Mr. James Tweedy, 1938 Tyson Lane, expressed concern with child safety

permission from the residents. He asked that this not be approved.

    Ms. Karen Gadol, 2558 Kingsfield, stated that as a physician that treats

on a 5 to 1 vote, with Mr. Bennett, Mr. Davis, Mr. Gates, Ms. Smith, and Mr.
