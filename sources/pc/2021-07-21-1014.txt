6. Requests
   A. R-9-21, Request to rezone from NC, Neighborhood Commercial, to PI-2,
      Public Institutional-2 District, an approximately 0.13-acre parcel located at
      210 Lucas St. identified by TMS No. 5171600056. The parcel is located in
      the Coleman Boulevard Overlay District. There is no request to change this
      designation. Mt. Pleasant Waterworks Pump Station 15 is housed on this
      site and proposing site improvements to the existing facility.

      Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

   The applicant not being present, Mr. Wren continued with the public hearing.

       Mr. Justin Lucey, adjacent property owner, stated that he is opposed to the
   request. He suggested that the property is currently non-conforming by zoning
   and maintenance of the property in regards to the buffer. He suggested that
   the requested zoning would make it easier to waive the various requirements
   that they are not currently complying with. He expressed concern with the
   buffer and suggested that it was not sufficient and suggested that the buffer
   around the property should be established and maintained. He expressed
   concern with drainage and suggested that development of the property would
   further exacerbate the drainage problems. He suggested that currently the lot
   is being used as a garbage dumping area and parking for nearby businesses.

      There being no further comments, Mr. Wren closed the public hearing.

   Mr. Bennett entered at this time (5:08 pm).

      Ms. Smith moved for denial. Mr. Davis seconded the motion. All in favor.

   B. R-10-21, Request to amend the Oakland Plantation PD, Planned
