      Mr. Wren called the meeting to order at 11:04 am

1. Approval of Agenda

     Mr. Millet moved for approval of the agenda. Mr. Gates seconded the motion.
All in favor.

2. Public Comment

    Mr. George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Rd, and representative for CAGE, stated
that he has attended the Forum meetings. He expressed his concern over the
alignment of Highway 41 to Porcher Bluff Road via Hungryneck Blvd/Billy Swails
Blvd connection. He referred to a map that he distributed to the Commission
showing a proposed location of the connection (attachment 1). He asked that the
Commission consider retaining the original road alignment as shown on his map.
He stated that this would divert traffic from Highway 41 directly to Billy Swails Blvd.

Ms. Gregg entered at this time (11:07 am).

There being no further comments, Mr. Wren continued with the agenda.

3. Presentation on the Draft Comprehensive Plan

    Ms. Boyles stated that staff would review the draft plan with the Commission
at this meeting. She stated that following the Town Hall meeting on January 23rd,
the Commission would complete a more in depth review with consideration of

possible revisions. She reviewed the draft plan through a PowerPoint Presentation

    Mr. Morrison reviewed the mobility element of the Comprehensive Plan with
the Commission.

Ms. Smith asked the population calculated for 2020. Mr. Morrison answered that
he is not sure the exact number, but it was approximately 110,000.

Mr. Millet asked if the 110,000 population is considered the buildout. Mr. Morrison
answered that it is the buildout of the parameters of this Comprehensive Plan.

Mr. Cuttino asked what is meant as “potential project”. Mr. Morrison answered
that the language could be changed to better identify the projects. He stated that
these projects are included in the mobility plan, but currently unfunded.

Mr. Millet asked if the mobility plan includes public transportation and suggested
that the Town should take the initiative. Mr. Morrison answered that a study has
been completed on the demand for public transportation. He stated that the next
step is to identify how those demands can be addressed. He stated that CARTA is
the federal transit authority. If the Town wanted to take the initiative for a project,
it would require the Town to fund the entire project. Mr. Millet asked if public
transportation is addressed in the plan. Mr. Morrison answered in the affirmative.

Ms. Gregg asked what Mount Pleasant Way would physically look like. Mr.
Morrison answered that it would be a minimum 10 foot wide paved pathway. He
stated that the first step would be a feasibility study that is included in the action
plan section of the Comprehensive Plan.

The Commission convened for a short break at 12:10 pm and reconvened at 12:19
pm.

Ms. Boyles reviewed the action plan with the Commission.

Ms. Smith asked how information would be forwarded to the implementation
committee. Ms. Boyles answered that staff would have the responsibility of
keeping the implementation committee updated on what areas need to be focused
on.

Mr. McNeill questioned the effectiveness of the implementation committee. Ms.
Boyles stated that this was a recommendation from the Comprehensive Plan
Forum.

Mr. Wren asked how the appendices would be accessed online. Ms. Boyles
answered that the maps and appendices would be hyperlinked within the
document.

Mr. Wren asked if the neighborhood character areas would dictate the zoning. Ms.
Boyles answered in the negative and stated that the underlying zoning is not
proposed to be changed.

Mr. McNeill asked if the neighborhood would determine what the zoning would be.
Ms. Boyles answered in the negative.

Ms. Smith asked if there was discussion on limiting hotels and self-storage facilities.
Ms. Boyles answered that there has been some discussion on this. She suggested
that further consideration and possible recommendations could be included in the
plan regarding this issue. She suggested that this issue is noted for additional
discussion at future meetings.

4. Confirmation of next meeting date and time

The Commission discussed the need for a meeting next week.

The Commission agreed not to meet until after the Town Hall meeting on January
23rd.

The Commission discussed having an overview of the Comprehensive Plan at the
January 23rd meeting prior to allowing public input. The Commission agreed that a
timeframe should be included on the agenda to give the public a better idea of
when the information would be presented and public comment accepted.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 1:14 pm.
