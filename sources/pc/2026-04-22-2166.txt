     A. PUBLIC HEARING, R-11-26, Ten Mile Community Overlay Zoning District,

     B.    PUBLIC HEARING, R-12-26, 210 Klink Lane, TMS No. 532-01-00-147,
           Request to rezone 210 Klink Lane from AB-1 to R-2 Staff Report

          Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

          Mr. John Rice, applicant, reviewed the request with the Commission.

          Mr. Edward Oswald, 158 Hobcaw, state that he also owns this property
     and a couple of other adjacent properties. He stated that he is in favor of
     the request and suggested that Klink Lane is not conducive to commercial
     traffic.

         Ms. Shauntay Brocco, 207 Klink Lane, stated that she is in favor of the
     request. She suggested that the area is more conducive to residential uses
     rather than commercial uses.

          There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

         Mr. Smyth moved for approval. Mr. Davis seconded the motion. Motion
     carried on a 6 to 1 vote with Mr. Ferrara, Mr. Smyth, Mr. Bennett, Ms.
     Sanford, Mr. Davis and Ms. Mansour in favor; Ms. Ireland opposed.

7.   Discussion of The Comprehensive Plan’s Recommended Future Land Use as
     it relates to Zoning.

    Ms. Reed reviewed staff comments with the Commission regarding properties
recommended for Public Institutional (PI) in the Comprehensive Plan but have a
different existing zoning.

    The Commission discussed the reasons for the possible zoning changes and
the best way to handle those issues.

Ms. Sanford left at this time (6:13 pm).

    The Commission suggested that starting with rezoning the Town-owned
property to PI could be an option. They also discussed what other options might
be available. Staff stated that they would explore possible options or solutions
for consideration.
