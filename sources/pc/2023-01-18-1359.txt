                TOWN OF MOUNT PLEASANT, SOUTH CAROLINA
                         PLANNING COMMISSION
                            JANUARY 18, 2023
                               MINUTES


PowerPoint Presentation

Present:    Kathy Smith, Chair, Garrett Lacy, Brett Bennett, Michael McCormick,
            Pam Ireland, Gary Davis, Bill Smyth, George Gates.
Absent:     Bruce Koedding.
Staff:      Michele Reed, Peter Stone, James Ward, Lynnette Lynes

      Ms. Smith called the meeting to order at 5:00 pm.

1.   Approval of the Agenda

     Mr. Davis moved for approval of the agenda. Ms. Ireland seconded the motion.
All in favor.

2.   Approval of Minutes from December 14th Meeting

   Mr. Bennett moved for approval of the minutes. Mr. Gates seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3.   Update on Planning Commission Recommendations

Mr. Stone reviewed Town Council decisions with the Commission.

4.   General Correspondence

All correspondence received was linked to the agenda.

5.   General Public Comment

    Mr. Stuart Whiteside, Seamon, Whiteside, and Associates, spoke regarding the
impact assessment. He stated that he is available to answer any questions. He
stated that they have included a 50-foot buffer as was previously approved due to
the development of All-American Blvd.

6.   Requests
     A. R-01-23      Request to amend the Patriots Point PD, Planned
         Development, specifically pertaining to the Charleston Harbor Resort
         and Marina, located on approximately 23-acres fronting the Charleston
         Harbor at the terminus of Patriots Point Rd. Proposal to allow for an
         additional 133 hotel units for a total of 350 hotel units development
         wide. The subject properties are further identified by TMS Nos. 517-00-
         00-002,    517-00-00-119,     517-00-00-106       and    517-00-00-107.
         Correspondence

     This item was withdrawn by staff.

     B.    Impact Assessment/Conceptual Plan Request for Impact Assessment
           and Conceptual Plan Approval for George Browder Blvd. Office
           development. An approximately 6.78-acre undeveloped tract of land,
           proposing to develop 79,200 SF of office space. Located between
           Lifepark Church and Park West Subdivision entrance. Subject property
           is further identified by TMS Nos. 598-00-00-404 and 598-00-00-473

          Mr. McCormick recused himself due to a conflict of interest.

          Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

         Ms. Smith moved for approval of the impact assessment and if the
     applicant desires to reduce the amount of onsite parking they do so through a
     legal shared parking agreement. Mr. Gates seconded the motion. All in favor
     with Mr. McCormick abstaining.

         Ms. Smith moved for approval of the conceptual plan with movement of
     the location of the office building as presented at this meeting and approval of
     a 50-foot buffer. Mr. Bennett seconded the motion. All in favor with Mr.
     McCormick abstaining.

7.   Other Business
     A. Adoption of 2023 Rules of Procedure

        Mr. Gates moved for deferral until the legal issues discussed in a meeting
     with staff are addressed and items not pertinent to this Commission are
     removed. Ms. Ireland seconded the motion. All in favor.
