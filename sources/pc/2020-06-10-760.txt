4.   General Public Comment

    Mr. Stuart Hewlett, 1 Darlington Lane, spoke regarding the sketch plan for 915
McCants. He expressed concern with there being a watershed issue and there is
flooding in the area. He stated that they are currently working with the Town to
install a private system and asked that no additional construction is allowed until the
system is installed.

    Mr. Austin VanOldst, 3567 Backshore Drive, expressed concern with the Carolina
Park sketch plan and the extension of Backshore Drive and that it would increase
traffic causing child and pedestrian safety issues. He suggested that there is not a
benefit for the extension and asked that it be removed.

    Mr. Adam Eeling, Cross Tree Lane, expressed concern with the Backshore Drive
extension. He expressed concern with child safety and increased traffic in the area.
He stated that there are a number of children that play in the area. He asked that
the extension not be included on the sketch plan and not completed.

    Mr. Michael Baer, 3496 Wilkes Way, stated that there are many citizens here in
opposition of Backshore Drive extension. He suggested that interconnectivity can
be accomplished through sidewalks and pedestrian path. He stated that there is a
petition with over 300 signatures in opposition of the request.

    Ms. Diane Tideman, 1567 Whiskey Road, expressed concern with the Backshore
Drive extension. She stated that the master plan did not show a roadway but only a
pedestrian path. She also expressed concern with pedestrian safety. She stated that
the proposed football stadium and commercial uses, would increase traffic in the
area.

    Mr. Mike Abrams, 3504 Wilkes Way, expressed concern with the extension of
Backshore Drive and suggested that in addition to the safety issues, it would affect
the property values. He suggested that it would also increase the traffic. He
expressed concern that the extension would increase crime in the area as well. He
suggested that the only benefit would be for Oceanside Academy.

    Ms. Jamie, Steen, 3541 Cross Trees Lane, expressed concern with students
walking from Oceanside Academy home. She expressed concern with vehicles
speeding in the area that could affect pedestrian safety. She suggested that the
extension would affect the safety of the students and residents. She expressed
concern with the extension causing additional cut-through traffic. She asked that
the request not be approved.

   Ms. Wendy Sar, 3492 Wilkes Way, expressed concern with child safety and that
the extension of Backshore Drive could be detrimental. She suggested that she
agrees to all of the previous comments and that the extension should not be
approved. She stated that the common area behind her home is frequently used
and would like it to remain.

   Ms. Diana Rason, Backshore Drive, expressed concern with the extension of
Backshore Drive. She stated that as a pediatric trauma nurse she is familiar with
MVA and expressed concern that the completion of the extension would impact

   Mr. Brian Bowerban, 1417 Darlington Lane, expressed concern with the McCants

5.   Sketch Plans from April Meeting
     A. Case # SP-01-20, Sketch Plan approval for 710 Sparks Street, a proposal
         to subdivide one (1) lot (approximately 1.30 acres) into three (3) Single
         family residential lots.

          Mr. Mitchell reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

        Mr. Chris Donato, Sitecast, and Mr. Ellis Leisman, Attorney, reviewed the
     request with the Commission.

        Ms. Smith moved for approval. Mr. Wren seconded the motion. Motion
     passed on a 5 to 1 vote with Mr. Campbell, Mr. Davis, Mr. Gates, Mr. Wren, and
     Ms. Smith in favor; Mr. Bennett opposed.

     B.    Case # SP-02-20, Sketch Plan approval for 915 McCants Drive, a proposal
           to subdivide two (2) lots, a total of approximately 0.98 acres, into three
           (3) Single family residential lots (each approximately 0.33 acres. Also
           includes a request for a Waiver from the Land Development Regulations
           to allow access from a private easement.

          Mr. Mitchell reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

         Mr. Randy Wilkes, owner, Mr. Ben Rope, and Brian Collie reviewed the
     request with the Commission.

         Mr. Campbell moved for denial based on flooding issues that should be
     resolved before approval is granted. Mr. Davis seconded the motion. Motion
     passed on a 5 to 1 vote with Ms. Smith opposed.

6.   Sketch Plans from May Meeting
     A. Case #: SP-05-20, Request approval of a modification to the Sketch Plan
