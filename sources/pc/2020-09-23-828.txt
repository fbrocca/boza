     Mr. Gates moved for approval of the minutes, noting that the date on tonight’s

3. Update of Planning Commission Recommendations

    Ms. Reed reviewed Town Council decisions with the Commission.

4. Correspondence

There being no additional correspondence, Mr. Wren continued with the agenda.

5. General Public Comment

   Walter Kaufmann, 1411 Barbara St, expressed concern with the sketch plan for
McCants. He stated that he is in favor of the request and asked that it should be
approved. He suggested that the proposed plans should provide additional relief
from drainage problems in the area.

    Mr. Stuart Hewlett, 1 Darlington Lane, spoke regarding McCants sketch plan
request. He expressed concern with the drainage in the area and that the drainage
would cross his property. He stated that Darlington Lane is not maintained by the
Town and has not been resurfaced. He referred to a Thomas and Hutton study that
indicated that this drainage system is the third worse in the Town. He expressed
concern that the proposed drainage plan would not resolve drainage issues. He
also expressed concern that adding additional lots would increase traffic.

   Ms. Maxine Sullivan, 1427 Barbara Street, expressed concern with the McCants
sketch plan request and that with the amount of drainage issues that she has seen
and suggested that it was due to the new construction on Darlington. She
suggested that no further construction should be allowed until the drainage issues
are resolved and a storm drain is installed. She distributed photos for the
Commission to review of the drainage problems with her property.

    Ms. Anna Richter, Goblet Ave, spoke regarding McCants sketch plan and stated
that she is in favor of the request. She stated that there have not been any drainage
issues on her property or traffic issues in her neighborhood.

    Mr. Randy Welch, 915 McCants, spoke regarding the sketch plan request. He
suggested that the drainage issues are due to the ground water rise/fall and not
the nearby construction. He also noted that the drainage pipe has been blocked.
