          Development District Ordinance. Proposed is to create a new mixed-use
          district known as the Town of Mount Pleasant Tract (TOMP Tract.) The

      TOMP Tract District allows for uses that are in accordance with the Town
      of Mount Pleasant Economic Strategic Plan and Economic Development
      Zoning District, with certain restrictions on uses as defined in the
      guidelines, in addition to 30 Attainable Housing units. Also requested is
      to amend the Carolina Park DA, Development Agreement, to have two
      public hearings held by Town Council.

     Ms. Reed reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     Ms. Farrell stated that staff is available for any additional questions.

    Ms. Jill Kajouras, 3657 Maidstone Drive, stated that the majority of the
Carolina Park residents were opposed to affordable housing as it was felt that
it would affect housing values. She expressed concern that this proposed
development would increase traffic in the area. She expressed concern
removal of the trees within the proposed 11 acre development and that this
proposal would not be in keeping with the neighborhood.

     There being no further comments, Ms. Smith closed the public hearing.

   Mr. Lacy moved for approval. Mr. Bennett seconded the motion. Motion
passed on a 5 to 1 vote with Mr. Smyth, Mr. Bennett, Mr. Koedding, Mr. Lacy
and Ms. Smith in favor; Mr. Davis opposed.
