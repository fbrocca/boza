      Mr. Wren called the meeting to order at 9:03 am.

1. Approval of Agenda

   Ms. Smith moved for approval of the agenda. Mr. Bennett seconded the
motion. All in favor.

2. Discussion on Nonconformities

   Ms. Reed and Ms. Boyles reviewed nonconformities with the Commission.

Mr. McCormick entered at 9:05 am.

Mr. Millet left at 9:55 am.

     The Commission discussed how to address nonconformities and what
changes to the definitions should be considered. The Commission discussed
including definition of a benign nonconformity and a detrimental nonconformity.

The Commission agreed that staff would work on suggested changes and bring
back to the Commission for discussion at the March regular meeting.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 10:33 am.
