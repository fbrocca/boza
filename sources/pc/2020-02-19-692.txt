B. Rezoning Case: R-20-2020 This petition is to rezone two parcels approximately
   0.47 acres located at the intersection of Bowman Road and White Flat Road.
   One parcel is zoned R-1, Low Density Residential District, and is improved with
   an office that was converted from a single family residence. The other parcel
   is zoned PD, Planned Development District, and is vacant except for an off-site
   sign for the Watermark Development. The applicant wishes to rezone both
   parcels to OP, Office Professional District.
   Ownership and Property Identification: Ross, Cangelosi & Associates, LLC.
   1242 White Flat Road. TMS No’s. 560-02-00-005, and -047.
   Zoning Information: Current Zoning is R-1, Low Density Residential District,
   and PD, Planned Development District; Proposed Zoning is OP, Office
   Professional District.

    Ms. Canon reviewed staff comments with the Commission. She stated that
the request for 1236 White Flat Road was withdrawn by the applicant.

Trevor Cangelosi and Jeff Ross reviewed the request with the Commission.

Mr. Mike Basher, 1235 White Flat Road, stated that there is a septic system
business on one of the adjacent properties, but it is a small business.

Mr. Greg White, 1233 White Flat Road, stated that the septic business is a home-
based business with only an office space located on site. He stated that his family
has owned the property for over 40 years and suggested that it should not be
assumed that the area is going commercial. He suggested that rezoning to a
commercial zoning would affect the residential character of the neighborhood.
He stated that he is opposed to the rezoning.

There being no further comments, Mr. Wren closed the public hearing.

Mr. Cangelosi suggested that his request should be considered as a separate
request and if other properties in the area would like to rezone at a later date,
then that should be separately considered.

  Mr. Neal moved for approval of the request with the withdrawal of 1236
White Flat Road. Mr. Millet seconded the motion. All in favor.
