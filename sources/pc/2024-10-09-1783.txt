PowerPoint Presentation

Present:     Brett Bennett, Chair, Garrett Lacy, Emily Sanford, Adam Ferrara,

Absent:      Gary Davis, Mike Tinkey.
Staff:       Michele Reed, Peter Stone, David Pagliarini, Eddie Bernard, Michael

      Mr. Bennett called the meeting to order at 5:01 pm.

1.   Approval of the Agenda

   Ms. Ireland moved for approval of the agenda. Mr. Ferrara seconded the
motion. All in favor.

2.   Correspondence

     All correspondence was linked to the agenda.

3.   General Public Comment

     Mr. James McKee, 1247 Palmetto Peninsula Drive, spoke regarding tree
protection. He suggested that there should be stricter requirements. He suggested
that the recommendations included in the Community Forestry Master Plan
(CFMP) should be reviewed and considered for inclusion in the zoning code. He
stated that the CFMP also recommended one point of enforcement and suggested
that this might be included in the zoning code.

    Ms. Michele Strauss, 545 Veranda View, spoke regarding storm runoff and
impervious surface coverage throughout the Town. She suggested that the
mitigation amount for replacement trees should be increased so that more trees
can be replanted within the Town. She also suggested that there be a tree
mitigation review board, mitigation should be required for all tree removal, and a
certified arborist should consult during the Board of Zoning Appeals (BOZA)
process.

    Mr. David Quick, 1502 Village Square, suggested that the review of the zoning
code should also consider what is needed for sustainability and resilience,
particularly in regard to flood waters. He expressed concern with impervious
surface lot coverage and suggested that this should be further reviewed and a
lower percentage considered. He expressed concern with tree removals and the
low number of tree coverage throughout the Town. He suggested that the CFMP
recommendations should be included in the zoning code. He suggested that
restoration of the tree canopy is very important and more should be done to ensure
the preservation and restoration of the Town’s tree canopy.

    Ms. Lisa Schniptke, 185 Mossy Oak Way, suggested that more time should be
taken to allow sufficient time to review the lengthy document. She suggested that
the changes within §156.827 pertaining to murals should be reviewed and murals
should be allowed to be seen from the main streets. She suggested that the
recommendations outlined in the CFMP should be implemented and included in
the zoning code as appropriate.

    Ms. Diedre Kennedy, 971 Cliffwood Drive, asked that the tree protection section
of the zoning code should be deferred to allow for better review and additional
enforcement considered. She expressed concern with the number of trees being
removed. She suggested that the existing tree canopy should be preserved and
retained, particularly during construction on lots.

     There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett continued with the agenda.
