5.   General Public Comment

    Ms. Cassandra Davis, 1148 Hero Road, spoke regarding spoke regarding the
rezoning of 1490 Boston Grill Road. She expressed concern with the requirement
to annex into the Town to receive water and sewer. She referenced state code 5-3-
1520 and 5-3-150 and suggested that the Town code should align with the state

code. She asked that the Town work with MPW to change the ordinance to require
annexation to receive water and sewer. She asked that the request be denied.

    Ms. Myra Snipe-Richardson, 2755 Earl Johnson Lane, President of Hamlin Beach
Community, suggested that the requirement to annex into the Town for water and
sewer is causing an issue with preserving the historic settlement communities. She
asked that removing the requirement of annexation would better preserve the
historic communities and maintain the character of the neighborhoods.

There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed public comment and
continued with the agenda.

     Waterworks worked with the Snowden Community to allow existing homes so

     the annexation would deteriorate the historic community. She asked that the
