   acre Oyster Park Commercial Development: to rezone from AB, Areawide

the amount required and that if these excess parking spaces were removed, the
larger buffer could be completed.

    Ms. Andi Free, 1117 Dawn View Terrace, stated that their back yard abuts

   J. Discuss possible revisions to Town’s definition for hotels and the number
      of bedrooms, parking, and their appropriateness in the Neighborhood
      Commercial Zoning District.

      Ms. Reed reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

       The Commission agreed that the definition for hotel and boutique hotel
   should be clarified with an inn having a lower number of rooms than a boutique
   hotel. They agreed that there should be a definition for a guest room versus a
   guest suite. They agreed that a single guest room should require 1 parking
   space, with a guest suite requiring 2 parking spaces. They agreed that NC should
   not allow hotels, but could allow an inn, to be defined as having no more than
   ten rooms.
