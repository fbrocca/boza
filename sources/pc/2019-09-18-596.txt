   1432 Hale Road / Rifle Range Road / Hale Road Parcel ID (TMS No.): 560-

   Ms. Canon reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

Mr. Eddie Twilly, 288 Indigo Circle, stated that he and his neighbors have
questions on if there will be any additional accesses allowed.

Ms. Laura Fox, 1507 Plover, expressed concern with the impact to the
neighborhood and that the rezoning might mean that there will be increased
traffic on Hale Road.

Ms. Angela Barnett, CCSD representative, stated that part of the reason for the
request is to help alleviate drop-off/pick-up traffic.

Mr. Bill Rumer, 1433 Hale Road, expressed concern with aesthetics and if there
would be any additional landscaping to screen the drop-off lanes and parking.

Ms. Denise Bruiser, 1504 Plover, expressed concern with bike and pedestrian
safety. She suggested that the study was done when part of the parking area
was closed. She suggested that there should be sufficient parking and area for
the vehicles. She agreed that there should be additional screening.

Ms. Sue Hickman, 1621 Wren, expressed concern with traffic and child safety.
She suggested that there should be more controls in place to better protect the
children walking to school.

Ms. Beth Fondren, 228 Indigo Bay Circle, suggested that there appears to be
plans and suggested that these plans for the school should be provided at this
meeting.

Mr. Danny Shram, 1516 Wren, stated that his understanding was that part of
the playground would be used as an access road running parallel to Hale Road
and suggested that this should be considered to keep traffic off Hale Road.

There being no further comments, Mr. Wren closed the public hearing.

G. Proposal to amend Section 156.201(I)(2)(c), of Chapter 156, Zoning Code,
   of the Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances pertaining to the vision corridor
   provision of the Critical Line Buffer. Proposed changes include language
   clarifying that the vision corridor provision only applies to residential
   property; clearing cannot occur for the benefit of an adjoining property
   owner; and that the 33 percent clearing requirement cannot be
   incremental and must remain unified.

   Ms. Canon reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachments 1).

Mr. Cap Prescott, 213 South Plaza Court, stated that as chairman of the
Architectural Review Board (ARB) they contacted Town staff regarding the
ability to have multiple view corridors and asked that this be considered. He
asked that the amendment be rejected.

Ms. LeAnn Adkins, 34 Hopetown Road, suggested that given the seriousness of
this amendment and the error noted by staff during review of staff comments,
that the accurate text should be presented and have a proper public hearing.

There being no further comments, Mr. Wren closed the public hearing.

   Ms. Smith moved for denial of the amendment as proposed and that in an
abundance of caution, due to the text error, that is should be correctly
advertised. Mr. Gates seconded he motion. All in favor.
