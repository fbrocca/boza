   Mr. Tim Cook, representative for the applicant, reviewed the request with the
Commission.

    Ms. Myra Richardson, 2755 Earl Johnson, president of Hamlin HOA, stated
that Charleston County currently has a moratorium within historic districts that
limits the number of home built to no more than four. She expressed concern
with the number of trees on the property and that they would be removed. She
also expressed concern that this would increase traffic in the area. Additionally,
she expressed concern with drainage as there is surrounding property that lies
lower than the proposed property. She asked that the request be denied.

There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett moved to Commission questions.

     Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

   Ms. Ireland moved for denial. Mr. Davis seconded the motion. Motion carried
on a 7 to 1 vote with Mr. Smyth, Mr. Bennett, Ms. Sanford, Mr. Davis, Ms.
Monsour, Mr. Tinkey and Ms. Ireland in favor; Mr. Lacy opposed.

C.    PUBLIC HEARING, Shem Creek Boat Landing, TMS No. 517-16-00-015, 033,
      083, 084, 085, 086, 087, R-07-25, Request to Rezone PI-1, seven parcels
      currently zoned R-2 Staff Report

Ms. Sanford left at this time (5:42 pm).

     Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

    Mr. Mel Mowrer, 525 Mill Street, expressed concern with the change in
zoning. He suggested that changing to PI-1 zoning, would mean that some uses
would be allowed without further approval from the Town needed.

    Mr. Benjamin Tory, 529 Mill St, expressed concern with the amount of traffic
and that there is a safety issue. He asked that a traffic study be required. He also
expressed concern with the number cars that park on the sidewalk, impeding
pedestrian access.

    Mr. Lucas Watson, 121 Simmons St, expressed concern with the number of
historic trees in the area and that further development of this site would mean
more runoff to the historic trees and could compromise the health of the trees.
He expressed concern with child safety due to the nearby school. He suggested
that some roadway controls such as speedhumps should be considered. He
suggested that additional parking and impervious surface would further degrade
Shem Creek. He suggested that the property should remain residential.

     There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

     Mr. Davis moved for denial. Motion failed due to the lack of a second.

    Mr. Lacy moved for approval. Ms. Ireland seconded the motion. Motion
passed on a 6 to 1 vote with Mr. Smyth, Mr. Bennett, Mr. Lacy, Ms. Mansour, Mr.
Tinkey, and Ms. Ireland in favor; Mr. Davis opposed.
