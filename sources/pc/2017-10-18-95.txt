B. Request approval of Sketch Plan for Schirmer Avenue Subdivision, four
   single family residential lots zoned R-1, Low Density Residential District, and
   UC-OD, Urban Corridor Overlay District, located at 1238 & 1242 Schirmer
   Avenue. TMS # 532-07-00-138

    Mr. Bernard reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachments 1
& 2).

Mr. Neal asked if the request is to subdivide the property into four lots. Mr.
Bernard answered in the affirmative. Mr. Neal asked what the density is for the
property. Mr. Bernard answered that it would be 16 units per acre since it is in
the UC-OD. Mr. Neal asked about drainage for the area. Mr. Mitchell answered
that there is a sizable ditch and showed it on the map for the Commission. He
stated that the onsite drainage would be handled through that detention system.

Mr. Pagliarini stated that he received correspondence from Coen Company, LLC
dated October 18, 2017, and read it for the record.

Mr. Eric Draper, representative for the applicant, reviewed the request.

Mr. Neal asked if the trees recommended for preservation would be saved. Mr.
Draper answered in the affirmative. Mr. Neal asked how access would be
handled with the shared driveway. Mr. Draper answered that it would be a one-
way drive. Mr. Neal asked about drainage. Mr. Draper answered the drainage
plan has not been finalized. Mr. Neal asked if the applicant was amenable to the
interconnectivity. Mr. Draper answered in the affirmative.

Mr. Wren asked if the existing structures would be removed. Mr. Draper
answered in the affirmative.

    Ms. Smith moved for approval of the request including all staff comments.

Ms. Smith thanked staff for their specific comments.

    Mr. Wren called for a vote on the motion. All in favor.

   residential lots zoned PD, Planned Development District, located at End of

waiver. Mr. Cook answered that lots 4-7 require a LDR waiver and showed them
on the sketch plan for the Commission. Mr. Neal asked if there would be any

Commission.

Mr. Wren asked about the LDR waiver. Ms. Canon stated that the process
changed last year regarding how LDR waivers are requested and the reason that
the previous requests for this subdivision did not come before the Commission.

Ms. Smith asked about staff comments 5-8. Mr. Cook reviewed these with the
Commission.

    Ms. Campaigne asked if Mr. Cook would be amenable to removing lot 18 to
preserve the trees, have additional open space, better connectivity to the trail
system. Mr. Neal agreed that the trees should be preserved.

Mr. Cook asked if the parcel adjacent to lot 18 could be allowed to be developed
and the interconnectivity removed. He stated because the adjacent property is
not being developed, the interconnectivity is no longer required.

Mr. Neal asked if interconnectivity is not required, could the proposed sketch
plan be approved. Mr. Pagliarini answered that he was not sure that the sketch
plan could be approved with that condition. Mr. Prause answered that if it was
part of the planned development ordinance, then an ordinance amendment
would be necessary. He suggested that the best course of action would be to
submit a new sketch plan.

Ms. Gregg asked about the connectivity and when it was approved. Ms. Canon
answered that she would need to confirm whether it was part of the Bartone
Tract request, which was not approved, or was a condition of the subject parcel
coming into the Pepper PD.

Mr. Pagliarini stated that the new submittal would have to be reviewed on its
own merits. He stated that only requirements included within the planned
development ordinance can be required and enforced.

    Ms. Campaigne moved for denial of the sketch plan and LDR waiver request.
Mr. Cuttino seconded the motion.

Ms. Campaigne stated that there have been several issues that need to be
addressed, so she would like to see a different sketch plan.

Mr. Neal stated that he is not concerned if there is connectivity with this property.

Mr. Wren called for a vote on the motion. All in favor.

F. Request to annex and zone TH, Townhouse District, an approximately 11.99
   tract of land comprised of 77 parcels, located along Dingle Road, known as
   the Royal Palms Townhomes. Development is not proposed to be included
   in the SB-OD, Sweetgrass Basket Overlay District, located on Dingle Road.
                                                              Planning Commission
                                                                  October 18, 2017

   TMS #578-00-00-162, 578-00-00-538 through -559, 578-00-00-595 through -
   596, 578-00-00-606 through -608, 578-00-00-610 through -620, 578-00-00-
   667 through -703, 578-00-00-711

   Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachments
1 & 2).

Mr. Wren asked if not being in SB-OD was a staff recommendation. Mr.
Rutherford answered in the affirmative.

There being no comments, Mr. Wren closed the public hearing.

Ms. Gregg asked if there is precedent for projects completed in the County and
then annexed. Mr. Prause answered that this is the first 75% annexation
completed under the new system and requested within the last 20 years.

Mr. Neal asked if they would have public water and sewer. Mr. Prause answered
that they already have water and sewer.

Ms. Smith asked if this would set a precedent for future townhome development
in the area. Mr. Prause answered that it is possible to develop in the county and
then annex into the Town. He stated that if the property is contiguous to the
Town, they are required to annex in order to receive water and sewer service.

Mr. Wren asked the reason for not taking ownership of the roadway. Mr.
Rutherford answered that the roadway is not built to town or county standards;
therefore, the roadway would remain a private road.

   Ms. Smith moved for approval including all staff comments. Ms. Campaigne
seconded the motion.

Ms. Gregg expressed concern with establishing a precedent by approving a non-
conforming development.

Ms. Campaigne stated that she appreciates the property not being included in
the overlay district, so that the neighborhood character is maintained.
