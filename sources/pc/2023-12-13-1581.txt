        Ms. Smith called the meeting to order at 4:04 pm.

1.   Approval of the Agenda

     Mr. Davis moved for approval of the agenda. Ms. Ireland seconded the motion.
All in favor.

2.   Correspondence

There being no correspondence, Ms. Smith continued with the agenda.

3. General Public Comment

    Mr. David Quick, 1502 Village Square, spoke regarding the draft tree ordinance.
He expressed concern with notification of tree removal and suggested that the
surrounding neighbors should be notified in advance similar to what is done for the
Boards and Commission notification for better transparency. He also suggested
that protection of long leaf pine trees should be considered. He suggested that the
diameter of a mitigation tree should be reduced.

     Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning, suggested that the Town should be known for
its tree canopy. He suggested that the mitigation should be placed in the same
neighborhood, there should be notification of tree removal requests, long leaf
pines should be protected, and the penalty for illegal tree removal should be
increased. He suggested that there should be more inspections before, during, and
after construction. He suggested that a tree commission should be considered as
well.
