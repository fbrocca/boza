     A. PUBLIC HEARING, Zoning Code Text Amendment, Chapter 156.039,
         Proposed modification to certain Residential Districts, establishing density

    provisions where these districts are designated on the Comprehensive Plan
    Future Land Use Map as Community Facilities. Text Amendment Staff Report

   Ms. Reed reviewed the text amendment with the Commission.

   Mr. Bennett asked if the process for approval would be the same. Ms. Reed
answered in the affirmative.

    Mr. Davis asked what issue is trying to be resolved. Ms. Reed answered that
this is to provide a means for Town Council to weigh in on how the density is
determined.

     Ms. Ireland asked how many of the community facility properties are zoned
Areawide Business. Ms. Reed answered that there are only a few. Ms. Ireland
asked how many have been redeveloped since the change. Ms. Reed answered
that she is only aware of one instance. Ms. Ireland asked if Planning Committee or
Town Council recommended it come back to the Planning Commission. Ms. Reed
answered that Town Council recommended the item come back to the
Commission. Ms. Ireland asked if at least one residential unit could be
constructed. Ms. Reed answered in the affirmative and stated that they would be
allowed to build one residential unit for the entire parcel; if more units were
desired, they would need to request a Comprehensive Plan amendment. Ms.
Ireland asked if it was discussed allowing one unit per acre. Ms. Reed answered in
the negative and stated that this is the reason for this discussion.

    Ms. Barber asked if the caveat would be included in the ordinance. Ms. Reed
answered in the affirmative. Mr. Barber asked if R-3 and R-4 zoning would have
the same requirements. Ms. Reed answered that most are R-1 and R-2. Mr. Barber
asked if it would apply to commercial development. Ms. Reed answered in the
negative. Mr. Barber asked about commercial uses. Ms. Reed answered that the
density for commercial properties through other means such as overlay districts.

     Mr. Davis asked the reason for the change. Ms. Reed answered that those
making comments would be able to address this. She suggested that the concern
is that the density would be determined through the Comprehensive Plan.

    Mr. Berry suggested that the challenge is finding a balance between property
rights and having a change of use.

    Mr. Hacker Burr, Trident Academy, expressed concern with the proposed
changes and that this could negatively affect the school’s financial stability. He
expressed concern about not having sufficient publicity and notice for the
requested changes. He suggested that this would remove the right of
redevelopment from the property owners.

    Mr. Erik Kreutner, Assistant Head of Trident Academy, suggested that the Town
has not properly notified the churches and schools of the proposed changes. He
stated that Town Council expressed concern with optics, and that normal
notification of zoning changes means posting a sign, but this was not done. He
stated that staff indicated at the previous Town Council meeting staff indicated that
the property owners would be made aware of any zoning changes. He suggested
that this was not done and suggested that the Town is failing the churches and
schools in this manner.

    Mr. Furman Buchanan, Rector at Christ Episcopal Church, provided the history
of Christ Church Parish. He is aware of the requested changes to add density
requirements to the Comprehensive Plan rather than rezoning the property. He
suggested that R-1 zoning is a more appropriate zoning rather than PI zoning. He
stated that they have discussed using some of the church property for residential
homes for their clergy. He suggested that this is a critical need for many churches.
He suggested that they would also like to provide affordable housing for teachers
and other critical workforce. He suggested that preserving the R-1 zoning and
providing reasonable density would enable the church to achieve their goal of
providing affordable housing for their church staff and local housing for public
service employees.

    Mr. Gordon Shrek, trustee for Mount Pleasant Presbyterian Church, suggested
that there were a couple of churches not included in the recommendations for
rezoning and asked the reason for this. Mr. Bennett noted that this is a public
comment period and could be addressed through Commission questions.

    Mr. Gilly Dyer, attorney for Christ Our King and the Church Diocese, distributed
a copy of their legal concerns for the Commission to review. He stated that the
Diocese has 13 properties that would be affected by this requested change. He
stated that the Diocese relies on these properties for operations. He suggested
that the proposed change would devalue the properties by requiring Town Council

approval to redevelop the property for more than one residential unit through a
planned development or Comprehensive Plan amendment. He suggested that this
“puts the church and school on a different playing field” than other residential
property owners. He suggested that the requested change would give zoning
regulation effect to the Comprehensive Plan that is prohibited by state statute. He
also suggested that there is not an issue that needs to be resolved and therefore,

    Mr. J.C. Taylor, Board Chair of Trident Academy, reiterated that the concern is
the taking of a right that would have detrimental financial ramifications. He
suggested that the financial impacts would be significant if approved because of
the change to the property rights. He asked that the Commission consider the
consequences to the churches and schools that are a vital part of the community.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

    Mr. Berry asked if this would apply to all the 82 properties and were there any
exemptions. Ms. Reed answered that St. Andrews PI-1 and Friendship AME Church
are currently zoned PI-1. She stated that some of the properties currently have a
non-residential zoning or are in a planned development, so they would not be
affected by this proposed change. She stated that the request was to look at
changes for the properties zoned R-1 and R-2 that are recommended for
community facility in the Comprehensive Plan.

    Mr. Davis asked if this was a legal issue. Mr. Pagliarini answered that executive
session could be considered for legal advice if necessary.

    Ms. Ireland suggested that the Commission makes a recommendation and the
legal ramifications would be handled and considered with Town Council. She
stated that this was previously done in the Comprehensive Plan with no issue. She
stated that it was previously recommended to rezone the properties, but this was
abandoned by Town Council. She suggested that community facilities are needed
within the Town and there is a public interest in having these facilities within the
Town and retaining them. She suggested that requiring Town Council approval for
a change is not a definite “no” but gives Town Council the opportunity to weigh in
on whether the requested action is appropriate for the neighborhood and the
Town. She suggested that there should be an exception to allow churches to have
housing for clergy.
