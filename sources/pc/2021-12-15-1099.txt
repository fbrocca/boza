F.   Proposal to amend Sections 156.325, Principal Use Table, and 156.326,
     Principal Use Conditions, Zoning Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code of
     Ordinances, pertaining to Boutique Hotels or Inns and Hotels / Extended
     Stays. Proposed is to make both Boutique Hotels and Hotels a
     Conditional Use in all zoning districts where it’s now a Permitted Use,
     subject to defined conditions.

     Ms. Boyles reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

    Mr. Charlie Carmody, 107 New Street, stated that he is speaking for several
residents within the Town particularly the property at the corner of Ben
Sawyer and Center Street. He stated that this property is currently under
contract for a hotel use and expressed concern with the zoning of this property
potentially being changed while under contract. He stated that this is in favor
of hotel uses being included as a conditional use, but would like consideration
for allowing hotel uses within all zoning districts.

   Mr. Michael Bennett stated that he is in favor of allowing hotel uses as a
conditional use within all zoning districts. He noted that the parking
requirements and traffic for hotels is less than for an office building or other
more intense uses.

     There being no further comments, Mr. Wren closed public comments.

   Ms. Smith moved for denial of the request. Mr. Koedding seconded the
motion.
