                TOWN OF MOUNT PLEASANT, SOUTH CAROLINA
                     SPECIAL PLANNING COMMISSION
                            AUGUST 23, 2023
                                MINUTES


Presentation

Present:     Kathy Smith, Chair, Garrett Lacy, Brett Bennett, Pam Ireland, Gary Davis,
             Michelle Mansour, Bruce Koedding.
Absent:      Michael McCormick (excused), Bill Smyth
Staff:       Michele Reed, David Pagliarini, James Ward, Liz Boyles, Michael Robertson,
             Peter Stone, Chris Lubert, Jake Carter, Lynnette Lynes
Also:        Kelly Cousino, Sean Scoopmire, Justin Wallace, with White & Smith

     Ms. Smith called the meeting to order at 3:32 pm.

1.   Approval of the Agenda

   Mr. Bennett moved for approval of the agenda. Ms. Ireland seconded the
motion. All in favor.

2.   Correspondence
3.   General Public Comment

There being no correspondence or comments, Ms. Smith closed public comment
and continued with the agenda.

4.   New Business
     A. Review of drafts of Subchapter H: Building Design and Subchapter F:
        Signs

        Mr. Sean Scoopmire reviewed the draft sign ordinance with the
     Commission.

        The Commission discussed whether murals should be allowed within the
     Town and agreed that murals should not be allowed.

         Mr. Justin Wallace reviewed the draft commercial design standards with
     the Commission.

     The Commission suggested that standards for a drive-thru should be further
     discussed.

    Ms. Cousino stated that the changes suggested would be included in the draft
    code and a final draft would be brought back to the Commission for a

    B.   Discussion of Hubs/Mixed Use Centers

       Ms. Reed reviewed the draft hubs and mixed-use centers with the
    Commission.

        The Commission discussed residential density allowed within hubs and
    residential density allowed outside a hub.

    The Commission agreed that the number of residential units allowed within the
    hubs should be further reviewed.

    Ms. Reed stated that some recommendations would be brought back to the
    Commission for consideration.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:53 pm.
