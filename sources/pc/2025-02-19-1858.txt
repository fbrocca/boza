C.   PUBLIC HEARING, R-02-25, Pepper Tract PD, TMS No. 615-00-00-044,
     Request to amend Pepper Tract Planned Development District
     Ordinance to modify Zoning Map and Permitted Uses. Staff Report

     Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

   Mr. Tim Cook, property owner and civil engineer, reviewed the request with
the Commission.

   Ms. Claudia Page, 2007 Welsh Pony, expressed concern that there is only
one way in and out of the neighborhood. She also expressed concern with the
amount of traffic that currently exists and that this proposed development
could increase traffic. She asked that there not be access from the commercial
properties to the residential portion of the neighborhood.

   Mr. Paul Barattini, 1162 Triple Crown Court, suggested that a 1,000-foot
separation between the residences and the proposed development is not a
                                                           Planning Commission
                                                              February 19, 2025
                                                                     Page 3 of 6

long distance. He suggested that there should not be connectivity to the
residences. He expressed concern with the actual activities that could be
associated with the flex space.

    Ms. Theresa Rowland, 2023 Welsh Pony, expressed concern with the
proposed location and asked that there be a condition restricting the
development from being moved from the proposed location. She expressed
concern with the berm and fence and suggested that this should be installed
regardless of what or where this proposed development is located. She
suggested that many of the homes are smaller in height than previously stated.

    Mr. John Nunes, 1211 Banker Court, suggested that if the berm is not
constructed, the fence should then be twelve feet. He suggested that the
location of the proposed development should be restricted to this location. He
suggested that all conditions and stipulations should be in writing to ensure
they are carried out and enforced.

     Mr. Mark Berry, 2012 Welsh Pony Drive, agreed with all previous
comments. He suggested that there should be a wall constructed to help
better screen the noise. He suggested that the 1,000-foot separation does not
lessen the amount of noise. He also suggested that the wall should be taller
to help prevent noise spill over to the residential units.

     Ms. Bobbie Grogan, 2011 Welsh Pony, suggested that there should be a
dedicated ingress/egress to the commercial development. She suggested that
if a dedicated ingress/egress cannot be accomplished, then some other traffic
flow solution such as a roundabout at the entrance to help reduce traffic into
the residential area. She suggested that the widening the buffer behind the
residential area should be considered to better separate the commercial area
from the residential and help with noise. She questions whether another
storage unit is needed and if the HOA could sufficiently enforce regulations.

   Mr. Robert Waterfield, 2044 Welsh Pony, suggested that a flex space should
be restricted. He expressed concern with traffic and that this proposed
development would increase traffic in the area on an already congested
roadway. He suggested that signalization and a reduction of the speed limit
should be considered for better safety.
                                                             Planning Commission
                                                                February 19, 2025

     Mr. Steven Catalano, 3847 Tupelo Church Lane, expressed concern with
traffic and noise. He that there is a way to have a balance between the
commercial development and the residential portion of the neighborhood by
having specific restrictions set forth in writing. He suggested that access for
the commercial development should be restricted, with dedicated entrances,
and a wall constructed to help screen noise. He suggested that the flex space
should be clearly defined in the PD, including the location and design
requirements to avoid any confusion in the future.

    Mr. John Rowland, 2023 Welsh Pony, suggested that there should be more
of a buffer between the commercial and residential uses. He suggested a
master plan should be determined so there is a better understanding of future
development in the area.

     Mr. Josh Cheney, 3964 Hanoverian, expressed appreciation for the
developer agreeing to build the wall for better noise suppression. He
expressed concern with potential adverse effects on the wetlands. He
suggested that the limits mentioned by the developer should be included in
the PD.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Bennett closed the public hearing.

    Mr. Ferrara moved for approval of the planned development (PD) with the
indoor flex space to be located at the top right quadrant and not moved more
than 5% from proposed location as represented in the conceptual plan
presented, the buffer wall to be studied and for the developer to work with staff
for a height to be 10 feet-12 feet; total height of flex space to be no more than
35 feet; and no removal of trees behind Welsh Pony. Mr. Smyth seconded the
motion.

    Ms. Ireland moved to amend the motion to include elimination of the
outdoor storage provision and to eliminate the hospice and substance abuse
uses. Mr. Smyth seconded the motion. Amendment passed on an 8 to 1 vote
with Mr. Ferrara, Mr. Smyth, Mr. Bennett, Ms. Sanford, Mr. Lacy, Ms. Mansour,
Mr. Tinkey and Ms. Ireland in favor; Mr. Davis opposed.
