                                  MINUTES


I.     Call to Order

Mayor Haynie called the Special Town Council meeting to order at 1:00 p.m.
Mr. Gary Santos, Mr. Jim Owens, Mr. Tom O’Rourke, Ms. Guang Ming
Whitley, Ms. Kathy Landing, Mr. Bob Brimmer and Mr. Joe Bustos were all
present.

II.    Public Comment

Mayor Haynie asked if there were any members of the public that wished to
speak. Hearing none, he stated the next item on the agenda was
consideration of adoption of a Resolution declaring the results of the
February 13, 2018 Special Election.

III.   Adoption: A Resolution declaring results of the February 13, 2018
       Special Election. (R.18029)

Mr. Santos moved for adoption, seconded by Ms. Whitley. All present voted
in favor.

Mr. DeMoura read the RESOLUTION BY TITLE ONLY. This Resolution SIGNED,
SEALED and DELIVERED this date.
IV.   Administration of Oath

Mayor Haynie administered the Oath of Office to Mr. Kevin Cunnane, and
congratulated him on his victory. Mr. Cunnane thanked everyone who
attended and those that assisted in his campaign stating he would work
hard for the citizens of Mount Pleasant.

V.    Adjourn

There being no further business, Mayor Haynie adjourned the meeting at
1:04 p.m.

Respectfully submitted

Christine Barrett
Clerk of Council
