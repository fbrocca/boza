VI.        PUBLIC COMMENT
            Cheryl Shoun, 205 King Street, Suite 400 Charleston stated she
       is the legal counsel for Matthews Development. She is speaking in
       support of the second agenda item under Planning New Business,
       which is the rezoning of property at Bowman Road and Hospital Drive.
       She said this property has been under contract since August of 2022.
       She urged Council support by voting in favor of the rezoning.
            Lauren Alligood, 159 Heritage Circle stated after her dog was hit
       and subsequently killed by a child riding an e-bike on the sidewalk in
       her neighborhood, she wanted Council to know that e-bike regulations
       are lacking. Ms. Alligood wanted to make Council aware of this dire
       situation and to implement new ordinances to keep the community
       safe.
