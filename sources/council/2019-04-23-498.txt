has to do with thinking about this as redevelopment which is a

IV.   Identification of Issues for Future Discussion
      Mayor Haynie stated that the Town is required to one Public Hearing
      and may have more than one. He stated that the Town needs to be
      strategic in selecting dates and times. He asked Council to send their
      thoughts or concerns to the Clerk and Town Administrator to add to
      future topics of discussion. He stated that Council will have the
      Planning Committee take the lead on the draft comprehensive plan.
      He stated that every Committee of Council needs to review this and
      bring it back to Council.
      Mr. Brimmer stated that this is his third round through the
      comprehensive plan and it is incredibly complex. He stated that staff
      has provided a good place to begin. He stated that he would like to
      add a few comments in terms of moving forward. (1) His view of a plan
      such as this is that it is a framework to work within. He stated that one
      of the criticisms of past comprehensive plans is how much they have
      been changed. He said this is a result of having a very prescriptive
      comprehensive plan that any type of deviation, requires a change in
      the plan. He hopes that this plan does not get to a point of being so
      prescriptive that it requires change on a constant basis. He believes
      there should be some flexibility and not limited to the plan. (2) He said
      the Transportation Plan is a critical portion of this document. He said
      transportation has the most complaints and the Town should take a
      close look at what is being prescribed and how to build that
      infrastructure to support the rest of the ideas in the plan. He stated
      that he will go back to a comment he made at the beginning of this
      process which is that this is a ten-year plan and every time you do
      them, they tend to get bigger. He said when he reviews this, he wants
      to ensure that he reviews it from a ten-year window. He said we do

unable to achieve.
Mr. Cunnane stated that he would like to note that he does not really
see a cross-reference of where certain ideas came from. He asked
what gave rise to the idea of hubs. He asked if there was a public
comment that indicated that they were desirable, or a group of people
that wanted it.
Mr. Ulma stated that it is a concept used all over the country in terms
of planning. He added that people likely did not say to incorporate
hubs in a future plan. He said the elements that were described is
where the genesis of these ideas for that topic came from. He said
they have also tracked on the website all of the comments and
information.
Mr. Cunnane stated that it connects everything else and if it is going to
get denied, it should happen sooner than later. He said if there are a
number of hubs around the Town and then it is decided that they no
longer want them, then the comprehensive plan falls apart. He said if
someone bought a strip mall in the 1960’s, he asked if the Town is
obligated that their great-grandchildren become millionaires on that
same piece of land. He said we do not see too many empty ones and
they are functioning and performing a service and yet the Town wants
to take all the strip malls and make them into little mini cities.
Mr. Brimmer stated that in reference to hubs, they are nothing new.
They have been in the last ten-year plan and believe they were in the
five year update prior to that. He said we have had the idea of hubs
here in Mount Pleasant for quite some time. He said what is
concerning residents is possibly the expansion of these hubs or more
of them; however, it is not a new concept here in the Town. He said it
may be useful for Council to understand and have a brief discussion
about the difference between a comprehensive plan, the zoning code
and Town ordinances, because they are all different. He hopes that
what can be avoided is getting down into the comprehensive plan and
discussing things that are better discussed as part of the ordinances or
zoning codes.
