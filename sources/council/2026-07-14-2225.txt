1. Call to Order
Mayor Will Haynie called the Special Town Council Meeting of the Mount
Pleasant Town Council to order on Tuesday, July 14, 2026, at 5:30 PM.
Council Members Daniel Brownstein, Kathryn Whitaker, Laura Hyatt,
Howard Chapman, Jake Rambo, Mike Tinkey, Alex Crosby and John
Iacofano were all present. Mayor Haynie reminded those in attendance
that this was a special council meeting with the sole purpose of making
appointments to boards and commissions, and that a substantial volume
of applications had been received for review. He noted that while public
comment was on the agenda, comments regarding unrelated matters —
such as the Highway 41 issue — would not be appropriate for this
meeting and should be reserved for the next regular meeting.

2. Public Comment
Mayor Haynie asked for a show of hands from those wishing to make
public comment. With only a small number of individuals indicating
interest, he allowed comment to proceed, noting that if the volume of
speakers were larger, time would have to be limited to preserve the
meeting's primary purpose.
Michelle Stress addressed the Council to express her interest in serving
on the Green Commission. She described over two decades of
experience working with municipalities, nonprofits, schools, homeowners
associations, and residents on environmental initiatives. She highlighted
her work on programs such as the Mount Pleasant Food Forest and a
school sustainability curriculum that received international recognition.
She also noted her leadership in native landscaping, habitat restoration,
and conservation efforts that engaged more than 1,000 households, with
ripple effects on James Island and the peninsula. She emphasized her

approach of promoting nature-based solutions through their practical
benefits — such as reducing heat, flooding, and property maintenance
costs — rather than through environmental guilt. She stated that her
greatest strength was moving ideas from concept to implementation and
expressed concern that many Green Commission initiatives had stalled
before reaching action. She conveyed that, if appointed, she would bring
her experience in community engagement, diplomacy, and
implementation to help the Commission become a catalyst for action.
Karen Bennett, a Summerville resident, addressed the Council regarding
the issue of unregulated dog breeding. She referenced a recent incident
in which hundreds of dogs died and described her longstanding efforts to
educate the public about the prevalence of unlicensed breeders
operating online and in local areas. She noted that shelters such as
those in Dorchester County were facing severe overcrowding, with dogs
being euthanized weekly due to lack of space. She called for the
licensing of dog breeders, drawing a comparison to other licensed
professions. She informed the Council of a bipartisan bill initiated by
South Carolina senators that had been tabled in January but was
expected to be reintroduced, and she urged support for the legislation.

3. Executive Session
Mayor Haynie announced that the Council would enter executive
session to consider personnel matters and applications for appointments
to the Green Commission, the Planning Commission, and the
Commercial Design Review Board.
Council Member Rambo moved to go into executive session for the
reasons stated by Mayor Haynie and listed on the agenda, seconded by
Council Member Iacofano. The motion carried unanimously.

4. Post Executive Session
The Council reconvened from executive session at 6:00 p.m.. Mayor
Haynie stated that no votes were cast and no action was taken during
executive session. The Council then considered appointments arising
from the deliberations.
Green Commission (1 Vacancy)
    Council Member Whitakaker moved to appoint William Bill Cosgrove
    to the Green Commission, seconded by Council Member Iacofano.
    Council Member Tinkey voted opposed with all others voting in
    favor. The motion carried with an 8-1 vote.
Planning Commission (1 Vacancy)
    Council Member Chapman moved to appoint Daniela Harris to the
    Planning Commission, seconded by Council Member Whitaker. The
    motion carried unanimously.
Commercial Design Review Board (1 Vacancy)
    Council Member Hyatt moved to appoint Robert Sachs to the
    Commercial Design Review Board, seconded by Council Member
    Rambo. The motion carried unanimously.


5. Adjourn
Mayor Haynie announced that there were no legal or contractual matters
to address and declared the Special Town Council Meeting adjourned at
6:01 p.m.
