      A. New Business


         1.      Discussion and possible action regarding an Economic
              Impact study relative to the restoration of Crab Bank
              Mr. DeMoura stated that Council acted previously and funded
              approximately $100,000 on modeling of what would happen
              when 800,000 cubic yards of material fell on Crab Bank for its
              renourishment and what it would do. He said if the placement
              is not done properly, it could have disastrous effects on
              commercial fishing, in particular, and it could be less than two
              years that this could happen. He added that the Corps of
              Engineers has been listening; however, they have not offered
              a commitment to placing the material in a way that staff
              believes will favor the Town and its residents best over the
              long term. He said the Police Judicial and Legal Committee
              recommended moving forward with a financial impact
              economic analysis on what would happen if the modeling
              comes to fruition and the businesses and those who use the
              area if Shem Creek is damaged. He stated that he has had
              discussions with several entities who are able to carry this
              task out and this morning was able to speak to a professor at
              University of South Carolina who has a history of being able to
              perform this work and actually did so for the Ports Authority.
              He expects a scope of services for this work sometime next
              week; however, there is no cost for the project at this time. He

                                                                    Page 80 of 88
     would like to request from Council an amount approved, not to
     exceed $75,000. He said he will continue to communicate with
     the Mayor on the progress.
         Mr. Chapman moved to accept the Town Administrator’s
     recommendation to attempt to obtain the economic impact
     study done relative to the restoration of Crab Bank, not to
     exceed $75,000; seconded by Mr. Santos.
     Mr. O’Rourke added that when Council voted previously to
     have the study done, he was the only one opposed. He said
     that a study will be done that tells Council how important this
     project is. He said it was $100,000 for the for the first study
     and up to $75,000 for this one and the cost is $200,000 to
     dredge. He believes that this Town is going to have to (and
     should), have periodic dredging of Shem Creek to ensure that
     the Town has a navigable channel. He views this as one more
     study to wave in front of people that will not listen. He said the
     funds may be better spent to develop a dredging cycle and
     keep the channel dredged as much as possible.
     Mayor Haynie stated that the Town may dredge the portion of
     Shem Creek that is not marked channel. He said the Town
     cannot dredge the federal channel. He said this study is not
     regarding the tides and the sand, but the economic impact.
     Council continued discussions.
         Mr. O’Rourke was opposed. All other present voted in
     favor. Motion carried 8-1.
