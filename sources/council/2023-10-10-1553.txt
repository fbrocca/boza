     Mayor Pro Tempore Gary Santos called the meeting to order at 6:00
p.m. Brenda Corley, Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Jake Rambo, John
Iacofano, Guang Ming Whitley and Carl Ritchie were all present. Mayor
Haynie was absent.
I.         PRAYER
       Rabbi Refson gave the Invocation.
II.        PLEDGE
       Mr. Ritchie led Council in the Pledge of Allegiance.
III.       APPROVAL OF AGENDA
           Ms. Whitley moved to approve the agenda, seconded by Mr.
       Chapman. All present voted in favor.
IV.        PUBLIC HEARINGS, AWARDS & PRESENTATIONS
          National Community Planning Month Proclamation
          Mr. DeMoura read the proclamation and presented it to Planning
          Committee Chair Guang Ming Whitley.
V.        APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM THE SEPTEMBER 12, 2023
       REGULAR AND SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETINGS, AND THE
       AUGUST 2023 FINANCIAL STATEMENT.
             Mr. Chapman moved to approve the minutes and financial
       statement, seconded by Ms. Whitley. All present voted in favor.
VI.        PUBLIC COMMENT
            Guang Ming Whitley, 809 Post Oak Drive stated she would like
       to thank Ms. Brenda Corley and that it has been an honor to serve with
       her for the last four years. She thanked Ms. Corley for voting her
       conscience throughout her time on Council, and she will be missed.
             Chris Colman, 118 Pitt Street stated he was here to thank Ms.
       Corley for the work she has done on Council. She is a friend, she
       listens, she has been kind and thoughtful in her deliberations and he
       appreciates what she has done. He also thanked each member of
       Council.
            Patrick Allen, Director of Development for Liberty Senior Living
       and he is here to speak on behalf of the expansion of South Bay which
       is a full service CCRC licensed facility. He said they hope, with
       Council’s approval, to move forward with the expansion of this
       community to provide additional needed senior services for the Mount
       Pleasant area. He said they are available to answer any questions,
       and they look forward to working with Town staff, the residents and the
       community as a whole as Liberty strives to provide excellent care for
       seniors in our community.

IX. COMMITTEE ACTION ITEMS
            Accommodations Tax Advisory Committee
           Funding Recommendations
                Committee Report
                 Mr. Iacofano moved to approve funding recommendations
           totaling $132,500 for Charleston Race Week at Patriots Point in
           the amount of $25,000; Charleston Restaurant Foundation,
           Lowcountry Oyster Festival in the amount of $9,000; Charleston
           Wine + Food Festival in the amount of $25,000; Coastal
           Cyclists, Ride the Lowcountry in the amount of $1,000; Cooper
           River Bridge Run in the amount of $25,000; Mount Pleasant
           Symphony, Fall & Winter Concerts in the amount of $7,500; SC
           Surf Soccer, Men’s Select Shootout in the amount of $7,500;
           SC Surf Soccer, Women’s Select Shootout in the amount of
           $7,500; and Southeastern Wildlife Exposition, SEWE in the
           amount of $25,000. Ms. Whitley seconded the motion.
               After some discussion, all present voted in favor.
