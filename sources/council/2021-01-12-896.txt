                    MOUNT PLEASANT TOWN COUNCIL
                       SPECIAL COUNCIL MEETING
                    Tuesday, January 12, 2021, 5:15 p.m.
                   Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
                          100 Ann Edwards Lane
                        Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                    MINUTES



I.         Call to Order
       Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:17 p.m.
       Council members Howard Chapman, Brenda Corley, Laura Hyatt,
       Kathy Landing, Jake Rambo, Tom O’Rourke, Gary Santos and Guang
       Ming Whitley were all present.
II.        Public Comment
            Kevin Cunnane, 3032 River Vista Way, congratulated athletes
       and parents for all their efforts to win championships. He stated that on
       April 27, 2020, the Will Haynie for Mayor Facebook page posted a
       photograph of a family at the Egypt Road facility using the park when
       they were not supposed to. He said he is not sure who took the picture,
       but it was posted on Will Haynie’s Facebook page. He said this is
       wrong.
III.       Presentation of the Civic Pride Award
       Nicole Harvey, Special Events Manager, along with Mayor Haynie and
       Town Council members, presented the Civic Pride Award to Dr.
       McLean and Emit Sheperd from Sheperd Integrative Dermatology for
       outstanding beautification of their outdoor office space.
IV.      Recognition of the 12U Football State Champions
      Councilman Gary Santos, Recreation Committee Chairman, together
      with Mayor Haynie and Town Council members recognized the Town’s
      Recreation Department, Twelve and Under Football State Champions,
      under Coach Belcher and a group of coaches.
