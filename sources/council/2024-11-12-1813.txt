                  SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                      Tuesday November 12, 2024
                              4:45 p.m.
                 Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
            100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                  MINUTES

1.       Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 4:46 p.m. Daniel Brownstein,
     Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Guang Ming Whitley, John Iacofano,
     Gary Santos, Jake Rambo and Carl Ritchie were all present. Mayor
     Haynie thanked all of the Veterans and Council Member Ritchie, the only
     Veteran serving on Town Council, for their service to our country.
2.       Public Comment
     There were no public comments.
3.       Executive Session
         Mr. Brownstein moved to go into executive session for all the
     reasons stated on the agenda, seconded by Mr. Santos. All present
     voted in favor.
     The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 4:47 pm.
     a. Personnel
       1. Consideration of applications to serve on the
          Accommodations Tax Advisory Committee
       2. Consideration of applications to serve on the Recreation
          Advisory Commission
     b. Legal and contractual
          Update on pending litigation captioned James N. Deierlein,
          Jr., et al. vs. the Commission of Public Works for the Town of
          Mount Pleasant, et al. (2023-CP-10-01688)
4. Post Executive Session
     Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
     any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
     an executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
          Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:41 p.m. stating no votes
     or action had been taken.
         Mr. Iacofano moved to appoint to the Accommodations Tax
     Advisory Committee, Livia Thomas to seat #4, Lodging, and Monique
     Semper to seat #6, Hospitality, seconded by Mr. Chapman. All present
     voted in favor.
         Mr. Rambo moved to appoint the following members to the
     Recreation Advisory Commission:
          Gregory Winch - Seat #1
          Richard Phillips - Seat #2
          Greer Hanlon - Seat #3
          Greg Hutton - Seat #4
          April Gift - Seat #5
          Chad Isom - Seat #6
          Derek Conrad - Seat #7

     Mr. Chapman seconded the motion. Mr. Iacofano and Mr. Santos
     opposed with all others present voting in favor. Motion carried 7-2.

5.       Presentation of the Kevin Williamson Award
     Mr. Santos presented the Kevin Williamson Award to flag football
     recipient, Camila Fergusson and tackle football recipient, Ryan Sullivan.
