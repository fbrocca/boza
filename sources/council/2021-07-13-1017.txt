VI.        PUBLIC COMMENT

            Marie-Louise Moreto of 1133 Liberty Court stated she wanted to
      address the issue of donations to the Town through a GoFundMe page
      established by the Town to solicit funding for a public/private enterprise
      to build a culture arts center. Ms. Moreto said she represents the
      Mount Pleasant Community Arts Center and where public and private
      support is welcome for this important goal, to date there has only been
      one substantial dontation made through the GoFundMe page. Ms.
      Moreto said her organization urges Council to refocus efforts in a way
      more likely to advance this goal, specifically to establish a commission
      to give the project legs. The Mount Pleasant Community Arts Center
      has been working for five years to make a community arts center a
      reality.

            Pat Sullivan of 1002 Plantation Court stated she is asking Council
      to honor the Mount Pleasant Comprehensive Plan in which it states
      that we must protect the integrity of our settlement communities by
      limiting further encroachment and deterioration of the original
      development patterns and ensuring any redevelopment is as
      compatible as possible with community standards. She stated this
      reflects four items on the agenda this evening where a developer wants
      to annex nineteen acres of the Philips Community into the Town and
      change the zoning to Dunes West Planned Development. She said it
      is not the right way to treat our settlement communities and asked
      Council to please vote no.

           George Freeman of 1450 Bowman Road stated he is the director
      of Community Activity of CAGE. Mr. Freeman said several years ago
      when he was president of the Six Mile Neighborhood Association he
      led the charge to annex the Six Mile community into the Town of Mount
      Pleasant. He said they were promised at that time that they would be
      able continue to maintain their quiet rural lifestyle. He said when the
                                                                      Page 7 of 43
Liberty Hill development was being created, they requested sidewalks
be finished in the Six Mile Community from Six Mile Road to the new
park. He said they found out that the Town has plans to put a ten foot
wide bike path instead of the sidewalk that they had requested. Mr.
Freeman said they feel the bike path will ruin the character of the
community as there are other features that will go along with it. He
said the plan below would be better and the Six Mile community streets
would remain quiet streets.




     Jeannette Lee of 2623 Linnen Lane stated she is in the County
and not the Town. She said she was surprised to come out of her
driveway to see the work being done on Hamlin Road. She wanted to
know why sidewalks were being put on Hamlin Road from Linnen Lane
to Laing Middle School without consulting the community. She said
they found out the water will be turned off to move the water lines, and
no notice was given. She asked that their community be respected
and information should be funneled through 7Mile/CAGE so they can
be made aware of what is happening in their community.

    Walter Brown, III of 437 Venning Street said he wanted to thank
Council for their service and would like to thank those that approached
Mount Pleasant Waterworks about the water tower on Simmons Street.

                                                              Page 8 of 43
He said this evening he is asking the Town to take custody of the tower,
accept the $50,000 that was offered by Waterworks to create a park.
Mr. Brown stated he would ask that the park be named for former
Commissioner Charlie Hindman and former employee Albert Wise. He
said the restoration of the tank can be paid for with the 1% franchise
fee that is received from Mount Pleasant Waterworks in lieu of other
fees, and if that is not enough, the fee can be raised to 2%. Mr. Brown
stated that fee has not been raised in approximately twenty years. Mr.
Brown said this is an opportunity to protect the landscape of the
Lowcountry. Mr. Brown said there are some marine vessels who do
not have the latest equipment for navigation. The shrimpers on Shem
Creek use the water tower to direct them back to the creek. Mr. Brown
thanked Council again for their service.

      Richard Habersham of 2938 Bennett Charles Road stated he is
the President of the Philips Community Association and he would like
to speak regarding Planning New Business items 1, 2, 8 and 9. Mr.
Habersham stated Charleston County established a historical district
for the settlement community and that 51% support of the community
was needed to be considered for this district, and 74% of the
community was in support. Mr. Habersham stated Charleston County
had first reading, and on July 22, second reading will be held with one
more reading required to be officially a historic district in the County.
Mr. Habersham said the items are part of Philips Community, not
Dunes West or Park West. He said they do not want to be
piecemealed. He said they are applying for the Federal register of
historical places, and one of the areas they are applying for is how the
community is laid out. He said making these properties part of Park
West will take away from the community.

      Claudia Maczyk of 1285 Six Mile Road stated she wanted to
speak about the widening of Highway 17 in the Seven Mile Area. She
said Goodwill Church which has been there for 183 years will be in
danger of being obsolete as people will not be able to get into the
church from a ten-lane road. She said many people in the area walk
to church, and to have to walk across ten lanes will destroy the church.
She asked Council to take another look at the plan and to come up with
an alternative to avoid destroying another historical community.



                                                               Page 9 of 43
            Myra Richardson of 2755 Earl Johnson Lane asked why progress
       always means the death of a black community. She said it seems as
       if Mount Pleasant plans all of this development and allows the
       infrastructure to go through the black community. She said that seems
       like the plan, for development to be built all around the donut holes,
       and when there is a problem with traffic, it will go right through the
       community, not once, not twice, but over and over. She said the Seven
       Mile Community is facing this problem right now, again, the death of a
       black community. Ms. Richardson stated that she tried all day to find
       out what is happening on Hamlin Road and why none of the residents
       were informed about a sidewalk being installed. She stated it was
       disrespectful. She said CAGE should be informed before work is done
       so the residents can be notified.

            Fred Smalls 2959 Elijah Smalls Road said he would like to speak
       about the nine acre tract which is located in the Philips Community that
       is currently up to be rezoned and annexed into the Town. Mr. Smalls
       said they have never opposed any individual or group that wanted to
       purchase property in Philips. He said he has never opposed any
       individual or group that wanted to sell or develop their property in
       Philips. He said they do oppose everyone who wants to come into the
       community and change the zoning to something other than what it is
       currently. He said anything that will change the zoning to commercial
       is a death sentence to the community. Mr. Smalls said their zoning is
       what protects them.
