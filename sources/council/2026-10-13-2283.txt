Ben Brown reported that the Gregory Ferry amendment at 120 percent
of area median income had passed and been recorded, and that the
townhomes were now drawing sales activity. He asked Council to
consider rental workforce housing on the Faison Road property,

commute from far away.

                                    T

moratorium. He cited the energy and water demands of hyperscale data

                      AF

      D

following a Zoom call with Middle Street Partners. He stated that mixed
use is generally viable only with high-density apartments above it.
