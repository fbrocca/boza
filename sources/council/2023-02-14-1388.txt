                 SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                Tuesday, February 14, 2023, 5:00 p.m.
                Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
           100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                  MINUTES

1.     Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m. Howard
     Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Gary Santos, Jake Rambo, John Iacofano,
     Guang Ming Whitley and Carl Ritchie were all present. Ms. Corley
     was not present at call to order but participated via telephone during
     the executive session discussion.
2.     Public Comment
     There were no public comments.
3.     Executive Session
         Mr. Santos moved to go into executive session to discuss the
     items as stated by Mayor Haynie and listed on the agenda, seconded
     by Mr. Chapman. All present voted in favor (Ms. Corley was not
     present for the vote).
        The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:01 p.m.
       a.Personnel
          (1)Consideration of applications to serve on the Commercial
             Design Review Board
          (2)Consideration of applications to serve on the Board of Zoning
             Appeals
          (3)Consideration of applications to serve on the Historic District
             Preservation Commission
       b.Legal and Contractual
          (1)Discussion and possible action regarding funding agreement
             and lease of property for economic development
          (2)Legal advice pertaining to a moratorium on certain multi-
              family structures
4.Post Executive Session
       Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
       any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
       an executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
           Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 6:05 p.m. stating no
       action had been taken. Ms. Corley left the meeting via telephone
       prior to the special Council meeting being reconvened.
             Ms. Hyatt moved to appoint Mr. John Hackenberg to the
       Historic District Preservation Commission, seconded by Mr. Santos.
       All present voted in favor.
           Mr. Chapman moved to appoint Mr. Richard Perez to the
       Commercial Design Review Board, seconded by Mr. Santos. All
       present voted in favor.
           Ms. Whitley moved to appoint Ms. Molly Hall to the Board of
       Zoning Appeals, seconded by Mr. Ritchie. All present voted in
       favor.
           Mr. Iacofano moved to approve the funding agreement and
       lease of property for economic development as discussed in
       executive session, seconded by Ms. Hyatt. All present voted in
       favor.
5.     Adjourn
     There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 6:07
     p.m.
