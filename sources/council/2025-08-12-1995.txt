Councilmember Chapman engaged Planning Director Michelle Reed in
detailed discussion about current entitlements. Reed explained the
property's AB (Area-wide Business) designation allows 4 units per acre
plus an additional 4 if dedicated to attainable housing. She clarified that
while theoretical entitlements exist, practical limitations include triggering
impact assessments (at 75 peak hour trips and 40,000 sq ft
commercial), building height restrictions (35 feet), parking requirements,

                                                                   Page 11 of 20
and open space requirements. Reed estimated approximately 130
residential units could be built by-right without triggering an impact
assessment, along with 40,000 square feet of commercial.
Chapman expressed concern about traffic from commuters, stating if
anybody has been at the bottom of the Ravenel Bridge and see all of the
cars coming into town every morning, most of them come here to work.
He advocated for workforce housing, arguing employees honored for
longevity could actually live in the town of Mount Pleasant. However, he
criticized being whiplashed into deciding between Belk's and the owners
and noted the planning committee and commission had wrestled with
what we felt was appropriate for the future. He expressed frustration that
despite council being accused of shutting Belk's down and shutting down
the movie theater, which couldn't be further from the truth, they must still
address the situation. Chapman suggested deferring to have the two
sides work together quickly to settle this issue.
Councilmember Iacofano expressed frustration about being placed in the
middle of what seems to be some kind of lease dispute. He
acknowledged something needs to be done with Towne Centre but felt
the conceptual designs were too vague. He noted Planning Commission
had recommended approval with conditions including: height limited to
55 feet on exterior, 60,000 square feet dedicated to family entertainment
if Regal closes, 14% green space (up from 10% shown), 6 market rate to
2 attainable housing units ratio, and 2 designated incubator locations.
Iacofano recognized the developer had made many requested changes
over 4 years but concluded there's still work to be done.

end up with.
