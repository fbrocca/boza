5. Presentation of LOWVELO Proclamation

  Town Administrator Eric DeMoura read the proclamation on behalf of
       D

  Mayor Haynie. It designated Saturday, November 7, 2026, as LOWVELO
  Day in the Town of Mount Pleasant. The proclamation recognized
  LOWVELO’s support for cancer research at MUSC Hollings Cancer
  Center and encouraged residents to support the event and its fundraising
  efforts.

  Chris Winn, representing LOWVELO thanked Council and town staff for
  their support. Mr. Winn reported that the event aimed to attract 2,000
  participants and had raised $4.6 million for cancer research in South
  Carolina over eight years. He said fundraising would continue through
  December 31 and encouraged residents to participate as riders,
  volunteers, or donors. Council Member John Iacofano offered to match
  Council donations up to $1,000.
