VI.       PUBLIC COMMENT
          John Evans, 15 Vincent Drive, stated that he is a lifetime resident
      of Mount Pleasant. He stated that on Tuesday, June 2, 2020, the
      Command office at the National Guard Armory on Mathis Ferry Road
      received a call from Washington, DC and needed 500 troops in
      Washington, DC immediately. He said Major Hawkins, who is the
      Administrative Officer at the Battalion began coordinating personnel
      and was able to gather 455 personnel who deployed to Washington,
      DC on Wednesday, June 3rd. He said they returned home today, June
      9, 2020 at 1:15 pm at the Armory where he personally greeted them.
      He stated that these troops immediately joined forces in Washington,
      DC with the federal agents who surrounded the White House. He has
      asked Councilman Gary Santos to read a note of appreciation into the
      Town Council minutes thanking these troops for the outstanding job
      while representing Mount Pleasant and South Carolina in Washington,
      DC.
            Councilman Gary Santos read the following: The Town of
      Mount Pleasant wishes to commend and thank soldiers of the 1st
      Battalion 118th Infantry South Carolina Army National Guard for their
      participation in the military assistance to civil authorities during the
      period June 3rd through June 8th, 2020 in Washington, DC.
         Margaret Blalock, 1417 Chimney Swift Lane, spoke about health
      conditions with respect to 5G technology she has suffered along with
      hypersensitivity.
          Michael German, 1707 Snowden Road, stated that he lives in the

      residence. However, Town Council and Mount Pleasant Waterworks

           1.       First and Final Reading: An Emergency Ordinance to
                temporarily suspend the provisions of Chapter 53 of the Town
                of Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances entitled
                Environmentally Acceptable Packaging and Products. (Ord.
                No. 20034)
                Mr. Pagliarini stated that Items X.A.1 and 2, by State Law,
                expired after 61 days, which was May 27, 2020. He stated that
                as a practical matter, Council is extending this with a new
                ordinance. He added that under State Law, this would apply
                for a maximum of 61 days. He indicated that because this is
                an emergency provision, these two ordinances would only
                require one reading and would require two-thirds majority. He
                stated that both items have the same language and provisions
                as this Council passed in March 2020. He stated that there is
                another emergency ordinance that expires later in June, which
                is the emergency ordinance for restaurant outdoor seating.
                     Mr. Santos moved to approve items X.A.1 and 2 and

2. Personnel


  a.       Consideration of applications submitted to serve as
       corporation counsel
       Mr. DeMoura stated that regarding the process, he stated
       that the Town has reached out to many locations to find
       candidates for this position. He said if Council desires to
       retain a search firm for this position, he has information
       on four search firms. He said these firms require a 25%
       fee, which is based on the hiring salary at the time the
       individual joins the Town. He said one firm has a slightly
       higher fee and has requested them to consider being at
       the 25% level. He stated that if Council chooses to
       consider a search firm, he will require direction to do so.
       Mr. Rambo stated that for a position of this level, this
       would likely be a recruited position and would be rare to
       randomly have someone apply that the Town may be
       considering. He stated that his preference would be to
       hire an expert recruiter to find a candidate.
       Ms. Hyatt asked if the fee is only paid to the recruiter if
       they find a candidate.
       Mr. DeMoura reviewed the process with Council.
       Mr. Rambo asked if a Special Town Council meeting is
       being scheduled in the next week or two to begin the
       interview process.
       Mayor Haynie stated that this is not yet clear until those
       being considered are contacted.
       Mr. Rambo stated that he is concerned this process will
