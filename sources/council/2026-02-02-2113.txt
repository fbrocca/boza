1. Call to Order
Mayor Will Haynie called the Special Town Council Meeting to order on
Monday, February 2, 2026, at 12:15 PM. He acknowledged and thanked
town staff for their work during the recent weather event. Council
Members Daniel Brownstein, Kathryn Whitaker, Laura Hyatt, Howard
Chapman, Jake Rambo, Mike Tinkey, Alex Crosby and John Iacofano
were all present.

2. Public Comments
Michelle Stress addressed the Council regarding the Recreation
Department's consideration of artificial turf. She questioned whether the
Recreation Department's primary purpose was to serve residents or to
generate revenue through tournaments. Ms. Stress emphasized that the
department's mission focuses on sustainability, affordability, quality, and
service to residents rather than revenue generation. She expressed
concerns about artificial turf's environmental impacts, including
microplastics shedding, excessive heat in summer, and the eight to
twelve year replacement cycle. Ms. Stress requested a true public
comment and design process, noting that the last one occurred over ten
years ago.

4. Approval to Accept the 2026-2030 Strategic Plan
Following the presentation, Council discussed the plan. Council Member
Brownstein commended the focus on improving public communication
and engagement. Council Member Iacofano expressed appreciation for
the comprehensive nature of the plan and specifically noted the
improvements in the permitting process. Council Member Tinkey
emphasized the importance of internet and broadband access
throughout town as a safety concern.
Council Member Hyatt moved to accept the 2026-2030 Strategic Plan,
seconded by Council Member Whitaker. The motion carried
unanimously.

5. Executive Session - Personnel Matters
a. Update on Personnel Matters Related to the Mount Pleasant Police
Department
b. Personnel Discussion Related to Staff Succession Planning
Council Member Chapman moved to go into executive session for the
reasons stated, seconded by Council Member Tinkey. The motion
carried unanimously.
The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 1:24 p.m.

6. Post Executive Session
Mayor reconvened the meeting at 2:21 p.m. stating no votes were cast

twenty minutes.

There being no further business, the special meeting of the Mount
