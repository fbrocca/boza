5. First Reading: An Ordinance to amend Zoning Code Section
   156.007 of Chapter 156 of the Mount Pleasant Code of
   Ordinances adding a definition for “Story” which includes
   “Building Floor.” (Ord. No. 17049)
  Ms. Farrell stated that the Town did not have a definition for a
  story, which is what this is. She said they already had definitions
  that were in other areas where the Town’s code, in particular the
  building and flood prevention codes, and what this does is clearly
  define within the zoning code what constitutes a story and a
  building floor. She said it has been recommended for approval.


                                                          Page 11 of 69
  Mr. Carrier moved for approval; seconded by Mr. Gawrych.
  Mr. Haynie asked Ms. Farrell if this would prevent, similar to the
  lawsuit, where someone knew how many stories they had, but
  did not count the ground floor.
  Ms. Farrell stated that this is in response and clarifies what
  constitutes an actual story. She said similar to this, a garage level
  could be counted as a building floor or a story and this provides
  that clarification.
  Mr. Haynie said that in Mount Pleasant, does this mean that if you

  corridors where it is most prevalent, this would define how they
  would be counted.
