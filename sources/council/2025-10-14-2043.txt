Councilmember Brownstein moved to request that item #8, Rice
Planters Amateur Golf Tournament be handled separately as it is a for-
profit entity rather than a non-profit organization. Mayor Haynie
seconded this amendment for discussion purposes.
The committee representative explained that while Rice Planters had
previously been a 501(c)(3) organization, they had let that status lapse,
but they still met the state requirements for accommodations tax funding
by driving "heads and beds" (hotel stays).
Councilmember Rambo noted that being operated under a for-profit
entity didn't mean the tournament itself was profitable, and mentioned
the tournament's long history in Mount Pleasant. Councilmember
Chapman emphasized that the accommodations tax was specifically
created to support events that bring visitors to stay in local hotels, which
the golf tournament accomplishes effectively.
The amendment to remove the Rice Planters Amateur Golf Tournament
from the recommendations failed.
Mayor Haynie expressed concern about the distribution of funds, noting
that international events like Charleston Race Week and the Cooper
River Bridge Run received reduced funding while local arts organizations
received their full requested amounts despite bringing in fewer visitors.
The original motion to approve all recommendations passed by a vote of
8-1, with Mayor Haynie voting opposed.
