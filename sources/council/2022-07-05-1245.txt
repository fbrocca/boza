           TOWN OF MOUNT PLEASANT, SOUTH CAROLINA
                 SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                        Tuesday, July 5, 2022
                             12:00 p.m.
                Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
           100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                 MINUTES


1.     Call to Order

     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 12:01 p.m. Council
     Members Brenda Corley, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman, Gary
     Santos, Jake Rambo, and Carl Ritchie were all present. Council
     Member John Iacofano was present via electronic means. Council
     Member Whitley arrived at 12:02 p.m.

2.     Public Comment

     There were no public comments.


3.      Executive Session: Discussion regarding a potential purchase
     of property




         Mr. Chapman moved to go into executive session to discuss the
     potential purchase of property, seconded by Mr. Santos. All present
     voted in favor.




     The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 12:03 p.m.
4.Post Executive Session



     Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
     any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed
     in an executive session during a properly noticed meeting.


        Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 12:25 p.m. stating no
     decisions had been made or votes taken.


         Mr. Santos moved to direct staff to negotiate and execute a
     contract for the purchase of real property in accordance with the terms
     and conditions presented in executive session. Further for staff to
     pursue necessary due diligence and take necessary steps to affirm
     said purchase and to execute all necessary documents to facilitate
     closing. Mr. Ritchie seconded the motion. Mayor Haynie called for
     the vote and all present voted in favor.


5.     Adjourn


     There being no further business the meeting was adjourned at 12:25
     p.m.


     Respectfully submitted,


     Christine Barrett
