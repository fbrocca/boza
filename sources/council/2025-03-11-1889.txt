                    SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                          Tuesday March 11, 2025
                                5:00 p.m.
                   Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
              100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                    MINUTES
1.          Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:02 p.m. Daniel Brownstein,
     Howard Chapman, Jake Rambo, John Iacofano, Guang Ming Whitley
     were all present. Gary Santos and Laura Hyatt arrived at 5:04 p.m.
2.          Public Comment
     Deidre Kennedy stated she heard the Town is planning to remove 13
     large pine trees on Equestrian Drive due to the roots infiltrating onto the
     street. She asked if the Town would take a second look and see if
     something else can be done since the last thing we want to do is remove
     trees.
3.         Executive Session
           Mr. Chapman moved to go into executive session for the reasons
     listed on the agenda and stated by Mayor Haynie, seconded by Mr.
     Iacofano. All present voted in favor.
            Mayor Haynie adjourned the meeting into executive session at 5:03
     p.m.
     (Mr. Santos and Ms. Hyatt arrived at 5:04 p.m.)
            Personnel
               Consideration of applications to serve on the GREEN
               Commission
            Legal and Contractual
               Discussion and possible action related to a contract for the
               purchase and sale of real property
                   Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:42 p.m. stating
               no action or votes were taken.
4.          Post Executive Session
     Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
     any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
     an executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
        Mr. Brownstein moved to appoint Mr. Don Bailey to the GREEN
     Commission, seconded by Mr. Iacofano. All present voted in favor.
5.       Recognition of Wando Girls Rugby State Champions
     Ms. Hyatt introduced the Wando Girls Rugby State Champions. Mayor
     and Council congratulated them on their Championship victory.




6.       Adjourn
     There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 5:47 p.m.
     Respectfully submitted,


     Christine Barrett
     Clerk of Council
     March 11, 2025
