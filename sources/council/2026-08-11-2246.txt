1. Call To Order
Mayor Will Haynie called the Special Town Council Meeting to order on
Tuesday, August 11, 2026, at 5:15 PM. Council Members Kathryn
Whitaker, Howard Chapman, Jake Rambo, Mike Tinkey, Alex Crosby
and John Iacofano were all present. Council Member Brownstein
entered at 5:17 p.m. Council Member Hyatt entered the meeting at 5:31
p.m. during executive session.
2. Public Comment
Mayor Haynie invited public comment. No members of the public were
present to address the Council.
3. Executive Session
3a. Personnel
There were no Personnel items.
3b. Legal And Contractual
Mayor Haynie announced the item for executive session: legal and
contractual discussion and possible action regarding an economic
development incentive package. The Council voted to enter executive
session.
   Motion to go into executive session for the stated reason was made
   by Council Member Chapman and seconded by Council Member
   Iacofano. The motion carried.
The meeting was convened into executive session at 5:17 p.m.
4. Post Executive Session
Following executive session, Council Member John Iacofano made a
motion regarding the economic development incentive package. Council
Member Rambo voiced opposition on philosophical grounds, stating that

5. Recognition Of Mount Pleasant Track Club State Champions
The Council recognized the state champion Mount Pleasant Track Club
for an outstanding 2026 season.
The Mayor and Council extended congratulations to the athletes and
coaches.
