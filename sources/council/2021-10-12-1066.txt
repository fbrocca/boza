decisions every single day. He said someone needs to stand up for
these responders with the mid-level titles.

employees here and having this longevity is to increase the manpower
on the apparatus. He stated this is very important and asked that

Ms. Corley stated the Human Resources Committee met to
review and consider the implementation of the Classification
and Compensation Study as well as a one-time bonus
regarding funding associated with ARPA.

The Classification and Compensation Study that was presented
included updates based on current market data and
comparisons with other local cities and counties in our region
and ensures that we have an affordable plan that allows us to
be competitive.

The Committee was also presented a plan for a one-time bonus
for those employees whose work was most impacted by
COVID.

The HR Committee unanimously recommended approval of
both the Classification and Compensation Study as well as the
one-time bonus.
