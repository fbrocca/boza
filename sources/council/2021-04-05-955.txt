             TOWN OF MOUNT PLEASANT, SOUTH CAROLINA
                  MOUNT PLEASANT TOWN COUNCIL
                     SPECIAL COUNCIL MEETING
                   Monday, April 5, 2021, 12:00 p.m.
                 Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
                        100 Ann Edwards Lane
                      Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

      In accordance with Governor McMaster’s Executive Order 2021-12
             Council Chambers will be limited to 50% occupancy.

                                   MINUTES



I.         Call to Order
          Mayor Pro Tempore, Tom O’Rourke called the Special Town
       Council meeting to order at 12:06 p.m.
       Tom O’Rourke, Howard Chapman, Brenda Corley, Laura Hyatt, Kathy
       Landing, and Gary Santos were present.
       Mayor Haynie joined the meeting virtually for executive session but
       was not present at call to order. Jake Rambo and Guang Ming Whitley
       were absent.


II.        Public Comment
       [None]


III.       Executive Session to discuss a personnel matter: Annual
       performance evaluation for the Town Administrator
          Mr. Santos moved to go into executive session; seconded by Mr.
       Chapman. All present voted in favor.
           Town Council convened into executive session at 12:08 p.m. at
       which point Mayor Haynie joined the meeting virtually.
           Town Council reconvened at 12:57 p.m., with Mayor Haynie
       having ended his virtual participation.
       No action or votes were taken in executive session.


IV. Post Executive Session
     Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
     any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed
     in an executive session during a properly noticed meeting
         Mr. O’Rourke moved to extend the length of the Town
     Administrator’s contract by one year and add an additional 4% to his
     salary beginning immediately; seconded by Ms. Corley.
     All present voted in favor. (Mayor Haynie had left the meeting, and Ms.
     Whitley and Mr. Rambo were absent.)


V.       Adjourn
     There being no further business, Council adjourned at 12:57 p.m.


Respectfully submitted,
Barbara Ashe MacDonald
April 5, 2021
