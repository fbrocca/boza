IX.     COMMITTEE ACTION ITEMS
      A. Health, Recreation & Events Committee
              Consideration of recommendation to extend the Recreation
              Advisory Commission terms for ninety days
                  Mr. Santos moved to approve the extension, seconded
              by Mr. Iacofano.
              Mr. Rambo moved to amend the extension so that instead of
              ninety days, the terms are only extended to the end of
              September.
              Mr. Pagliarini said the motion to extend the Commission by
              ninety days would take it to October 31.
              Mr. Rambo withdrew his motion.
              Mayor Haynie asked if when it expires, all the terms would
              expire. Mr. Pagliarini responded in the affirmative as when
              the Commission was created, the terms were not staggered.
              Mayor said a report is supposed to be coming due and
              asked if that changed the date of the report to October, or
              should Council still expect it in July. Mr. Pagliarini stated
              there is no date specified for the report, and his
              understanding is that by extending the term, it would be the
              end of October unless Council specifies a date for the report.
              Ms. Hyatt said the discussion in Committee was that we
              would receive the report on time, and the ninety-days would
              give Council time to review the report and make decisions
              about future plans.
              Mr. Pagliarini stated his recommendation would be that there
              is an amendment made to specify the due date so the
              Recreation Advisory Committee has a clear understanding of
              the timeframe.
              Ms. Whitley moved to amend the motion to make the due
              date for the report July 31, seconded by Mr. Santos.
