                  Mount Pleasant Municipal Complex

1. Call to Order
   Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:01 p.m. Daniel Brownstein,
   Howard Chapman, Laura, Hyatt, Guang Ming Whitley, Jake Rambo, John
   Iacofano, Gary Santos, and Mike Tinkey were all present.
2. Public Comment
   Mike VanHorn spoke to remind Council that there is a candidate forum
   this Thursday from 6:30 to 9:00 p.m. and invited the Mayoral and Council
   candidates to participate. He also thanked Ms. Whitley for everything she
   had done for the last eight years.
3. Executive Session
   Councilmember Santos moved to go into executive session to discuss the
   items read by Mayor Haynie and as listed on the agenda, seconded by
   Mr. Chapman. All present voted in favor.
   The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:02 p.m.
    A. Legal and Contractual
            Review and discussion of an Economic Development grant
            application
    B. Personnel
           Consideration of applications to serve on the Recreation
           Advisory Commission (3 vacancies)
4. Post Executive Session
   Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
   any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
   executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
   Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:24 p.m. stating no votes or
   action had been taken.
  Ms. Hyatt moved to approve the economic incentives for WIN 808 LLC as
  recommended by staff in executive session, seconded by Mr. Iacofano.
  All present voted in favor.
  Mr. Iacofano moved to reappoint Gregory Winch, Richard Phillips and
  Greer Hanlon to the Recreation Advisory Commission, seconded by Mr.
  Brownstein. All present voted in favor.
5. Recognition of Special Olympics Golf Champions from the North
   America Golf Championship in Pennsylvania and the Special
   Olympics Fall Games in Myrtle Beach
  The Special Olympics Golf Champions were recognized by Mayor and
  Council.




6. Adjourn
  There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 5:29 p.m.
  Respectfully submitted,


  Christine Barrett
  Clerk of Council
  October 14, 2025
