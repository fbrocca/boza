                                   MINUTES
I.         Call to Order
       Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 12:00 p.m. Guang Ming
       Whitley, Howard Chapman and Jake Rambo were all present. Gary
       Santos participated electronically. Kathy Landing, Tom O’Rourke,
       Brenda Corley and Laura Hyatt were all absent.
II.        Public Comment
       Mr. Howard Chapman, 824 Natchez Circle stated he is a life member
       of the American Society of Civil Engineers and today is the 100th
       birthday of the organization. Their members help plan, design and
       build buildings and infrastructure. They also provide for a grade of our
       infrastructure annually. This annual grade is sent to congress and
       helps to decide how much funding we receive. Mr. Chapman stated
       he would like the Town to send a congratulatory note for this
       occasion.
III.       Adoption: A Resolution declaring Election results of November 2,
       2021. (R.21111)
           Mr. Rambo moved to adopt the resolution seconded by Mr.
       Chapman. All present voted in favor.
       Mr. Chapman stated he would like to congratulate the Election
       Commission for their hard work to quickly process the election results.
IV.        Adjourn
       There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 12:03
       pm.
