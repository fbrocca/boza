                 MOUNT PLEASANT TOWN COUNCIL
                      Special Council Meeting
                  Tuesday, August 12, 2025, 5:00 PM
                         Council Chambers
                  Mount Pleasant Municipal Complex
           100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                  MINUTES

                     Click here to listen to the recording.
1. Call to Order
   Mayor Pro Tempore Guang Ming Whitley called the meeting to order at
   5:00 p.m. Daniel Brownstein, Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, John
   Iacofano, Jake Rambo, Gary Santos and Mike Tinkey were all present.
   Mayor Haynie was absent.
2. Public Comment
   Mr. Grady Johnson, Executive Director for the Harbor Entrepreneur
   Center expressed his gratitude to the Town for their support.
3. Executive Session
       A. Legal & Contractual Items
               (1)   Legal advice related to pending litigation captioned
                     James N. Deierlein, Jr., et al. vs. the Commission of
                     Public Works for the Town of Mount Pleasant, et al.
                     (2023-CP-10-01688)
               (2)   Legal advice and contractual matters related to the
                     Harbor Entrepreneur Center
               (3)   Legal advice and contractual matters related to Faison
                     Road property owned by the Town of Mount Pleasant
       B. Personnel
               (1) Consideration of applications to serve on the GREEN
                    Commission (4 vacancies)
       Councilmember Santos moved to go into executive session for the
       reasons stated by Mayor Pro Tempore Whitley and listed on the
       agenda, seconded by Councilmember Chapman. All present voted
       in favor.
       The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:01 p.m.
4. Post Executive Session
  Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
  any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
  executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
  Mayor Pro Tempore Whitley reconvened the meeting at 5:55 p.m. stating
  no votes were taken.
  Councilmember Brownstein moved to allocate $100,000 from the
  Economic Development budget to the Harbor Entrepreneur Center,
  seconded by Councilmember Iacofano. All present voted in favor.
  Councilmember Tinkey moved to reappoint Rachel Honderd, Jim
  Westerhold, Olivia Flynn and Mary Conley to the GREEN Commission,
  seconded by Councilmember Hyatt. All present voted in favor.
  Councilmember Iacofano, with respect to item 3.A.(3), moved to direct
  legal counsel to take action as discussed in executive session, seconded
  by Councilmember Brownstein. All present voted in favor.
5. Adjourn
  There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 5:56 p.m.
  Respectfully submitted,


  Christine Barrett
  Clerk of Council
  August 12, 2025
