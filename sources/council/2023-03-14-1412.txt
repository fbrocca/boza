                  SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                   Tuesday, March 14, 2023, 5:00 p.m.
                 Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
            100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                  MINUTES

1.       Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:00. Brenda Corley, Howard
     Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Gary Santos, John Iacofano, Guang Ming Whitley
     and Carl Ritchie were all present. Jake Rambo was absent.
2.       Public Comment
          Gary Davis, 849 Bulls Bay Boulevard, stated he was there to speak
     in support about leasing the property in East Cooper Plaza as an arts and
     cultural center.
          Eugenia Felsinger, 960 Scotland Drive stated East Cooper Plaza is
     in her neighborhood. She spoke in support of leasing the property as an
     arts center, and would like to see strong development between this area
     all the way to Dragoon. She would also like to see a few more
     handicapped parking spaces.
           Mark Chestnut, 155 Wingo Way spoke in support of a performing
     arts center.
         Pam Hricik, 945 Pine Hall Road spoke in support of an arts center.
         Abby Hricik, 945 Pine Hall Road spoke in support of an arts center.
         Melanie Casson, 1481 Center Street Extension stated she was in
     support of in arts center.
          Daniel Brownstein, stated he was there to advocate for the Town
     leasing the larger of the two spaces being considered for an arts center.
         Perry Roark, 2004 Brick Kiln Parkway spoke in support of having a
     space that can support all art forms.
          Liz Duran, Wakendaw Lakes, stated there has never been a
     community theater that was sponsored by the Town and she would like to
     see the larger space considered.
3.       Executive Session
           Ms. Whitley moved to go into executive session for the reasons
     stated on the agenda, seconded by Mr. Chapman. All present voted in
     favor.
     The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:18 p.m.
       a. Personnel

          (1) Consideration of Police, Judicial & Legal Committee
              recommendation for Municipal Judge appointment

          (2) Consideration of applications to serve on the Commercial
              Design Review Board

          (3) Consideration of applications to serve on the Culture, Arts and
              Pride Commission

          (4) Consideration of applications to serve on the Board of Zoning
              Appeals

       b. Legal and Contractual

          (1) Discussion and possible action related to options for a Town of
              Mount Pleasant community arts facility

          (2) Legal advice on potential litigation regarding a commercial
              piece of property
4. Post Executive Session
     Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of any
     section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in an
     executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
           Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:59 p.m. stating no
       votes or action had been taken.
           Ms. Whitley moved to appoint Ms. Kelley Young as a municipal
       judge for the Town of Mount Pleasant, seconded by Mr. Ritchie. All
       present voted in favor.
