7. First Reading: An Ordinance to amend Chapter 150, Building
   Regulations, by adding a new section establishing new
   procedures and guidelines ensuring the protection of property
   from unmanaged stormwater and construction site sediment
   while minimizing impacts to existing trees and buffers. New
   guidelines shall apply to all properties in the Town of Mount
   Pleasant on which a single family detached residence is a
   permitted use. Also proposed is an amendment to chapter 153,
   Stormwater Management and Water Quality, by increasing the
   Single Family Residential National Pollutant Discharge Elimination
   System (NPDES) Fee from $25 to $225. (Ord. No. 18103)
  Kevin Mitchell, Town Engineer, stated that this is a proposal to
  amend Chapter 150. He said it is part of the Town’s building
  regulation to create a new subsection to address stormwater
  management runoff and tree preservation for single family
  residential construction.




                                                           Page 16 of 49
He said the intent and objective is to bring the tree management
for single family construction as well as the grading and
stormwater management under one program to be administered
by one individual and through one permitting process. Currently,
these are individual and separate, and this improvement will
streamline the internal process as well as the communication
with the building and owner of the developed property. He said it
will also create a new site improvement permit and will be only
related to detached single family residentially zoned property.

  until the point where traffic at peak times does not flow well,
  creating significant delays. He said with this connection, those

  now there are vehicles parked all along the roadway, vehicles
  attempting to maneuver through, weaving back and forth,
  creating a very dangerous situation. He said the neighborhood
  has reached out to Council over the past several weeks asking for
  some relief. He has discussed this issue with staff to find solutions.
  He stated that what he would like to suggest is to bring this to the
  Transportation Committee next month and consider a temporary
  closure of the connection between Center Park South and the
  Foxmoor neighborhood. He said that what has caused this is the
