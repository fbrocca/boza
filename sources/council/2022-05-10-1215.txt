   B. Economic Development Committee

        Discussion and possible action regarding potential uses for
        Faison Road property.


                                                                Page 29 of 39
Ms. Hyatt stated what came from Committee is that direction is
needed from Council regarding this property as there has been
some interest, but a decision needs to be made as to what to do
with the property.
Mr. Chapman said there was a lot of discussion on various
possibilities for this site at the Council Retreat, and he was
adamant on using it for workforce housing as well as extending
the senior center. He stated the discussion around purchasing
the property centered on using it for economic development so
that should be the use. He stated it is not fair to keep discussing
this without giving staff direction.
Mr. Santos stated he had said many times the property should
be able to generate revenue for the town. Putting fields there
would generate revenue for the town without any major
expense.
Mr. Iacofano said we need to find the best and highest use for
the property.
Mr. Iacofano moved to request staff procure a consultant to
develop a concept for the space, seconded by Mr. Santos.
Mayor Haynie stated we have a letter from the consultant that
the majority of the property is wetlands. He said filling and
building is causing an issue all over town. He asked Mr.
Iacofano if his motion would include a delineation of wetlands so
we would know how much of the property is highland.

                                                        Page 30 of 39
Mr. Iacofano amended his motion and Mr. Santos amended his
second.
Mr. Rambo asked if we already have that information and Mr.
DeMoura responded in the affirmative. He stated the property is
38 acres with 33 highland and 5 acres of lowland. Mr. DeMoura
said when the permits expire at the end of 2025, if they are not
extended, then the delineation would reverse.
Mr. Rambo said consultants are expensive and he cannot vote
to spend money when there is no consensus from this body on
the use of the property.
Ms. Corley said there is nothing wrong with conservation and
making a statement that we are not going to participate in fill and
build. She said we need field space but we have the Rifle Rane
Property. She said the referendum will give a good indicator as
to what the residents want.
Mayor Haynie said he spoke to the Director of Operations for the
airport and it would be a hazard to the operation of the airport to
have fields with lights right next to the runway.
Ms. Whitley said she likes the idea of a consultant but thinks it is
a bit premature as we have not narrowed down the possibilities.
She said the original purpose was for economic development
and that a combination of that along with open space and
preservation could be positive. She suggested putting a request
for proposals out to the community to see what would be
brought to the table, but she will not be supporting a consultant
at this time.
Mr. Chapman said we have been talking about doing something
for people who try to live and work in our community and
Housing for All could help us with that at this location.
Ms. Whitley suggested Housing for All could submit a Request
for Proposal.
Mayor Haynie called for the vote and the motion fails with a 7-2
vote. Mr. Iacofano and Mr. Santos voted in favor with Mayor
Haynie, Mr. Ritchie, Ms. Whitley, Mr. Rambo, Mr. Chapman, Ms.
Hyatt and Ms. Corley voting opposed.



                                                         Page 31 of 39
Ms. Whitley moved to direct staff to draft a Request for
Proposals for the Faison Road property development options
with a narrow scope of a combination of open space wetland
preservation and economic development with an option of
workforce housing, seconded by Mr. Iacofano.
Ms. Corley said she thinks the residents would want to keep that
space green.
Mayor Haynie asked if Ms. Whitley would amend her motion to
include preservation of green space. He said not just to leave it
there vacant but perhaps with ecofriendly boardwalks or ways to
interact passively with walkways. He said he took her motion to
mean that the only options are development with some
preservation.
Ms. Whitley amended her motion to include some combination
or focus on open space.
Ms. Whitley said she does not want someone to come back with
36 acres of economic development park.
Mr. Iacofano amended his second.
Ms. Whitley said economic development is defined as
businesses that can locate anywhere. Given that this property
was bought for the intent of economic development, narrowing
the focus to preserve the wetlands that are there and use the
highlands for economic development purposes with the potential
for workforce housing is the scope of the motion.
After further discussion, Ms. Whitley said she would like to
regrade her motion to direct staff to construct a Request for
Proposal with a focus on wetlands preservation for the wetlands,
and economic development with an option for workforce housing
for the highlands. Mr. Iacofano amended his second.
Mayor Haynie called for the vote. Ms. Corley, Mr. Rambo and
Mr. Santos all voted in opposition with Mayor Haynie, Ms.
Whitley, Mr. Ritchie, Mr. Iacofano, Mr. Chapman and Ms. Hyatt
all voting in favor. The motion passes with a 6-3 vote.
Mayor Haynie called for a break at 8:00 p.m., and reconvened
the meeting at 8:05 p.m.

D. Health, Recreation and Events Committee

  a. Discussion on a Recreation Capital Project Referendum

     Ms. Hyatt stated the Committee would like some direction from
     Council as to whether to hold the referendum this year, or wait

                                                            Page 33 of 39
until the next election, as well as which items will be placed on
the referendum.
Ms. Whitley said there was some talk about moving it to 2023
but that would delay the Town moving forward with projects.
She stated if it does not pass this year, that will give us time to
understand why it failed and to create a better referendum. She
is in favor of moving forward in 2022.
Mr. Iacofano agreed.
Mayor Haynie said since this is a ballot referendum the
language must go to the Charleston County Board of Elections
and Voter Registration and first reading would need to be in
June.
Mr. Pagliarini said that timeline is correct, without having a
special council meeting. Final reading would need to be in July.
After further discussion, Ms. Whitley moved to hold a special
Council meeting to finalize the details of the referendum,
seconded by Mr. Santos.
Mayor Haynie asked in what month and Ms. Whitley stated prior
to committee day, or whatever timing is needed to be ready for
first reading at the June Council meeting.
Mr. Rambo said this can go back to the committee and they
should be ready to draft the language.
Ms. Whitley withdrew her motion and Mr. Santos withdrew his
second.
Mayor Haynie said in a general election there will be 50,000
votes cast and it would take 25,000 votes to pass. In a
municipal election, there are about 17,000 votes cast and it
would only take about 7 or 8,000 votes to pass.
Ms. Whitley said if we are going to impose a tax on 90,000
residents it would be appropriate to have the most voter
participation.
Ms. Hyatt said this would go back to the Health, Recreation and
Events committee for continued discussion.
