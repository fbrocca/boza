                  SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                 Tuesday, September 12, 2023, 5:00 p.m.
                 Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
            100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                  MINUTES
1.       Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m. Brenda Corley,
     Howard Chapman, Gary Santos, Jake Rambo, John Iacofano, Guang
     Ming Whitley and Carl Ritchie were all present. Laura Hyatt was absent.
2.       Public Comment
     There were no public comments.
3.       Recognition of Cub Scout Pack 502, Boy Scouts of America
     Troop 502B and Troop 502G for becoming a Guiness World
     Records Title Holder
     Mayor and Council recognized and congratulated members of Cub
     Scout Pack 502 on their accomplishment. Mr. Nathan Schutte stated
     over 700 blankets were collected to build the fort and will go to
     community homeless and animal shelters. He thanked the Town for
     allowing them to use the Mount Pleasant Waterfront Park for the project.
4.       Executive Session
     Mayor Haynie stated there is a need to go into executive session to
     receive an update on pending litigation entitled James N. Deierlein, Jr.,
     et al vs. Commission of Public Works for the Town of Mount Pleasant.
          Mr. Chapman moved to go into executive session, seconded by Ms.
     Whitley. All present voted in favor.
     The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:06 p.m.
          Legal and Contractual
             Update on pending litigation entitled James N. Deierlein,
             Jr., et al vs. Commission of Public Works for the Town of
             Mount Pleasant, et al (2023-CP-10-1688).
5. Post Executive Session
     Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
     any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
     an executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
          Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:57 p.m. stating no votes
     or action had been taken.
6.       Adjourn
     There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 5:57
     p.m.

     Respectfully submitted,

     Christine Barrett
     Clerk of Council
     September 12, 2023
