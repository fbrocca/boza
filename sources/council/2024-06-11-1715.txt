                   SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                         Tuesday June 11, 2024
                               5:30 p.m.
                  Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
             100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                    MINUTES
1.        Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m. Daniel Brownstein,
     Howard Chapman, Guang Ming Whitley, Jake Rambo and John Iacofano
     were all present at call to order. Carl Ritchie was absent. Laura Hyatt
     and Gary Santos arrived during executive session.
2.        Public Comment
     Denise Fugo, Executive Director, Housing for All Mount Pleasant, 2222
     Ashley River Road, stated her comments are in regard to Faison Road.
     She said most of her life she has submitted requests for proposals for a
     number of agencies and typically when the contract does not get executed
     by the person that submitted the winning bid, the entity will go back to the
     other agencies that submitted responses. Ms. Fugo said Housing for All
     Mount Pleasant would like to partner with the Town for the affordable
     housing piece. She said they collect poll data on their website for people
     in need of rentals of housing in Mount Pleasant and it is very interesting
     data. She stated all their employers need help and this project is more
     economic development than it is housing. Ms. Fugo said Housing for All
     Mount Pleasant did not get the winning bid, and now we are two years
     behind and the housing crisis is much more severe than it was in 2020.
     She is asking if they can help expedite the project, and stated they have
     investor groups that they work with and are raising money internationally.
     She asked Council to consider Housing for All Mount Pleasant to assist
     with this project.
3.        Executive Session
     Mr. Rambo moved to go into executive session for the reasons stated on
     the agenda, seconded by Ms. Whitley. All present voted in favor. (Mr.
     Santos and Ms. Hyatt were not present for the vote, and Mr. Ritchie was
     absent from the meeting.) The motion carried with a 6-0 vote.
     The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:32 p.m.
