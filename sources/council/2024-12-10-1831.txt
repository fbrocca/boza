Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 6:01 p.m. Daniel Brownstein,
Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Guang Ming Whitley, Jake Rambo, John
Iacofano, Gary Santos and Carl Ritchie were all present. Mayor Haynie
stated this is Council Member Carl Ritchie’s final Council meeting as he
has been promoted to be the Sheriff of Charleston County.
Council Member Ritchie it has been an honor and a privilege to serve the
citizens of Mount Pleasant for over thirty-five years as a police officer,
police chief, and now a council member. He thanked Town Council, Mr.
DeMoura, and the residents of Mount Pleasant and said he looked forward
to serving the County as the Sheriff.

          for the first amendment to Ordinance 19058 Patriots Annex
          Development Agreement for a proposed development to be known
          as Patriots Annex. The development is proposed to consist of a mix
          of uses to include multi-family residential, office, retail, hotel and
          conference, amphitheater, museums and public plazas. The
          property comprising the proposed development is an approximately
                                                                      Page 1 of 20
30-acre parcel part of a larger 162.86-acre tract of land. The subject

by the Charleston County ROD Office in Plat Book L23, Page 0080.

grand lawn with an amphitheater facing the water and the new

Michael Bennett stated he is the lessee of the land being discussed
