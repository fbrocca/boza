                                  MINUTES


I.     Call to Order
       Mayor Haynie called the Special Town Council Meeting to order at
       11:46 a.m.
       Council Members Bob Brimmer, Joe Bustos, Kevin Cunnane, Kathy
       Landing, Tom O’Rourke and Gary Santos were present.
       Council Members Jim Owens and Guang Ming Whitley were absent.
II.    Public Comment
       [None]
III.   First Reading: An Ordinance authorizing the issuance of a not
       exceeding $4,000,000 Waterworks and Sewer System Revenue Parity
       Bond, Series 2018A of the Town of Mount Pleasant, South Carolina.
       (Ord. No. 18056)
       Mr. DeMoura stated that staff supports as a recommendation to
       Council, the borrowing of a revenue bond for Mount Pleasant
       Waterworks and asks that First Reading be considered. He stated that
       the Chief Financial Officer for Mount Pleasant Waterworks, Mark
       Coffin is present or Paul Trouche, Legal Counsel.
       Mr. Trouche stated that this would be a revenue bond, not a general
       obligation bond. It is issued under parity with the other revenue bonds
       that stand under the general bond ordinance adopted in the early
      1990’s. He said this is for the Park West force main project and it is an
      attractive loan at 2.1% from the State Revolving Fund. He said the bids
      came in so well, that originally, this was going to be a not exceeding $6
      million-dollar loan and they have since then, downsized as they did
      not have to borrow as much as originally anticipated.
      Mr. Bustos made a motion to approve the $4 million-dollar bond;
      seconded by Mr. O’Rourke. All present voted in favor.
      Mr. DeMoura read FIRST READING BY TITLE ONLY.
IV.   First Reading: An Ordinance to amend Zoning Code Section
      156.325(C), Use Table, of Chapter 156 of the Mount Pleasant Code of
      Ordinances, to add a Hospice Care Facility as a Permitted Use in the
      Economic Development District. (Ord. No. 18057)
      Mayor Haynie stated that this is to add a Hospice Care Facility as a
      permitted use in the Economic Development district. He stated that
      this is the Hospice Care Facility off Long Point Road on Wando Park
      Boulevard.
      Mr. Santos moved to approve; seconded by Mr. Bustos. All present
      voted in favor.
      Mr. DeMoura read FIRST READING BY TITLE ONLY.
V.    Adjourn
      There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:49 a.m.

Respectfully submitted,
