      1.       Approval to name the boardwalk at Shem Creek

      2.      Approval to create a capital fund account to house
           donations for a Maritime Heritage Center
           Ms. Whitley stated that the Magwood family is working towards
           erecting an iconic bronze statue to be placed at the boardwalk,
           for those in this community. She stated that the family is in the
           process of acquiring 501c3 status to create a Maritime Heritage
           Center and raise the funds. Because it takes six to eight
           months for the 501c3 status, they were hoping the Town could
           create a capital fund account to house donations on behalf of
           this future Maritime Heritage Center.
                Ms. Whitley moved to approve; seconded by Mr. Santos.
           All present voted in favor.
      3.       Approval to proceed with the Town’s annual Christmas
