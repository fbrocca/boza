I. Planning Committee
  Continued discussion regarding the Urban Corridor Overlay
  District (UCOD) and potential removal of Ben Sawyer Boulevard
  from the district: Staff informed the committee that they had
  conducted an initial analysis of the properties and zoning along the
  Ben Sawyer Boulevard portion of the overlay district. Using a series
  of aerial photos and maps, vacant properties were identified, and
  existing developments approved under the overlay provisions were
  described. Staff pointed out some considerations to keep in mind
  should the overlay be removed, including the nature of future
  development using the existing, underlying zoning, and the
  numerous nonconforming situations which would be created when
  current developments are no longer allowed under the general
  zoning districts. The committee received the information, asked
  various questions, and deferred the item until the May meeting to
  enable time to study the background materials.
