Daniel Brownstein, Kathryn Whitaker, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman,

     [None]

3. Executive Session
Council Member Chapman moved to go into executive session for the

Council Member Iacofano. The motion carried unanimously.

The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:02 p.m.

4. Post Executive Session
Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:50 p.m. announcing that no
votes were cast and no action was taken.
Regarding item 3a:
Council Member Chapman moved to grant town staff the authority to
proceed as discussed in executive session, seconded by Mr. Iacofano.
The motion carried unanimously.
Regarding item 3b:
Mayor Haynie made a statement in the form of a motion to commend the
handling of the investigation into the current issue in the Police
Department by the Chief and the head of Human Resources. The Mayor
noted that Council received up-to-date information and commended the
Chief for the information made available to the public the previous day,
which he described as a fantastic update. The Council agreed with the
path forward as laid out in executive session, believing it to be on the
right track not only for the current matter but for improvements moving
forward. The motion also included thanking and commending all officers
dutifully serving and protecting the Town of Mount Pleasant. Council
Member Chapman seconded the motion. The motion carried
unanimously.

5. Approval of the 2026 Blessing of the Fleet Beneficiaries
Nicole Harvey, Community Affairs and Marketing Division Chief,
presented recommendations for the 2026 Blessing of the Fleet
beneficiaries. She explained that for many years, East Cooper Meals on
Wheels had received all net proceeds from the festival, which was
approaching its 39th year.
Ms. Harvey described that the Magwood family works with staff to review
applications from area nonprofits serving Mount Pleasant residents. For
2026, they recommended two organizations:
  ● The Lonan Foundation, which works with children who have a
    parent diagnosed with cancer, providing specific care and
    resources to help them through traumatic events.
  ● East Cooper Mount Pleasant Kiwanis Club, which conducts local
    projects with schools, the Ronald McDonald House, Lucy
    Beckham High School, and Wando High School.
Ms. Harvey described both as great local organizations full of wonderful
Mount Pleasant people helping our community.
