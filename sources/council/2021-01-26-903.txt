III.       Review of Town of Mount Pleasant financial position
            Eric DeMoura, Town Administrator, provided Mayor Haynie and
       Town Council with the following presentation on the Town’s financial
       position and stated that the Town’s position is very strong. He said
       this is despite many challenges over the last decade such as new
       police and fire vehicles, as well as facilities and roadways. He stated
       that the Town has the highest bond ratings and have been able to
       maintain those ratings.

Mr. O’Rourke commended the Town Administrator and staff because
with their philosophy, they assist Council. He said when things looked
dismal, Council did not give instructions to staff to implement the cost

item is the borrowing of 0.8% which is unheard of and this is because

will not be in a positive financial position in five years if we do not.
Ms. Landing agreed and stated that people in other states wish to be
where they are safe and community oriented and they want to move
here. She said the Town is a position to recruit the right businesses to
generate revenue for the Town. She said this is a great year to do
community building activities.
Mr. DeMoura stated that there are some challenges ahead. Business
license, with the new legislation that has passed, will impact the
Town. Construction is the major portion of the Town’s business
license. He stated that talent protection is also another challenge that
is important and the Town must remain competitive in salaries and
benefits. He said there is a “talent war” with other government entities
and the private sector as we become more skill oriented. He said
engineers are being absorbed by the private sector, and police
officers are being attracted away from the Town which is a major
concern. He said what contributes to the Town’s success are staff
members. He said, additionally, there is the infrastructure which the
Town contributes a great deal towards and there is a list of new
capital projects on the way as well. He said we also have a Town to
maintain such as roads and pavement preservation; however the
stormwater portion is really the challenge going forward.

VI.       Review of Mount Pleasant Way
      Mr. DeMoura stated that this is the newest most sizeable capital
      project the Town has currently. He said this is really “the” project. He
      stated that Transportation Director, Brad Morrison would be reviewing
      this portion with Council and will discuss the first phase of this project;
      where it could go, what it could look like, the cost associated with the
      project, and then obtain Council’s feedback.
          Brad Morrison, Transportation Director, stated that the Mayor
      mentioned legacy projects staff feels that this is truly one of those
      projects, both for this Council, future Councils, staff who are involved
      and the community. He recognized James Aton, who has overseen
      the master planning process for the Town over the past 14 months
      and has developed this presentation. He said the Mount Pleasant
      Way was borne out of the comprehensive plan process and is a
      Town-wide multi-use path network. It is more of a linear trail system
      and linear park for the Town. It is all about connections.
