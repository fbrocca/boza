1. Call to Order
  Mayor Will Haynie called the special Town Council meeting to order at
  4:30 p.m. on May 13, 2025.

2. Public Comment
  Pam Ireland, president of the Harbor Watch Homeowners Association,
  addressed the council regarding legal matters to be discussed in
  executive session. She spoke about two Board of Zoning Appeals cases
  involving the marsh adjacent to Harbor Watch neighborhood. Ireland
  emphasized that the board made well-founded decisions in both cases
  based on different circumstances and the required criteria under the
  code. She expressed support for both decisions and stressed that
  obtaining a variance is just one step in the building process, with
  separate scrutiny required for permits.

  Ms. Hyatt presented a proclamation declaring May 2025 as National
  Tennis Month in the Town of Mount Pleasant. The proclamation
  highlighted the benefits of tennis, the efforts of the United States Tennis
  Association (USTA) and local tennis clubs in promoting the sport and
  encouraged residents of all ages to stay active and play tennis.
  Bill Ennis, representing the USTA, thanked the council for their support
  and mentioned a recent public-private project where the USTA and

8. Recognition of Special Olympics State Basketball Tournament
   Champions
  Shelley Davis presented the MPRD (Mount Pleasant Recreation
  Department) Basketball Stingers, noting that the unified program has
  been running for over 20 years. The team won gold medals at the local
  tournament on Daniel Island and went undefeated at the state
  tournament in Columbia.
  Davis also recognized other Special Olympics athletes present in the
  audience, including golf and swim teammates. She invited the council
  members to participate in a kickball game against the gold medalist
  team in the summer.
  Mayor Haynie mentioned that some of the athletes had traveled to
  Denver twice for the All-America City competition.
