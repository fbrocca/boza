VI.        PUBLIC COMMENT
       Denise Fugo, 515 Mountain Laurel Lane, Goose Creek, stated she is
       the Executive Director of Housing for All Mount Pleasant and is here
       to ask Council for their support to move forward with affordable
       housing as the plans for Towne Centre are considered.
Morgan Bentivegna, 320 Broad Street, Charleston stated the
applicant for Towne Centre is asking for a deferral on first reading of
the project as they work to adjust the Planned Development
amendment.
Pamela Marsh, 8 Tabby Lane, Isle of Palms spoke in support of
naming the dog park at the Memorial Waterfront Park after Captain
Richard Cliff, Jr.
Adam King, 948 Lansing Drive spoke in support of naming the dog
park at the Memorial Waterfront Park after Captain Richard Cliff, Jr.
Richard Cliff, 4168 Colonel Vanderhorst Circle stated his family is
grateful Council is considering naming the dog park at the Memorial
Waterfront Park after his son because he loved his country, he loved
Mount Pleasant, and he loved his dogs.
Larry Roberts, 917 Royall Avenue, spoke about the flooding on
Royall Avenue stating he asked a supervisor working on the project
about the flooding and water gushing out of the manhole cover after
the drainage project was complete. He said the supervisor told him
the problem was because a hydraulic gate was not working. He is
going to write a letter but needs to know to whom to address it.
Charles Fletcher, 2788 Latrobe Court, stated he is the Vice
President of real estate construction for Roper St. Francis Health
Care and is here to answer any questions on agenda item VIII.A.3.
Ellen Moore, 1469 Gunnison Street stated there was a successful
North Mount Pleasant farmers market last weekend across from
Costco and the sense of community was terrific. She also wanted to
ask leadership to not vote to approve the amendment to the Wilson
Tract with the massive building plans.
Herb Sass, 413 Hobcaw Drive, Charleston County Council Chair,
stated he wanted to talk about the third transportation sales tax
referendum Charleston County has on the ballot in November. He
said it is very important, and it is not a new tax. He said the first tax
will be timing out and this third one will follow in right behind it for
greenbelts, CARTA and other programs such as bus rapid transit,
annual allocations for bike and pedestrian paths, and intersection
improvements. He said he would like to point out how successful the
half cent sales tax program has been with the first project being $75
Million for the completion of the Ravenel Bridge. He said the second
        program completed in Mount Pleasant was Johnnie Dodds Boulevard
        to keep the traffic flowing. Chairman Sass said the third project was
        Highway 17 out to Wando High School, on which SCDOT and the
        Town worked on together. He said it also put in half the cost of Billy
        Swails Boulevard and currently plans are in the works for
        improvements to Highway 41. He said he would be glad to answer
        any questions.
