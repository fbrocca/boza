B.       Presentation of Charleston County’s interim improvement
     plans for SC Highway 41
          Cal Oyer, stated that he is the Project Manager for the Highway
     41 Corridor Improvements project. He stated that in early 2019 after
     public input and discussion with Town staff and Council, they
     understood there was a real need to have some type of interim fix at
     Highway 41 and US 17. He stated that they began designing one and
     identified a scope at that time that included adding a southbound
     lane to US 41, starting at Highway 41 to US 17. At that time the Town
     of Mount Pleasant agreed on that scope and provided $200,000 for
     the design. He said as the process proceeded, they found that with
     different types of improvements, they could provide the same traffic
     relief quicker and less expensive. He said this is what they are
     proposing to do this evening.




                                                                Page 3 of 60
Mr. Oyer stated that the southbound lane at Joe Rouse and Highway
41 would provide an alternate type of intersection called a
Continuous Green-T intersection. He said it is not a roundabout and
will allow southbound traffic to proceed without any interruptions,
except if there is a pedestrian crossing. He said this can be
implemented by changing pavement markings at that intersection.
He said the results were that the travel time from Harper’s Ferry to


                                                          Page 4 of 60
Hamlin Road would be cut approximately in half, which is the same
results they had by putting in the southbound lane and has actually
improved. He said based on these findings they began looking at the
cost and time savings, which is significant. He said the benefits of
moving to the change in scope is that the construction costs, which
were originally estimated to be approximately $3 million could be
reduced to under $1 million. He said construction impacts will be
eliminated to three neighborhoods that would have had to go
through construction changes twice. He said they also eliminate the
need for the wetlands permits and impacts to wetlands and
eliminate the need to obtain any right-of-way. He said this will
reduce utility relocations significantly and deliver this traffic relief to
the Town residents much sooner than the previous scope showed.
He said their design permitting should be complete in early 2020 and
feel they will be able to implement the construction and complete it
in the summer of 2020, which is a savings of approximately six
months from the previous proposal. He said they are here this
evening to request Council’s approval to move forward with the
change in scope for the project.
Mayor Haynie stated that there was a vote of Council to do the
interim lane and it involved a cost share with the County. He asked
Legal Counsel if Town Council needs an act of Council to change the
previously authorized action to do the interim lane.
Mr. Pagliarini stated that unless there is a substitute change, he

Mayor Haynie asked if this presentation could be given at the

Mr. Oyer responded in the affirmative.
Mayor Haynie stated that this evening he would like to open it up for
questions from Council and the public.
Mr. O’Rourke asked if the 30-day delay sets the project back from a
timeframe standpoint.


                                                                 Page 5 of 60

Transportation (SCDOT) for their official review. He said the delay
