IV.      Update on Operational Status of the Town (Town
      Administrator)
      Mr. DeMoura, Town Administrator, stated that he is happy to report
      that all is going very well, operationally and are strong. He said they
      have been able to meet the first three operational objectives which
      were to retain critical services to the public. He said the Town has
      been able to do so and even enhance those critical services when
      able. He said they were able to put more resources towards
      vegetative debris collection which is significant this time of year. He
      said in addition, the Town has also been able to maintain day to day
      operations, keeping morale high. He said staff is working. Most of the
      Town employees are either required to be in the office or working in
      the field. He said police, firefighters, public service/sanitation workers,
      building inspectors and others are on the job. He said for those
      employees where this is not a reqirement, they have been able to
                                                                       Page 5 of 34
maintain day to day operations through teleworking, which has gone
very well. He said the Town has not had to do this in the past so this
has been a learning experience for everyone. He said it has worked
out very well and he is proud of the men and women of the Town. He
said there are very few employees who do not have the option of
teleworking and for them, thanks to the creativity of staff, many have
been deployed into the field to inspect public assets and
infrastructure. He said the employees developed the idea and an App
where they go into the field and check on the conditions of assets
such as sidewalks, roads, street lights, traffic lights and other assets.
He said these are the things that are sometimes difficult to do in the
course of normal business, so the staff has been able to put everyone
to work. He said the parts of the Town that are not operating are the
court system, boards and commissions, recreation programs which
are not operating. He said the fourth operational objective was to
mitigate as much as possible as a local government, the economic
impact on the community and businesses. He believes that the Town
has been a very good resource to the citizens and business owners.
There are Town staff dedicated towards business support. He said
there is a great deal of information coming from the federal
government that staff has been dedicated to in order to be a good
resource to them. He said the last and fifth objective is recovery
planning and staff continues to actively work on this which involves
returning everyone to normal operations. He said they are looking to
do this through a layered approach where portions of the operation
are brought back into Town Hall and assess how this is performing.
He said the value in this is if there is another spike in this particular
virus or another, staff would have a tested method of how to
approach coming back to normal operations. He said if they find that
the employees or the public are at risk or exposed in some way, due
to something that is being done at the Town with their operations,
they will be able to pull back the areas they were not ready for at that
time. He said Council may be hearing, as part of park re-openings
discussions, that the Recreation Department has done a good job
with the phased plan of how to get back to normal. He stated that the
only thing brand new to report is that anti-body testing is available to
the first responders beginning this week, which is voluntary. He said
they are hopeful that there is acceptable aggregate information that
will help with the Town’s planning as the Town comes out of this


                                                               Page 6 of 34
     situation. He thanked Mayor Haynie and Town Council for their
     leadership during this pandemic.

      town finances and considerations for proceeding with meetings
      to adopt FY 2021 Budget (Finance Committee Chair)
      Mr. O’Rourke stated that this budget will be unlike any other budget
      that Council has had previously. He said because of this, the Finance
      Committee will be scheduling a meeting. He said Council will then be
      presented with a plan by Town Administrator, Eric DeMoura and
      Chief Financial Officer, Marcy Cotov on how to move this forward and
      help make some difficult decisions. He said it will be unique and
      revenues will be down substantially, and Council will have work to do.
      He said Mr. DeMoura and Ms. Cotov are prepared. He said Council
      may wish to have a Budget Committee meeting as opposed to a
      Finance Committee meeting for this; however, it is Council’s decision.
      Mayor Haynie stated that the Town ends their fiscal year on June
      30th. He said last week, this was being discussed and thought that the
      year would end breaking even without there being any deficit. He

                                                                      Page 15 of 34
asked Mr. DeMoura if this still holds true today because things
change rapidly.
Mr. DeMoura stated that preliminary estimates, he believes the Town
will meet budget with what has already come in and what revenues
are still anticipated. He said, once again, Department Managers have
managed their budgets well and expect to return money on the
expenditures side which will be helpful. He stated that the most
significant obstacle is the financial picture for next year where the
Town anticipates almost all revenue categories to be impacted. He
said this will be the challenge.
Mayor Haynie stated that the City of Charleston is expecting as much
as a $42 million decline in revenue; however, they are more heavily
impacted by Accommodations and Hospitality Taxes than the Town.
Mr. DeMoura stated that the revenue categories are different and
come in at different times throughout the year. He said similar to the
Town returning to normal operations through a layered plan, it is very
similar with the budget to have phases or tiers of action depending on
the severity. He stated that action has already been taken and
Council will see more recommendations in the proposed budget
different from what Council saw previously, that will help to prepare
the Town for the impact. Then there will be layers depending upon
the severity in the drops in revenues.
