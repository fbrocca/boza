                                    MINUTES
1.       Call to Order
     Mr. Rambo called the meeting to order at 4:49 p.m. Daniel Brownstein,
     Laura Hyatt, John Iacofano and Carl Ritchie were all present. Mayor
     Haynie was absent. Guang Ming Whitley arrived at 4:50 p.m., Howard
     Chapman arrived at 4:57 p.m. and Gary Santos arrived at 4:49 p.m.
2.        Public Comment
            Marie Louise Moreto, 1133 Liberty Court stated she was
     representing the Mount Pleasant Community Arts Center Committee and
     she is also a member of the Mount Pleasant Artists Guild (Ms. Whitley
     arrived at 4:50 p.m.). She stated support of local artists is essential, and
     she believes before a final decision on a temporary arts space is made,
     the basic needs of art organizations and the artists should be considered.
     She stated it is her opinion that the Riverview Worship Hall has the most
     potential to be used as a temporary arts center. She said local artists and
     performers can be a potent force for spreading the word and generating
     enthusiasm for this project.
            Carol Hallman, Parsonage Woods Lane thanked Council for this
     effort to find a temporary arts center. She stated it has been difficult for
     the Seaside Community Choir and the Mount Pleasant Community
     Orchestra to find a suitable place for weekly rehearsals. She asked that
     Council please schedule a time for the orchestra and choir directors to
     visit any potential facility before a final decision is made as there are many
     factors to consider.
         Diane Vincent, 242 6th Avenue stated she is an artist, art therapist
     and an art teacher and said she just found out about this exciting news
     about a potential community arts center. She said it would be nice to have
     a space where she could see clients or have art therapy groups. (Mr.
     Chapman arrived at 4:57 p.m.)
          Eric Vincent, 242 6th Avenue stated he is a children’s book author
     and illustrator, and he is excited to hear about this possible arts center.
     He said he would like to speak in support as this kind of space would be
     enormously helpful for community outreach. (Mr. Santos arrived at 4:59
     p.m.)
          Lucy Richmond, Vice President of the Mount Pleasant Artists Guild
     stated her organization highly supports this project.
