                                  MINUTES


I.     Call to Order
           Mayor Haynie called the Special Council meeting to order at 1:02
       p.m.
       Council Members Tom O’Rourke, Laura Hyatt, Brenda Corley, Guang
       Ming Whitley, Jake Rambo, Kathy Landing, Howard Chapman and
       Gary Santos were present.


II.        Public Comment
       [None]


III.       First Reading: An Ordinance to amend Section 30.35(F) of the
       Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances and to add Section 30.35(H) in
       order to allow Council participation and voting by electronic means.
       (Ord. No. 20038)
       Mr. Pagliarini provided legal opinion on the changes to the ordinance.
           Mr. Rambo moved to approve; seconded by Ms. Whitley.
       Mr. Chapman stated that he is not in favor of requiring the video
       portion. He stated that from an electronic standpoint, Council would
       be eliminating the ability for someone to pick up the issue if they have
       electric problems. He stated that he has had this issue as well. He
       said the easy way to find out if members are present is to conduct a
       roll call. He stated that Council may not have the visual means to
       participate; however, a cellular phone would likely be more
       convenient and available. He added that he is not in favor of only
       allowing a visual means.
           Mr. Chapman moved to strike the portion that requires the video
      portion and still allow for council members to be able, in an
      emergency, to use a telephone to participate and vote.
      Mr. Pagliarini stated that if this ordinance goes forward, this motion
      would be addressed at final reading.
      Mr. Chapman asked if there is anything in State law that would
      preclude using a telephone.
      Mr. Pagliarini responded in the negative.
      Council continued with discussions.
      Mr. Pagliarini clarified the intent of the ordinance. He said if there is
      an actual emergency situation that would prevent Council meeting as
      a whole where it was mandated by conditions that there be an
      emergency meeting by electronic means, this may be accomplished.
      Mayor Haynie stated that this is first reading and there are situations
      that may have not been considered.
      Mr. Pagliarini stated that assuming that Council moves forward with
      this ordinance today, all points brought forward will be considered.
      Mr. Chapman stated that he would like for this to be all encompassing
      and general.
           Mr. Chapman opposed. All others present voted in favor. Motion
      carries 8-1.


IV.      Discussion of process forward for hiring in-house general
      counsel
      Mayor Haynie stated that this item was discussed at the Police,
      Judicial and Legal Committee. He stated that Council members have
      all been provided with new applicants. He stated that prior to moving
      forward with the interview process, all Council must be in agreement
      to do so.


V.        Executive Session
          Mr. Rambo moved to adjourn into executive session; seconded
      by Ms. Whitley. All present voted in favor.
