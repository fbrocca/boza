VI.        PUBLIC COMMENT
           Julie Allison, 500 Pitt Street stated she is speaking in support of
      designating Edwards Park as historic. She added that if it is
      designated as historic and signage is needed, there are many Old
      Village residents who would be happy to help with the expense of the
      signage.
            Sharon Hox, 1249 Hidden Lakes Drive stated she is speaking in
      favor of the need for effective local hate crime legislation. She said as
      a member of the Jewish Federation of Charleston she is too aware of
      recent hate events in Mount Pleasant, and it pains her to have to deal
      with these issues in today’s world. She asked Council to help everyone
      by eliminating this despicable behavior. She said recognition of hate
      crimes with penalties to enforce punishment will help us regain a more
      just society.
            Dan Freemeyer, 235 Rowens Creek Drive stated he is the Co-
      Chair of the East Cooper Faith Council and he speaks on behalf of their
      member congregations in support of the hate/intimidation ordinance.
      He commends Mayor and Council for showing they care about the
      religious freedoms of their citizens. He said this is a necessary step to


                                                                      Page 3 of 8
        protect these freedoms for others and asked them to pass the
        Ordinance, thanking them for their support.
               William Walz, stated he works for Atlantic South in North
        Charleston, at 1939 Remount Road and is speaking in favor of the
        annexation of the 3.45 acre tract on Boston Grill Road and Ida Road.
        He said they are trying to get sewer service to the lot, and the only way
        to do that is by applying for annexation.
              Janet Newham, 510 Pitt Street stated she wanted to thank
        Council for considering giving Edwards Park it’s own historic
        designation. She said e-mails were forwarded that represent just a
        small fraction of residents who are in support of that designation. She
        said they are working with the Historic Commission to have a marker
        designed that recognizes the rich history of that area and the residents
        are going to pay for the marker. She said Ms. Ann Edwards could not
        be here this evening and asked that her tremendous support be
        passed along to Council.

      B. OLD BUSINESS
             Final Reading: An Ordinance to extend the Building Permit
        Allocation System and amend Section 156.070, Et Seq. of the Town
        of Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances (Ord. No. 23035)
        October 18, 2023 Planning Commission Minutes
        November 6, 2023 Planning Committee Minutes
        December 13, 2023 Planning Commission Minutes
        January 2, 2024 Planning Committee Minutes
            Ms. Whitley moved for final reading, seconded by Mr. Rambo.
            Mr. Rambo moved to amend the ordinance to reduce the
        number of single-family dwelling units by half so the top line would
        go to 1,200, which results in going from 480 annually to 240
        annually, which is distributed by 120 from January to June, and 120
        from July to December, seconded by Ms. Hyatt.
             Mayor Haynie called for the vote. Ms. Hyatt, Mr. Rambo and
        Mr. Santos voted in favor with Mr. Brownstein, Mr. Chapman, Mr.
        Iacofano, Ms. Whitley and Mayor Haynie voting opposed. The
        motion to amend fails with a 3-5 vote.
             Mr. Brownstein moved to amend the ordinance to reduce it to
        a two-year program and to change the caps to two fifths of the value
        shown, seconded by Mr. Iacofano.
              Mayor Haynie called for a vote on the amendment. Mr.
        Brownstein and Mr. Iacofano voted in favor with Mr. Chapman, Ms.
        Hyatt, Mr. Rambo, Mr. Santos, Ms. Whitley and Mayor Haynie
        voting opposed. The motion to amend failed with a 2-6 vote.
             Mayor Haynie called for the vote on final reading of the
        ordinance and all present voted in favor.
        This Ordinance SIGNED, SEALED and DELIVERED this date.
