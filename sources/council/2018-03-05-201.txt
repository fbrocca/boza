                     MOUNT PLEASANT TOWN COUNCIL
                           Special Council Meeting
                           Monday, March 5, 2018
                   12:00 p.m. – Committee Meeting Room
                rd
               3 Floor - Mount Pleasant Municipal Complex
             100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                  MINUTES

I.     Call to Order

       Mayor Haynie called the Special Town Council Meeting to order at
       12:35 p.m. Mr. Bustos, Ms. Landing, Ms. Whitley, Mr. Owens, Mr.
       Cunnane and Mr. Brimmer were all present. Mr. O’Rourke and Mr.
       Santos were absent. Mayor Haynie said this meeting is primarily for
       Council to review and be briefed on security measures for school
       safety.

II.    Public Comment

       Mayor Haynie asked if anyone would like to speak. There being no
       one, the public comment period was closed.

III.   Executive session for the purpose of discussing the Mount Pleasant
       Police Department’s involvement in school security and readiness

       Mayor Haynie stated the next item is executive session for the
       purpose of discussing the Mount Pleasant Police Department’s
       involvement in school security and readiness. Mayor Haynie said
      there is information that involves law enforcement resources, tactics,
      responses, and this information needs to be discussed in order to keep
      our schools safe. Mayor Haynie said items that are not tactical will be
      discussed in the public session. Mayor Haynie asked if there is a
      motion to go into executive session to discuss the item published.

      Mr. Owens moved as stated, seconded by Mr. Bustos. All present
      voted in favor.

      The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 12:38 p.m.
      Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 1:36 p.m. stating no votes
      had been taken.

IV.   Post Executive Session
      Committee may take action on any item listed on an executive
      session agenda or discussed in an executive session during a properly
      noticed meeting.
      Ms. Whitley moved to augment the Police Department’s budget in
      order to enhance school security as discussed in executive session,
      seconded by Mr. Cunnane. All present voted in favor.
V.    Discussion concerning authorizing staff to explore an automatic aid
      and operational agreement for the 41 corridor and the new Wando
      River bridge between the Mount Pleasant Fire Department and the
      Charleston Fire Department

      Mr. Cunnane stated there was no Fire Committee meeting scheduled
      for this month and the Charleston Fire Department is opening a new
      station on the other side of the Highway 41 bridge. He said this
      morning from the back portion of Dunes West he completed a Google
      Maps search and compared the response time from there and from
      Paul Foster Road. From Paul Foster Road, it is a twenty minute
      response time, and the new fire station that the City of Charleston is
      opening is an 11 minute response time. He said the closest

      professional fire crew should respond. Mr. Cunnane said this is to
      authorize staff to begin pursuing an agreement. He said he spoke to
      the Legal Department and these agreements are easy to establish as
      there are many in place already. Mr. Cunnane said he wanted to get
      this moving as soon as possible so as not to wait twenty minutes for
      the Mount Pleasant department to respond when there is a station
      much closer.

      Mayor Haynie asked if a motion was needed or if any Council
      Members had any comments, deferring to the Chair of the Fire
      Committee first. Mr. Brimmer said he thinks it is a great idea and he is
      happy to have staff working on it in preparation for a Fire Committee
      meeting next month. Mr. Brimmer said he does have some questions,
      but it could possibly be ready for full Council in April if that is suitable
      to Mr. Cunnane. Mr. Cunnane responded in the affirmative.

      Mayor Haynie asked if a motion is needed. Mr. DeMoura said he
      understands the intent and staff will be ready for the Fire Committee
      meeting in August.

      Mayor Haynie thanked Mr. Cunnane for bringing this to the attention
      of Council.

VI.   Adjourn


      There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 1:39
      p.m.

      Respectfully submitted,

      Christine Barrett
      Clerk of Council
