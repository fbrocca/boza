                                   MINUTES

1. Call to Order
         Mayor Haynie called the Special Town Council meeting to order at
     9:01 a.m. Council members Howard Chapman, Carl Ritchie, John
     Iacofano, Jake Rambo, Laura Hyatt and Guang Ming Whitley were
     present. Ms. Corley arrived at 9:03 a.m. Mr. Santos was absent.
2.       Public Comment
     [None]
3.       Executive Session
         Christiane Farrell, Assistant Town Administrator reviewed the
     schedule with Council.
     (Ms. Corley arrived at 9:03 a.m.)
          Marcy Cotov, Chief Financial Officer, stated that the Government
     Finance Officers Association (GFOA), has partnered with Common
     Ground Institute and Urban Three and this was an opportunity for them to
     provide a resource for the Town.
       a. Short List presentations for Faison Road Request for Proposals
       b. Discussion of presentations
          Mr. Rambo moved to adjourn into executive session for the reasons
     stated on the agenda; seconded by Ms. Whitley. All present voted in favor.
     Council adjourned into executive session at 9:07 a.m.
         Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 1:46 p.m. stating no votes
     or action were taken during executive session.
4. Post Executive Session
       Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of any
       section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in an
       executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
