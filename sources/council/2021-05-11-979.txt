       A.       NATIONAL BOATING SAFETY WEEK PROCLAMATION
                Town Administrator, Eric DeMoura read the National Boating
            Safety Week Proclamation, which was then presented to Walter
            Runck, United States Coast Guard Auxiliary, by Mayor Haynie
            and Town Council.
       B.       CITIZEN RECOGNITION
            Mayor Haynie and Town Council recognized Jerry Walkowiak,
            Manager of Belle Hall Chick-fil-A, for answering the call to help our
            community in the rain to direct traffic during the first round of
           COVID vaccines administered at Seacoast Church. Mayor Haynie
           presented Mr. Walkowiak with the Key to the Town as well as a
           Certificate of appreciation.
      C.      RECOGNITION OF NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP DANCE
           TEAM
               Gary Santos, Chairman, Recreation Committee, stated that the
           Crusaderettes Dance Team from Christ our King-Stella Maris
           School, made history last month by earning the school’s first
           national championship title. He said this dance team is made up of
           22 girls, from 5th thru 8th grades and coached by Britney Carimi.


V.       APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM THE APRIL 5, 2021 SPECIAL
      TOWN COUNCIL MEETING, THE APRIL 13, 2021 TOWN COUNCIL
      MEETING, AND THE MARCH 2021 FINANCIAL STATEMENT.
          Mr. Chapman moved for approval; seconded by Mr. Santos. All
      present voted in favor.
