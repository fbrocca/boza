                 SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                Tuesday, December 13, 2022, 5:00 p.m.
                Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
           100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                 MINUTES

1.     Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m. Brenda Corley,
     Howard Chapman, Carl Ritchie, Gary Santos, Guang Ming Whitley,
     John Iacofano and Laura Hyatt were all present. Mr. Rambo was
     Absent.
2.     Public Comment
         Lynne Moore of 3567 Somerset Hills Court stated she moved
     here from North Carolina five years ago and opened up South Bay, a
     luxury continuing care retirement community, and subsequently
     opened her own senior care management firm. She stated she
     wanted to introduce herself as she has submitted her application to
     serve on the Recreation Advisory Commission.
3.     Executive Session
          Ms. Whitley moved to go into executive session for the reasons
     listed on the agenda, seconded by Mr. Chapman. Mayor Haynie read
     the items listed and called for the vote. All present voted in favor.
     The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:03 p.m.

4.Post Executive Session


       Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
       any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
       an executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
           Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:45 p.m. stating no
       votes or action had been taken.
           Ms. Hyatt moved that staff proceed on Faison Road as directed
       in executive session, seconded by Ms. Whitley. All present voted in
       favor.
          Mr. Chapman moved to reappoint Mr. Corey Barber and Ms.
       Anne Shaw to the Historic District Preservation Commission,
       seconded by Mr. Iacofano. All present voted in favor.
           Ms. Corley moved to appoint Ms. Lynne Moore to the
       Recreation Advisory Commission, seconded by Ms. Hyatt. All
       present voted in favor.
           Mr. Iacofano moved to reappoint Lisa Crowley to the
       Accommodations Tax Advisory Committee, seconded by Mr.
       Ritchie. All present voted in favor.
5.     Adjourn
     There being no further business, Mayor Haynie adjourned the meeting
