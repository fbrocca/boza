                   SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                    Tuesday, April 11, 2023, 5:00 p.m.
                  Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
             100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                   MINUTES

1.       Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m. Brenda Corley,
     Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Jake Rambo, John Iacofano and Carl
     Ritchie were all present. Mr. Santos arrived at 5:07 p.m. during the
     executive session. Ms. Whitley was absent.
2.       Public Comment
           Mr. Howard Chapman, 824 Natchez Circle stated Isle of Palms
     Council Member Jan Anderson asked him to pass along her sincere ,
     personal appreciation for the police and public safety assistance during
     the incident on Good Friday.
3.       Executive Session
          Mr. Chapman moved to go into executive session for the reasons
     stated on the agenda, seconded by Mr. Ritchie. All present voted in favor.
     The motion carried 7-0.
         The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:02 p.m.
       a. Personnel
          (1) Consideration of applications to serve on the Planning
              Commission
          (2) Consideration of applications to serve on the Culture, Arts and
              Pride Commission
          (3) Consideration of applications to serve on the Historic District
              Preservation Commission
          (4) Consideration of applications to serve on the Historical
              Commission
4. Post Executive Session
     Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of any
     section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in an
     executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
         Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:56 p.m.
         Ms. Corley moved to reappoint William Smyth and appoint new
     member Michelle Mansour to the Planning Commission, seconded by Mr.
     Rambo. All present voted in favor. The motion carried 8-0.
            Mr. Chapman moved to reappoint Clyde Hopper, Deborah
     Sutherland, Bryan Blalock and Courtney Theis to the Historical
     Commission, seconded by Mr. Rambo. All present voted in favor. The
     motion carried 8-0.
          Mr. Rambo moved to appoint Chase Barrett to the Culture, Arts and
     Pride Commission, seconded by Ms. Hyatt. All present voted in favor.
     The motion carried 8-0.
         Mr. Iacofano moved to appoint Christian Hayes to the Historic District
     Preservation Commission, seconded by Mr. Ritchie. All present voted in
     favor. The motion carried 8-0.
5.       Adjourn
     There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 5:58
     p.m.
     Respectfully submitted,


     Christine Barrett
     Clerk of Council
     April 11, 2023
