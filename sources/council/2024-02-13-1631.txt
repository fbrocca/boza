                  SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                      Tuesday February 13, 2024
                              4:45 p.m.
                 Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
            100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                  MINUTES
1.       Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 4:45 p.m. Carl Ritchie, Gary
     Santos, John Iacofano, Jake Rambo, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman and
     Daniel Brownstein were all present. Guang Ming Whitley was absent.
2.       Public Comment
     There were no public comments.
3.       Executive Session
           Mr. Rambo moved to go into executive session for the reasons
     stated on the agenda, items 3 a through e, seconded by Mr. Chapman.
     All present voted in favor.
     The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 4:47 p.m.
       a. Discussion of and possible action on contractual items related
          to Town owned property on Faison Road
       b. Legal advice related to Shem Creek Development Group, LLC
          v. the Town of Mount Pleasant, Case no. 2020-001387
       c. Consideration of applications to serve on the Board of Zoning
          Appeals
       d. Consideration of applications to serve on the Planning
          Commission
       e. Consideration of applications to serve on the Commercial
          Design Review Board
4. Post Executive Session
     Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
     any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
     an executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
          Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:50 p.m. stating no votes
     or action had been taken.
          Mr. Chapman moved to request staff to come up with a concept to
     determine the best plan to move forward on the Faison Road property
     as discussed in executive session, seconded by Ms. Hyatt. All present
     voted in favor.
          Mr. Brownstein moved to reappoint Aaron Butner and Elliott Barrow
     to the Board of Zoning Appeals, seconded by Mr. Rambo. All present
     voted in favor.
        Ms. Hyatt moved to appoint Mike Tinkey to the Planning
     Commission, seconded by Mr. Ritchie. All present voted in favor.
          Mr. Iacofano moved to reappoint Richard Perez to the Commercial
     Design Review Board, seconded by Mr. Rambo. All present voted in
     favor.
5.      Presentation of Public Services Department accreditation by the
     American Public Works Association
     Mr. Weis presented the accreditation award from the American Public
     Works Association and thanked Mayor and Council for their support.




6.       Recognition of Oceanside Girls Tennis Champions
     Ms. Hyatt presented the Oceanside Girls Tennis Champions to Mayor and
     Council.
