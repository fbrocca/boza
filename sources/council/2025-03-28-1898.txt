1. Call to Order
   Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. Daniel
   Brownstein, Howard Chapman, Guang Ming Whitley, Jake Rambo,
   John Iacofano and Gary Santos were all present. Laura Hyatt was
   absent.
2. Public Comment
   There were no public comments.
3. Adoption: A Resolution declaring Special Election results of
   March 25, 2025 (R.25032 FINAL)
   Mr. Brownstein moved to adopt, seconded by Mr. Rambo. All
   present voted in favor.
   This Resolution SIGNED, SEALED and DELIVERED this date.
4. Administration of Oath of Office to Council Member-Elect Mike
   Tinkey
   Mayor Haynie administered the Oath of Office to Council Member-
   Elect Mike Tinkey. Mayor and Council congratulated Mr. Tinkey
   and welcomed him as a Council Member.
5. Adjourn
  There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at
  1:05 p.m.
  Respectfully submitted,


  Christine Barrett
