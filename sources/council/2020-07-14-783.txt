          Josh Dix, 640 Rutledge Avenue, Charleston, stated that he is
     the Government Affairs Director for the Charleston Trident
     Association of Realtors. He stated that this would allow
     commercial operators coming into the Town to promote small
     business. He said this reduction would allow them to recruit and
     retain small businesses coming to Town instead of losing them to

VI.       PUBLIC COMMENT
            Jaqueline Brown, 1805 Snowden Road, stated that they have
      been fighting to connect to public sewer and have been doing so for a
      long time. She said the community does not understand why it is so
      difficult for them to connect. She said her house is near where Mount
      Pleasant Waterworks will connect, and she is unable to connect. She
      said the first phase was supposed to be the entire community and now
      they are required to annex into the Town.
          John Evans stated that he would like to thank and recognize our
      Public Services Director, Jody Peele, and his department. He stated
      that Mr. Peele and his department have always responded to the
      Town’s needs.
          Merrilee Waters stated that she is the President of the Snowden
      Community Civic Association. She stated that Town Council has the
      power to do the right thing and make sure that something that should
      have been completed a long time ago, gets completed. She stated that
      she received sewer during the first phase and was not required to
      annex. Now a small majority of the community is being required to
      annex. She is asking for Council to do the right thing.
         David Humphries stated that he is with the law firm of Haynsworth,
      Sinkler and Boyd. He represents the enclave of QC, LLC, and they are
