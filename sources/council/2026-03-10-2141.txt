1. Call to Order
  Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m. Council Members
  Daniel Brownstein, Kathryn Whitaker, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman,
  Jake Rambo, Mike Tinkey and Alex Crosby were all present. Council
  Member John Iacofano was absent.
2. Public Comment
  [None]
3. Executive Session
  Council Member Chapman moved to go into executive session for the
  reasons stated by Mayor Haynie and listed on the agenda, seconded by
  Council Member Crosby. All present voted in favor.
  The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:02 p.m.
    A. Legal and Contractual
                   Consideration and possible action regarding an Economic
                   Development incentive package
    B. Personnel
              (1) Consideration of applications to serve on the Commercial
                  Design Review Board (1 Vacancy)
              (2) Consideration of applications to serve on the
                  Accommodations Tax Advisory Committee (1 Vacancy)
4. Post Executive Session
  Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
  any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
  executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
  Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:57 p.m. stating no votes or
  action had been taken.
  Council Member Hyatt moved to approve the economic development
  incentive package based on the terms discussed in executive session,
  seconded by Council Member Chapman. Council Member Rambo voted
  opposed with all others present voting in favor. The motion carried with a
  7-1 vote.
  Council Member Chapman moved to appoint Taylor Craig Porter to the
  Commercial Design Review Board, seconded by Council Member
  Brownstein. All present voted in favor.
  Council Member Tinkey moved to reappoint Livia Thomas to the
  Accommodations Tax Advisory Committee, seconded by Council Member
  Crosby. All present voted in favor.
5. Adjourn
  There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 5:59 p.m.
  Respectfully submitted,


  Christine Barrett
  Clerk of Council
  March 10, 2026
