                  SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                  Tuesday, October 10, 2023, 5:00 p.m.
                 Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
            100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                  MINUTES

1.       Call to Order
     Mayor Pro Tempore Gary Santos called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m.
     Brenda Corley, Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Jake Rambo, John
     Iacofano, Guang Ming Whitley and Carl Ritchie were all present. Mayor
     Haynie was absent.
2.       Public Comment
     There were no public comments.
3.       Recognition of Town of Mount Pleasant Aquatics Staff
     Mayor Pro Tempore Santos and Steve Gergick recognized Lifeguards
     Nicholas Pessillo, Michael Hamlin, Geoffrey Dreyer, Lucas Herold, and
     Swim Team Coach Paul Curran for their quick thinking and lifesaving
     efforts to a Mount Pleasant resident in distress.
4.       Executive Session
           Mr. Rambo moved to go into executive session to discuss the items
     listed on the agenda and read by Mayor Pro Tempore Santos, seconded
     by Ms. Whitley. All present voted in favor.
     The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:10 p.m.
      a. Legal and Contractual

          (1) Legal advice on Liberty Hill Farms Development Agreement
              (DA) Amendment. Request to amend the current
              Development Agreement for five years and to amend the
              development schedule

          (2) Legal advice and possible action on pending litigation entitled
              CKC Properties, LLC vs. Town of Mount Pleasant, et al (2021-
              CP-10-4416 and 2021-CP-10-5211)

      b. Personnel
               Consideration of applications to serve on the Planning
               Commission
5. Post Executive Session

     Committee may take action on any item, including any subsection of any
     section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in an
     executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
          Mayor Pro Tempore Santos reconvened the meeting at 5:57 p.m.
     Mr. Pagliarini stated we are out of executive session and no votes were
     taken.
         Ms. Whitley moved that the Town file a Notice of Appeal on the
     pending litigation entitled CKC Properties, LLC vs. Town of Mount
     Pleasant, seconded by Mr. Rambo. All present voted in favor.
          Mr. Chapman moved to appoint Michael Adam Ferrara to the
     Planning Commission, seconded by Ms. Whitley. All present voted in
     favor.
