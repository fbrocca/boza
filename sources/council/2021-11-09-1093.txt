   STAFF SUMMARY

   Stormwater System Annual Inspection Reporting

   • Part of the Town's MS4 Permit obligations is to ensure that permitted stormwater
     systems, once constructed, are properly maintained and functional.

   • Owners of stormwater systems permitted and constructed after 2007
     are required to submit an Annual Inspection Report to the townshowing
     the functionality of theirBMPs. (153.050)

   • Currently, the ordinance is written so that only licensed professional engineers can
     perform this inspection.

   • Over 143 annual inspections are currentlymore than one year overdue. There are over
     80 structures coming due for their post construction inspection in the next year.

                  Ms. Corley stated the Human Resources Committee met
             to review and consider wage increases for part-time employees
             following the recent implementation of the Classification and
             Compensation Study for full-time employees. The proposed

             competing with the Town for the same pool of candidates for
             part-time employees. As with the full-time wage increases, the

             an affordable compensation plan that allows us to be
