              TOWN OF MOUNT PLEASANT, SOUTH CAROLINA
                    MOUNT PLEASANT TOWN COUNCIL
                         Special Council Meeting
                   Monday, December 2, 2019, 12:30 p.m.
           Council Chambers, Mount Pleasant Municipal Complex
             100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                   MINUTES

I.         Call to Order
       Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 12:34 p.m. Ms. Guang
       Ming Whitley, Ms. Brenda Corley, Ms. Laura Hyatt, Ms. Kathy Landing,
       Mr. Tom O’Rourke, Mr. Jake Rambo, Mr. Howard Chapman and Mr.
       Gary Santos were all present.


II.    Public Comment
       There were no public comments.


III.   Overview of active threat
            Mr. O’Rourke moved to go into executive session, seconded by Mr.
       Chapman. Mayor Haynie stated the reason for executive session is to
       receive a review of building security and evacuation procedures in the
       event of an active threat. Mr. O’Rourke and Mr. Chapman concurred.
       All present voted in favor.


IV.    Executive Session for review of building security and evacuation
       procedures
           The meeting was adjourned to executive session at 12:35 p.m.


V.     Post Executive Session
