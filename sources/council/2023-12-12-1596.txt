                   SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                      Tuesday, December 12, 2023
                               5:00 p.m.
                  Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
             100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                    MINUTES


1.        Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m. Daniel Brownstein,
     Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Guang Ming Whitley, Jake Rambo, John
     Iacofano, Gary Santos and Carl Ritchie were all present.
2.        Public Comment
          Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning Street stated he would like to recommend
     that Council consider three applicants for the Historic District Preservation
     Commission, Meghan Gravitt, Miles Martschink, and Warren Player. He
     said he thought they would all be excellent commission members. He
     also thanked Robert Crosland and Gene Gallogly for their service and
     said that Mr. Corey Barber was doing an excellent job on the commission.
          Denise Fugo, Housing for All Mount Pleasant, wished Mayor and
     Council happy holidays and said she wanted to be sure they are aware of
     Housing for All Mount Pleasant’s full support for the thirty affordable
     homes to be built on Faison Road. She said they were available to assist
     in any way.
3.        Executive Session
        Personnel
            Consideration of applications to serve on the Historic District
            Preservation Commission, the Commercial Design Review
            Board, and the Construction Board of Adjustments
                  Mr. Rambo moved to go into executive session to discuss
            applications to serve on the Historic District Preservation
            Commission, the Commercial Design Review Board and the
            Construction Board of Adjustments, seconded by Mr. Chapman.
            All present voted in favor.
           The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:03 p.m.
4.       Post Executive Session
     Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of any
     section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in an
     executive session during a properly notice meeting.
     Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:30 p.m. stating no votes
     were cast or actions taken.
         Mr. Rambo moved to appoint John Ratliff to the Construction Board
     of Adjustments, seconded by Mr. Chapman. All present voted in favor.
        Mr. Chapman moved to appoint to the Historic District Preservation
     Commission James Bakker and Meghan Gravitt, and to reappoint
     Warren Player, seconded by Mr. Brownstein. All present voted in favor.
         Mr. Iacofano moved to appoint Matt Senecal to the Commercial
     Design Review Board, seconded by Mr. Ritchie. All present voted in
     favor.
