III.        Review of Town operational objectives
        Mayor Haynie stated that he has asked staff to update Council on the
        Town’s operational objectives.
        Mr. DeMoura stated that over three weeks ago, and at the onset of
        this crisis, staff has put into place several operational objectives
        which has been amended one time. He stated that the operational
        objectives are first, an emphasis on ensuring continuity of community
        lifelines and critical services. He stated that this does not mean only
        Town of Mount Pleasant organizational services such as police, fire
        and sanitation. He said it also means working to ensure continuity of
        services for healthcare facilities and utility providers. He said the
        second operation objective is to reduce the risk of exposure to public
        workers, which means the police officers, firefighters and sanitation
        workers, understanding that their health is critical to make sure that
the previously mentioned critical services may be maintained and
continued. He said the third operational objective is to reduce the risk
of exposure to the community to try and protect the community as
best as possible at the local government level. He said Council has
seen this for many weeks now and was exemplified through the
closing of many Town events and through limitations of the size of
gatherings. He said lastly and the newest added operational objective
is for the Town to mitigate the economic impact to both this
organization (Town of Mount Pleasant), and through the community.
He said internally, Council has received communications on this and
have discussed what is being done internally to reduce costs and
stabilize revenue. He stated that Council is aware of the hiring freeze
that is currently in place. The Town is also taking steps to try and
mitigate the loss outside of the Town, community-wide, as best as
possible. He said this is seen through the promotional planning of
businesses, Help Biz Stay Buy Another Way and is also seen on the
Town’s website and through other resource portals. He stated that
these are the four operational objectives. He stated that he does not
wish to miss this opportunity to mention how well the Town staff has
performed from the very beginning. He said staff is committed and he
is so proud of the work they have all done.
Mayor Haynie stated that staff has handled this very well and to date,
is not aware of any interruption of services.
Mr. DeMoura stated that due to this unprecedented event, staff has
had to put into place several things that were originally aimed at the
critical services, which have been maintained since the first day. He
stated that staff wanted to maintain services in order to keep the
community moving. He said that this has gone very well. He stated
that the tele-working portion for staff has been going very well. He
stated that once this crisis has ended, staff will come together as a
team and assess what technological needs will be required in the
future. He said Mayor and Council may see at some point ways that
teleworking may be improved.
Mayor Haynie asked Mr. DeMoura to provide information to Council
on what the first responders are doing to minimize risk of exposure.
Mr. DeMoura stated that they have communicated and trained on
personal protection protocols and this has been widely used within all
of Town operations, especially with first responders. He said how to
sanitize properly, stay safely apart, how to interact with the public,

      in tight quarters such as a fire station. He said those have all gone

VII.       Discussion and possible action to prohibit the enactment of
       any additional restrictions under Town of Mount Pleasant Code
       of Ordinances, Section 41, Civil Emergencies, unless approved
       by Council


                                                                        Page 8 of 12
      Mr. Rambo stated that he requested for this item to be added to
the agenda. He stated that he supports Mayor Haynie and admires
his work and leadership and knows how hard he is working for the
Town. He said given this, there is a right and a wrong way to do
things. He said the Town attorney sent a detailed opinion on whether
a municipality has the authority to issue a stay-at-home order. He
said at that time, it was a gray area, but what the attorneys were very
clear on is that a stay-at-home should be issued by an act of Council.
He said it serves to more strongly protect the Town from any potential
litigation or damage claims as a result of such an order. He said
much to the shock of some Council members, yesterday morning,
Mayor Haynie posted on Facebook that he was having the same
legal department draw up documents to issue a stay-at-home order.
He said that action is what prompted this agenda item from a group of
Council members. He said this item has nothing to do with any
Council member attempting a “power grab” but has everything to do
with checks and balances that exist in good government. He said
there is one thing that he has heard very clearly from a majority of
Council members. If they are going to go against the opinions of both
our own lawyers and now the Attorney General of South Carolina,
putting the Town in a potentially libel and litigious situation, that this is
a decision that should be made by all Council members. He added
that he has received a great deal of feedback from residents that
worry about the potential of the Mayor not having the ability to act
quickly as this situation progresses. He agrees wholeheartedly with
this, which is why these emergency power exist to begin with.
Furthermore, he has absolutely zero desire to sit here and play
politics so he will not be making the motion on this item. He said we
are all united on one thing, we want to do what is best for our people,
our neighbors, our friends, our family. We all have a responsibility to
protect this community. He addressed Mayor Haynie and stated that
he knows the Mayor has the best intentions in the world and loves the
Town and its people and knows he is doing this for all the right
reasons, but we have to do this the right way.
