                 Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
                        100 Ann Edwards Lane
                      Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                 MINUTES



I.        Call to Order
      Mayor Will Haynie called the meeting to order at 12:32 p.m.
      Council Members: Brenda Corley, Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt,
      Kathy Landing, Tom O’Rourke, Jake Rambo, Gary Santos, and
      Guang Ming Whitley were all present.


II.       Public Comment
      Michael German, 1707 Snowden Road, stated that he lives in the
      Snowden Community which is not in the Town of Mount Pleasant. He
      is here once again, as he has been previously. He will continue to be
      present at Council meetings until his community receives some
      resolution. He stated that they gave the Town of Mount Pleasant and
      Mount Pleasant Waterworks rights of ways to run sewer lines across
      their properties. However, they are still without sewer connection. He
      lives in the County of Charleston and chose not to annex into the
      Town for various reasons. He stated that their community is never
      included in discussions and they never know what is going on in the
      Town. He said a group of people should not be making decisions
      about their settlement community of Snowden without any of them
      being included. He said they only want an opportunity for quality of
      life. He said in this day and time, this is deplorable and hopes we can
      all do better in the future, because it is not a complicated process.
      Wendy Bauerschmidt, 1320 Woodlake Court, stated that she would
      like to express her appreciation and gratitude for the work that
      Council has done during this difficult period, as well as Town staff.
      She said that this pandemic allows Town Council members the time
      to review the comprehensive plan. She asked Council to keep in mind
      the real commitment to provide workforce housing.
III.      Review, discussion and possible action regarding the
       Comprehensive Plan and timeline to proceed with first reading
           Jeff Ulma, Director of Planning, stated that he and Liz Boyles will
       be providing a brief refresher presentation, focusing in on the primary
       issues that Council would like to address. He said the Planning
       Department’s link to the Town website has been tracking the process
       the entire time which the citizens may view. He said the last
       document prepared was the Planning Commission’s
       recommendation. He said staff also took Council’s feedback from the
       Council Retreat and other meetings and hope they have captured the
       essence of the concerns and made those adjustments. He said Ms.
       Liz Boyles, will review the plan in greater detail with Council.
