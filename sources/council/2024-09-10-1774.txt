avenue to promote the historic value of Patjen’s Post Office which was
built in 1899, and was the first freestanding post office in Mount
Pleasant. On October 13, 2023, Alhambra Representatives met with
Town staff members where Alhambra was advised by James Ward
that the Town believed it owned Patjen’s Post Office. Mr. Ward advised

evidence. She said they proceeded to compile such evidence and
found historical documents and information, including the Town
Council minutes of June 7, 1971, evidencing the Town of Mount
Pleasant, provided permission to Alhambra Garden Club to place
Patjen’s Post Office in its present location. Ms. Scott said their

Pagliarini. She said Alhambra, with the help of Town Council Member

           commission.

vote.
