1.   Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 11:30 a.m. Howard
     Chapman, Jake Rambo, John Iacofano and Mike Tinkey were all
     present.
2.   Public Comment
     [None]
3.   Adoption: A Resolution declaring Election results of November 4,
     2025. (R.25111) (R.25111 FINAL)
     Mr. Rambo moved for adoption, seconded by Mr. Tinkey.
     Mayor Haynie stated that after the provisional and fail-safe ballots
     were addressed, the election numbers have changed from what was
     printed in the Resolution. Mr. Pagliarini stated that it is correct and
     we will need an amendment to reflect the correct numbers. He stated
     the document that reflects the correct numbers is Exhibit A, page 3 of
     6, at the top right, it says Official Results, and at the bottom Election
     Summary, 11/5/2025 at 10:31 a.m., and page 2 of 7.
     Mr. Iacofano moved to amend the resolution to reflect Exhibit A Page 3
     of 6, Official Results from 11/6/2025 at 10:31 a.m., seconded by Mr.
     Chapman.
     Mayor Haynie says this changes the numbers, but it does not change
     the outcome or order of finish. Mr. Pagliarini said that is correct, and
     if this vote is favorable, the resolution will be changed and executed to
     reflect the correct numbers. Mayor Haynie said we have every
     confidence that these numbers came from the Charleston County
     Board of Voter Registration and Elections and Mr. Pagliarini responded
     in the affirmative.
     Mr. Chapman said the numbers on Exhibit A on page 3 of 6 are what
     came from the recount.
     Mayor Haynie called for the vote on the amendment and all present
     voted in favor.
     Mayor Haynie called for the vote on the amended resolution and all
     present voted in favor.
     This Resolution SIGNED, SEALED and DELIVERED this date.
4.   Adjourn
     Mayor Haynie congratulated the winners of the election and with no
     further business to come before Council, adjourned the meeting at
     11:34 a.m.
     Respectfully submitted,


     Christine Barrett
     Clerk of Council
     November 7, 2025
