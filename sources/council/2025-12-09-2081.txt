1. Call to Order
  Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:01 p.m. Council Members
  Daniel Brownstein, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman, Jake Rambo, Mike
  Tinkey, Alex Crosby and John Iacofano were all present. Kathryn
  Whitaker was absent at call to order and arrived during Executive
  Session.
2. Public Comment
  [None]
3. Executive Session
    A. Legal and Contractual

              (1) Discussion and possible action regarding an Economic
                  Development incentive package
              (2) Legal advice pertaining to Shem Creek Development, Inc.
                  v. Town of Mount Pleasant
              (3) Legal advice pertaining to Deierlein et al v. Town of
                  Mount Pleasant
    B. Personnel

              (1) Consideration of applications to serve on the Planning
                  Commission (1 Vacancy)
              (2) Consideration of applications to serve on the Culture, Arts
                  and Pride Committee (1 Vacancy)
      Council Member Iacofano stated he would be recusing himself from
      item 3.A.(2).
       Council Member Chapman moved to go into executive session for the
       reasons stated by the Mayor and listed on the agenda. Council
       Member Rambo seconded the motion. All present voted in favor.
       The meeting was adjourned to Executive session at 5:03 p.m.
       Council Member Whitaker arrived during Executive Session.
4. Post Executive Session
   Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
   any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
   executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
   Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 6:02 p.m. stating no votes or
   action had been taken.
   Council Member Iacofano stated in regards to Item 3.A.(1), he would like
   to move to negotiate terms as presented in Executive Session, seconded
   by Council Member Brownstein.
   Council Member Rambo stated this is to give tax incentives to develop an
   undeveloped property off of Wingo Way and he is against it for a number
   of reasons. He stated the core function of government is public safety
   and public recreation, not to line the pockets of developers. He said this
   goes against what we are elected to do as this will knock down trees and
   clear a property currently in a natural state, and it gives tax incentives to
   do it, and for those reasons he will be a no vote.
   Mayor Haynie called for the vote. Council Member Rambo voted
   opposed with all others present voting in favor. The motion carried with
   an 8-1 vote.
   Council Member Hyatt moved to appoint Ashley Richardson to the
   Culture, Arts and Pride Commission, seconded by Council Member
   Iacofano. All present voted in favor and the motion carried.
5. Adjourn
   There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 6:05 p.m.
   Respectfully submitted,


   Christine Barrett
