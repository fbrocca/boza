IV.          PUBLIC HEARINGS, AWARDS & PRESENTATIONS


         A. Public Hearing: A Public Hearing to receive input on a proposed
            Sixth Amendment to the Development Agreement by and between
            CDM of Charleston, LLC and the Town of Mount Pleasant, South
            Carolina. The proposed amendment is to Exhibit C, Carolina Park
Land Use Table, to allow Communication Towers as a Conditional
Use in the Carolina Park-Active Park (CPAP) District. The use is
currently only allowed in the Carolina Park-Industrial (CPI) District.
Carolina Park is comprised of approximately 1,608.50 acres of
land and whose location is generally described as being on the
north side of US Highway 17 between Park West and Pepper
Plantation developments.
