    Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 6:08 p.m. Brenda Corley,
Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Gary Santos, Jake Rambo, John Iacofano
and Guang Ming Whitley were all present. Carl Ritchie was absent.
I.         PRAYER
           Mr. Eric DeMoura gave the Invocation.
II.    PLEDGE
       Mayor Pro Tem Gary Santos led the Pledge of Allegiance.
III.       APPROVAL OF AGENDA
            Mr. Chapman moved to approve the agenda, seconded by Ms.
       Corley. All present voted in favor. The motion carried 8-0.
IV.        PUBLIC HEARINGS, AWARDS & PRESENTATIONS
       Mayor Haynie stated he and a delegation of representatives had just
       returned from Denver, Colorado where they competed and won the
       All-America City designation for the third time. He thanked everyone
       for their support and congratulated the delegation on the hard work
       that went into a successful presentation.
V.          APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM THE MAY 9, 2023 REGULAR
        AND SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETINGS, AND THE APRIL 2023
        FINANCIAL STATEMENT.
             Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes and financial
        statement, seconded by Ms. Corley. All present voted in favor. The
        motion carried 8-0.
VI.            PUBLIC COMMENT
             Joe Elmore with the Charleston Animal Society stated they will
        be celebrating their 150th anniversary later this year and he wanted to
        thank the Town for their generous partnership.
             George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Road, stated the Community
        Action Group for Encouragement has petitioned the County to build a
        sidewalk on Old Georgetown Road, but they are only able to complete
        a small portion. He asked the Town to work with the County to
        complete the entire sidewalk.
             Cheryl Shoun, 205 King Street stated she is here on behalf of
        her client for the rezoning of property on Bowman Road and Hospital
        Drive. She said they have been working diligently with staff on this
        request, and the seller acknowledges its legal and contractual
        obligations and intends to comply. She said her client has no intent to
        donate the property and she urged Council to pass the rezoning
        request on the agenda for final reading this evening.
             Mary Martenich, 501 Wando Park Boulevard stated she is with
        Seamon Whiteside representing St. Peter’s Church for the annexation
        and rezoning of property and she is available for any questions.
             Walter Smalls, 1248 Bracey Court stated he is the Trustee Pro
        Tem at Olive Branch AME Church and they are requesting a favorable
        vote for the rezoning of property and amendment of the Johnson Tract
        development.

        There are no Consent Agenda items.
