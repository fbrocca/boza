IV.          PUBLIC HEARINGS, AWARDS & PRESENTATIONS
       A.         PUBLIC HEARING: To receive comments on a proposed
            Amendment to Development Agreement By and Between Carolina
            Park Development LLC and Town of Mount Pleasant, South
            Carolina. Proposed is to amend Exhibit C to the Development
            Agreement, Governing Regulations, entitled “Planned
            Development District Development Standards: Amending the PD
            District for Carolina Park”, to create a new mixed-use district called
            TOMP Tract, including permitted uses and development
            standards, on an approximately 38.3-acre tract of land located off
            Faison Road and owned by the Town of Mount Pleasant. This
            tract will include up to 30 attainable housing units, increasing the
            total dwelling units for Carolina Park from 2,030 to 2,060.
            Further amendments include the following:
       A. Transfer the ownership of Ben Marino Way to the town;
       B. Release use restriction to permit cell tower use on town
          active park; and
       C. Extend the Development Agreement for two (2) years.
     Carolina Park is comprised of approximately 1,608.50 acres of
     land and whose location is generally described as being on the
     north side of US Highway 17 between the Park West and Pepper
     Plantation developments.
     There being no speakers, Mayor Haynie closed the public hearing.
B.        Public Hearing: A Public Hearing to receive input on a
     proposal to amend the Town of Mount Pleasant Code of
     Ordinances, Chapter 152, Flood Damage Prevention, and Chapter
     155, Land Development Regulations, to establish new land
     development standards.
     The proposal is to amend Chapters 152 and 155 of the Mount
     Pleasant Code of Ordinances, pertaining to newly established land
     development standards to encourage building and development
     practices for subdivisions of land, and for construction of new
     homes. Specifically proposed is to establish in the Land
     Development Regulations a 10’ setback from the perimeter of the
     subdivision that would prohibit fill and grading, that could cast
     stormwater on to adjacent properties. Also proposed is an
     amendment to the Flood Damage Prevention regulations that
     would modify Building Practices in Flood Zones and Low-Lying
     Areas, prohibiting the construction of slab on grade type of
     foundations for new residential construction.
     Ms. Reed reviewed the public hearing.
          David Quick, 1502 Village Square stated he is here to speak
     in favor of the changes and urged Council’s support.
         Ray Hobbs, 2505 Charter Oaks Drive, stated he is here to
     urge Council to vote in favor of the amendment.
     There being no other speakers, Mayor Haynie declared the public
     hearing closed.
