                  SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                  Tuesday, January 10, 2023, 5:00 p.m.
                 Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
            100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                  MINUTES

1.     Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m. Brenda Corley,
     Laura Hyatt, Jake Rambo, Carl Ritchie, John Iacofano, Gary Santos,
     Guang Ming Whitley and Howard Chapman were all present.
2.     Public Comment
     Warren Player, 10 Pirates Cruz at the corner of Middle Street stated
     he is here to voice additional comments that all of the citizens in that
     area of the Old Village have made about Mr. Thomas lot and what he
     wants to do with his house. He said he served on the Old Village
     Historic Commission for several terms, as well as a variety of other
     committees over the years. He asked if Council does not back the
     commissions, then why have them. He asked them to back the
     decisions of the various committees.
3.     Executive Session
     Mr. Rambo moved to go into executive session to discuss the items
     listed on the agenda, seconded by Mr. Santos. All present voted in
     favor.
     The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:02 p.m.

4.Post Executive Session
       Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
       any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
       an executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
           Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:57 p.m.
          Mr. Chapman moved to reappoint Elissa Morrison and Richard
       Lambert to the Commercial Design Review Board, seconded by
       Ms. Hyatt. All present voted in favor.
           Ms. Corley moved to direct the Town Administrator to negotiate
       as discussed in executive session in regard to a potential lease of
       property in support of community arts, seconded by Ms. Whitley.
       All present voted in favor.
           Ms. Whitley moved to proceed as discussed in executive
       session with regards to pending litigation Craig Thomas vs. the
       Town of Mount Pleasant, seconded by Mr. Ritchie. All present
       voted in favor.
