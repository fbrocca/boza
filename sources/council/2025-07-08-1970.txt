                 MOUNT PLEASANT TOWN COUNCIL
                       Special Council Meeting
                    Tuesday, July 8, 2025, 5:00 PM
                         Council Chambers
                  Mount Pleasant Municipal Complex
           100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                  MINUTES

                         Listen to the recording here.


1. Call to Order
  Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m. Daniel Brownstein,
  Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Jake Rambo, John Iacofano, Gary
  Santos and Mike Tinkey were all present. Guang Ming Whitley was
  absent.
2. Public Comment
  Michelle Stress stated she would like to thank Council for listening to the
  residents and establishing the Tree Task Force.
3. Executive Session
  Councilman Chapman made a motion to go into executive session for the
  reasons stated on the agenda and read by Mayor Haynie, seconded by
  Councilman Iacofano. The motion carried unanimously and the meeting
  was adjourned into executive session at 5:03 p.m.
       A. Legal & Contractual Items
         Discussion and possible action on pending litigation captioned
         James N. Deierlein, Jr., et al. vs. the Commission of Public Works
         for the Town of Mount Pleasant, et al. (2023-CP-10-01688)
       B. Personnel
               (1)    Consideration of applications to serve on the Board of
                      Zoning Appeals (1 vacancy)
               (2)    Consideration of applications to serve on the Historic
                      District Preservation Commission (1 vacancy)
               (3)    Consideration of applications to serve on the Culture,
                      Arts & Pride Commission (1 vacancy)
                (4)   Consideration of applications to serve on the
                      Commercial Design Review Board (2 vacancies)
                (5)   Consideration of applications to serve on the newly
                      established Tree Task Force (7 vacancies)


4. Post Executive Session
   Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
   any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
   executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
   Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:44 p.m. stating no votes or
   action were taken.
   Councilman Rambo moved to direct the Town Administrator to proceed
   as discussed in executive session in the Deierline item, seconded by
   Councilman Santos. The motion carried unanimously.
   Councilman Iacofano moved to appoint Cheryll Woods Flowers to serve
   on the Board of Zoning Appeals, seconded by Councilwoman Hyatt. The
   motion carried unanimously.
   Councilman Tinkey moved to appoint Krystine Edwards to the Historic
   District Preservation Commission, seconded by Councilman Brownstein.
   The motion carried unanimously.
   Councilwoman Hyatt moved to appoint Karen Smalls-Horlback to the
   Culture, Arts and Pride Commission, seconded by Councilman Tinkey.
   The motion carried unanimously.
   Councilman Chapman moved to reappoint Timothy Kiel and appoint
   Daniel Cruz to the Commercial Design Review Board, seconded by
   Councilman Iacofano. The motion carried unanimously.
   Councilman Rambo moved to appoint David Quick, Michelle Mansour,
   Shanna Harris, James McKee, Edward Oswald, Deidre Kennedy and
   George Grice to the Tree Task Force, seconded by Councilwoman Hyatt.
   Councilman Brownstein stated he will be voting no on this item, which is
   not a reflection on any one person, but he feels a more balanced group is
   needed.
   The motion carried with a 7-1 vote with Councilman Brownstein voting
   opposed with all others present in favor. The motion carried.
