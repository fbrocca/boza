      Mike VanHorn, 1204 Iron Bridge Drive, stated he would like to have
      revisited Ordinance 25008 from last month that Councilman
      Brownstein proposed for the Town to have three readings for
      ordinances versus the current two. He thanked Council for having
      the meetings streamed, however the sound is barely audible,
      especially from the podium microphone. Mr. VanHorn said in
      September of 2013, the Town made an Ordinance regarding texting
      while driving. He said he would like to see cell phone use while
      driving eliminated, with a hands-free or pay the fee campaign. He
      said there are too many accidents caused by people on the phone or
      not paying attention. Mr. VanHorn said he would like the Town to host


                                                                                                               Page 2 of 22
a candidate forum to give a chance for the Council Members and
residents an opportunity to meet the candidates.
Michael Winter,1486 Indian Street said he would like to discuss
Ordinance 25008 which was to allow for amendments at first reading.
He said the idea as he understood it was to give the public a clearer
picture of what Council would be voting on at second reading, and
another chance to voice their opinion. He said he appreciates the
intent behind this change, he believes it could lead to rushed
amendments made in the moment, only to be reconsidered or
undone at the second reading. Mr. Winter said he thinks Council and
the public would benefit from more time to reflect before any
modifications are made to proposed ordinances. He said in addition,
under this proposed system, amendments can still be made at the
second reading, meaning the public would still not have clarity on
what will be voted on when they provide their input. He said if the
goal is to improve transparency, he suggests moving to a three-
reading format. He said the first reading would remain unchanged,
and at the next month’s meeting, the public could provide input before
any changes are made. He said at second reading, Council could
introduce amendments, with the final unmodified vote taking place at
third reading. He said this would allow for clearer decision-making
and ensure the public has an opportunity to respond to what is
actually being decided.
Allison Hunt, 40 Patriots Point Road stated she is here in her role as
the Executive Director of the Patriots Point Development Authority.
She said the Authority is in support of the Development Agreement
that Patriots Point Development Authority is part of for Patriots Annex,

economic impact and benefit of the Patriots Annex development to
the Town of Mount Pleasant will be substantial. She said the
Authority is supportive of all the efforts of Patriots Annex to move
forward. She thanked Council for their time.
