                                 MINUTES

1.       Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m. Daniel
     Brownstein, Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Guang Ming Whitley, Jake
     Rambo, John Iacofano, and Gary Santos were all present (Mr. Ritchie’s

     Tony Kohnle, 1415 Hamlin Park Circle, stated he wanted to introduce

3.       Executive Session
          Mr. Chapman moved to go into executive session for the reasons
     stated on the agenda, seconded by Ms. Hyatt. All present voted in
     favor.
     The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:02 p.m.
         Personnel
             Consideration of applications to serve on the Historic
             District Preservation Commission
         Legal and Contractual
             Discussion regarding contractual and proprietary matters
             related to the Harbor Entrepreneur Center
4.       Post Executive Session
     Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
     any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed in
     an executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
     Mayor Haynie reconvened the meeting at 5:31 p.m. stating no action or
     votes were taken. Ms. Whitley was not present when the meeting

           Mr. Chapman moved to appoint Mr. Foxworth and Mr. Craft to the
