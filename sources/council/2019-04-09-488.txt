aspects and agreed that the ordinance was now more appropriate
and reasonable. Key features include the following:
 An annual permit & fee
 Creates one process for all types of STRs (whole house, room(s)
  in house/now considered a Bed & Breakfast, ADU)
 A reduced safety inspection program which will only require an
  affidavit for meeting safety requirements (fire and building
  codes)
 A requirement for General Liability Insurance
 Requires business license for just one (1) unit rented (can now
  have up to four (4) without a business license)
 Establishes limits on number of STRs throughout the Town
  (Capped at 1 percent of all dwelling units in town)
 Prohibits use of a structure as a STR that does not meet the
  definition of a dwelling unit
 Prohibits STR activity within any zoning district or neighborhood
  that does not allow it (multi-family zoning district and HOA’s
  with restrictions)
 Establishes parking requirements (1 additional off-street space
  for every bedroom rented)
 “Grandfathering” provisions
Staff will prepare the ordinance for review by the Planning
Commission.
