1. Call To Order
Mayor Pro Tem Howard Chapman called the Special Town Council
Meeting to order at 5:00 PM. noting that Mayor Haynie was under the
weather but participating remotely via Teams. Mayor Pro Tem Chapman
confirmed audio connectivity with the Mayor before proceeding. Council
Members Daniel Brownstein, Kathryn Whitaker, Laura Hyatt, Jake
Rambo, Mike Tinkey, Alex Crosby and John Iacofano were all present.

2. Public Comment
Ray Hobbs addressed the Council. Mr. Hobbs expressed gratitude on
behalf of residents living near Porchers Creek for the town's efforts to
protect wetlands. He requested that as the Council considers wetland
ordinances, they include impact statements from Mount Pleasant
residents living adjacent to development projects.
Mr. Hobbs specifically referenced a current project where Crescent
Homes is attempting to develop the Leland Park subdivision on parcel
614-00-00-004. He explained that nine residents are adjacent to the
proposed roadway, which would be built five feet from marsh areas with
critical lines on one side and two wetland areas on the Jack Leland
Road access. Mr. Hobbs described this as a one-lane gravel road that
historically served only three residents until 1980, connecting the
Porcher family home to the old Georgetown Highway. He noted that only
526 feet of the road remains and serves as a private driveway for
parcels 006 and 007. Mr. Hobbs stated that Crescent Homes wants to
destroy this roadway and green space, which would impact six adjacent
homes and nineteen other homes on Charter Oaks Drive due to the
removal of the green space.
No other speakers came forward for public comment.
