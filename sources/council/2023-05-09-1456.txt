                                    MINUTES

1.           Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m. Brenda Corley,
     Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Gary Santos, Jake Rambo, John
     Iacofano, Guang Ming Whitley and Carl Ritchie were all present.
2.           Public Comment
          Howard Chapman, 824 Natchez Circle stated at the public hearing
     on 526 that was held in Council Chambers, he was asked to thank Mayor
     and Council for working with Grassy Creek and Tidal Walk on the issue
     of shifting the exit from the truck terminal. Ms. Angie Anderson specifically
     asked Mr. Chapman to thank Council. Mr. Chapman said on Friday of
     last week, he, Mayor Haynie and Council Member Ritchie attended an
     event by the African American Settlement Historic Commission where a
     tree was planted in memory of former Council Member Thomasena
     Stokes-Marshall. The African American Settlement Historic Commission
     presented to Mayor and Council a piece of a board that belonged to the
     Long Point School, as a token of appreciation for the assistance in moving
     the school house. Mr. Chapman would like the board to be placed in the
     History Room here at Town Hall.
3.           Recognition of Lacrosse State Champion Teams
