                  MOUNT PLEASANT TOWN COUNCIL
                     SPECIAL COUNCIL MEETING
                 Tuesday, September 15, 2020, 2:30 p.m.
                 Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
                        100 Ann Edwards Lane
                      Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                  MINUTES


I.    Call to Order
          Mayor Haynie called the Town Council Meeting to order at 2:30
      p.m.
      Council Members Howard Chapman, Brenda Corley, Laura Hyatt,
      Kathy Landing, Tom O’Rourke, Jake Rambo, Gary Santos and
      Guang Ming Whitley were present.
      Mayor Haynie called for a moment of silence to honor the loss of long
      time resident, Captain Wayne Magwood.


II.       Public Comment
          Carmen Nash, 1230 Gannet Road, Mount Pleasant, stated that
      she would like to recommend amending the proposed temporary
      ordinance to include potential risks of wearing masks.
          Rima Shretta, 123 Civitas Street, stated that she is an
      epidemiologist and works with the University of Oxford in
      collaboration with WHO (World Health Organization), on COVID-19.
      She worked with WHO before moving to Charleston. She asked that
      the public rely on the scientists to provide scientific data. She asked
      Council to reinstate the ordinance and extend it indefinitely until we,
      as a state, are well below the threshold of safe.
           Cynthia Nirenblatt, 516 Walden Street, Mount Pleasant, stated
      that COVID-19 cases and near related deaths are down because of
      mask wearing mandates. She asked Council to pass the mask
      ordinance mandate.
    Mike Noviella, 221 Bayfront Drive, Mount Pleasant, stated that at
the Town Council meeting on September 8th, both sides were heard.
He urged Town Council to vote “no” on the mask mandate.
     Olga Blandford 1742 Central Avenue, Sullivan’s Island, stated
that we are being manipulated and brainwashed.
     Adam LeRay, 1728 Battalion Drive, Charleston, stated children
know that masks make them sick. He said Council has a duty to
protect the children. He does not wear and mask and neither do his
children.
    Tim Kiel, 605 Pelzer Drive, Mount Pleasant, stated his wife is a
nurse and takes samples for COVID-19 and masks protect her
patients and the patients’ masks protect her, her mask protects him
and his mask protects those at this meeting. He said to pass the
mask mandate ordinance.
    Melissa Munsell, 2751 Christ Church Court, Mount Pleasant,
stated that she is the manager in the Emergency Department at
Roper Mount Pleasant Hospital and takes seriously the responsibility
she has for this community. From Friday, September 11th until today,
there was a surge in admissions and Roper experienced a 100%
increase in hospitalizations from COVID-19. She has worn a mask for
her 27 year career and has never been sick from wearing a mask.
She and Roper St. Francis support the Town in passing the mask
mandate ordinance.
    Jackie Klingenburg, Hogan’s Alley, Mount Pleasant, stated that
she does not believe it is the role of the government to provide
medical mandates. Nothing supporting that shows that masks work.
     Suzie Vahala, 2031 Prospect Hill Drive, asked if everyone would
like to choose a mask to keep everyone safe or be responsible for
choosing a casket for your friends and neighbors.
     Denis Blythe, 610 Pelzer Drive, Mount Pleasant, stated that
after the last Town Council meeting, he and many other speakers
were called out for not sanitizing and specifically for not sanitizing the
microphone. The virus is not transmitted from a microphone to him.
He did wear a mask the entire time. He chooses to do what he can to
help others to live and hopes they will do the same for him.

III.       Final Reading: A temporary Ordinance requiring persons to
       wear face coverings in certain circumstances in the Town of Mount
       Pleasant to reduce risk of exposure to COVID-19 during the public
       health emergency and recovery. (Ord. No. 20058)
          Ms. Whitley moved to approve; seconded by Mr. Chapman.
       Mr. Chapman, Ms. Hyatt, Mr. Rambo, Ms. Whitley and Mayor Haynie
       were in favor.
       Ms. Corley, Ms. Landing, Mr. O’Rourke and Mr. Santos were
       opposed.
       Motion to approve carries 5-4.
       This Ordinance SIGNED, SEALED and DELIVERED this date.
IV.        Adjourn
       There being no further business, meeting adjourned at 3:08 p.m.


Respectfully submitted,
Barbara Ashe MacDonald
