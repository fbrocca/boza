                                MINUTES

      *Meeting rescheduled from Tuesday, January 9 to Wednesday,

1.       Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m. and noted this
     meeting had been rescheduled from Tuesday, January 9 due to severe
     weather. Daniel Brownstein, Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Guang
     Ming Whitley, Jake Rambo, John Iacofano and Gary Santos were all
     present. Carl Ritchie was absent.
2.       Public Comment
     [None]
3.       Recognition of 12U Football State Champions
     Ms. Hyatt, on behalf of Mayor Haynie and Town Council introduced
     congratulated the Mount Pleasant Recreation Department 12 U football
     team for their state championship.
