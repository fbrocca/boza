                                 Minutes


I.    Call to Order

      Mayor Pro Tempore Santos called the meeting to order at 12:00 p.m.
      Mayor-Elect Haynie, Mr. Owens, Mr. Brimmer, Mr. Carrier and Mr.
      Bustos were all present. Mayor Page, Mr. Gawrych and Mr. Smith
      were absent.

II.   Adoption: A Resolution declaring election results of November 7,
      2017. (R.15112)

      Mr. Pagliarini stated the Resolution number on the agenda was
      incorrect. The correct number is 17111. He wanted to state that for
      the record.

      Mr. DeMoura said the election results were attached as an Exhibit to
      the Resolution and the action needed would be a motion to adopt.

      Mr. Bustos moved to adopt Resolution 17111, seconded by Mr. Owens.
      All present voted in favor.

      Mr. DeMoura read the RESOLUTION BY TITLE ONLY. This Resolution
      SIGNED, SEALED and DELIVERED this date.
