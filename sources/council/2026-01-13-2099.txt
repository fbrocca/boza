1. Call to Order
The special meeting of the Mount Pleasant Town Council was called to
order by Mayor Haynie on Tuesday, January 13, 2026, at 5:00 PM.
Council Members Daniel Brownstein, Kathryn Whitaker, Laura Hyatt,
Howard Chapman, Jake Rambo. Mike Tinkey, Alex Crosby and John
Iacofano were all present.
2. Public Comment
There were no speakers for public comment.
3. Executive Session
  A. Legal and Contractual
             (1) Discussion and possible action regarding an Economic
                 Development incentive package
             (2) Discussion of a contractual matter related to the Gary
                 Santos Recreation Park
  B. Personnel
             (1) Consideration of applications to serve on the Planning
                 Commission (1 Vacancy)
             (2) Consideration of applications to serve on the Historic
                 District Preservation Commission (2 Vacancies)
             (3) Consideration of applications to serve on the Board of
                 Zoning Appeals (1 Vacancy)
Council Member Rambo moved to go into executive session for the
reasons stated, seconded by Council Member Chapman. The motion
carried unanimously.
 The meeting was adjourned into executive session at 5:01 p.m.
4. Post Executive Session
  Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of
  any section, listed on an executive session agenda or discussed
  in executive session during a properly noticed meeting.
 Mayor reconvened the meeting at 6:03 p.m. stating no votes or action
 were taken.
 Regarding item 3.a.(1) Council Member Whitaker moved to have staff
 take action as discussed in executive session, seconded by Council
 Member Brownstein.
 Council Member Jake Rambo expressed opposition to the proposal,
 stating that he truly believed this is not something our citizens want us to
 do and indicated he would vote against it.
 Mayor Haynie called for the vote and Council Member Rambo voted
 opposed with all others voting in favor. The motion carried.
 Regarding item 3.A.(2) Council Member Rambo moved to proceed as
 discussed in executive session, seconded by Council Member
 Brownstein. All present voted in favor and the motion carried.
 Mayor Haynie stated he would recuse himself from discussion and
 voting on item 3.B.(1) and left the dais, turning the meeting over to
 Mayor Pro Tem Chapman to chair.
 Council Member Iacofano moved to reappoint Gary Davis to the
 Planning Commission, seconded by Council Member Hyatt. The motion
 carried with an 8-0 vote, with Mayor Haynie recusing himself.
 Mayor Haynie returned to the dais and continued the meeting.
 Regarding item 3.B.(2), Council Member Tinkey moved to reappoint
 Warren Player and Meghan Gravitt to the Historic District Preservation
 Commission, seconded by Council Member Rambo. All present voted in
 favor and the motion carried.
 Regarding item 3.B.(3), Council Member Tinkey moved to reappoint
 Giles Branch to the Board of Zoning Appeals, seconded by Council
 Member Brownstein. All present voted in favor and the motion carried.
 5. Recognition of Bands of Wando State Champions
 The Wando Bands State Champions were not present at the meeting.
 Mayor Haynie acknowledged their achievements in absentia, noting their
 long history of excellence and numerous championships that bring pride
 to the town.
