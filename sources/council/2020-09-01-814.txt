           Mr. Pagliarini provided Council with information on the updated
       ordinance.

      Mr. Chapman stated that he would like to suggest a few
amendments. The first addresses all retail establishments, which
goes into greater detail than the current ordinance, because the
current ordinance addresses grocery stores and pharmacies which
are listed on page 3 and 4 as retail establishments. He said other
establishments such as liquor stores, craft, music, and florist
establishments are not necessary. He said the second issue on page
4 addresses exemptions. He said he is dealing with what
pediatricians have stated which is that six years old should be the
exemption, not twelve years old. He said if you attend Charleston
County Schools, you are required to wear a mask if you are six years

    Mr. Chapman put this in the form of a motion.

    Mr. Chapman stated that the motion is to revise the retail
