                    SPECIAL TOWN COUNCIL MEETING
                         Tuesday October 8, 2024
                                5:00 p.m.
                   Municipal Complex, Council Chambers
              100 Ann Edwards Lane, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

                                    MINUTES

1.        Call to Order
     Mayor Haynie called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m. Daniel Brownstein,
     Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Guang Ming Whitley, John Iacofano,
     Gary Santos and Carl Ritchie were all present. Jake Rambo was absent.
2.        Public Comment
     [None]
3.        Executive Session

         Mayor Haynie moved to defer appointments to the Recreation
     Advisory Commission and the Accommodations Tax Advisory
     Committee to the next Council meeting stating that voter registration
     deadlines have been extended due to the hurricanes. Mr. Chapman
     seconded the motion.

     Mr. Pagliarini asked if that included continuing to advertise the positions
     and Mayor Haynie responded in the affirmative.

         Mr. Iacofano voted no with all others present voting in favor. The
     motion carried with a 7-1 vote.

     Mayor Haynie stated there is no reason to go into executive session.
          Personnel

     Council may take action on any item, including any subsection of

5.       Adjourn
     There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 5:04 p.m.
