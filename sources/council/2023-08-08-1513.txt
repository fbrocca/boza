       A. Public Hearing: A Public Hearing to receive input on a proposed
          amendment to the Development Agreement by and between DRP
          SC 2, LLC (the “Developer”) and the Town of Mount Pleasant,
          South Carolina. Proposed is to extend the Development
          Agreement for five (5) years, and to amend the development
          schedule due to unexpected delays, including the Covid Pandemic
          and Supply Chain Issues. Liberty Hill Farms is comprised of
          approximately 209.651 acres of land and whose location is
          generally described as being between Rifle Range Road and
          North Lakeshore Drive in the Six Mile Community.
         Ms. Reed reviewed the public hearing and stated based on a
         committee recommendation, the item is not on the agenda for
         action, and it would likely appear in September. She stated the
         public hearing is still being held because it had been advertised in
         the newspaper.

VI.             PUBLIC COMMENT
             Phil Mullins, 2225 DeWees Creek Road stated he lives in Oyster
        Point and he has some concerns about the ordinance regarding e-
        bikes. He said his neighborhood is off of Rifle Range Road and Six
        Mile and there is hardly enough room to put cars on, much less bikes.
        Currently as the ordinance is written he will have to ride on Rifle Range
        Road at 14 miles per hour, and that will create some road rage. He
        stated his other concern is his grandchild lives in the Groves, and the
        children ride their bikes every day to Moultrie Middle School. He said
        having them ride on Coleman Boulevard would be dangerous. He
        would like to see the speed limit in the ordinance be lowered so that
        the children can ride on the sidewalks legally.
