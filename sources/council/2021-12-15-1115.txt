            Howard Chapman, 824 Natchez Circle, stated he would like to

      the highest prices in the state and there is no reason for that. He would
      like to ask Mayor Haynie to invite those companies that sell petroleum
      in Mount Pleasant to sit down and find out why we are paying much
      more than everyone else.

B.    Recreation Committee
          Approval to proceed with developing a recreation
     referendum for November 2022.
     Mayor Haynie stated this is not approving a referendum, but a
     consensus that we will move forward with the concept. This
     needs to go through the Health, Recreation and Events
     committee as well as Police, Judicial and Legal for the
     wording.
     Ms. Hyatt stated this was discussed at the Recreation
     Committee meeting and the Committee is on board with

                                                             Page 7 of 11
starting this discussion. She said at some point the Committee
would like it to appear on an agenda to prioritize projects.
Mayor Haynie said it was preliminarily discussed at both
Recreation and Police, Judicial and Legal Committees and
asked Mr. Pagliarini about the timing. Mr. Pagilarini stated
there is both a ninety and sixty day lead-in time before the
election and the Town’s best interest would be served if final
reading was held in July, if not sooner.
Mayor Haynie said it will go to full Council and input can be
given at that time. He said the bulk of this will be discussed by
Recreation, and Police, Judicial and Legal will be tasked with
the wording of the referendum.
Ms. Whitley said Mount Pleasant is a world-class town and we
are deserving of world-class amenities. She said we do not
have the budget to reach some of these capital projects and
she feels that giving the residents an opportunity to see
specifically where their dollars are going and impose a
sunsetting ten-year fee on themselves to achieve these things
would be great for the Town. Ms. Whitley said she plans on
speaking during public comment at every meeting, and she
looks forward as a member of the Police, Judicial and Legal
Committee seeing the recommendations from Recreation. She
said it should also go to the Finance Committee as well as
there is a range of dollar amounts, and we want to think of
what is reasonable that would be likely to pass with the
citizens.
    Ms. Hyatt moved to proceed with developing a recreation
referendum for November 2022, seconded by Ms. Whitley.
Mr. Rambo said he believes this should be on the agenda for
the Council Retreat to receive input and have discussion as a
group.
Ms. Corley asked how public input is collected. Mr. DeMoura
said there are several different ways. The first would be
Council’s contact with constituents. Another way would be by
a series of charettes. In the past surveys of recreation users in
the Town have been sent out with the assistance of the
College of Charleston’s Riley Center.

                                                        Page 8 of 11
            Mr. Chapman said Council can also have public hearings to
            invite people to give input. He said in order for this to succeed,
            we must convince the citizens why it is needed. He said the
            key is to get the information out and hear from our
            constituents.
                 Mayor Haynie called for the vote and all present voted in
            favor.
