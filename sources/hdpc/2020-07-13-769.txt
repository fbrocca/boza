i. 744 Pitt Street – Survey 5959 – (TMS 532-09-00-069) Applicant is seeking
   Final Review for demolition of a single-family home.

     Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission
(attachment 1).

   Mr. Will Dupree and Ms. Mary Dupree reviewed the request with the
Commission.

    Ms. Linda Balzac, stated that the home is not historic, but is a contributing
factor

     Mr. Alan Smith, 115 Friend St, stated that he is familiar with the home and
it is a typical 1970s home. He stated that Pascullis did very few residential
homes. He suggested that the home does not fit the Old Village Historic
District vernacular and there are several significant building violations to the
property.

    Ms. Carol Tinkey, 208 Venning St, stated that previously this home was
considered as modern village architecture and was a contributing structure,
and therefore a previous request for demolition was denied. She suggested
that the home should be preserved as it is close to being historic.

    Ms. Carmen Scott, 415 Church Street, suggested that the property should
not be considered historic because it does not meet the guidelines in terms of
years and is not a significant architecture. She suggested that delaying the

    family. She suggested that relying on an obituary as justification is ill-based.

k. 217 Church Street – Survey 6198 – (TMS 517-15-00-025) Applicant is
   seeking a recommendation to Planning Commission and Town Council for
   removing property from the Old Village Historic District (OV-HD) Overlay
   to the Boulevard Overlay District (BOD). Correspondence – AMENDED
   7.10.2020

     Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission
(attachment 1)

   Ms. Laura Beck, representative for owner, reviewed the request with the
Commission.

    Ms. Balzac suggested that if the request is approved it would set a bad
precedent. She suggested that this is historic and fits within the historic
district.

   There being no comments, Mr. Crosland closed public comment.

    Mr. Barber moved for denial of the request for removal from the Old Village
Historic District as it would set a precedent, it is against the provisions of the
Comprehensive Plan and properties within register of historic places. Mr.
Crosland seconded the motion. All in favor.
