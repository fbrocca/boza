6.    Business
      A. 11 Pierates Cruze – Survey 6071/Non-Historic– (TMS 532-09-00-017)
         Applicant is seeking Final Review for alteration/addition to home and
         new construction for two accessory structures

         Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

         Ms. Balzac reviewed the request with the Commission.

           Warren Player, 10 Pierates Cruze, stated that he is opposed to the
      accessory 2-car garage and asked that it be denied. He suggested that as the
      guidelines state, accessory structures should not be located in the front yard.
      He suggested that the size is not in keeping with the neighborhood, and is not
      in keeping with the massing and height.

           Mr. John Utes, 4 Pierates Cruze, suggested that the request is not in
      keeping with the neighborhood. He suggested that the existing paint color is
      also not appropriate with the neighborhood. He suggested that a 2-car garage
      is not appropriate in the front yard.

         There being no further comments, Ms. Montgomery closed public
      comment.

          Mr. Crosland moved for denial of the two accessory buildings, with
      approval of the changes to the stoop, doors, and windows. Mr. Barber
      seconded the motion. All in favor.
