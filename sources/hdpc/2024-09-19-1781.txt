                 TOWN OF MOUNT PLEASANT, SOUTH CAROLINA
             SPECIAL HISTORIC DISTRICT PRESERVATION COMMISSION
                              SEPTEMBER 19, 2024
                                   MINUTES


PowerPoint Presentation


Present:      David Creech, Chair, James Bakker, Warren Player.
Absent:       Meghan Gravitt, Corey Barber.
Staff:        Bryan Hankinson, James Ward, Lynnette Lynes

        Mr. Creech called the meeting to order at 5:00 pm.

I.     Approval of Agenda

     Mr. Creech moved for approval of the agenda. Mr. Player seconded the motion.
All in favor.

II.    Discussion and possible action of multi-story accessory structure restrictions
       and accessory structure height restrictions within the OVHD.

    Mr. Will Taylor, 309 Bennett St., stated he would like to request that the
restriction of one and one-half stories for buildings be amended to be in line with
the national definition. He reviewed this request through a PowerPoint
presentation.

Mr. Taylor asked for an additional five minutes.

    Mr. Player moved to allow an additional five minutes for Mr. Taylor’s
presentation. Mr. Creech seconded the motion. All in favor.

      Mr. Taylor continued with his presentation.

    Ms. Linda Balzac, architect, suggested that 156.313.5.A. should be amended to
be in line with the national definition.

There being no further comments, Mr. Creech closed public comment.

    Mr. Creech moved to recommend to Town Council to change the height for all
accessory structures in the Old Village Historic District to have a maximum of 25
feet as recommended in the draft zoning code and to remove the restriction of the
one-half story requirement. Mr. Player seconded the motion. All in favor.

III. Discussion of Items for Next Agenda

The Commission agreed to add the following item to next month’s agenda:
   ➢ Discussion and possible action on amendments to the Design guidelines.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 5:28 pm.
