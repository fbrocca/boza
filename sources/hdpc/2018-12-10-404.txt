      A. 116 Beach Street – (TMS 517-15-00-140) Applicant is seeking After-the-
         Fact Final Review for a dock. CORRESPONDENCE

             Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission
       (attachment 1).

Mr. Phil Clarke, Clarke Design Group, reviewed the request with the
Commission. He stated the dock was built with all approvals and constructed
smaller than originally shown on the approved plans. He stated that he has
signatures from several surrounding neighbors in favor of the request.

Mr. Harvey Lemon, 319 Bayview Drive, stated he is not in favor of the dock,
particularly the inclusion of a roof structure. He stated that he is in support
of Mr. Peter Dodd’s letter. He suggested that having an after-the-fact
approval does not allow for comment from the neighbors. He stated that
104 Beach Street had a covered dock, but was removed after requests from
Bayview residents. He suggested that covered docks should not be allowed
as it impedes the view of the harbor.

Mr. Jenkins Bailey, 311 Bayview Drive, stated he is also not in favor of a roof
structure on the dock. He suggested that the covered dock is not good for
the harbor or the view from the neighborhood.

There being no further comments, Mr. Hirshorn closed public comment.

Ms. Dovre agreed that every dock should be equally considered.

Ms. Wilson asked when the restriction was put into place. Mr. Rutherford
answered that he is not sure the exact date.

Mr. Hirshorn agreed that some of the dock structures pre-date the
implementation of the restriction.

Ms. Wilson asked if this issue relates to the historic district. Mr. Rutherford
answered that anyone concerned with a dock in the historic district could
raise that concern; it does not have to be a concerned raised only from
someone that lives in the historic district or in the vicinity of the dock in
question.

Ms. Rowe asked if a covered dock is restricted. Mr. Rutherford answered in
the affirmative and stated that it is included in the guidelines.
                                          Old Village Historic District Commission

Mr. Hirshorn asked if OCRM has a similar restriction on covered docks. Mr.
Rutherford answered in the negative and stated that the OCRM approval
would not supersede the Commission’s purview.

Ms. Montgomery stated that the guidelines should be upheld.

Mr. Hirshorn asked what action the Commission can take. Mr. Rutherford
answered that the Commission can approve the structure and dock, or the
Commission can deny the covered structure and require it to be removed.
Mr. Hirshorn asked the applicant if they were aware of the restriction. Mr.
Clarke answered that the owners were not aware of the restriction. He
stated that the dock was included with the tree removal request, but it was
not included on the plans that the Commission reviewed and approved. He
stated that the owners had the understanding that the staff approval
included approval for the construction of the dock.

Ms. Wilson asked if there is a photograph of both 116 and 112 Beach Street
docks. Mr. Rutherford answered in the negative.

Mr. Hirshorn suggested that a deferral might be appropriate to allow the
owner to speak to this issue.

Ms. Wilson stated that this is a permitted structure that has the support of
the immediate neighbor. She suggested that this should be taken into
consideration.

Ms. Montgomery suggested that the guidelines should be upheld regardless
of if the structure was constructed with the proper permits, but not
approved by the Commission.

Ms. Caroline Johnson, 112 Beach Street, stated that she wanted to make it
clear that the dock and structure were constructed with all of the
appropriate approvals aside from the Commission. She stated that they
were under the impression that they had all of the necessary approvals. She
stated that it was not their intention to deceive the Commission.

Mr. Rutherford stated that the dock was not included in the submittal to the
Commission. He stated that the dock approval would not have required

plans, the dock would not have been reviewed. He stated that staff would

   seeking Final Review for revisions to alterations of a single-family home.

     Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission
(attachment 1).

Mr. Ken Spicer reviewed the request with the Commission.

There being no comments, Mr. Hirshorn closed public comment.
                                           Old Village Historic District Commission

Ms. Dovre stated that she is in favor of the request.

Ms. Wilson expressed concern with roll down shutters and asked if high
impact glass windows were considered. Mr. Spicer answered in the
affirmative and stated that he prefers the proposed shutters as opposed to
the windows being shattered and having to replace the entire window. Ms.
Wilson stated that there have been several changes to this home that has
basically demolished the home. She stated that the home was originally
evaluated as a renovation, but is really now new construction. She stated
that the proposed shutters have a commercial look and suggested that they
are not appropriate for a harbor-front home. She stated that if considered,
she would like to see how the shutter housing is designed into the soffit. She
stated that as currently proposed, she suggested that it would not be
appropriate for a harbor-front home.

Mr. Spicer showed the location of the shutter housing for the Commission.
He stated that the housing would be painted the same color of the window
trim. He suggested that the shutter housing would not be seen from the
harbor.

Ms. Wilson suggested that she would possibly be amenable to allowing the
shutters if the housing was concealed in the ceiling of the porch, but she
would have to see how this on a plan before making a decision.

Mr. Hirshorn agreed that the proposed changes significantly alter the façade
to a more industrial look and feel. He stated that a metal seamed roof would
be preferable.

Ms. Montgomery stated that she is not opposed to the proposed roof, but is
concerned with the proposed shutters as well. She suggested that different
shutters should be considered.

Ms. Rowe stated that she would prefer to see a metal roof and impact
windows.

Ms. Hirshorn asked if impact windows could be considered. Mr. Spicer
answered in the affirmative.

Ms. Dovre stated that she does not have an issue with the asphalt roof or the
roll up shutters.

Ms. Wilson suggested that the material changes are significant and affect the
aesthetics of the house from the harbor. She stated that it is an aluminum
commercial product and suggested that it is not appropriate.

Mr. Hirshorn stated that the harbor-front façade is held to a higher standard

possible. He stated that there has been significant latitude with previous
