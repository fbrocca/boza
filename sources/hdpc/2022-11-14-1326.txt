VIII.   Discussion and Action of Changes to Design Guidelines

        Ms. Forbus reviewed the proposed changes with the Commission.

        Mr. Miles Martschink, 135 Toomer Lane, suggested that the guidelines should
    be adhered to, specifically, pool and HVAC equipment, trash cans, and fences. He
    suggested that the current fence guidelines should be kept and if additional height
    is desired, then it should be done with plantings and not taller fences.

        Ms. Montgomery moved to allow an additional two minutes to Mr. Martschink
    for comments. Mr. Barber seconded the motion. All in favor.

        Mr. Martschink suggested that more care should be taken to where outdoor
    equipment and trash cans are located. He suggested that they should not be
    allowed in the front yard. He also stated that the trash cans are taller than 42
    inches, which means that they are seen over fencing. He suggested that the current
    guidelines should be kept.
