C. 128 Chicco Lane – (TMS 532-05-00-032) Applicant is seeking Final Review
   for a new single-family home.

      Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment
1).

    Ms. Joann Cook, 631 Pitt Street, expressed opposition with the proposed
front setbacks for the home. She suggested that the home should be more in
line with the streetscape.

    Ms. Bess Lee, owner, stated that she has worked with staff in order to
preserve the trees on the lot. She stated that because of the trees on the lot,
the front setback was the only place where some deviations could be
considered.

   Ms. Rowe moved to deny the application as presented due to the setback
encroachments. Mr. Hackenberg second the motion. Motion passed on a 3 to
1 vote with Ms. Rowe, Mr. Gallogly and Mr. Hackenberg in favor; Ms.
Montgomery opposed.
