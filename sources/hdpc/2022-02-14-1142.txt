VI. Appeal of Staff Level Approval, 101 Pitt Street- Survey #5934/Historic
    (TMS#532-01-00-079) Applicant is appealing staff level approval.

    Mr. Deford reviewed the procedure for appeal. He stated that the notice for
appeal is outlined in FOIA, which is 24-hour notice. He clarified that taking action
on the staff approval at the previous meeting would not have been appropriate and
a violation of FOIA. He stated that the Commission or any aggrieved party can
appeal an administrative or staff decision as noted in §156.433(H)(1).

   Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

    Ms. Carole Tinkey and Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning Street, reviewed their
position with the Commission.

    Mr. Chris Colman, 118 Pitt St, suggested that the Commission should have the
authority to overrule staff on a decision without an appeal being submitted. He
stated that he agrees with the Mr. and Ms. Tinkey that this project does not fit
within the guidelines of a staff approval. He suggested that the rules and
procedures should be strictly reviewed and scrutinized, and changes made to
provide better procedures.

    Mr. Ben Towll, 101 Pitt Street, suggested that they have applied and
been approved in accordance with the guidelines. He also suggested that their
business is providing valuable jobs and desire to be a good neighbor to those
around them. He stated that he is open to resolving the issues as much as
possible and within the guidelines.

    Ms. Eva Suarez, design firm for Posthouse, stated that they plan to re-attach
the gate so that it would be to the height of the enclosure, but were told to halt
any work until after the appeal process. She stated that the enclosure was located
next to the electrical panel water heater. She stated that the gate would be raised
to the height of the enclosure and a kick plate added to the bottom.

   There being no further comments, Ms. Montgomery closed public comment.

   Ms. Montgomery moved to convene into executive session.

Mr. Deford moved for a point of order and stated that a reason for the executive
session would need to be stated. He noted that he was not prepared to go into
executive session for any substantive legal advice; therefore, all information could
be provided in open session. He stated that reasons for an executive session are
outlined under SC state statute and suggested that none of those reasons would be
appropriate in this instance.

Motion failed due to point of order and lack of a second.

    Ms. Miller rebutted that it was noted at the previous meeting that any staff
level approvals could be appealed. She stated that staff followed the outlined
procedures in approval of this request. She stated that there are times when an
after-the-fact submittals and approvals are completed.

    Mr. and Ms. Tinkey rebutted that they have attended all of the meetings
regarding this address. She suggested that the historic windows, the shed roof, and
the placement of the structure is against to the historic guidelines. She suggested
that located on the other side of the building would be more appropriate. She
suggested that it was against Town code. Mr. Tinkey asked that the appeal be
upheld and the applicant re-apply for review and approval by the entire
Commission. He suggested that after-the-fact approvals are against the guidelines
and should not be allowed.

    Ms. Shaw moved for denial of the appeal and to uphold staff’s decision and that
the work be completed as was submitted and approved. Mr. Barber seconded the
motion. Motion passed on a 3 to 1 vote with Ms. Shaw, Mr. Barber, and Ms.
Montgomery in favor; Mr. Gallogly opposed.

The Commission convened for a break at 6:11 pm and reconvened at 6:19 pm.
