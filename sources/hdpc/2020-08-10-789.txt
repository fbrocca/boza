Present:     John Hackenberg, Robert Crosland, Gene Gallogly, Corey Barber.
Staff:       Austin Rutherford, Jane Yager-Baurmind

      Mr. Rutherford called the meeting to order at 5pm and stated that a
temporary chair would need to be elected. The Commission agreed that Mr.
Gallogly should chair the meeting.

I.    Approval of Agenda

   Mr. Hackenberg move to approve the minutes. Mr. Barber seconded the
motion. All in favor.

II.   Approval of Minutes July 13, 2020

   Mr. Crosland moved to approve the minutes. Mr. Barber seconded the
motion. All in favor.

III. Correspondence

   Mr. Rutherford stated that the correspondence was provided to the
Commission and included with the agenda.

IV. General Public Comment

     Ms. Ashley Woody, 110 Friend Street, expressed concern with the tree
protection ordinance and the construction at 104 Friend Street. She stated that
recently there was damage to a historic tree that was not approved and suggested
that more should be done to protect the trees.

There being no further comments, Mr. Gallogly continued with the agenda.

      Correspondence

    Mr. Rutherford reviewed the different options of rescheduling meetings to
review the historic designation of structures with the Commission.

The Commission agreed that those structures proposed for historic designation
should be done in batches over the course of several meetings.
