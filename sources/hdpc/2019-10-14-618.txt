C. 237 Middle Street – Survey 6045 – (TMS 532-09-00-022) Applicant is
   seeking Final Review for the demolition of a home with a previous
   approval for renovation.

     Mr. Rutherford reviewed the staff report with the Commission
(attachment 1).

Mr. Sam Lisi, Village Restoration, reviewed the application with the
Commission.

Ms. Cheryl Shell, 241 Middle Street, stated that she is opposed to the
demolition of this home because it was built in 1837. She expressed
concerned about losing the history of the home and neighborhood.

Ms. Rowe asked if this home is considered historic. Mr. Rutherford answered
the Commission needs to determine if the structure should be declared
historic.

   Ms. Rowe moved to approve the demolition and that the structure is not
considered historic based on justification B, C, & D as listed in staff comments.
Ms. Montgomery second the motion. All in favor.
