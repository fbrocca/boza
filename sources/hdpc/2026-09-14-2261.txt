     Ms. Heather Evans, 318 Morrison, expressed concern with the
encroachment into the setbacks. She expressed concern with the massing of
the home and privacy issues. She suggested that the pool would be seen from

being considered because the construction might cause stress and not
because the tree is in poor condition or hazardous. She suggested that

denied, require an updated and corrected tree protection plan, real privacy
mitigation, and accurate renderings before approval.

     Ms. Amanda Pritchett, 314 Morrison, expressed concern with the
