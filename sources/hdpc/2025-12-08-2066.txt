5.    209 McCormick St., Survey Card 4218/Non-Historic – TMS 5171500072,
      Preliminary Review for the construction of new additions on an existing
      1920’s primary structure.

     Mr. Hankinson reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     Ms. Linda Balzac, architect, reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Mr. Mac Jenkinson, 809 Pitt St, stated that the property is not historic. He
expressed concern with the proximity of the home to the setbacks. He
expressed concern with the height of the fence and that it causes a child safety
issue. He expressed concern with the number of trees proposed for removal.

    Ms. Caroline Jenkinson, 809 Pitt St, expressed concern with the pool and
that it could mean additional fill that could cause additional flooding. She
expressed concern with the proposed encroachment into the setback and that
it would mean the home would be too close to their property.

   There being no further comments, Ms. Gravitt closed public comment and
moved to Commission questions.

    Ms. Gravitt moved to re-open Commission questions. Mr. Player seconded
the motion. All in favor.

     The Commission provided comments.
