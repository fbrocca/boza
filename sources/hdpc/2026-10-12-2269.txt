2.   2 Pierates Cruze, Survey Card 6061/Non-Historic – TMS 5320900007,
     Preliminary Review for maintenance on the primary structure, pool
     replacement, pool house, and hardscaping.

     Mr. Hankinson reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

    Ms. Kim Floyd, and Mr. Bryan Plumley, owners, reviewed the request with
the Commission and stated that they are available for questions.

   Mr. Will Smith, designer, asked for clarification about the porkchop eaves
and suggested that this is a “poor man’s eave return”.

    Mr. John Durlack, 1 Pierates Cruze, stated that he resides next to the
requested property. He expressed concern that the height, scale, and mass is
too large and would be visible from Alhambra Hall and their property. He
suggested that the addition would be intrusive, noisy, and affect their use of
their backyard. He suggested an engineer be consulted to ensure proper
drainage of the property.

There being no further comments, Ms. Gravitt closed public comment.

   Mr. Craft asked about the setback relief request. Mr. Smith answered that
the pool is closer to the home. Mr. Hankinson clarified that a structure is
considered attached if there is a connecting elevated walkway and therefore
would have to meet the primary structure setbacks.

    Mr. Craft asked if the addition needed to be raised due to flood elevation.
Mr. Smith answered in the affirmative. Mr. Craft asked the height of the roof.
Mr. Smith answered that it is approximately ten feet. Mr. Plumley stated that
the total square footage is approximately 400 square feet.

    Mr. Hankinson suggested that because it is considered a part of the
primary structure the Commission could approve encroachment into the
primary structure setbacks.

   Mr. Smith stated that the accessory structure would be separated from the
primary structure. Mr. Player suggested that this should be well shown on
subsequent plans.

    Ms. Gravitt asked if the deck is included in the square footage for the
porch. Mr. Smith answered that only the covered deck portion of the
accessory structure is included in the porch percentage.

    Mr. Craft asked about the changes of the new pool from the existing pool.
Mr. Plumley answered that the original plan was to keep the pool in the same
location of the existing pool, but due to the new accessory setback
requirements, they are asking for a variance to keep the pool in the same
location. Mr. Hankinson stated that a setback variance would be needed from
the Board of Zoning Appeals prior to receiving approval from this Commission.

    Mr. Smith stated that they are trying to be as environmentally sensitive to
the site as possible.

      Mr. Foxworth asked if moving the pool to the other side of the lot was
considered. Ms. Floyd answered that this was considered, but the neighboring
property would be developed as well. Mr. Foxworth suggested that the bac of
the structure would be preferable to the pool. Mr. Foxworth suggested that
it is too massive and should be scaled down. He suggested that it appears to
be more of an ADU.

    Mr. Craft asked about the windows of the accessory structure and if they
could be proportioned to be more in keeping with the main structure. He
suggested that the dental details should be reviewed. Mr. Craft suggested that
the back of the structure is bare and suggested that there should be more
windows.

    Mr. Craft suggested that the partial gable and roofline should be restudied
to help with the scale and mass. He suggested a hip roof would be more in
keeping with the historic district.

Mr. Smith thanked the Commission for their comments.

    Mr. Player suggested that the drainage should be maintained on the
property, particularly around the pool area. He asked for details on the
handrails. He suggested that the chart included in the plan should be reviewed
to ensure the information is correct. He stated that fencing was not addressed

      Review for alterations to a previously approved COA.

     Mr. Hankinson reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     Ms. Balzac, architect, reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Mr. Craft expressed concern with the dentil molding and asked that details
be provided. He suggested that there are existing casing details and suggested

transom should be considered.

     Ms. Edwards agreed that the door should be simplified.

    Mr. Craft asked if the windows on the rear dormer are being proposed.
Ms. Balzac answered in the affirmative to provide an egress. Mr. Craft
suggested that the dentil molding should be resolved and the casings
corrected.

    Mr. Craft asked if the front stoop needs railing. Ms. Balzac answered that

be re-studied.

location of the fencing for the Commission. Mr. Player asked about the cupola.
He suggested that the cupola is not consistent with the character of the
