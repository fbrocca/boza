VI. Business -
    A. Alhambra Playground – No Survey/Non-Historic – (TMS 5320900032)
        Applicant is seeking Final Approval for fencing along the exterior and
        around the play equipment.

        Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

        Mr. Frankie Pettit and Mr. Andy Weis, Public Services, reviewed the
    request with the Commission.

         Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning, suggested that fence guidelines should be
    reviewed as a whole. He suggested that aluminum is not noted as a preferred
    material. He suggested that more trees should be planted, the ground
    material reviewed to ensure that it is not toxic or a danger to children, and the
    parking be maintained as currently configured. He suggested that the staging
    is important as well during construction to allow use during the construction
    time.

       Mr. Robert Byrnes, 9 Pierates Cruz, stated that he is not in favor of an
    aluminum fence and that if allowed it would set a bad precedent.

       There being no further comments, Mr. Crosland closed public comment
    and moved to Commission questions.

         Mr. Player moved for deferral to see a detail of each fence and how
    designed, drawn to scale, the location of all fencing, including removal of chain
    link fence.

    Mr. Crosland noted that there has not been time for deliberation, so a motion
    is not appropriate at this time. The Commission deliberated.

       Mr. Crosland moved to open public comment for additional Commission
    questions. Mr. Barber seconded the motion. All in favor.

The Commission discussed the following changes:
   ➢ Show complete fence details—drawn to scale, dimensions, elevations, and
      materials.
   ➢ 3D model of accessory structures
   ➢ 3D model can be a “sketch-up” or box depiction showing massing.
   ➢ More panoramic view of streetscape
   ➢ Show specific changes of after-the-fact changes (clouded, approved and
      changed elevations)

IX. Review fencing requirements and violations.

    Mr. Hankinson reviewed the current requirements for fencing and violations
with the Commission.

     The Commission discussed the following changes:
     ➢ Notification and updates on violations within the historic district at meetings
     ➢ Show dimension between pickets

X.    Review HDPC application and review process.

     The Commission agreed that this was discussed with the checklist item
discussion.

XI. Discussion on historic designation of Edwards Park.

     The Commission discussed the historic designation of Edwards Park.

    Ms. Miller reviewed the history of Edwards Park and eligibility requirements
with the Commission.

    The Commission discussed whether the park should be designated as a historic
place.

    The Commission agreed that the historic designation should be forwarded to
the Planning Committee for consideration of designating the park historic.
