                                    MINUTES


Present:      Louisa Montgomery, Chair, Robert Crosland, Gene Gallogly, Corey
              Barber, Anne Shaw.
Staff:        Kate Miller, Joe Juan, Aaron Bennett, Lynnette Lynes

         Ms. Montgomery called the meeting to order at 5:13 pm.

I.    Approval of Agenda

   Mr. Crosland moved for approval of the agenda. Mr. Gallogly seconded the
motion. All in favor.

II.   Approval of Minutes August 9, 2021 Meeting

    Mr. Gallogly moved for approval of the minutes. Mr. Crosland seconded the
motion. All in favor.

III. Correspondence

All correspondence received was linked to the agenda and distributed to the
Commission.

IV. General Public Comment

    Mr. Chris Colman, 118 Pitt St, spoke regarding revisions to the zoning code. He
stated that the non-conformity ordinance revisions allow a site to be renovated
and the use changed without requiring changes to the parking. He stated that he
has presented options for changes that could be considered.

There being no further comments, Ms. Montgomery continued with the agenda.

V.    Consent Motions

      Mr. Crosland moved for consent approval of the following items:
        ➢ 436 Church Street – Survey 6191/Historic – (TMS 532-01-00092) Applicant
           is seeking Final Review for accessory structure (pool and terrace) and
           fencing.
