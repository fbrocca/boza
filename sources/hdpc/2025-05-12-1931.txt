D.    303 Hibben St., Survey Card 6230/Non-Historic - TMS 5320100100,
      Preliminary Review for the demolition of an existing primary structure
      and construction of a new elevated primary structure.

     Mr. Hankinson reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

   Ms. Louisa Montgomery, 404 Church St, expressed concern with shed roofs

     There being no further comments, Ms. Gravitt closed public comment.

     The Commission provided comments.

E.    313 Queen St., Survey Card 6096/Non-Historic - TMS 5320500061,
      Preliminary Review for the demolition of an existing primary structure
      and construction of a new primary structure.

     Mr. Hankinson reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     Ms. Heather Wilson, architect, asked if she could show revised drawings.

    Ms. Gravitt moved for approval for submittal of additional drawings. Mr.
Player seconded the motion. All in favor.

     Ms. Wilson reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Ms. Erica Holland, 303 Queen St, suggested that the demolition of the
existing home is not necessary. She suggested that the proposed home is not
in keeping with the character of the neighborhood. She expressed concern
with drainage in the area and that this project could further exacerbate
drainage in the area. She suggested that the proposed plan does not meet the
new tree protection requirements. She suggested that a drainage plan should
be considered to ensure that there is not increased runoff to other properties
in the vicinity. She suggested that a moderated approach to development

She stated that she has a copy of her remarks for the Commission.

    Ms. Gravit moved for approval for submittal of the letter. Mr. Player

    Mr. John Boensch, 204 Queen St, stated that he is opposed to the request.
