VI. Business -
    A. 102 Mary Street – Survey 6255/Non-Historic – (TMS 5171500066)
        Applicant is seeking Final Review for a hardship relief request for the
        installation of artificial turf strips into the driveway.

         Mr. Hankinson reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

        Ms. Jenny Strauss and Mr. Ted Strauss, owners, reviewed the request with
    the Commission.

         Ms. Gravitt entered at this time (5:24 pm).

        There being no comments, Mr. Creech closed public comment and moved
    to Commission questions.

        Following Board questions, the homeowners offered final comments. Staff
    had no final comment. The Commission moved to comments and
    deliberation.

        Mr. Player moved for denial as the request does not meet the three criteria
    for a substantial economic hardship and that the design solution caused the
    problem. Mr. Creech seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 3 to 2 vote
    with Mr. Creech, Ms. Gravitt, and Mr. Player in favor; Mr. Barber and Mr.
    Bakker opposed.

XI. Discussion of Enforcement of After–the–Fact changes to COAs.

     The Commission discussed after-the-fact changes to the certificate of
appropriateness (COA). Mr. Ward stated that this would be reviewed by legal and
possible language to the guidelines would be brought back to the Commission for
consideration.

XII. Discussion of Detention Ponds in the OVDH.

    Mr. Creech stated that he requested discussion of this issue. The Commission
discussed what options are available to help deter drainage infrastructure from
being in the front yard. Mr. Ward recommended that any drainage concerns be
referred to the Engineering and Development Services Department for further
review and approval as opposed to adding language to the guidelines.
