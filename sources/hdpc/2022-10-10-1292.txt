          F. 111 Hyer Street- Survey 6077/Non-Historic (TMS# 532-09-00-059)
             Applicant is seeking final approval for demolition and construction of
             primary structure along with construction of pool and garage.

          This item was removed by staff due to noncompliance.

          G.   975 Pitt Street- Survey 5889/Non-Historic (TMS# 532-13-00-033)
               Applicant is seeking final approval for fencing and hardscaping.

               Ms. Forbus reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

              Mr. Steven Dematto and Mr. Lee Richardson reviewed the request with
          the Commission.

              Mr. Richard Fitzgerald, owner of Schweers Lane, expressed concern that
          the fence is not in keeping with the character of the Historic District because
          of the proposed height and appears to encroach into Schweers Lane. He
          stated that Schweers Lane is a private drive and provides access to several
          homes.

          There being no further comment, Ms. Montgomery closed public comment.

              Mr. Barber moved for approval of the request based on the condition that
          paving does not extend past the property line or that they receive proper
          approvals to build pavers outside property line and that fence is not taller than
          42 inches when measuring from the inside of the property. Ms. Montgomery
          seconded the motion. All in favor.
