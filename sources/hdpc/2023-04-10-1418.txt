VI.   Business
      A. 223 Pitt Street – Survey 5923 / Historic (TMS# 5320100038) Final
          Approval for partial demolition, hardscaping, and parking pad.

           Mr. Keb Ballard, architect, reviewed the request with the Commission.

          Ms. Linda Balzac, 422 Hobcaw Drive, suggested that according to the
      ordinance, the proposed parking would be on the side yard and that the
      amount of concrete is being reduced. She suggested that the reduction of
      concrete would make it look softer and would be less obtrusive.

           Mr. Ballard suggested that the parking is on the side or rear yard.

           There being no comments, Mr. Crosland closed public comment.

         Mr. Crosland moved for approval as submitted. Mr. Gallogly seconded the
      motion. All in favor.
