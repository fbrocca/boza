             Mr. William Taylor, 309 Bennett, stated that he has spoken with the
        owners regarding privacy and the possibility of being able to overlook their
        property. He stated that the owners have agreed to keep the shutters closed
        for the windows facing their property. He also asked that the magnolia tree
        be preserved as it provides shade and additional screening.

            Mr. Crosland closed public comment.

            Mr. Gallogly moved for approval with the condition that the cable railings
        are eliminated and replaced with railing that conforms with the guidelines,
        windows existing in historic portion to be true divided light, the porch addition
        on the harbor side is approved, the pervious driveway material to be approved
        by staff, windows on non-historic portion to be single divided light, house will
        be located eight feet from the property line instead of seven feet, and approval
        of the false window as presented. Mr. Barber seconded the motion. All in
        favor.
