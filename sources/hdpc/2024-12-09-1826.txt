      Mr. Hankinson reviewed staff approvals with the Commission.

8.    Action to adopt newly revised HDPC Guidelines.

     Mr. Hankinson reviewed the changes to the guidelines with the Commission.

    Mr. Creech moved for approval for accessory structure to be a minimum of ten
feet from the primary structure alignment for through lots. Mr. Player seconded
the motion. All in favor with Ms. Gravitt abstaining.

    Mr. Creech moved for approval of accessory structures to be a minimum of six
feet from the primary structure alignment for corner lots. Mr. Player seconded the
motion. All in favor.

   Mr. Creech moved for approval of the guidelines as amended. Mr. Bakker
seconded the motion. All in favor.
