VI.   Business -
      A. 102 Mary Street - Survey 6255/ Non-Historic (TMS 517-15-00-066)
          Applicant is seeking final review for alteration to previously approved
          plans for artificial turf.

            Ms. Forbus reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

            Ms. Jennifer Strauss reviewed the request with the Commission.

          Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning St, spoke regarding 102 Mary Street, and
      asked that the request be denied as the guidelines prohibit artificial turf on
      frontage and concrete pavers. He suggested that other requests for turf were
      denied at 131 Live Oak and 201 Bank, and 959 Pitt. He suggested that there
      are other appropriate pervious materials that could be used. He asked that
      the request be denied so there is not a precedent set for future requests.

         There being no further comments, Ms. Montgomery closed public
      comment and moved to Commission questions.

         Ms. Montgomery moved for approval as it is technically the rear of house.
      Ms. Shaw seconded the motion. Motion failed on a 2 to 3 vote with Ms. Shaw
      and Ms. Montgomery in favor; Mr. Barber, Mr. Gallogly, and Mr. Crosland
      opposed.

         Ms. Montgomery clarified that the request is denied due to failure of the
      motion for approval.
