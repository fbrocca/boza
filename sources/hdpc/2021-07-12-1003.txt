D. 300 CENTER STREET – No Survey/ Non-Historic – (TMS 532-09-00-282)
   Applicant is seeking Final Approval for addition and new fence.

   Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

   Ms. Balzac reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Mr. Tom Foster stated that he is opposed to the request. He suggested
that having the addition detached for a minimal amount from the main
structure is not in the spirit of the setback requirements. He asked that the
request be deferred in order to appropriately review the request. He
suggested that the request should be required to have a COA. He suggested
that the request would adversely affect his property and property values. He
read a letter from his neighbors for the record.

   There being no further comments, Ms. Montgomery closed public
comment.
