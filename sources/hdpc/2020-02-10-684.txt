VI. Reconsideration of request for an encroachment permit for dock at
    101 Mary Street. (This item referred to HDPC from Transportation
    Committee of Town of Council on September 30 and December 2,
    2019; Deferred by Commission January 13, 2020) –Revised Dock
    Plan, Revised Staff Report, Correspondence from November. –
    Correspondence from January – Correspondence from February

     Mr. Rutherford reviewed this application with the Commission
(attachment 1).

  Ms. Mary Shahid and Mr. Hamlin O’Kelley reviewed the request with the
Commission.

Mr. Ted Strauss, 102 Mary Street, stated that he and his wife are in full
support of the request.

Mr. Louis Horton, 138 Live Oak Drive, stated he is favor of a dock at 101
Mary Street.

Mr. Chris Colman, 118 Pitt Street, suggested that the Town is the upland
owner and the entity that has the right to submit for a permit from OCRM.
He suggested that the request should benefit the good of the Town as a
whole and not an individual homeowner. He expressed concern with the
master dock program and that the previous permits associated with that
master dock program are no longer valid.

Ms. Carol Tinkey, 208 Venning Street, expressed concern with the distance
between docks and waterfront access.

Ms. Helen Cook, 113 Bennett Street, stated she has no issue with the dock
and suggested that it would be a benefit for the neighborhood.

Ms. Linda Balzac, 422 Hobcaw Drive, suggested that it might be beneficial
to have a pictorial representation of the dock.

VII. Business
     a. 208 McCormick Street – (TMS 532-09-00-263) Applicant is
        seeking Final Review for the demolition of a shed.

    Mr. Rutherford reviewed the application with the Commission
    (attachment 1).

    Mr. Brian Maiden reviewed the application with the Commission.

    Ms. Balzac suggested that the shed should not be considered historic.

    Mr. Mark Regalbuto, 754 Pitts Street, suggested that the condition of the
    structure should be a consideration. He suggested that the shed does
    not add to the “historic fabric” of the historic district. He stated that he is
    in favor of the request.

    There being no further public comment, Mr. Hackenberg closed public
    comment.

         Mr. Hackenberg moved to approve demolition of the shed because
    it is a public safety hazard. Mr. Gallogly seconded the motion. Motion
    passed on a 3 to 1 vote with Mr. Hackenberg, Mr. Gallogly, and Mr.
    Crosland in favor; Mr. Barber opposed.
