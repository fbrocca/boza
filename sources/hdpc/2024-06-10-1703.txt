STAFF REPORT

Present:       David Creech, Chair, James Bakker, Meghan Gravitt, Corey Barber,
               Warren Player.
Staff:         Bryan Hankinson, Lynnette Lynes

         Mr. Creech called the meeting to order at 5:00 pm.

I.    Approval of Agenda

     Mr. Player moved for approval of the agenda. Mr. Bakker seconded the motion.
All in favor.

II.   Approval of Minutes

     Mr. Bakker moved for approval of the minutes. Mr. Player seconded the motion.
All in favor.

III. General Public Comment

     Ms. Barbara Smith, 410 Royall Ave, spoke regarding the expansion of the Old
Village Historic District. She stated that currently her property is not in the HD and
is opposed to her property to be included in the historic district as it would limit
the flexibility of the property and limit the opportunity for her descendants to live
in Mount Pleasant.

      There being no further comments, Mr. Creech continued with the agenda.

IV. Correspondence
V. Consent Motions

There being no correspondence or consent motions, Mr. Creech continued with the
agenda.
