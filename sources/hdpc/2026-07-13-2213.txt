H.   Discussion on docks and roofed structures on docks.

    Mr. Hankinson stated that this was requested for discussion. He stated that
any docks or features on a dock would be required to be approved by BCM.

    Mr. Player stated that eight of the 64 docks have roof structures. He asked the
reason for not allowing the roof structures and suggested that they should be
allowed. He suggested that a height limit, materials allowed, etc. should also be
determined. He asked that the requirements for OCRM should be provided as well
as surrounding municipalities.

    Ms. Gravitt expressed concern that it would set a precedent as well as would
be considered putting an accessory structure in the front yard, since the water is
considered a frontage.

   Mr. Foxworth expressed concern that it could open the door for more extensive
construction and could be difficult to enforce the restrictions.

     The Commission discussed the pros and cons of allowing the covered docks.

    Mr. Hankinson stated that he would provide the additional information at the
next meeting for further discussion if desired. The Commission agreed to complete
their own research on the subject and bring back to the Commission in the future
if necessary.
