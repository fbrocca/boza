VI.   Business-
      A. 320 McCants Drive - Survey 6085/ Non-Historic (TMS# 5320500204)
          Applicant is seeking Final Approval for pool and fencing. Deferred from
          previous meeting

         Ms. Miller reviewed the request with the Commission.

         Ms. Katie Duncan, Landscape Architect, reviewed the request with the
      Commission.

          Ms. Shannon Lofton, owner, stated that they would like to provide a safe
      place for their children to play.

          Mr. Andrew Lofton, owner, stated that their children’s safety while playing
      is their main concern and would like to have an enclosed space for them to
      play. He stated that the adjoining neighbors submitted letters in favor

          Mr. Brian Pazdziorny, 3695 Shutesbury Street, stated that he works for
      Aquatica Pools and helped with the design of the pool. He stated that the pool
      is designed low to the ground and will not be seen from the street.

      There being no other public comments, Mr. Crosland moved to Commission
      questions.

          Ms. Miller offered as rebuttal that the guidelines states that ideally the
      pool should be in the rear yard, with the Commission having purview over
      setbacks.

         Ms. Duncan suggested that with approval of the garage, the orientation of
      the property has been determined. She suggested that a detached garage
      would not normally be allowed in the front yard with a six foot setback.

         The Commission moved to comments and deliberation of the request.

          Mr. Barber moved for approval of the fence based on Fences and Walls #8,
      and denial of the pool based on Decks and Pools #4 that states that the
      streetscape should not be negatively impacted. Mr. Crosland seconded the

C.   208 Middle Street - Survey 6015/ Non-Historic (TMS# 5320900028)
     Applicant is seeking Preliminary Comment for full demolition with new
     construction of a primary and accessory structure and pool.

     Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     Ms. Liz Stanton reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Mr. Warren Player, 10 Pierates Cruz, stated that he has reviewed the plans
and walked the site. He stated that proposed orientation aligns with the house
a few houses down. He suggested that the proposed design would be an
improvement to the neighborhood.

    Ms. Alice Ann Richter, 214 Middle Street, suggested that the apartment is
an eyesore and should be removed. She suggested that an arborist report
should be submitted prior to allowing removal of the trees. She expressed
concern with drainage and that it could affect her property. She suggested
that the house seems forward from other homes on the street and would be
incongruous with the streetscape.

    Mr. Todd Beals, 202 Middle Street, expressed concern with the shared
easement and shared driveway and how this will affect access. He also
expressed concern with drainage and ensuring that there is no runoff to
surrounding properties.

    Joseph Serato, 214 Middle Street, expressed concern with the drainage,
particularly the close proximity of the proposed house to the property line and
their home.

    Ms. Stefany Beals, 202 Middle Street, discussed whether there will be a
fence located due to the easement. She stated that their irrigation system

removal and asked that as many trees be preserved as possible.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Crosland moved to Commission

D.    Vacant Lot (Lot 25-A) – No Survey / Non-Historic, Royall Avenue (TMS#
      5320900251) Applicant is seeing Final Approval for new construction,
      accessory structure, and pool.

     Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     Ms. Liz Stanton reviewed the application with the Commission.

     There being no comments, Mr. Crosland moved to questions.

    Mr. Phil Clarke suggested that the parking could be moved to the left-hand
side and if amenable the request approved with that contingency. He
suggested that the site plan could possibly be adjusted to better accommodate
the parking more to the rear.

    The Commission discussed if this could be done and that a site plan
showing the exact location of the parking would be needed. Mr. Crosland
suggested that an updated site plan would be needed in order to see the exact
location of the driveway.

    Ms. Miller stated that based on the discussion, the current request could
be denied in order for the applicant to restudy and change the site plan for
resubmittal. The changes to the site plan would probably constitute a
