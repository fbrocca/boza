     Ms. Miller called the meeting to order at 5:0 4 pm. Ms. Miller reviewed the
procedure with the public and stated that a temporary chair should be appointed.

    Mr. Gallogly moved for Ms. Montgomery as temporary chair. Mr. Crosland
seconded the motion. All in favor.

I.    Approval of Agenda

   Mr. Gallogly moved for approval of the agenda. Mr. Crosland seconded the
motion. All in favor.


II.   Approval of Minutes December 14, 2020

   Mr. Crosland moved for approval of the minutes. Mr. Gallogly seconded the
motion. All in favor.

III. Correspondence

   Ms. Miller stated that all correspondence was distributed to the Commission
and linked to the agenda.

IV. General Public Comment

    Ms. Joanna Keegan, 206 Live Oak Drive, expressed concern with the addition of
a fence at 204 Live Oak Drive. She suggested that the new fence is not warranted
as the existing fence coordinates with the other fencing and is in character with the
neighborhood. She asked that any fencing should be sensitive to protected trees

proposed fencing would potentially damage the historic trees on the lot and in the

    Mr. Mark Keegan, 206 Live Oak Drive, agreed with Ms. Keegan that the current

permit for 101 Mary Street. He stated that he is opposed to the dock and is in the
process of appealing this permit with OCRM. He asked that the Town rescind
approval of the encroachment permit. He asked that the Commission support
opposition to the construction of the dock.

V.   Consent Motions

There being no consent motions, Ms. Montgomery continued with the agenda.

VI. Business
    A. 237 Middle Street – Survey 6045/Non-Historic– (TMS # 532-09-00-022)
       Applicant is seeking Final Review for addition/alteration to previously
       approved plans.

        Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

        Ms. James Selvitelli, architect, reviewed the request with the Commission.

         Ms. Sharon Shell, 241 Middle Street, expressed concern with the proposed
     6 foot fence and suggested that it is not in keeping with the HDPC guidelines.
     She asked that the fence not be 6 foot tall. She also expressed concern with
     the variance for the accessory structure.

         Mr. Selvetelli stated that the variance for the accessory structure and the
     height of the fence was previously approved and is not being changed with
     this submittal.

     There being no further comment, Ms. Montgomery closed public comment.

       Mr. Colman thanked the Commission for consideration of this project.

IX.      Review, discuss and adopt amended Design Guidelines for Exterior Paint
         Colors

       Ms. Miller reviewed the proposed paint colors with the Commission.

    Mr. Colman thanked the Commission for this effort. He suggested that most of
the colors on the historic paint listing are more appropriate for the Charleston
historic district and not Mount Pleasant. He suggested that the paint color should
be more reviewed on an individual basis.

    Ms. Montgomery moved for approval of the paint colors as presented. Mr.
Gallogly seconded the motion. All in favor.

X.     Discussion on Historic District expansion

    Ms. Miller reviewed this with the Commission. She suggested that a more
complete list be proposed for any additional properties that should be included.
She stated that she would work with SHPO in determining which properties should
be included and would bring back to the Commission for a recommendation.

    Mr. Colman suggested that there are properties that could be designated
historic and should be considered for inclusion into the historic district so that they
are preserved.

    The Commission agreed that this be deferred to identify additional properties
for a more comprehensive list.

XI.    Adoption of 2021 Rules of Procedure (Bylaws)

    Mr. Gallogly moved for approval. Mr. Crosland seconded the motion. All in
favor.

XII.   Election of 2021 Chair and Vice-Chair

   Mr. Crosland moved to defer this item to the March meeting. Ms. Montgomery
seconded the motion. All in favor.

XIII. Discussion of Items for Next Agenda

The Commission agreed to discuss the following items at the next meeting:
   ➢ Update on CLG on historic district expansion
   ➢ Approval of materials for Porches and Stoops
   ➢ Signage design guidelines and ordinance

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 6:42 pm.
