VIII. Discussion on membership for seats on the HDPC.

    Mr. Rutherford reviewed a request from a Council member to consider changes
to the qualifications for the member seats by designating that two seats would be
at-large and not required to live within the historic district.

Ms. Carol Tinkey, 208 Venning Street, suggested that designated alternates should
be considered. The alternate would only be required to attend in the event there
is an issue regarding meeting quorum requirements or if a seat became vacant. She
stated that she is not in favor of having at-large seats for the Commission.

   The Commission agreed to not move forward with at-large seats.

IX. Commission training

    Ms. Julia Copeland, Town Attorney, addressed the Commission on the logistics
