VIII. Discussion of the Process of Expanding the Old Village Historic District

   Mr. Hankinson and Mr. Ward reviewed the current powers and duties regarding
the expansion of the historic district with the Commission.

    The Commission discussed whether a new survey is needed and if additional
areas outside of the current district should be included in the survey to determine
any properties of historic significance that should possibly be included in the
historic district.

   Mr. Creech moved to direct staff to draft a letter requesting a historic survey be
completed for the existing historic district, Royall Avenue and Whilden Street to
determine historic properties and possible expansion of the historic district
boundaries. Ms. Gravitt seconded the motion.

     Mr. Creech moved to amend the motion to include a survey of Simmons Street
in addition to the previously mentioned streets. Mr. Barber seconded the motion.
All in favor.

Mr. Creech called for a vote on the motion as amended. All in favor.

IX. Discussion of Items for Next Agenda

The Commission agreed to add Discussion of Amendments to the Guidelines for the
next agenda.
