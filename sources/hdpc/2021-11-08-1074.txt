IV. General Public Comment

    Mr. Chris Colman, 118 Pitt St, spoke regarding the proposed noise ordinance
and suggested that it is not stringent enough. He also expressed concern at 125
Pitt St and preservation of historical structures. He suggested that more care
should be taken to preserve more materials. He also spoke regarding amendments
to the ordinance and suggested that the non-conformity ordinance will have an
impact on the historic district and should be reviewed. He suggested that the
setbacks and conformity of historic property setbacks and should be previewed to
prevent any discrepancies that would affect the OV.

d. 206 Bank Street – Survey 6111 and .01/Historic – (TMS 532-05-00-009)
   Applicant is seeking Final Review for garage alterations and pool
   equipment re-location.

     Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

     Mr. Paul Kime reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning St, stated that where the stairs are located,
 there is artificial turf. He suggested that this should not be allowed and that
 impervious material should be considered.

     Ms. Millie Horton, 210 Bank St, expressed concern between what was
 approved compared to what has been constructed. She stated that the pool
 equipment was approved to be in a covered structure, but now is not and is
 within four inches of the property fence. She also expressed concern with
 the overall drainage and the pervious/impervious ratio. She expressed
 concern with drainage and that Bank Street has had drainage issues in the
 past. She expressed concern with the lot coverage and the amount of
 impervious surface being allowed.

    There being no further comments, Ms. Montgomery closed public
 comment.

    Ms. Netter stated that if preferred, they would be willing to have sod as
 opposed to the artificial turf in order to have the pool equipment and stairs.
