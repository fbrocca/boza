All in favor.

3.   General Public Comment

    Ms. Barbara Smith, 410 Royall Ave, spoke regarding the proposal to expand the
historic district. She stated that there are a number of residents that are opposed
to the expansion as evidenced by signing a petition in opposition. She asked that
the expansion should not be considered and that it is an unwise use of taxpayer
funds. She asked that the reason for wanting the expansion should be provided to
her and the public. Additionally, she would like to see a copy of the letter to Town
Council requesting the expansion and historic district survey.

   Mr. Chuck Olmstead, 520 Royall Ave, expressed concern with changing the
boundaries of the historic district. He stated that he is opposed to the expansion
and does not see a need for the historic district to be expanded.

    Mr. Daniel Brownstein, 1391 Downsbury Drive, thanked the Commission for
their service.

9.    Action Vote on OVHD Survey Recommendation Letter

     Mr. Hankinson reviewed the draft letter with the Commission.

    Mr. Creech moved for approval of sending a letter to Town Council

second.

a 3 to 1 vote with Mr. Bakker, Ms. Gravitt, and Mr. Player in favor; Mr. Creech
opposed.
