VI. Appeal of Staff Level Approval - 416 Church Street – Survey 6194/Non-
    Historic – (TMS 532-01-00-097) Remove existing style fence, replace with
    wood fencing.

   Ms. Shaw recused herself due to a conflict of interest.

   Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

    Ms. Ansley Tolman, representative for Nicole Dickens, and Ms. Nicole Dickens
reviewed the appeal with the Commission through a PowerPoint presentation.

     Mr. Joe Schuster stated that the fence was not installed per the guidelines as it
was erected with the “bad” side of the fence facing her property and exceeds the
height limitation. He asked that at a minimum the fence should be reviewed by the
full board. He also asked that the whole in the fence should be repaired. He
suggested that an after-the-fact approval that does not meet the ordinances or
guidelines should be allowed or approved.

   Mr. Dan Shaw, homeowner, stated that they tried to have a living fence on the
property but it did not grow. He stated that the fence was taken down because it
was not thriving. He stated that there have been many disputes with Ms. Dickens.

  Mr. Crosland moved to convene into executive session for legal advice. Ms.
Montgomery seconded the motion. All in favor.

The Commission convened into executive session for legal advice at 5:29 pm and
reconvened at 5:37 pm. No action taken while in executive session.

    Ms. Tolman rebutted that staff cannot give an after-the-fact approval. She
stated that it the staff approval should be reversed.

     Ms. Miller rebutted that after-the-fact applications have been received and
approved in the past and are allowed when “mistakes” occur. She suggested that
this is a code enforcement issue and civil issue.

   There being no further comments, Ms. Montgomery closed public comment.

   Mr. Crosland moved for denial of the appeal and to uphold staff's approval as it
was handled according to procedure. Mr. Gallogly seconded the motion. All in favor
with Ms. Shaw abstaining.
