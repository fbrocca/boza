IV. General Public Comment

     Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning, spoke regarding staff approvals. He suggested
that the procedures for staff approvals should be reviewed and amended to allow
review of staff approvals when deemed necessary. He referred to 101 Middle
Street and suggested that the guidelines state that a roof or roof structure shall be
permitted. He suggested that unless it can be shown that the roof was legally
constructed, any changes or replacement should not be allowed. He asked that the
staff approvals be deferred for this month until the review has been completed. He
also referred to the fence at 101 Pitt Street has been constructed at 8 feet, 1 inches
instead of having a 48 inch fence as stated in the guidelines.

VI. Business -
    a. 234 Middle Street- Survey#6011/Non-Historic (TMS#532-09-00-025)
       Applicant is seeking final approval for revisions to previously approved
       plans. Deferred from last meeting

        Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

        Mr. Chuck Baker, representative for applicant, Ms. Julie O’Connor, and Mr.
     Cameron Baker reviewed the request with the Commission.

         Ms. Polly Player, 10 Pierates Cruz, spoke regarding setbacks and referred
     to the sections of the Town Code. She suggested that the home would be 16
     feet forward of other homes on the street and suggested that this would not
     meet the ordinance or guidelines requirements. She asked that the
     application be denied.

         Ms. Carol Burns, 9 Pierates Cruz, expressed concern with the signage and
     windows that are currently painted orange. She read the ordinance referring
     to a street block and suggested that the homes located on corner lots should
     not be included for justification of streetscape. She stated that other homes
     on the street were denied having the homes closer to the street as it was
     inharmonious with the neighborhood. She suggested that the request should
     be denied.

         Mr. David Creech, 212 Center St., expressed concern with the
     enhancement of the historic district and that the tree protection is part of the

enhancement of the district. He expressed concern that the reduced tree
protection area would be a detriment to the tree. He also expressed concern
with the pitch of the main roofline and that it would affect the limbs of the
tree. He asked that the house be allowed to be moved back on the lot to
further protect the tree.

    Ms. Anita Yantis, 155 Wingo Way, expressed concern with the comments
that have been raised on this request. She stated that her understanding was
that the only issue was the connection of the garage.

    Mr. Paul Brady, 226 Middle St, asked that the streetscape be honored and
the home align with the other homes on the street. He stated that he is
opposed to the garage as it is too close to the property line. He expressed
concern with drainage and that there is not a comprehensive plan for this area
to address the drainage. He suggested that the home should be placed farther
back on the lot in order to better address the drainage. He expressed concern
with the home lying dormant for the past three years.

    Ms. Melissa Levesque, 4068 Harleston Green Lane, stated that she is in
favor of the request. She stated that there is some angst with issues that are
not in the Commission’s purview that have already been settled and approved.
She suggested that there is a balance that should be achieved between
property rights, excellent, compatible architecture and being too restrictive.

    Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning St, spoke regarding protection of the historic
tree. He suggested that the site plan is deceiving as the tree would be trimmed
to avoid the home. He suggested that rules and regulations should be upheld
and that allowing the reduced setback, would damage the tree.

    Mr. Warren Player, 10 Pierates Cruz, suggested that the request should be
denied strictly on the streetscape and setback requirements. He spoke
regarding drainage and that the requirements state that construction should
be accomplished without negatively impacting or damaging neighboring
properties. He suggested that the Town should encourage the owner to install
a pump to handle the stormwater runoff and it should not be the neighboring
property owner’s responsibility to pump off the stormwater. He suggested
that the existing drainage basin would be affected.

         Ms. Cheryl Shoun, 241 Middle Street, and attorney representing interested
     parties, asked for those in opposition of the request to signify by a show of
     hands. She suggested that the Commission should require adherence to the
     ordinances and guidelines. She expressed concern with the pool and
     suggested that it is not a right but a privilege and does not have to be
     approved. She suggested that there has not been a taking by eminent domain
     as suggested by the applicant. She suggested that a hardship cannot be used
     when purchasing the property.

          Ms. Jeannie Powers, 163 Harbor Watch Way, suggested that often it is
     difficult to build on older lots because of established trees. She read a letter
     from Ms. Donna Liptak for the Commission.

     There being no comments, Ms. Montgomery moved to Commission questions.

         Ms. Montgomery moved to staff and applicant rebuttals.

        There being no further comments, Ms. Montgomery closed public
     comment.

        Mr. Crosland moved for denial of the request. Mr. Gallogly seconded the
     motion. All in favor.
