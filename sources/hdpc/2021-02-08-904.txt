VI. Appeal to the Commission of Staff Level COA for fencing at 204 Live Oak Drive

   Ms. Miller reviewed the appeal procedures with the Commission and public.

   Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

   Mr. Mark Ghegan and Ms. Joanna Ghegan, 206 Live Oak Drive, reviewed their
appeal with the Commission.

   There being no comments, Ms. Montgomery closed public comment.

    Ms. Montgomery moved that the record is insufficient for review as it is unclear
where the proposed live fence would be located that this be remanded back to staff
for clarification and rehearing. Mr. Crosland seconded the motion.

    Mr. Barber moved to amend the motion to provide a site plan including 206 Live
Oak Dr. Ms. Montgomery seconded the amendment. Amendment passed on a 3
to 1 vote with Mr. Barber, Mr. Gallogly, and Ms. Montgomery in favor; Mr. Crosland
opposed.

   Ms. Montgomery called for a vote on the motion as amended. All in favor.

Mr. Deford noted that the rehearing must be heard within 60 days.

IX. Discussion on Historic District expansion

    Ms. Miller updated the Commission on the process for expanding the historic
district and information received from SHPO. She suggested that the Commission
defer discussion until the Settlement Community Task Force has made their
recommendations.

    Ms. Montgomery moved for staff to investigate dwellings in the Old Mount
Pleasant area encompassing the area from Whilden Street to Coleman Boulevard
and Ben Sawyer Boulevard, and Center Street and Royall Avenue. Mr. Barber
seconded the motion. All in favor.
