VI. Business- STAFF REPORT
    A. 616 Pitt Street – Historic 5947 – (TMS 532-05-00-043) Applicant is seeking
       Final Review to replace the garage’s eight-foot wide doors with nine-foot
       wide doors.

         Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission
    (attachment 1).

    Mr. Robert Smith, representative for applicant reviewed the request. He
    stated that they would like to change out the existing doors for nine-foot
    painted composite door with divided light windows at the top.

    There being no comments, Mr. Hirshorn closed public comment.

    Ms. Rowe asked about the door material. Mr. Smith stated the doors would
    be a white composite door. Ms. Rowe expressed concern with the doors being
    changed from wood to a composite.

    Mr. Hirshorn stated that the original approval of the garage was conditioned
    on having the wood doors. He suggested that the wood doors should remain.

    Ms. Montgomery suggested that a wood door could be used and allowed to
    be painted white. Ms. Rowe suggested that painting the door would change
    the look of the door.

    Mr. Rutherford stated that the Commission cannot stipulate a particular paint
    color; the paint color must only meet the guidelines of being a historic paint
    color.

    Mr. Hirshorn suggested that a nine-foot wooden door might be a solution.
