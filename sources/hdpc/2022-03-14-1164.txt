    c. 234 Middle Street - Survey 6011/ Non-Historic (TMS# 532-09-00-025)
       Applicant is seeking Final Approval for demolition, new construction,
       accessory structure, and pool.

        Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

       Ms. Julie O’Connor, architect, and Mr. Craig Thomas, owner, reviewed the
    request with the Commission. 155 Wingo Way

        Ms. Cheryl Shoun, 241 Middle St, stated that architectural ambiance of the
    Town and historic district should be upheld. She stated that the ordinance
    stated that the setbacks and guidelines should be upheld when considering a
    request and buildings should be harmonious with the existing streetscape.
    She suggested that this request would destroy the existing streetscape.

        Mr. Warren Player, 10 Pierates Cruz, stated that the drainage in this area
    has been an issue for many years. He suggested that the neighboring homes
    have been built to meet the streetscape setbacks. He suggested that this
    request does not meet the guidelines. He stated that a drainage plan has not
    been submitted to date and suggested that drainage can be designed and
    engineered so that it does not affect the neighboring property. He suggested
    that the drainage plan should be approved prior to the house design being
    approved. He suggested that the setbacks can be met by designing a smaller
    home and eliminating the pool. He asked that the request be denied.

       Mr. David Creech, 212 Center St, he suggested that in looking at the design,
    a portion of the home was not meeting the setbacks.

    Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning, suggested that the drainage plan and TPZ
plan should be considered with the house design as they would have an effect.
He suggested that the approved TPZ appears to not meet the variance. He
expressed concern with preservation of the historic tree and that there should
be an arborist to monitor the construction of the home to ensure the historic
tree is not compromised.

   Ms. Miller reiterated her comments for the Commission.

    Ms. O’Connor rebutted that the homeowners have worked on this design
for over a year and the plan before the Commission is the best design given all
the constraints.

   There being no further comments, Ms. Montgomery closed public
comment.

     Mr. Crosland moved for a continuance to next month for more information
on the drainage in regard to setback relief. Mr. Barber seconded the motion.
All in favor.
