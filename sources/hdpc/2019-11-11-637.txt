VI. Consideration of request for an encroachment permit for dock at 101 Mary
    Street. (This item referred to OVHDC from Transportation Committee of
    Town Council on September 30, 2019, Continued by OVHDC on October 14,
    2019) – Encroachment Application, Staff Report, Correspondence

     Mr. Rutherford reviewed the staff report with the Commission (attachment 1).

Mr. Lewis Seabrook, 1037-F Chuck Dawley Blvd., reviewed the application with the
Commission.

Ms. Molly Fair, previous owner of 101 Mary St, stated that this is not a new
structure, but rebuilding the dock. She stated that this is part of a dock master plan
for several of the lots on this block. She suggested that the request would not affect
the character of the Old Village and asked the Commission to approve the
rebuilding of the dock.

Mr. Curtis Ravenel, owner of 101 Mary Street, stated that the dock should be
considered a historic area and asked the Commission to approve the dock.
                                                Old Village Historic District Commission
                                                                     November 11, 2019
                                                                               Page 3 of 6

Mr. Chris Coleman, 118 Pitt Street, stated that the dock has not been in use since
Hurricane Hugo. He suggested that the Commission does not have purview to
approve structures encroaching on Town or public right-of-way. He asked the
Commission to deny the request for the proposed dock.

Mr. Hamlin O’Kelly, 652 Coleman Blvd., attorney for Ms. Fair and Mr. Ravenel,
stated that as explained by staff, the Commission has purview to approve moving
forward with submittal of the encroachment permit. He stated that the
Commission received correspondence in favor of the request and asked the
Commission to approve the encroachment permit.

Mr. Martin Bluford, 132 Hibben Street, expressed concern with what the impact
this would have for the area and the neighborhood. He suggested that a better
plan should be submitted showing exactly what would be constructed and how this
would impact the view.

Ms. Elizabeth Middour, 200 Hibben Street, expressed concern with the request for
a dock and suggested that it would impede the view of the harbor from the street.
She suggested that this would impact the view of the vista. She stated that she is
opposed to the request and asked that the Commission deny the request.

There being no further comments, Ms. Rowe closed public comment.

   Mr. Hackenberg moved to deny the request. Ms. Montgomery second the
motion. All in favor.
