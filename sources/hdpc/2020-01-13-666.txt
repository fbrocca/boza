XI. Discussion Regarding Paint Color in the Old Village Historic District.

   Ms. Dolan stated that this was added for discussion by a Commission member.

The Commission discussed whether paint color should be reviewed and approved
by the Commission.

Mr. Chris Colman, 118 Pitt Street, shared he is in possession of many books on
home colors and he has offered to share the books with the Commission.

Ms. Carol Tinkey, 208 Venning Street, asked what happened to the guidelines and
ordinance that the residents worked on with staff. She suggested that the
Commission veered from referencing them. Ms. Tinkey suggested that Mount
Pleasant should be the community that others look to for guidance regarding
historic district guidelines rather than Mount Pleasant looking to other
communities on what they are doing. She suggested that the reasons for
Commission decisions should be justified and the specific guidelines or ordinances
should be referenced when making motions.

The Commission agreed that paint colors should be approved by the Commission
and asked staff to bring back a color palette and verbiage on how to address the
painting of brick for the next meeting.
