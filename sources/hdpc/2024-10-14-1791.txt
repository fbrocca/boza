G.    HDPC-026-2024, 109 CENTER ST, Survey 6059/Non-Historic, TMS#
      5321300001. Applicant is seeking Final Review for changes to a prior COA.

     Mr. Hankinson reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

    Ms. Melissa Voorhees, Ms. Cindy Klein, and Ms. Kate Campbell reviewed the
request with the Commission.

    Mr. Henry West, 116 Center St, stated that they are opposed to the extension of
the back porch at the outdoor kitchen. He suggested that the scale and size of the
structure is closer to the street, over the setback, and “bulky”. He stated that they
would like to have that portion removed as it was constructed after the last meeting.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Creech closed the public comment and
moved to Commission questions.

    Mr. Player moved for denial of the decking and to remove the portion of the
decking not approved by the Commission. Mr. Barber seconded the motion.

    Mr. Barber moved to amend the motion to include the findings that the deck
addition impacts the streetscape, which is the reason for denial. Mr. Player seconded
the motion.

    Mr. Barber moved to amend the motion for approval of the at-grade fence.
Mr. Creech seconded the motion. All in favor.

   Mr. Creech called for a vote on the amendment to include the findings for
denial of the decking. All in favor.

historic district and if any changes should be considered.

structure and to have further discussion at a future meeting.
