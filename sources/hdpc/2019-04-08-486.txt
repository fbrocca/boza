VIII. Recommendation to the Transportation Committee of Town Council for the
      Design of New Street Name Signs within the Old Village Historic District -
      CORRESPONDENCE

   Mr. Morrison stated that he is available to answer any questions regarding the
Town’s street sign replacement program.

Ms. Rowe expressed concern with conflicting information the Commission has
received regarding whether the historic district is exempted from the program. She
stated that comments from the public have indicated that the historic district is
exempt, while staff has indicated that this is not the case. Mr. Morrison reviewed
the sign replacement program with the Commission and stated that this was
approved by Town Council in 2016. He stated that the program included
replacement of all street signs within the Town to comply with the federal
requirements. He noted that the existing obelisks would not be removed.

Mr. Louis Horton, 138 Live Oak Drive, distributed a picture of the corner of Live Oak
Drive and Magwood Lane showing the number of signs located at that intersection
and suggested that this was sign clutter. He suggested that the signs are not
needed and should not be installed.

Mr. Brett Bennett, 131 Hibben Street, stated that he is opposed to the street signs
being installed throughout the historic district. He suggested that the federal
requirements offer an exemption for historic districts. He stated that he has
contacted surrounding municipalities such as the Town of Sullivan’s Island and they
have indicated that they have no intention of replacing signs in their historic
districts. He suggested that the historic district should be exempted from the
program and the street signs should not be installed.

Mr. Joe Keenan, 902 Pitt Street, expressed concern with the conflicting information
on the exemption. He suggested that the Commission should maintain the integrity
of the historic district. He suggested that instead of spending funds for
                                                Old Village Historic District Commission
                                                                             April 8, 2019
                                                                               Page 8 of 8

replacement signs, the money should be spent on resurfacing streets in the historic
district such as Pitt Street. He asked the Commission to deny the request.

Mr. Myles Martschink, 135 Toomer Lane, stated he submitted a letter outlining his
reasons why the Commission should deny the request. He suggested that the
Commission should receive legal advice on whether the signs are required to be
installed in the historic district.

Mr. Rutherford stated the installation of the signs is not in the purview of the
Commission. He stated that the reason for this issue being brought before the
Commission was to determine if a special street sign design was desired by the
Commission. He stated that the replacement sign program has been approved by
Town Council and staff is moving forward with implementation of that program.
He suggested that if desired, the Commission can send back a recommendation to
the Transportation Committee regarding this issue.

    Ms. Montgomery moved that the OVHDC will not consider any such changes or
designs until ordered to do so from the Town Council through the Transportation
Committee. Ms. Dovre second. All in favor.
