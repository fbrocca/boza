B.   109 Center Street – Survey 6059 and 6059.01/ Non-Historic (TMS#
     5321300001) Preliminary Review for demolition, new construction,
     accessory structure and pool.

     Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

    Ms. Kate Campbell, Beau Clowney Architects, reviewed the request with
the Commission.

    Mr. Crosland moved to approve submission of additional information from
the applicant. Mr. Gallogly seconded the motion. All in favor.

    Mr. Henry West, 116 Center St, expressed concern with encroachment of
the setbacks. He suggested hat the restriction of the setbacks is what helps
maintain the character of the neighborhood. He expressed concern with the
massing of the proposed structures. He expressed concern with the view
corridors being changed along the harbor with the proposed structures. He
suggested that the openness of the areas along the harbor and streets should
be preserved.

    Mr. Dave King, Center Street, expressed concern with a wall being built as
part of the proposed development. He suggested that this would be out of
character of the neighborhood.

    Mr. Warren Player, 10 Pierates Cruz, suggested that the subdivision is being
done to subvert the guidelines and requirements. He suggested that the
structure facing Middle Street is essentially a garage. He suggested that there
are several setback encroachments that would be needed for the proposed
structures. He suggested that the design should be reconsidered.
