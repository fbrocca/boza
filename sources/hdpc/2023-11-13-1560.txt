P.    131 Middle Street – Survey 6024/Historic – (TMS 5320900005 and
      5320900032) Applicant is seeking Final Review for construction,
      alterations, and renovations of Alhambra Hall and the surrounding park.

     Ms. Miller reviewed the application with the Commission.

   Mr. Frankie Pettit, Public Services Dept, reviewed the application with the
Commission.

    Mr. Mike Tinkey 208 Venning, thanked the commission and staff for their
work on this project. He asked that the park should be staged to allow use of
the park and Alhambra Hall during construction. He suggested that the
waterfront area should be considered resilient and water quality tested to
determine if the area could be used oyster bed restoration. He asked that the
tree between Alhambra Hall and the park be protected and possibly have
angled parking to better protect those roots.

    Ms. Janet Newham, 510 Pitt Street, expressed concern with the Fourth of
July parade being held during the construction and asked that this be
considered in the construction plans.

    Mr. Brian Henson, 1026 Cliffwood drive, expressed concern with the
playground and accessibility for special needs so that all children can play. He
asked that the equipment and areas surrounding the playground be accessible
and meet ADA requirements.
