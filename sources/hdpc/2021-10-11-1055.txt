Staff Report

Present:       Louisa Montgomery, Chair, Corey Barber, Anne Shaw.
Absent:        Robert Crosland, Gene Gallogly.

        Ms. Montgomery called the meeting to order at 5:01 pm.

I.     Approval of Agenda

     Mr. Barber moved for approval of the agenda. Ms. Shaw seconded the motion.
All in favor.

II.    Approval of Minutes September 13, 2021 and September 20, 2021

     Mr. Barber moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Shaw seconded the motion.
All in favor.

III. Correspondence

Ms. Miller stated that no correspondence was received.

IV. General Public Comment

       Mr. Chris Colman, 118 Pitt St, asked that the Commission consider
recommending changes to the ordinances. He suggested that there are quality of
life issues affecting the Old Village Historic District, particularly the noise from the
Post House. He suggested that the non-conformity ordinance should be reviewed
and changed, particularly with the building on lot lines and setbacks. He suggested
that this should be reviewed and changes made.

There being no further comments, Ms. Montgomery continued with the agenda.
