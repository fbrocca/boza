D.   121 Live Oak Dr., Survey Card 6245/Historic – TMS 5171500060,
     Preliminary Review for the construction of addition to a historic primary
     structure. Correspondence

     Mr. Hankinson reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

    Ms. Nicole Lee and Ms. Kate Campbell, Beau Clowney Architects, reviewed
the request with the Commission.

   Ms. Jeane Welsh, 716 Bradford, stated that this request should be denied
and the encroachment not approved.

    Ms. Catherine Poston, owner, stated that she likes everything about the
new home; however, the stairs are steep and difficult for her and her mother
to navigate. She stated that due to several foot issues, surgeries, and post-
surgery infections means she is unable to navigate the stairs as currently
configured.

     There being no further comments, Ms. Gravitt closed public comment.

    Mr. Craft asked the amount of the encroachment and the driveway. Ms.
Campbell answered that the driveway is adjacent to the lot. Mr. Craft asked
about the magnolias. Ms. Campbell answered that the magnolias are near the
retaining wall and are located on the property. Mr. Craft suggested that the
side yard is narrow, given the depth of the lot.

    Ms. Gravitt suggested that the hyphen does not meet the guidelines as it
does not appear subordinate to the main structure. Ms. Campbell answered
that at the last meeting, the detached addition was not desired and the reason
for the hyphen.

    Ms. Edwards asked if the applicant considered putting the elevator to the
rear of the home, possibly near the fireplace or instead of the fireplace. Ms.
Campbell answered that because of the grade change, having the elevator to
the rear of the home would mean having to navigate stairs and the reason for
having the addition and the proposed location of the elevator.

    Mr. Player suggested that there should be a restudy of the plan to
determine if the addition could be moved so it is not encroaching into the
setback.

    Ms. Gravitt agreed and stated that the hyphen has been treated well, and
the massing and scale is appropriate.

    Mr. Craft noted that there is a lot going on with the side of the lot with the
driveway and the addition. Mr. Player noted that this is also a reason for the
comments from the neighbors. Ms. Campbell answered that they would
review this. Mr. Craft suggested that addition is a sensitive solution if the
encroachment could be addressed. He suggested that the fireplace is another
nice addition and helps to “clean the whole house up”.

    Mr. Player asked if the shutters were changed. Ms. Campbell answered in
the affirmative and stated that they did find photographic evidence that the
existing shutters were not original so they were changed for this submittal.

   Mr. Craft suggested that there should be a horizontal pier at the hyphen.
Ms. Campbell agreed and stated that this would be reviewed as well.

     Ms. Edwards asked about the roof near the stairs. Ms. Lee answered that
it is the overhang for the door.

    Mr. Craft suggested that the width of the hyphen and addition could be
narrowed to eliminate the encroachment.

    Ms. Edwards asked about the stairs at the hyphen. Ms. Campbell
answered that the stairs were eliminated to lessen the hyphen.

    Ms. Campbell asked for clarification if the Commission was comfortable
with the single roof above the front door.

   Mr. Hankinson clarified the guidelines regarding encroachments.

   Mr. Craft stated that the front roof line is a good correction. The
Commission agreed.
