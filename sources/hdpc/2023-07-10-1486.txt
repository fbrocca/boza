VIII.      Discuss possibility to determine building line/front yard setbacks and discuss
           screened front porches.

          Ms. Miller reviewed the current ordinance and design guidelines with the
       Commission as it pertains to front yard setbacks.

          The Commission discussed providing clarification to the guidelines regarding
       how setbacks are applied and in determining setbacks.

           The Commission discussed screened porches and if they are appropriate for the
       front façade. The Commission agreed that screened porches were appropriate.

 IX.       Discuss and possibly amend Rules of Procedure regarding closing public
           comment.

           Mr. Crosland moved for approval to move item H of the meeting procedures to
       item E and other remaining meeting procedures accordingly renumbered. Mr.
       Barber seconded the motion. All in favor.

  X.       Discussion of Items for Next Agenda

       The Commission agreed the following items should be included on the next agenda:
          ➢ Discussion and possible amendment to preliminary comment procedures
          ➢ Discussion and action on the proposed changes to the guidelines on setbacks
             and screened porches

       There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 7:35 pm.
