      for a new home and accessory pool. Correspondence.

     Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission
(attachment 1).

     Ms. Heather Wilson reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Mr. Jeff Kirkland, 817 Pitt St, expressed concern with the drainage. He
stated that his workshop is adjacent to the proposed pool and expressed
concern that because this is the lowest point of the property, there would be
additional drainage. He suggested that there should be some type of catch

    basin in this area. He asked that approval for the pool be deferred until the
    drainage for the lot can be determined.

        Mr. Lowell Grove, 833 Pitt St, expressed concern with the live oak that is
    adjacent to his lot that would impact this property as well. He expressed
    concern that the home might encroach into the protection zone. He agreed
    with Mr. Kirkland that all drainage approvals should be completed before
    approval of any additional comments.

       Ms. Balzac expressed concern with tree protection and drainage issues and
    suggested that a very structured system should be in place and adhered to
    preserve the tree and mitigate any drainage.

    before he could submit to the Commission and asked if this process changed.

        There being no further comments, Mr. Crosland closed public comment.

        Mr. Barber moved for approval with the condition that the size of the porch
    be reconsidered. Point of order noted that the motion is not valid as it is in
    direct opposition to the design guidelines.

        Mr. Crosland moved for approval with the condition that the fence is
    approved at staff level. Mr. Hackenberg seconded the motion. Motion failed
    on a 2 to 2 vote with Mr. Hackenberg and Mr. Crosland in favor; Mr. Barber
    and Mr. Gallogly opposed.

        Mr. Barber moved for approval as it meets the guidelines with staff
    approval of fencing and additional front porch footage as presented by the
    applicant at the meeting. Mr. Crosland seconded the motion. Motion passed
    on a 3 to 1 vote with Mr. Barber, Mr. Hackenberg, and Mr. Crosland in favor;

VIII. Review, discussion, and possible adoption of revised Design Guidelines for
      paint and allowing more items for Staff Approval through Minor Work
      applications.

   Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

    Mr. Maiden stated that he supports more items being approved at staff level.
He expressed concern with limiting paint colors to Charleston historic colors and
suggested that other historic colors such as those allowed within Sullivan’s Island
should be considered. He suggested that the requirement for a building permit for
accessory structures should be reviewed so that more accessory structures must
be scrutinized.

   Ms. Balzac agreed that more items should be allowed staff level approval.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Crosland closed public comment.

    Mr. Barber moved for approval of the revised guidelines as presented. Mr.
Crosland seconded the motion. All in favor.
