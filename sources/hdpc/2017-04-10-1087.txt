VI. Request for Guideline Consultant Discussion
    Mr. Pohlman stated that this item was placed on the agenda by the
    Planning Director, Christiane Farrell. It was to update the Commission
    members on some of the ordinance changes requested through the
    guideline revisions in November and also require additional guidance
    from Commission members in reference to re-doing the guidelines
    again. Mr. Pohlman stated that there are three ordinances that will be
    going to the Planning Commission that will be posted on late
    Wednesday or early Thursday which will be the alteration of accessory
    structure heights to a maximum of 20 feet and 1 ½ stories tall, as well as
    requiring these structures to reside in the rear of the property. He
    stated that there are also the two separate parking amendments
    requested by the Commission that will be put into one ordinance. The
    first one being the requiring of two, instead of one off-street parking
    space, as well as an additional space for every 1,000 gross square feet
    over 2,500 square feet. Since that is gross, it would include a pool house
    or accessory dwelling unit. As well as revising wording of the allowance
    of one on-street parking space per every lot, with approvals of SCDOT as
    well as OVHD. In a recent review of the guidelines, there was some
    ambiguity as to whether or not the OVHDC should have been reviewing
    and approving these. He stated that the Commission has been doing so
    historically and will be should the ordinance pass.
