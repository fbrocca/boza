IV. General Public Comment

    Mr. Jeff Kirkland, 817 Pitt Street, asked on who to contact when a neighbor has
an issue with work being done. Ms. Montgomery suggested that any concerns or
issues with property in the historic district can be reported to the PLAN
Department. She stated that those issues not under the purview of the PLAN
Department will be forwarded to the appropriate department. Ms. Miller
concurred and stated that the PLAN Department and the Building Inspections
Office work closely together on any issues within the historic district. He also
expressed concern with drainage in the Pitt Street area.

With there being no further public comment, Ms. Montgomery continued with the
agenda.
