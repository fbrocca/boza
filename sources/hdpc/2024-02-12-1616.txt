STAFF REPORT

Present:       David Creech, Chair, James Bakker, Meghan Gravitt, Corey Barber,
               Warren Player.

I.    Approval of Agenda

     Mr. Player moved for approval of the agenda. Mr. Barber seconded the motion.
All in favor.

II.   Approval of Minutes January 8, 2024

     Mr. Player moved for approval of the minutes. Mr. Barber seconded the motion.
All in favor.

   Ms. Janet Newham, 510 Pitt Street, spoke regarding the historic designation of
Edwards Park and briefly reviewed the history of Edwards Park with the
Commission. She stated that the community is working with the Historical
Commission on a historical marker.

IV. Correspondence

All correspondence was linked to the agenda.

V.    Consent Motions

There being no consent motions, Mr. Creech continued with the agenda.

       Mr. Creech moved for denial as it does not meet the guidelines. Mr. Player
    seconded the motion. All in favor.

    f. 131 Middle Street – Survey 6024/Historic – (TMS 5320900005) Applicant
       is seeking Final Review for Alhambra Hall playground fencing installation
       and parking.

       Mr. Hankinson and Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the
    Commission.

       Mr. Andy Weis and Mr. Frankie Pettit reviewed the request with the
    Commission.

        Ms. Sara Wendell, 735 Pitt Street, stated that they enjoy having a gate in
    the fence and would like the gate to remain with the new fence. She stated
    that the ATSM guidelines are not a requirement. She suggested that the
    equipment being spaced out throughout the park is preferred, with the
    pavilion being moved from the center of the park.

    There being no further comments, Mr. Creech closed public comment and
    moved to Commission questions.

       Mr. Creech moved for denial, with applicant to come back with a 42 inch
    wood fence design to surround the entire site, with approval of the parking.
    Mr. Player seconded the motion.

        Mr. Bakker moved to amend the motion for the applicant to come back
    with a 48 inch fence for the entire site. Mr. Barber seconded the motion. All in
    favor.

    Mr. Creech called for a vote on the motion as amended. All in favor with Mr.
    Barber abstaining.

The Commission convened for a short break at 7:34 pm and reconvened at
7:38 pm.
