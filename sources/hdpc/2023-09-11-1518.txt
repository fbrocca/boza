III. General Public Comment

    Ms. Louisa Montgomery, 404 Church St, suggested that the new residents of
the historic district should be given a copy of the guidelines that can be shared with
their contractor. She suggested this would help to alleviate misunderstandings
regarding work within the historic district and for any renovations.

    Ms. Janet Newham, 510 Pitt St, suggested that there are plans that have been
approved that are not congruent with the historic district. She suggested that Ms.
McIver, Ms. Susannah Smith-Myles, and Ms. Mary Julia Royall historical books
should be available as a reference of the history of Mount Pleasant. She referenced
the history of the Darby Building, Greenwich Village, and Royall Ave.

     Mr. Kenny Craft, 204 Spooner Lane, expressed concern with the historic
architecture within the historic district and that there has been a deviation from
classic historic architecture. He referenced the pork chop eave and that it was not
widely used until 1925. He suggested that having a more resolved eave is a better

VIII. Vote on HDPC Rules of Procedure Revisions

   Mr. Hankinson and Mr. Ward reviewed the changes to the meeting procedures
with the Commission.

    Mr. Crosland moved for approval of meeting procedures #6 to read that there
is no time limit for Commission questions and recommendations or staff/applicant
responses. Mr. Barber seconded the motion. All in favor.

IX. Discussion of shed roof requirements in HDPC Guidelines.

    The Commission discussed whether shed roofs should be allowed within the
historic district.

    The Commission agreed that shed roofs should be allowed for porches and as
an option for gabled dormers and directed staff to bring back draft language for
inclusion in the guidelines.
