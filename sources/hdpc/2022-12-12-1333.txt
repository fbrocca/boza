      b.   320 McCants Drive- Survey 6085/ Non-Historic (TMS# 532-05-00-204)
           Applicant is seeking Final Approval for pool and fencing

           Ms. Forbus reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

           Ms. Katie Duncan, designer, reviewed the request with the Commission.

           There being no comments, Ms. Montgomery closed public comment.

           Mr. Crosland moved for denial based on the pool not meeting guidelines as
