III. Public Comment & General Correspondence

     Mr. Jim Berg, 402 Patjens Lane, expressed concern with parking in the Old
Village Historic District (OVHD). He suggested the Commission consider asking
Town Council to strengthen the parking requirements in OVHD similar to what the
City of Charleston has done in the downtown area such as time limits, signage, etc.

Mr. Hirshorn thanked Mr. Berg for his comments and agreed that this issue should
be further discussed by the Commission. Ms. Rowe agreed with Mr. Hirshorn.

Mr. Miles Martschink 135 Toomer Lane expressed concern with parking as well. He
stated that it involves more than parking for businesses and residences; the
overflow is destroying the right-of-way and affecting the aesthetics of the OVHD.
He suggested that more than one parking space be required. He also suggested
upholding the requirements of the OVHD such as parking to the rear and limiting
other hardscape would help with aesthetics and drainage issues.

Mr. Rutherford clarified that the ordinance for new construction requires a
minimum of two parking spaces and for every 1,000 square feet and an additional
space for each 1,000 square feet above 2,500 square feet.

Mr. Hirshorn suggested a parking study might be appropriate. Mr. Rutherford
stated this issue has been addressed in the new Comprehensive Plan update. Mr.
Hirshorn stated that he would speak with staff to determine the proper procedure
going forward.

6.    107 Carr Street – Survey 6108/6108.01 – (TMS 532-05-00-051) Applicant
      is seeking Final Review on a new single-family home, accessory dwelling
      unit, detached garage, and pool construction with demolition of the
      existing single-family home and detached garage.

      Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with Commission (attachment
1).

Mr. Hirshorn asked if the town staff have not approved the tree encroachment
at this time. Mr. Rutherford answers in the affirmative. Mr. Hirshorn
suggested that the application should not be heard by the Commission until
staff has comments on the encroachment.

Ms. Campbell reviewed the plans with Commission. She stated she has been
working with town staff regarding the tree encroachment issue and is waiting
for approval of the revised site plan. She asked that the Commission consider
the request.

Mr. Hirshorn asked staff how to proceed. Mr. Rutherford answered that the
Commission has approved requests in the past conditioned upon additional
approvals and suggested this could be done if the Commission desired. Mr.
Hirshorn asked if there would be additional hardscape around the pool. Ms.
Campbell answered in the negative. Mr. Hirshorn expressed concern with
moving forward with the request.

Ms. Millie Horton, 210 Bank Street, expressed concern with drainage issues
and asked how much fill would be added to the lot. Ms. Campbell answered
that the intention is to keep the grade as close to existing as possible. She
stated that approximately 24 inches of fill would be added and showed the
location the site plan for the Commission.

There being no further comment, Mr. Hirshorn closed public comment.

Ms. Dovre stated that she is comfortable with the request moving forward and
conditioned upon approval of the tree encroachment.

Mr. Hirshorn asked staff about the review timeline. Mr. Rutherford answered
that he is not sure when the review would be completed.

Ms. Rowe thanked the applicant for addressing the Commission’s concerns
regarding the massing. She stated that she is comfortable with the design
contingent upon approval of the tree encroachment.

Mr. Hirshorn stated that there were several concerns last meeting such as
massing and lot coverage. Additionally, there is now a tree encroachment
issue. He suggested that all these issues should be resolved before approval
is given. Mr. Rutherford noted that an as-built survey is required to confirm
lot coverage. Mr. Hirshorn stated that he could consider moving forward with
the condition that the outstanding issues are resolved.

    Ms. Rowe moved for approval of 107 Carr Street with the following
conditions,
   • Tree encroachment must be approved by town arborist
   • Must meet the 40 percent impervious coverage as required in the
       ordinance
