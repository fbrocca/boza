E.   304 Morrison Street – Historic 6164/Survey 6164.01 – (TMS 532-01-00-
     054) Applicant is seeking Final Review for a side setback encroachment
     for a HVAC system, new fence, and brick walkway.– CORRESPONDENCE,
     VIDEO CORRESPONDENCE

     Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

   Mr. Jake Whiley and Ms. Lauren Whiley, owners, reviewed the application
with the Commission.

    Ms. Robin Colman, 118 Pitt Street, expressed concern with the raised HVAC
system at 304 Morrison Street, as it is extremely noisy and an eyesore. She
shared video of the HVAC unit with the Commission. She expressed concern
with the use of a chain link fence and brick walkway to the rear of the Morrison
Street property. She asked that the HVAC unit be moved.

    Mr. Chris Coleman, 118 Pitt Street, expressed concerns with the HVAC unit
at 304 Morrison. He suggested that the HVAC unit can be viewed from
Morrison Street. He stated that there have been changes to the elevation and
materials used that were not approved. He suggested that the HVAC unit is
an eyesore and noisy. He suggested that the side setback encroachment is not
warranted and should not be approved. He suggested that the elevated HVAC
unit is not appropriate and does not meet the guidelines. He asked that the
request be denied.

    Mr. Matthew Scarlant, 120 Pitt Street, stated that he had a survey
completed that shows that the HVAC unit and stand is approximately six inches
from the property line. He suggested that the approved plan should be utilized
and this amended request not approved.

    Mr. Louis Schweers, GLS Engineering, stated that he was consulted on
options for location of the HVAC unit. He suggested that it is unusual to have
an elevated HVAC unit and that a split system could be installed near the
structure with the air handler installed inside the structure.

    Mr. Ross Appel, attorney for Mr. and Ms. Coleman, suggested that there
are less intrusive alternatives that could be taken to locate the HVAC unit on
the property where is would not be unsightly and cause additional noise. He
