VI. Business -
    A. 216 Bennett Street - Survey 6150/Historic (TMS #5320100064) Applicant
        is seeking final approval for after-the-fact approval of a fence.

           Ms. Miller reviewed staff comments with the Commission.
                                           Historic District Preservation Commission

                                                                           Page 2 of 5



   Ms. Sarah Canon, owner, reviewed the request with the Commission.

    Ms. Carmen, Scott, 415 Church St, stated that their ADU was built within
the allowed setbacks. She asked that the request be denied as it is not within
the guidelines and there is no economic hardship. She suggested that the
contractor was put on notice that the fence was not within the guidelines. She
suggested that the Town also put the owners on notice that the fence and was
not built.

    Mr. James Scott, 415 Church St, stated that he included a timeline to the
Commission on their efforts and notifications to the contractor of the
violations. He stated that they are not in favor of the after-the-fact approval.
He stated that after being notified of the violations, the work continued on the
fence. He suggested that there are no hardships to approve the after-the-fact
approvals. He suggested that approval of the request would set a bad
precedent.

    Ms. Linda Balzac, 422 Hobcaw, stated that the fence guidelines have been
in place for over 25 years. She suggested that there is no precedent for
approving the request and asked that it be denied.

    Mr. Chris Coleman, 118 Pitt St, suggested that there are not definitive
hardships established in the guidelines. He suggested that after-the-fact
approvals should not be considered as there is nothing in the guidelines
outlining those approvals.

    Mr. Louis Horton, 108 Live Oak Dr, suggested that the fence cannot be seen
from the street. He suggested that additional landscaping could be planted to
further screen the fence. He suggested that the six foot should be reviewed.

   Mr. Crosland closed public comment and moved to Commission questions,
applicant and staff response, and deliberation.

    Mr. Crosland moved for approval with the condition that the height of the
fence is reduced to six feet, but can remain a solid construction. Mr. Gallogly
seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 2 to 1 vote with Mr. Gallogly and
Mr. Crosland in favor; Mr. Barber opposed.
