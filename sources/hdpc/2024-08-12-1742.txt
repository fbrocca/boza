III. General Public Comment

    Ms. Barbara Smith, 410 Royall Ave, spoke regarding expansion of the historic
district. She stated that she has a petition in opposition of the expansion of the
historic district. She expressed concern that the expansion would not be a benefit
for the properties that may be included. She also expressed concern with Town
funding being used for a survey of the additional properties.

    Ms. Sara Mitchell, 313 Middle St, stated that she has a petition in opposition of
the expansion of the historic district. She suggested that the restrictions of the
historic district would negatively impact those properties that could be included in
the expansion. She stated that many of the residents do not want the expansion
of the historic district. She suggested that Town funds should not be used for
conducting a survey. She also expressed concern with no notification of the survey.

    Ms. Jerry Eddings, 127 Rose Lane, stated that she is also concerned with the
cost of a survey for expansion of the historic district and also what the benefits

would be for additional properties being placed in the historic district. She
suggested that there is not a compelling reason for expansion of the historic
district.

     Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning, suggested that the historic survey is an
important tool in identifying those properties that could be historic. He suggested
that this would provide the information, but not necessarily mean that the
expansion would take place. He stated that it could mean the creation of a separate
historic district with different guidelines.

    Mr. Ronald German, 411 Bank St, stated that her family has lived in the Town
for generations. He suggested that before any changes are made, there should be
ample notification of what would be proposed. He expressed concern that if the
historic district is expanded, it would be stricter requirements for improvements.
He expressed concern with gentrification and that larger homes are being built in
place of existing smaller homes.

There being no further comments, Mr. Creech closed public comments.
