8.    Discussion on plaques signifying Historic homes.

    Mr. Hankinson reviewed the premise for this discussion with the Commission
to have a plaque designating which homes are considered historic within the
district.

The Commission agreed that this should be deferred until after the re-survey of the
historic district is completed.

9.    Action to adopt HDPC Guidelines.

    Mr. Hankinson stated that some legal issues have arisen regarding a couple of
the new guidelines that were approved in December and recommended that the
approval be deferred.

    Mr. Bakker moved for deferral of adoption of the guidelines for further legal
review. Ms. Gravitt seconded the motion. All in favor.

10. Action to adopt Rules of Procedure.

     Ms. Gravitt moved for approval. Mr. Bakker seconded the motion. All in favor.
