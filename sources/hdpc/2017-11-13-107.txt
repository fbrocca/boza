       Guideline Revision regarding Garages & Accessory Buildings #6
       Mr. Pohlman stated that this is in reference to:
       Garages and Accessory Buildings #6; addition of definitions; Elimination of
       Sidewalks Driveways and Streets #7
       Reason:
       To further clarify the intent of the Guideline; elimination of redundancy for
       parking requirement.
                                                     Old Village Historic District Commission
                                                                          November 13, 2017
                                                                                   Page 2 of 33


1. Current Wording of Garages and Accessory Buildings #6:
   Pergolas and trellises, being absent in historical context, should be placed
   in rear yards and not visible from public rights of way.
   Revised Wording:
   Pergolas, trellises, and arbors should be placed in rear yards and not
   visible from public rights of way. The structure should be proportional and
   harmonious to the primary structure on the lot.
2. Addition of the following terms to Definitions:
   Pergola - an archway in a garden or park consisting of a framework
   covered with trained climbing or trailing plants.
   Trellis - a framework of light wooden or metal bars, chiefly used as a
   support for fruit trees or climbing plants.
   Arbor - a shady garden alcove with sides and a roof formed by trees or
   climbing plants trained over a wooden framework.
3. Guideline to be eliminated:
   Sidewalks Driveways and Streets #7: A minimum of one off-street parking
   space should be provided on all residential lots. A standard parking space
   should be 9 feet x 18 feet.
   Reason:
   Current parking ordinance is in excess of this guideline.
Mr. Pohlman stated that this has been posted on the Town’s website for
thirty (30) days to receive any comments from the public. He stated that the
Commission will need to make a motion to accept or deny the changes.
