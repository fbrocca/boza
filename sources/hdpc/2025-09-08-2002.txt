3.   General Public Comment
4.   Correspondence

Mr. Hankinson stated that all correspondence was linked to the agenda.

   Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning, thanked the Commission for their service on the
Commission.

    Mr. David Quick, 1502 Village Square, expressed concern with the massing of
homes in the historic district, particularly on Church Street. He stated that the
historic homes help set the character of the streetscape and the historic character
should be preserved. He also expressed concern with flooding and that the larger
homes have more impervious surface and can exacerbate the flooding issue. He
suggested that these issues should be considered when reviewing the requests.

    Ms. Darlene Carey, 201 Live Oak Drive, expressed concern with the size of
newer homes as well. She stated that many of the homes on her street have been
renovated or newly constructed. She agreed with Mr. Quick that the larger homes
on Church Street negatively affect the historic character.

6.   Business
     A. 313 Queen St., Survey Card 6096/Non-Historic – TMS# 5320500061,
         Final Review for the demolition of an existing non-historic primary
         structure and the construction of a new primary structure.

         Mr. Hankinson reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

         Ms. Heather Wilson, architect, reviewed the request for the Commission.

         Mr. Trent Holland, 317 Queen Street, expressed concern that the pool and
     courtyard might be out of character, particularly because it affects the location
     of the driveway that is currently shown encroaching into the tree protection
     zone of a historic oak tree. He expressed concern with flooding and runoff and
     that while the courtyard is being considered a catch basin, it is a pool. He
     expressed concern that some of the materials proposed are not in keeping
     with the historic district.

         Ms. Susan Harris, 224 Queen Street, agreed with Mr. Holland’s comments.
     She expressed concern with the encroachment into the setbacks and that this
     should not be allowed. She suggested that the design is not in character with
     the historic district. She suggested that the approval of more and more homes
     being allowed to encroach into the setbacks should not be allowed.

     There being no further comments, Ms. Gravitt closed public comment and
     moved to Commission questions.

         Mr. Player moved for denial of the request as presented. Mr. Foxworth
     seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 4 to 1 vote with Mr. Foxworth, Ms.
     Edwards, Ms. Gravitt, and Mr. Player in favor; Mr. Craft opposed.
