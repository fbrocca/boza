III. Board Discussion: Discussion on imagery for guidelines. Revised Guidelines
     with Images
       Mr. Keenan provided an update from the board members and general public
       at the Planning and Development Committee meeting last week. He stated
       that the update was provided by the past Chair, Brett Bennett.
       Voting in favor of the OVHDC recommendations on parking requirements and
       accessory structure height limitations was unanimous. The recommendation
       to adopt the Secretary of the Interior guidelines for designated historic
       structures was approved by 3-1 with Mr. Elton Carrier being opposed. He
       stated that he prefer the present model including its fixed 1936 date not be
       changed. The Commission’s recommendation that a Preservation or Planning
       Consultant be engaged to support the enhancement and reformatting of the
       OVHDC’s design guidelines was deferred pending staff research on fee
       estimates. Lastly, the Committee voted to recommend to Town Council that
       an ordinance specifying qualifications for candidates for the OVHDC be
       amended so that one seat be open to non-residents who possess expertise or
       experience in architecture, preservation and/or related categories. This vote
       was passed 3-0. Mr. Joe Bustos was excused at the time.
