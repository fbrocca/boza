IV. General Public Comment

    Ms. Carole Tinkey, 208 Venning St, spoke regarding minor work to the Post
House. She stated that this was an illegal addition that should not be allowed. She
also suggested that the addition is not in keeping with the guidelines and changes
the historic structure and affects the character of the district and is not in harmony
with the district. She asked that the request be denied.

   Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning St, spoke regarding the minor work to the Post
House. He asked that this request should be denied. He suggested that it does not
conform with DHEC regulations, is not harmonious to the historic structure, and
