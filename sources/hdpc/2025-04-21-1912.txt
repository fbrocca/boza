9.   Discussion of New Requirements for Historic Windows.

     The Commission discussed the requirements for historic windows.

    The Commission agreed to restrict the window pattern for historic structures
and not allow a deviation from the window pattern. Historic homes with non-
historic windows can be allowed to deviate from current window pattern with
preference to historic patterns.
