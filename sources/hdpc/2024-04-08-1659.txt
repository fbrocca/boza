IX. Discussion of changing or expanding the historic district boundaries.

    Ms. Miller introduced this item and stated that any changes or expansion of the
historic district would require Town Council approval.

     The Commission discussed possible expansion of the historic district.

    The Commission asked staff to bring back additional information on the age of
structures along Royall Avenue to determine the feasibility of expanding the historic
district.

X.    Discussion of proposed changes to HDPC Guidelines.

     The Commission discussed proposed changes to the guidelines.

     The Commission discussed shed roofs and how it can be bettered clarified,
including indicating proportions.

The Commission discussed porches and suggested that there should be more
direction included in the guidelines.

The Commission discussed garages and accessory structures and suggested that it
would be helpful to include diagrams in the guidelines.

XI. Discussion of Items for Next Agenda

The Commission agreed that the following items should included on next month’s
agenda:
   ➢ Continued discussion on proposed changes to the guidelines
   ➢ Continued discussion on changing or expanding the historic district boundaries.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 7:25 pm.
