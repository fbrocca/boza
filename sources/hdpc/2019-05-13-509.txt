F.744 Pitt Street – Survey 5959 – (TMS 532-09-00-069) Applicant is seeking
   Final Review for various exterior alterations including new porches,
   dormers, and roof.

  Mr. Rutherford reviewed the request with the Commission.

Ms. Linda Balzac, architect, reviewed the request with the Commission.

Ms. Montgomery suggested that the revisions will make the home more
livable.

Ms. Dovre suggested that the design is simple and elegant.

Ms. Rowe suggested that the design is harmonious with the neighborhood.

Ms. Montgomery asked about the covered deck. Ms. Balzac answered that
they would like to encroach into the setback approximately four feet similar
to the current deck and cover it.

   Ms. Montgomery moved for approval of the request including approval to
encroach up to four feet into the rear setback with approval of the handrails
to be handled by staff. Ms. Dovre seconded the motion. All in favor.
