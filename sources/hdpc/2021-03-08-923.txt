V.    General Public Comment

      Ms. Carole Tinkey, 208 Venning St, expressed concern regarding 204 Live oak
  appeal and read a portion of the SHPO guidelines for the Commission. She
  suggested that a living fence should only be allowed if it has the vegetation. She
  also expressed concern with drainage issues. She suggested that these should be
  addressed.

      Ms. Allison Dailey, 204 Live Oak Dr, spoke regarding the appeal of the fence.
  She stated that she would like to state the following points for the record:
     ➢ The existing fence was old and rotting, and termite infested.
     ➢ The existing landscape would be maintained and no trees would be removed
         or damaged.
     ➢ Picket fence is not the only type of fence found in the streetscape—chainlink,
         stockade, and shadowbox fencing can also be seen from the street.
     ➢ The proposed fencing would be in the sideyard. The first 20 feet of picket
         fence would not be removed, and the proposed fencing would start behind
         the front corner of the house, meeting the sideyard definition.
     ➢ 30 property owners living within a half mile of the property are in favor of
         the proposed fence.
     ➢ Landscape, drainage, and fence removal are not within the purview of the
         Commission.
     ➢ The fence meets the HDPC guidelines.
  Ms. Dailey expressed concern that the appeal was not brought because of issues
  relating to the historic guidelines. She suggested that the initial request was
  properly approved by staff and asked that the staff decision be upheld.

     Ms. Ashley Woody, 110 Friend Street, has comments regarding item VIII A and
  would speak when the item is reviewed.

  There being no further comments, Ms. Montgomery closed public comment and
  continued with the agenda.
