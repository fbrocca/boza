B. 301 Center Street – (TMS 532-13-00-122) Applicant is seeking Final
    Review to replace the existing driveway with drivable grass, addition
    of an uncovered patio and pool to the rear of the home, new
    fireplace to an existing covered porch, and to unscreen a portion of
    the existing rear porch.

    Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission
(attachment 1).

Mr. JR Kramer, Remark, reviewed the request with the Commission.

Ms. Montgomery asked about the size of pool. Mr. Rutherford answered
nine foot by twelve feet. Ms. Montgomery asked if the fireplace is
woodburning. Mr. Kramer answered it was a gas fireplace. Mr.
Hackenberg asked if this was a wading pool. Mr. Kramer answered that
the pool was a plunge pool and only four feet deep and will not be a hot
tub.

There being no comments, Ms. Rowe closed public comment.

Mr. Hackenberg recommended using grass for impervious coverage.

Ms. Dovre liked the idea of the pool and grass.

Ms. Montgomery recommended that the concrete should be removed and
replaced by grass, there should be no cable railings and the fire bowls need
to be lowered.

Mr. Gallogly agreed with staff in decreasing impervious coverage.

Ms. Dovre did not approve of the setback encroachment, the fire bowls or
the cable railings.
   Mr. Gallogly moved for approval of grass for impervious surface
coverage. Ms. Montgomery second the motion. All in favor.

   Ms. Montgomery moved to deny the pool as currently designed with the
fire bowls raised, more information is needed about the pool equipment,
the cable railing is not appropriate, and the rear yard encroachment not in
keeping with adjoining lots. Mr. Gallogly second the motion. Motion
passed on a 4 to 1 motion with Ms. Montgomery, Mr. Gallogly, Ms. Rowe
and Mr. Hackenberg in favor, Ms. Dovre opposed.

 Ms. Montgomery expressed concern with the HVAC noise. Mr. Rutherford
 responded this was already approved by the Commission

D.975 Pitt Street – Survey 5889 – (TMS 532-13-00-033) Applicant is seeking
    Preliminary Comment for the demolition and replacement of a single-
    family home.

    Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission
 (attachment 1).

 Mr. Joel Wenzel, Thomas & Donzinger, 138 St. Phillips Street, Charleston,
 SC. Mr. Wenzel reviewed the design with the Commission.
 Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning Street. expressed concern with the design of
 the home.

 Mr. Bob Burns, 9 Pierates Cruz. expressed concern with the design of the
 home.

 There being no further comments, Ms. Rowe closed public comment.

 Ms. Rowe asked Mr. Rutherford about the demolition issue for this home.

 Mr. Rutherford answered that the Commission could discuss the
 demolition, but they did not need to approve or deny at this time. He
 reiterated that staff comments recommend that this be deferred.

 Mr. Hackenberg agreed with staffs recommendation to defer.

 Ms. Dovre also agreed with deferring this item.
Ms. Montgomery expressed concern with the roof line and asked if the
home sustained any damage during Hurricane Hugo. Mr. Lee Richardson,
975 Pitt Street, answered that he does not believe there has been any
damage from previous storms. He stated that if the demolition is not
approved, then there is no reason to continue with the request.

Mr. Rutherford stated that based on the information provided, that the
application does not meet the requirements to allow demolition.

Mr. Gallogly agreed that there does not appear to be a need to demolish
the existing structure.

Mr. Richardson explained the home should be demolished based on the
economic rehabilitation rule, and that any improvements would be over
the 50% requirement and therefore the entire structure would have to
come into compliance with current building code. Mr. Rutherford stated
the economic rehabilitation was not provided in the application and cannot
be considered with this request.

   Mr. Gallogly moved to defer the application. Ms. Montgomery second
the motion. All in favor.

E. 131 Live Oak Drive – Survey 6246/6246.01 – (TMS 517-15-00-139)
    Applicant is seeking Final Review to install artificial turf in the front
    yard

    Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission.
(Attachment 1).

Ms. Jennifer Dickerson, 131 Live Oak Drive, reviewed request with the
Commission.

Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning Street, stated he is opposed to turf and
suggested that it does not fit within the historic district

Ms. Dickerson stated that he originally had the same concerns with using
turf for the front yard and wanted to make sure that it close to the
neighboring yards. She stated that they have been battling fungus and pet
issues over the years. She suggested that it would be a vast improvement
with the aesthetics of the yard.
There being no comments, Ms. Rowe closed public comment.

Mr. Hackenberg has no issue with the turf and stated that the yard does
not receive a lot of sunlight which is a problem. He asked if it could also be
placed on the side of the house. Ms. Dickerson answered in the negative
and stated that they had concerns with how it would look over the
exposed roots.

Ms. Dovre asked how the turf is would be cleaned. Ms. Dickerson
answered that it can be cleaned with a cleaning solution administered
through a garden hose attachment.

Ms. Montgomery expressed concern with the use of artificial grass in this
area as it is a historic street. She suggested that the turf would not be
appropriate for this area.

Mr. Gallogly expressed concern with setting a precedent

Ms. Rowe agreed that turf would not be appropriate for the historic
district. She also suggested that it would be difficult to determine which
types of turf would be allowed.

   Mr. Gallogly moved to deny the request for installation of turf. Ms.
Dovre second the motion. Ms. Rowe, Ms. Montgomery and Mr. Gallogly in
favor. Mr. Hackenberg and Ms. Dovre Opposed.
