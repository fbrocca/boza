C. 128 Chicco Lane – (TMS 532-05-00-032) Applicant is seeking Final Review
   for a new home.

   Ms. Dolan reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

Ms. Heather Wilson, representative for the homeowner, reviewed the
application with the Commission.

Ms. Joann Cook, 631 Pitt St, expressed concern that property owner is asking
for special consideration regarding floor height in a flood zone and suggested
that the request should not be approved.

Ms. Kate Campbell, Beau Clowney Architects, suggested that the rule was
established to keep the homes lower in height, preserve the streetscape, and
to prevent homes being artificially built higher than adjacent homes in order
to have a better view of the harbor.

Ms. Linda Balzac suggested that the request should be granted in order to
better waterproof the floor system.

     Mr. Gallogly moved for approval the request including the allowance of
the base floor to be at flood plus two, the two feet of fill and an as-built survey
is completed to ensure that the construction does not exceed the 40%
impervious lot coverage. Ms. Montgomery seconded the motion. All in favor.

D. 237 Middle Street – (TMS 532-09-00-022) Applicant is seeking Final

   Ms. Dolan reviewed staff comments with the Commission (attachment 1).

Mr. Chris Brooker, owner, reviewed the application with the Commission.

Ms. Linda Balzac, architect for the Calhoun’s at 106 Center, wanted to confirm
that the fill for 237 Middle Street would not affect 106 Center and asked to
have the pool at 237 Middle moved an additional eight away from the property
line a total of fourteen feet.

Ms. Ann Calhoun, 106 Center Street, expressed concern with the impact the
construction might have on the historic oak tree.

    Ms. Montgomery moved for approval with staff recommendations of the
additional fill and bulkhead to be level with the neighbor’s yard, the pool to be
moved two additional feet from property line with the condition that it does
not interfere with the historic tree protection zone. Mr. Gallogly seconded the
motion. All in favor.

E. 107 Carr Street – (TMS 532-05-00-051) Applicant is seeking Final Review
   for after-the-fact revisions resulting from a staff site inspection.
   Correspondence

     Mr. Rutherford reviewed staff comments with the Commission
(attachment 1).

Ms. Kate Campbell, Beau Clowney Architects, reviewed the request with the
Commission. Mr. Wes Whittle, Eastec Property, reviewed flood plus one
guideline and the impact it is having on the construction of this home and the
required materials.

Mr. Matt O’Neil, owner, thanked the Commission for their time and stated
that they have been working with staff to build the best possible home for the
neighborhood.

    Ms. Montgomery moved for approval of the alterations as presented. Mr.
Gallogly seconded the motion. All in favor.
