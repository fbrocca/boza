III. Board Discussion: Request for Report from Planning Committee &
     Guidelines Revision Discussion
        Update on their presentation to the Planning Committee.
        Mr. Bennett stated that he and Mr. Keenan made a presentation to the
        Planning Committee. They has asked the OVHDC to provide them with more
        background and detail with respect to architectural design guideline updates
        and recommendations that have been made to Town Council for a variety of
        ordinance changes He stated that their comments and recommendations
        were well received. They had a lot of dialogue and questions from members
        of Town Council. Their recommendations included several items; (1) to
        increase the Town’s focus on compliance and enforcement with the design
        guidelines, including whatever resources may be necessary to better manage
        them; (2) also described a number of guidelines that were pending at the
        time and essentially warned them that over the next several months, there
        is likely to be a succession of guideline updates as we gather public input and
        refine the guideline document as it exists. They also proposed to Town
        Council that a preservation consultant be hired to assist in the reformatting
                                                        Old Village Historic District Commission
                                                                                 January 9, 2017
                                                                                      Page 2 of 10


    and improving the design guideline document as it presently exists to
    provide more detail, illustrations and drawing so that applicants can be
    better prepared as to expectations of the commission. Regarding
    amendments to ordinances, three were highlighted. (1) that the Town boost
    the parking space requirements for each home; (2) that the accessory
    structure height allowances and siding requirements be changed to become
    more conservative than they presently exist; and (3) that the Town adopt
    the National Park Service and South Carolina Department of Archives and
    History definition or model for designating historic properties. That
    definition entails a 50 year rolling definition to designate historic properties
    among other criteria. Presently the Town ordinance is a fixed date effective
    January, 1936 and by definition that means that properties that would be
    eligible for historic designation are not being properly designated. The
    Commission made the recommendation that this be changed, and presently
    it is up to the Planning Committee and Town Council to make the decision of
    whether to pursue or not.
    Ms. Rowe moved to accept the guideline revisions as presented; seconded by
    Mr. Keenan. All present voted in favor.
    Mr. Bennett stated that these changes would be adopted and effective this
    date.
