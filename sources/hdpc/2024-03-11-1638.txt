STAFF REPORT

Present:       David Creech, Chair, James Bakker, Meghan Gravitt, Corey Barber,
               Warren Player.
Staff:         Bryan Hankinson, Michele Reed, Kate Miller, David Pagliarini, James
               Ward, Lynnette Lynes

         Mr. Creech called the meeting to order at 5:00 pm.

I.     Approval of Agenda

     Mr. Player moved for approval of the agenda. Mr. Barber seconded the motion.
All in favor.

II.    Approval of Minutes February 12th, 2024 Meeting

     Mr. Player moved for approval of the minutes. Mr. Barber seconded the motion.
All in favor.

III. General Public Comment

   Mr. Ken Craft, 204 Spooner Lane, spoke regarding the porkchop eave detail and
that more recent eaves approved have not been historic. He suggested that the
Commission should pay more attention to this historic detail to keep the historic
character of the district. He also spoke regarding the eaves on 753 Pitt Street and
suggested that the historic eaves should be approved.

      There being no further comments, Mr. Creech closed public comment.

IV. Correspondence

      All correspondence received was linked to the agenda.

V.     Consent Motions

There being no consent motions, Mr. Creech continued with the agenda.

VI. Business
    A. Alhambra Playground – No Survey/Non-Historic – (TMS # 5320900032)
        Wood fencing that meets all design guidelines to surround the
        perimeter of the property.

         Mr. Hankinson reviewed staff comments with the Commission.

       Mr. Andy Weis and Mr. Frankie Pettit reviewed the request with the
    Commission.

       There being no public comment, Mr. Creech closed public comment and
    moved to Commission questions.

        Mr. Creech moved for approval with the with the condition that the fence
    is similar to the upper right photo presented with the application, with
    horizontal on two sides, top and bottom with a cap and 2x2 pickets at 42 inches
    high, facing the public right-of-way; solid boards between horizontals along
    back portion of the fence for a solid privacy fence; 6x6 posts with 6 inch
    protrusion above the cap with a beveled finish. Mr. Bakker seconded the
    motion. Motion passed on a 4 to 1 vote with Mr. Barber, Mr. Creech, Mr.
    Bakker, and Ms. Gravitt in favor; Mr. Player opposed.
