1.    Gather Mount Pleasant, Central Mount Pleasant Tracks 4-1 & 4-5, 559-00-
      00-017 & 559-00-00-538, Final Review of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

     Ms. Spencer recused herself due to a conflict of interest.

     Mr. Carter and Mr. Stone reviewed staff comments with the Board.

   Mr. Mack Cross, Mr. Todd Richardson, Mr. Austin Diehl, and Mr. Chris
Magaldi reviewed the request with the Board.

   Ms. Meghan Posner, 1562 Hancock, expressed concern that the project as a
destination should be more centrally located. She suggested that the proposed
uses would infringe on the neighborhood and the quality of life. She also
suggested that the project is too expansive for the neighborhood and the
proposed functions should be carefully considered.

    Mr. Daniel Franck, 1556 Hancock St, expressed concern with the proposed
project and that the proposed uses would negatively impact the neighborhood.
He expressed concern that the project would increase traffic onto the entrance
road to the neighborhood. He expressed concern with pedestrian and child
safety, particularly because Hancock Street is the only entrance into the
neighborhood. He expressed concern with the parking and that the increased
traffic would cause more pedestrian accidents. He suggested that the amount of
proposed parking would not be sufficient for the traffic that will be generated.
He expressed concern that the increased traffic would impede access for
emergency vehicles.

     Ms. McClay Franck, 1556 Hancock St, suggested that the amount of traffic
that will be generated will be a detriment to the neighborhood and would
negatively impact child safety. She suggested that the retail hours would not be
in line with the small-town neighborhood. She suggested that the additional
patron traffic will cause more impact to the adjacent park. She suggested that
the lighting plan would cause additional light pollution. She suggested that the
project does not include any landscaping, buffers, or sound abatement to
alleviate the burden that will be placed on the neighborhood.

   Mr. Tray Adams, 1336 Harkin Road, stated that he is in support of the project.
He suggested that this project makes sense for the area and should not be denied

because there is some push back. He suggested that for each person opposed to
the project, there are approximately ten that are in favor of it, but do not vocalize
their support.

     Mr. Rick Schaltegger, 1611 Florentia, suggested that while the previous
speaker noted that there are many in favor of the project, they might not live in
the nearby neighborhood. He stated that he has additional pictures for the Board
to review.

    Mr. Lambert moved to accept the additional material. Mr. Magoon seconded
the motion. All in favor.

    Mr. Schaltegger reviewed the pictures with the Board. He noted that there
is not sufficient parking for residents to park in their garages or on driveway and
therefore have to park in nearby parking areas. He expressed concern that there
currently only a few traffic lights to control traffic.

    Ms. Carolyn McKellar, 1519 Cecile St, expressed concern with events being
held and that there is only one entrance into the neighborhood. She expressed
concern with child and pedestrian safety and that the project would be in close
proximity to the neighborhood. She suggested that this project would impact
access for patients trying to access the nearby medical facilities. She suggested
that live music and events are too close in proximity to residential homes.

    Ms. Tomi Deacons, 1612 Florentia, expressed concern with the proximity of
the project to a residential neighborhood. She expressed concern that the venue
and event industry is currently booming, but suggested that it will be fleeting and
could mean that the project could become vacant. She asked that the Board not
approve the project at its current proposed location.

    Mr. Jarod Bonino, 1391 Rivella Drive, stated that he and his surrounding
neighbors are excited for the development. He suggested that this is a good
project.

   Mr. Ryan Krischer, 1312 Langford Rd, stated that he reviewed the plans and
suggested that it is a good plan and is excited for the project to be completed.
He suggested that this would provide nearby retail.

    Mr. Jeff Fetterman, 1626 Florentia, expressed concern with trash pickup and
that it would cause additional noise in the early morning hours. He stated that
the neighborhood was not notified or consulted about this project. He stated
that they were told when they purchased their home that the extra parking was
available only for use by the neighborhood. He suggested that there is not
sufficient handicap parking and that this project is not a good fit for the area.

    Mr. Bowen Smith, 1510 Cecile St, stated that he was told over a year ago that
this development was in the works. He suggested that some of the proposed
retail is not needed and sufficient parking is not provided. He suggested that
commercial development should fit in with existing neighborhoods and not be
destructive. He stated that he supports the architecture, but it should not be
abutting residential homes.

    Ms. April Dickerson, 1578 Hancock St, expressed concern with the potential
of restaurant waste causing more rodents in the area. She also expressed
concern with increased patrons and concern with child safety in proximity to
establishments that serve alcohol. She expressed concern with the parking and
that there is currently not sufficient parking. She expressed concern with the
increased possibility of child predator in the area.

   Mr. Joe Broerman, 1556 Appling Drive, stated that he is in favor of the project
and is looking forward to having a venue for more family-friendly events.

    Mr. Andrew, Kohn, 1558 Hancock St, suggested that those in favor of the
project do not live right across from the proposed project. He suggested that this
project was mirrored off of Gather Greenville that was in a commercial corridor,
not in a residential neighborhood. He expressed concern with increased noise.
He stated that Hancock St is a dead end street and expressed concern that there
would be increased traffic with those that are lost or trying to find parking . He
suggested that the neighborhood was not notified of this plan when purchasing
their homes.

   There being no comments, Mr. Lambert closed public comment.

   Mr. Lambert moved to enter into executive session for legal advice. Mr. Kiel
seconded the motion. All in favor.
