     1. Moultrie Plaza Outbuildings, 604 Coleman Blvd, TMS # 517-16-00-002,

     2.    Grand Patriot, 40 Patriots Point Rd, TMS # 517-00-00-001, Final Review of
           Site, Landscape, and Architecture Public Correspondence

          Mr. Lubert and Mr. Robertson reviewed staff comments with the Board.

        Mr. Ian Duncan, Ms. Kristina Harvey, and Mr. Richard Ellison, reviewed the
     request with the Board.

         Mr. Charlie Carmody, 107 New St, stated that the developer has done
     quality work in the past and is confident that this will be a quality
     development.

    Ms. Diedre Kennedy, 901 Cliffwood, suggested that the plans reviewed in
June did not comply with the master plan previously approved. She suggested
that the only change was to the infrastructure. She expressed concern that
access to the waterfront has been changed, the buffers have been reduced,
and hardscapes in the roadways that were supposed to be vegetative. She
stated that the Board has the authority to require changes and asked that the
Board request that the developer consider changes to bring back the grand
lawn as it was shown on the master plan.

    Ms. Lisa Schnipke, 185 Mossy Oak Way, stated that she has followed the
project since first introduced in December 2024. She suggested that the public
access that meets the requirements of Waterfront Gateway and the initial
plans have been changed. She suggested that the current plans seem to be
more private and does not provide the public access and view corridors that it
should. She suggested that the Board should required those initial elements
of the development that provided more public access and the Board should
require more than the minimum so there is a quality development that
includes access for the residents.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Kiel closed public comment and
moved to Board questions and comments.

    Mr. Lambert moved for approval including all staff comments. Mr. Cave
seconded the motion. All in favor.
