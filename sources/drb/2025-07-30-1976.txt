D.   General Public Comment
E.   General Correspondence

   Ms. Diedre Kennedy, 971 Cliffwood Dr, spoke regarding tree removals and mass
development. She suggested that removal of protected trees is not in keeping with
the Community Forest Master Plan (CFMP). She suggested that more should be
asked by developers to preserve the tree canopy.

    Ms. Michele Stress, 545 Veranda View, agreed with Ms. Kennedy’s comments
and suggested that more tree preservation for commercial development should be
considered. She suggested that the tree removal of historic or mature trees means
more development, flooding, and significant loss of tree canopy. She suggested
that the shade from a mature tree cannot be replaced by a 3 inch sapling. She
suggested that removal of trees also increases the heat islands throughout the
Town because of not having more shade. She suggested that the safety, character,
and resiliency of the Town should be preserved.

   There being no further comments and all correspondence was linked to the
agenda, Ms. Richardson continued with the agenda.
