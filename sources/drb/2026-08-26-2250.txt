4.    Dominion Energy Shed Expansion – Revisions, 1277 Chuck Dawley Blvd,
      TMS # 560-01-00-002, Final Review of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

     Mr. Stone and Ms. Marshall reviewed staff comments with the Board.

    Mr. Zach Swalhah, Dominion Energy, and Mr. Alan Reinhart, arborist,
reviewed the request with the Board.

     Mr. Sachs entered at this time (5:40 pm).

   Mr. Lambert moved for approval of submittal of an arborist report. Mr. Kiel
seconded the motion. All in favor.

     Mr. Reinhart reviewed the arborist report with the Board.

     Mr. Lambert asked the size of the tree. Mr. Reinhart answered that it is an
80 inch diameter standard height (dsh) due to having six trunks. He stated that
they have considered relocating the tree, but there are concerns with viability.

    Ms. Morrison asked if the mitigation proposed would meet the Town’s
requirements. Mr. Stone answered that being a larger tree would mean
additional mitigation required.

    Mr. Lambert asked the mitigation requirements. Mr. Stone answered it
would be 50% mitigation. He stated that that the 160 inch tree canopy
requirement would have to be met. Mr. Lambert suggested that planting five
5 inch trees would provide a good canopy and buffer.

    Mr. Kiel suggested that he is comfortable with removal of the tree as it is
not a great tree.

   Mr. Sachs agreed that the tree appears to be in questionable condition and
planting additional trees would be amenable.

     Mr. Cave stated that this is a reasonable compromise.
