6.    Parker’s Kitchen, 3272 N Hwy 17, TMS #598-0-00-404, Final Review of
      Site, Landscape, and Architecture

     Mr. Lubert and Mr. Carter reviewed staff comments with the Board.

    Ms. Valerie Vanhoesen, architect, Mr. Justin Hoffer, and Ms. Sarah Hamblin
reviewed the request with the Board.

     Mr. Aton reviewed the access points to the site with the Board.

    Ms. Morrison moved for approval for submittal of additional material. Ms.
Richardson seconded the motion. All in favor.

    Mr. Rogers Hook, 750 Dragoon Drive, spoke regarding the proximity of the
proposed development to LifePark Church. He suggested that this is an
incompatible land use. He expressed concern with the proximity of the
playground and prayer garden to the proposed development. He expressed
concern for child safety, the elderly and pedestrians. He asked that the request
be denied.

    Ms. Star Good, 1568 Country Club Drive, expressed concern with external
signage and asked that no signage is allowed on the roadway.

    Mr. Bill Good, 1568 Country Club Dr, expressed concern with access from
George Browder and asked that the Board restrict access to the proposed site
to All American Blvd. He expressed concern that the proposed development
would increase traffic and pose a safety hazard.

    Ms. Brandy Botto, 1569 Thomas Barksdale, expressed concern with access
to George Browder and that the buffer be increased to further screen the
development. She asked that all lighting be positioned to prevent any spill
over. She asked that the atmosphere of LifePark Church be considered when
deliberating.
