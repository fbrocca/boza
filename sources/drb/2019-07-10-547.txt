 2.   Midtown Mixed Use, Midtown Ave., TMS# 559-00-00-017, Preliminary
      Approval of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

      Mr. Stone and Ms. Johnson reviewed staff comments with the Board
 (attachment 1).

 Mr. Jason Hutchins, Thomas and Hutton, and Mr. Nick Galizia, McMillan, Pazda
 and Smith, reviewed the project with the Board.

 Mr. Swails referred to page 23 of the plans and asked about the recessed
 entryway and if there would be a balcony above the entryway Mr. Galizia
 answered in the negative.

 Mr. Lambert asked about if the entrance and parking area would be one way
 with angled parking and suggested that 90 degree parking would be a better
 option and more functional. Mr. Hutchins agreed and stated that this could
 be reviewed.

 Mr. O’Neal agreed that there should be 90 degree parking spaces with the
 addition of island cutouts for additional landscape.

 There being no comments, Ms. Santilli closed public comment.

 Mr. O’Neal stated that Mr. Wurst reviewed the project in his absence and
 offered the following comments:
    ➢ projections in/out lack clarity and legibility and appear as interventions
       and not well integrated. They should be treated more similarly to each
       other and as a foil to the brick warehouse feel, it would be more
       successful.
    ➢ Corners are clumsy and lack hierarchy.

     Mr. Swails moved for preliminary approval including all staff comments.
 Mr. O’Neal seconded the motion. All in favor.

5. AAA Carwash Revision, 691 Long Point Rd., TMS# 556-00-00-440, Final
   Approval of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

    Mr. Bernard and Ms. Johnson reviewed staff comments with the Board
(attachment 1).

Mr. Denny Gappens reviewed the project with the Board.

Mr. Lambert asked about vegetation and if there would be plantings in the
retention area. Mr. Gappens answered that he was not sure, but there were no
changes in the landscape from the previously approved plan. Mr. Lambert
stated that he would prefer to raise the height of the screen wall for better
screening of the equipment.

Mr. O’Neal asked if all of the retention would be underground. Mr. Gappens
answered in the negative.

Mr. Swails suggested that there should be plantings around the retaining wall.
Mr. Gappens stated that this would be reviewed.

Mr. O’Neal agreed that there should be plantings and the wall height should be
increased.

6. Kids R Kids Revision, 3650 Bessemer Road, TMS# 583-03-00-183, Final
   Approval of Site, Landscape and Architecture

    Mr. Bernard and Ms. Johnson reviewed staff comments with the Board
(attachment 1).

Mr. Giles Branch, Earthsource Engineering, reviewed the project with the Board.

Mr. Swails asked about the changes to the driveway. Ms. Johnson answered
that the driveway was changed based on Transportation Department
comments.

Mr. Lambert asked about the middle of the driveway. Mr. Branch answered that
this is a temporary obstruction.

Mr. O’Neal suggested that the original plan should be required to be submitted
with the revised plans. He suggested that there should be better up-front
coordination prior to coming before the Board would be beneficial. He asked
about the fence on top of the drainage pipe that could not be addressed. Mr.
Branch answered that there is a 12-inch pipe that crosses the fence but the post
is not as close to the pipe as originally thought. Mr. O’Neal stated that he is
comfortable with the fence.

There being no comments, Ms. Santilli closed public comment.

    Mr. O’Neal moved for approval including addressing staff comments with
the exception of the fence on the drainage pipe. Mr. Swails seconded the
motion. All in favor.

   7. Publix Endcap, 1000 Johnnie Dodds Blvd., TMS 535-00-00-020, Final
      Approval of Site, Landscape and Architecture

        Mr. Stone and Ms. Johnson reviewed staff comments with the Board
   (attachment 1).

   Mr. Thomas Martin, Earthsource Engineering, and Mr. Losse Knight reviewed
   the project with the Board.

   Mr. Swails asked about the courtyard and suggested that this could be used for
   outdoor seating. Mr. Knight answered that the tenants have not been finalized,
   but could be used as commonplace for the entire shopping center or outdoor
   seating.

   Mr. Lambert asked about the rear of the building and if the rear of the property
   could be screened. He suggested that the dumpster was in an “awkward”
   location and if relocated, could provide a few more parking spaces.

   Mr. O’Neal asked about the drainage layout at the courtyard and suggested that
   it should be more centered so it is not in the pedestrian pathway. He questioned
   the amount of concrete at the rear of the building. He suggested that an
   outdoor entryway could be considered as opposed to a courtyard that could be
   utilized for a possible restaurant use or prominent retail use. He suggested that
   it could provide more interest. He agreed with relocation of the dumpster
   should be considered.

   There being no comments, Ms. Santilli closed public comment.

   Mr. O’Neal suggested that the in-twelve pipe location should be reconsidered
   so that it does not impede the footings. He suggested that the courtyard should
   be more integrated with the endcap.

      Mr. Swails moved for approval with addressing staff comments. Mr.
   Lambert seconded the motion. All in favor

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 6:24 pm.
