4.   Rivertowne Daycare and Swim School, Rivertowne Pkwy, 583-05-00-
     419, Preliminary Review of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

     Mr. Stone and Mr. Carter reviewed staff comments with the Board.

     Mr. Mark Stec reviewed the request with the Board.

     Mr. Adam Ferrara, 1660 Rivertowne Country Club Drive, expressed
concern with the required buffer and the circulation of traffic in and out of the
site. He suggested that there is already a lot of traffic and having a drop-off
for the daycare could exacerbate the existing traffic. He also expressed
concern with the massing of the building. He expressed concern with
pedestrian safety.

There being no further comments, Ms. Morrison closed public comment.

    Mr. Magoon moved for approval of a reduction of the buffer from 100 feet
to 75 feet. Mr. McCarthy seconded the motion. All in favor.

    Ms. Spencer moved for preliminary approval with Board comments
addressed as follows:
   ➢ Break up long monotonous elevations
   ➢ Study elevations facing main roadways
   ➢ Match buildings for a more campus look regarding materials
   ➢ Adjust proportions of windows and roof
   ➢ Create adequate play space for daycare
   ➢ Access to Rivertowne Parkway and connectivity to adjacent site
   ➢ Address all staff comments
Mr. Lambert seconded the motion. All in favor.
