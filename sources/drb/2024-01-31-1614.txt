    9.    Parkers, Wood Park Dr & Hwy 41, TMS #594-05-00-219, Preliminary
          Review of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

         Mr. Lubert and Mr. Carter reviewed staff comments with the Board.

        Mr. Daniel Ben Yisrael, developer, Zack Meyers, and Joshua Cox, engineer,
    reviewed the request with the Board.

         There being no comments, Mr. Lambert closed public comment.

        Mr. Kiel moved for approval using option 3 site layout, but rotating the
    building 90 degrees; maintaining the option 3 gas canopy and traffic and
    parking flow; bringing back additional options for frosted glass; having only one
    curb cut on Woodland Park Drive; and addressing all other staff comments. Mr.
    Cave seconded the motion.

       Mr. Lamber moved to amend the motion to rotate the building 90 degrees
    so the rear of the building is parallel to Hwy 41. Mr. Kiel seconded the
    amendment. All in favor.

         Mr. Lambert called for a vote on the motion as amended. All in favor.

The Board convened for a break at 7:04 pm and reconvened at 7:12 pm.
