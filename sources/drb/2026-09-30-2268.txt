F.   Business
     1. 7-Eleven – Revisions, 1515 N Highway 17, TMS # 559-14-00-041, Final
         Review of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

        Mr. Stone and Ms. Marshall reviewed staff comments with the Board.

        Mr. Todd Green, representative, reviewed the request with the Board.

        Mr. Lambert asked which bollards have been approved and which are being
     requested. Mr. Stone answered that there are five existing that were
     requested for removal. Mr. Lambert asked about wheel stops and if these
     could be utilized. Mr. Green suggested they would be similar to the bollards.

         Ms. Richardson suggested that the previous discussion was to allow the
     bollards in front of the building but trees for the other locations.

         Mr. Kiel asked if bollards are preferred by the applicant but not preferred
     by the Town. Mr. Stone answered that the consent approval included working
     with staff and after discussions, an agreement was not reached and therefore
     the applicant is back for a determination.

        There being no comments, Mr. Cave closed public comment.

          Mr. Cave suggested that the plan is unsatisfactory as it shows no
     concession. He suggested there should be a combination of plantings and
     bollards.

         Ms. Richardson suggested that it should not come back to the Board and
     staff’s decision should stand.

         Ms. Richardson moved for approval with the condition that staff
     requirements for plantings instead of bollards is met. Mr. Kiel seconded the

2.   Shem Creek Boat Landing Expansion, TMS # 517-16-00-033 & 517-16-00-
     015, Final Review of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

     Mr. Stone and Ms. Marshall reviewed staff comments with the Board.

    Mr. Ryan Mattie, JMT and Ms. Patty Newshutz, Charleston County PRC
reviewed the request with the Board.

    Mr. Kiel stated that one of the neighbors was concerned about drainage
and asked if he was contacted. Ms. Newshutz answered that they did contact
the neighbors to review the proposed drainage plan and they was comfortable
with the proposal. She stated that she also provided contact information for
Charleston County Department of Transportation so the neighbors could
request the storm drains be cleaned.

     There being no comments, Mr. Cave closed public comment.

    Ms. Morrison moved for approval. Mr. Kiel seconded the motion. All in
favor.

3.   Market at Oakland Tobacco and Wine, Oakland Market Rd, TMS # 600-
     00-00-075, Preliminary Review of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

     Mr. Stone and Ms. Marshall reviewed staff comments with the Board.

   Mr. Jason Milner, engineer, and Mr. James Virkaik, architect, reviewed the
request with the Board.

    Ms. Morrison asked for a review of the architecture. Mr. Verkaik reviewed
the drawings with the Board.

    Mr. Kiel asked where the mechanical equipment will be located. Mr.
Verkaik answered that it would be ground level, screened, landscaped. Mr. Kiel
suggested that there should be more windows. Ms. Morrison concurred and
stated that there are percentage requirements that need to be met.
