     Mr. Cross, Mr. Todd Richardson, and Mr. Luke Jarrett reviewed the request with
     the Board.

         Ms. Tomi Dinkens, 1612 Florentia, stated that the entire neighborhood had
     several concerns with the project, particularly the accesses from Fortier Drive.
     She stated that she is opposed to any access near the townhomes as it will
     increase traffic and exacerbate the parking in the area. She expressed concern
     with the development causing additional traffic and suggested that an additional
     access from Midtown would be preferred. She stated that she is in favor of the
     relocation of the tree and the proposed location on the site.

         There being no further comments, Mr. Lambert closed public comment.

         Ms. Morison moved for approval with staff comments, with the exception to
     allow tree at the corner, further study and development of the future phased
     building for better understanding of how this will be integrated into the site,
     further study on removal of drive access on Fortier and consideration of other
     access points. Ms. Richardson seconded the motion.

         Ms. Morrison moved to amend the motion to clarify that the future phased
     building is being sited as proposed due to the grading and site accessibility. Ms.
     Richardson seconded the motion. All in favor.

     Mr. Lambert called for a vote on the motion as amended. All in favor.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 6:22 pm.
