F.   Business
     1. Gather, Central Mount Pleasant Tracks 4-1 & 4-5, 559-00-00-017 & 559-
         00-00-538, Final Review of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

        Ms. Spencer recused herself due to a conflict of interest.

         Mr. Pagliarini reviewed the decision from BOZA regarding this project. He
     stated that it is appropriate for this issue to be heard. He recommended that
     the Board proceed to deliberation since public comment and presentation has
     already taken place.

        The Board discussed whether to move deliberation. The Board agreed to
     hear from the applicant to review the project with a five-minute presentation
     time limit.

         Mr. Todd Richardson, Synchronicity, reviewed the project with the Board.

         Mr. Jeff Federman, 1626 Florentia St, suggested that the developer does
     not have the right to make changes to Hancock Street. He suggested that the
     development is more intensive than was originally planned. He expressed
     concern that there is not sufficient parking. He suggested that the community
     often does not have sufficient parking. He expressed concern with noise
     pollution, pests, and smells.

    Ms. Nancy Leon, 1580 Hancock, suggested that the neighborhood streets
are not wide enough for 2-way traffic. She asked the capacity of the proposed
brewery and expressed concern that the brewery would cause additional noise
and traffic.

   Ms. Michele Godfrey, 1646 Florentia, expressed concern with the parallel
parking because the streets are too narrow.

    Ms. Gerry Godfrey, 1646 Florentia, expressed concern with the noise
pollution and the businesses staying open for late hours. She suggested that
the Board should review the project site.

   Mr. Mack Cross, 1496 Goblet Avenue, developer of the project, stated that
they have been working on this project for almost a year. He stated that he is
open to meeting with any of the residents to review the project. He stated
that this project meets the density requirements and is intended to be a
family-oriented center.    He suggested that the project meets the
requirements.

    Ms. Karen Loren, 1617 Florentia, suggested that the project placed in front
of the neighborhood is not going to work, it is too commercial.

    Mr. Andy McKellar, 1519 Cecile Street, stated that the neighborhood asked
for additional buffer and landscaping as well as changes to the parking, but the
developer refused to make those changes. He suggested that there should be
more noise and light buffers. He suggested that it should not go forward as
currently proposed.

    Mr. Dave Ferm, 1616 Florentia, stated that the project would be 60 feet
from his home. He expressed concern that deliveries would block his driveway
as there is not sufficient space or parking.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Lambert closed public comment
and moved to Board questions.

   Mr. Lambert moved to approve the application as presented subject to staff
comments with exception that a hard barrier not be required adjacent to the
