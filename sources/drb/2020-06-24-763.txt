3. 7-ElevenTupelo, 3930 Highway 17, 615-00-00-173, Final Approval of Site,
   Landscape, and Architecture

     Mr. Stone and Ms. Johnson reviewed staff comments with the Board
(attachment 1).

   Mr. Pat Budronis reviewed the request with the Board.

   Ms. Theresa Rowland, 2023 Welsh Pony Dr, suggested that the building
should front US17 and not Tupelo Forest. She expressed concern with public
and child safety, drainage, spillover of lighting, and additional proposed
commercial that would affect the road as well.

    Mr. John Nunes, 1011 Banker Court, expressed concern with the apron of
the roadway and suggested that there is not sufficient space to make the turn.
He suggested that because of the lack of turn around, it could mean a truck
would be blocking traffic to the neighborhood. He expressed concern over
traffic safety and that supply trucks parking could cause potential accidents.
He expressed concern with traffic safety and stated that they are meeting next
week to try to resolve some of the neighborhood concerns and suggested that
the request should be deferred until after they have an opportunity to meet.

    Ms. Christina Cicchitto, 1347 Paint Horse Court, expressed concern with
traffic safety and that traffic would be accessing Tupelo Forest Drive and would
cause increased traffic in addition to the proposed car dealership. She
expressed concern with deceleration of traffic to turn into the site from US17
and that it could cause potential accidents.

    Mr. John Rowland, 2023 Welsh Pony Drive, expressed concern with
drainage of the site and how it would be handled. He also expressed concern
with potential flooding of the gas tanks.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Lambert closed public comment.

    Ms. Spencer moved for approval including staff comments. Mr. O’Neal
seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 4 to 1 vote with Mr. Lambert, Mr.
Magoon, Ms. Spencer, and Mr. O’Neal in favor; Mr. McCarthy opposed.
