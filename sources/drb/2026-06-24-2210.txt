F.   Business
     1. Shem Creek Boat Landing Expansion, TMS # 517-16-00-033 &517-16-00-
         015, Preliminary Review of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

        Mr. Lubert and Ms. Marshall reviewed staff comments with the Board.

         Mr. Ryan Mattie, JMT and Ms. Patty Newshutz, Charleston County PRC
     reviewed the request with the Board.

         Ms. Morrison moved for approval for additional presentation time. Ms.
     Richardson seconded the motion. All in favor.

         Mr. Kiel asked about parking. Ms. Newshutz answered that the proposed
     parking is what is needed. Mr. Kiel asked about drainage and if Mr. Sarrafzadeh
     has been contacted as he had some concerns about the drainage for this
     project at the last preliminary review. Ms. Newshutz answered they have not
     contacted him. Mr. Kiel suggested that he should be contacted. Ms. Newshutz
     stated that they would be happy to meet with him to try and address his
     concerns. Mr. Kiel asked for clarification on whether the amount of proposed
     parking is sufficient. Ms. Newshutz answered that she is unsure.

        Mr. Lambert asked if only one tree is being requested for removal. Ms.
     Newshutz answered in the negative and stated that a total of three trees are
     requested for removal.

         Ms. Richardson asked if pervious pavers could be used around the historic
     live oak. Mr. Mattie answered that that the arborist report lays out a plan to
     protect the tree. He added that the drainage should be sufficient for the area,
     and the surrounding properties should see a reduction in runoff.
