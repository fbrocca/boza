   D. Moultrie Plaza, 630 Coleman Blvd., TMS# 517-16-00-002, Final Approval of
      Site, Landscape, and Architecture

        Mr. Stone and Ms. Johnson reviewed staff comments with the Board
   (attachment 1).

   Mr. Giles Branch and Mr. Wentz Chesson reviewed the request with the Board.
   Mr. Branch stated that they are willing to work with staff to address all staff
   comments.

   Mr. Wurst asked which tree was removed. Mr. Branch answered that there was
   a 42 inch oak that was removed after the survey was completed. Mr. Wurst
   asked about sidewalk access and a bay window on the south side facing
   Coleman. Mr. Chesson reviewed the accesses with the Board. He stated that if

   it were to be a bay window it would need to be weighted glass. He noted that
   once the adjacent site was built out, the south elevation would face a building.

   Mr. O’Neal expressed concern with meeting ADA requirements for the sidewalk
   given the grading of the site. Mr. Branch stated that it would meet ADA
   requirements and reviewed where the slope would be managed for the Board.
   Mr. O’Neal suggested that this be reviewed. Mr. Branch stated that the existing
   sidewalk would be ADA compliant and connect the retail spaces without having
   to walk out into the parking area.

   Mr. Lambert asked if it was possible to achieve the 2% cross load. Mr. Branch
   answered in the negative.

   Mr. Wurst suggested that the south elevation should be broken up with some
   type of detail or window.

   Mr. O’Neal suggested that a blown up plan of the ADA elements should be
   considered to ensure.

        Mr. Wurst moved for final approval including all staff and Board comments
   including the addition of a window on south facade facing Coleman Blvd and
   working with staff on the final configuration of the sidewalk for ADA compliance
   and access at the existing restaurant space. Mr. Lambert seconded the motion.
   All in favor.
