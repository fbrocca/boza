F.   Business
     1. Scrubby’s and Dutch Bros, Hwy 17 & George Browder Blvd,
         TMS # 598-00-00-473 & -404, Final Review of Site, Landscape, and
         Architecture

        Mr. Lubert reviewed staff comments with the Board.

        Mr. David Buck, Foresite Group, reviewed the request with the Board.

         Ms. Morrison moved for approval for an additional five minutes of
     presentation time. Ms. Richardson seconded the motion. All in favor.

        Mr. Ray Stubblefield, architect, Mr. Todd Walker, and Mr. Patrick
     Monaghan, reviewed the request with the Board.

         Ms. Morrison moved for approval to submit additional materials. Ms.
     Richardson seconded the motion. All in favor.

        There being no comments, Mr. Kiel closed public comment.

        Ms. Richardson moved for denial to address staff and board comments. Mr.
     Cruz seconded the motion.

         Ms. Richardson amended the motion to include addressing the following
     comments:
        ➢ Address all staff comments
        ➢ Restudy to save all grade A and B trees
        ➢ clean up arborist report to show correct mitigation, classification, etc.
        ➢ look at site plan changes to preserve grand trees and have traffic flow
           for both uses
        ➢ match materials between all structures to be more cohesive
        ➢ lower board and batten to match brick water table
        ➢ drip line analysis for tree #64 and #135 for pervious pavers to better
           preserve trees
     Mr. Lambert seconded the motion. All in favor.
