9.    Seafair Retail Village, 40 Patriots Point Rd, TMS # 517-00-00-001, Final
      Review of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

     Ms. Richardson recused herself due to a conflict of interest.

     Mr. Lubert and Mr. Carter reviewed staff comments with the Board.

    Mr. James Wilson, Mr. Richard Ellison, and Ms. Kristine Harvey reviewed
the request with the Board.

   Ms. Christine Von Kolnitz, 1017 Cottingham, suggested that this is a huge
opportunity for the Town to have a trail system along the waterfront. She
suggested that the grand lawn should be moved closer to the waterfront. She
                                                               Design Review Board
                                                                 December 3, 2025
                                                                        Page 5 of 7

also suggested that the trail should be widened in sections for better access
and to account for future tidal changes.

    Ms. Deidre Kennedy, 901 Cliffwood Dr, stated that the Waterfront Gateway
District was established to serve the public first and prioritize access, ecology,
and civic life. She suggested that the proposed plans do not address the grand
lawn, promenade, natural buffers are not clear or missing. She suggested that
these omissions seem to be a pattern with this developer. She suggested that
this is a one-time opportunity to have something special that can give back to
the residents. She suggested that it should be correctly developed.

    Ms. Lisa Schnipke, 185 Mossy Oak Way, suggested that there should be
canopy trees that will feed the native wildlife and the migratory birds. She
suggested that this development will affect the resiliency of the Town. She
suggested that the amount of impervious surface would increase the
opportunity for flooding. She suggested that plan meets the minimum tree
standards, but they should do above the minimum. She suggested that the
amount of parking does not seem sufficient for the area and the proposed
development.

     Ms. Michelle Stress, 545 Veranda View, suggested that the plan is not
“amazing”, it is “lack luster”. She suggested that it looks status quo and the
plans should be innovative and responsibly developed. She stated that this is
a community asset and sustainable waterfront design should be accomplished.
She asked that the land be treated as a public asset to ensure that it becomes
a legacy for generations to come.

     Mr. David Quick, 1502 Village Square, suggested that Patriots Point has
become a “hodge-podge” and accomplished with little thought, while the City
of Charleston has developed a well thought out waterfront area. He suggested
that there should be assurances that there are bountiful pedestrian access,
sufficiently buffered for nature including restoring native plants and trees for
the migratory birds and other wildlife. He suggested that there should not be
any light spillover, glazing on the windows to prevent birds from crashing into
the windows, and mass transit.

   Mr. James McKee, 1247 Palmetto Peninsula Dr, suggested that there should
be bike and pedestrian access to the waterfront that is shaded with the correct
                                                               Design Review Board
                                                                 December 3, 2025
                                                                        Page 6 of 7

tree species. He suggested that this is an opportunity to replace some of the
lost tree canopy within the Town. He suggested that this is an opportunity to
require more than the minimum.

    Ms. Pam Ireland, 820 Fiddler’s Point Lane, recited a portion of the goal and
purpose of the Waterfront Gateway, “it gives highest priority to increasing
public access to and views of natural waterways, integrating first class public
spaces near natural waterways, preserving existing view corridors of
surrounding water and marsh from public streets and other public places, and
developing new and improved public parks and gathering places among
waterfront locations”. She suggested that this development is blocking some
of these views and only alleyways to the waterfront instead of more purposeful
accesses to the waterway. She also suggested that there should be a main
waterfront gateway promenade as a main feature of the development instead
of a “supporting player”.

    Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning St, suggested that this is a great opportunity
and if the standards of the Waterfront Gateway District are implemented, it
would be a gem for the Town. He suggested that detailed plans for the
walkways and the landscaping that would be used should be included. He
suggested that the open space is critical as well as the size and species of trees
that will be used. He stated that the impact to the surrounding existing areas
need to be carefully considered as well. He suggested that there should be
more green space along the water.

    Ms. Joan Spier, 1848 West Canning Dr, suggested that the plan does not
take into account the utilization of the residents as there is not good waterfront
access. She suggested that every standard, ordinance and building code
should be adhered to and followed.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Kiel closed public comment and
moved to Board questions.

    Mr. Cave moved for approval including staff comments. Mr. Kiel seconded
the motion. All in favor with Ms. Richardson abstaining.
