H. Butcher and Boar Tree Removal, 760 Coleman Blvd, 532-02-00-050, Final
   Approval of Site and Landscape

   Mr. Luly reviewed staff comments with the Board (attachment 1).

Mr. Blake Middleton reviewed the request with the Board. He stated that the
tree is in decline. He referred to the arborist report.

Mr. Kichanan asked when the photo was taken. Mr. Middleton answered that
it was taken several months ago. Mr. Kichanan asked if there is a more recent
photo of the tree. Mr. Middleton answered in the affirmative and showed a
picture from his phone for the Board.

Mr. O’Neal asked what trees would be used for mitigation. Mr. Middleton 3 4-
inch oaks would be placed on the site for mitigation.

Mr. Kichanan stated that even though there is a lot of rebound, the tree could
survive. He stated that based on the location of the tree and proximity to the
building, he would be in favor of removal.

Mr. Wurst thanked the applicant for the arborist report.

    Mr. Kichanan moved for approval of the request with mitigation as required
