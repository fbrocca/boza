       6. FCA (Fiat Chrysler Automobiles) Master Site Plan, Tupelo Forest Drive
          and Hwy 17, TMS# 615-00-00-044, Preliminary Approval of Site,
          Correspondence

           Mr. Stone and Ms. Johnson reviewed staff comments with the Board
       (attachment 1).

       Mr. Jay Claypoole reviewed the request with the Board.

       Mr. John Noonis, 1011 Banker Court, expressed concern with the layout of
       the project. He expressed concern with the language within the planned
       development (PD) and the restriction outlined in the PD. He expressed
       concern that this use would impact the residential homes adjacent to the
       project. He expressed concern with traffic, given that there is only one
       ingress/egress for the subdivision and expressed concern with regard to
       emergency vehicles being able to access the neighborhood if there was an
       accident at the entrance.

       Mr. John Rowland, 2023 Welsh Pony Drive, expressed concern with being
       able to appropriately mitigate stormwater runoff given the amount of
       impervious surface proposed.

       Ms. Theresa Rowland, 2023 Welsh Pony Drive, expressed concern with the
       plans reviewed at this meeting being approved when it is known that there
       are revisions that would be coming forward. She expressed concern with
       buffers and lighting for the project. She expressed concern with this project
       bringing increased traffic to the area and that it would be exacerbate the
       issue. She expressed concern that this project would negatively impact her
       property values.

       There being no further comments, Mr. Lambert closed public comments.

           Mr. McCarthy moved for deferral of the project. Mr. Magoon seconded
       the motion. All in favor.
