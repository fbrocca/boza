   Ms. Morrison moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Richardson seconded the
motion. All in favor.

D. General Public Comment

    Ms. Lisa Schnipke, 185 Mossy Oak Way, suggested that the Waterfront should
be optimized for the public good and referred to the Waterfront Gateway
ordinance. She suggested that the grand lawn would not optimize use of the
waterfront as proposed for the Patriots Point project. She suggested that the
mixed-use requirement for this area should include cultural and entertainment
hubs, foster a sense of community, and preserve existing view corridors when
developing waterfront parks and gathering spaces. She stated that the area is also
a migratory stop for birds and suggested that the landscaping should include bird-
friendly plants to create habitats. She suggested that the land should be smartly
developed that provides amenities for the residents.

    Ms. Diedre Kennedy, 971 Cliffwood Dr, expressed concern with Patriots Point
Annex project. She suggested that the conceptual plan does not meet the
requirements and is being presented in a “fragmented” fashion. She suggested that
the Waterfront Gateway district was designed to be a rideable, walkable, mixed-use
area that protects natural resources and provides access to the waterfront. She
suggested that the conceptual plans do not meet the intent of the Waterfront
Gateway. She suggested that Town Council recommended community engagement
through workshops, but this has not come to fruition. She suggested that the
proposed plan does not provide an area for residents to utilize, but is more geared
toward tourist needs.

    Mr. Ian Duncan, 1427 Topaz, that the Patriots Point Annex project is a privately
funded development and suggested that the plan will address all of the concerns
raised during public comment.

There being no further comments, Mr. Kiel continued with the agenda.
