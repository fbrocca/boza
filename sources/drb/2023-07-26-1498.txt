4.   Queens Ct Duplexes, Queens Ct, TMS # 517-06-001; 108; 109; 110; 111;
     112; 113; 114; 115; 116; 117, Final Review of Site, Landscape and
     Architecture

     Mr. Stone and Mr. Carter reviewed staff comments with the Board.

   Mr. Todd Richardson and Mr. Luke Jarrett, Synchronicity, reviewed the
request with the Board and asked for an additional 5 minutes.

   Ms. Morrison moved to approve an additional five minutes of presentation
time. Mr. Kiel seconded the motion. All in favor.

     Ms. Holly Kingston, 304 Hawthorne St, expressed concern with the
proposed height of the buildings and suggested that it should be 35 feet. She
also expressed concern that the number of units exceeded what was allowed.
She also expressed concern with overflow parking and that this has not been
addressed.

    Mr. Grover Maxwell, 1103 Garland, expressed concern with the size of the
units and with protection of protected trees. He suggested that there should
be additional landscaping between this development and the neighborhood.
He expressed concern with the height of the buildings. He expressed concern
with the previous agreement for a contribution to Bayview Civic Club.

     There being no further comments, Mr. Lambert closed public comment.

    Mr. Kiel moved for approval to allow the segmented arch, allowing the
brick and working with staff the color palette. Ms. Morrison seconded the
motion. Motion passed on a 4 to 3 vote with Ms. Richardson, Ms. Morrison,
Mr. Lambert, and Mr. Kiel in favor; Mr. Belton, Mr. Cave, and Mr. Perez
opposed.
