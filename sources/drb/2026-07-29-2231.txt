        Mr. Lubert and Ms. Marshall reviewed staff comments with the Board.

         Mr. Luke Jarrett, architect, and Mr. Todd Ussery reviewed the request with
     the Board.

          Mr. Kiel asked if a transom window is being requested. Mr. Jarrett
     answered in the affirmative. Mr. Kiel asked about the brick color facing
     Coleman Blvd and expressed concern that the construction is not matching the
     previously approved color palette included on the drawings. Mr. Todd Ussery
     stated that the brick color approved is what is being used for the buildings as
     shown in the drawings. Mr. Kiel asked if the gray brick would be utilized. Mr.
     Ussery answered that the drawings currently do not show the brick colors
     being used. Mr. Jarrett concurred that the building colors shown on the plans
     are not the same as what was approved. Mr. Kiel stated that he would like to
     know the specific color palette and plan for the project. Mr. Jarrett agreed and
     stated that he would get that information.

         Ms. Morrison asked if the window change on the third floor, #8 on the
     drawings, would be changed to centered with the windows on the second
     floor. He stated that change #7 was to have the transom window with 2 lights
     and an infill window underneath.

         Mr. Cave asked if the #7 change would still accommodate a shower. Mr.
     Jarrett answered in the affirmative. Ms. Morrison asked if the original window

2.    Ira Rd Restaurant, 1434 Ira Rd, TMS # 559-13-00-004, Preliminary Review
      of Site, Landscape, and Architecture

     Ms. Richardson recused herself due to a conflict of interest.

     Mr. Lubert and Ms. Marshall reviewed staff comments with the Board.

     Mr. Eric Garris, architect, reviewed the request with the Board.

   Mr. Lambert asked about the base of the building and suggested that the
changes do not show a base, middle, and top. Mr. Garris answered that there
are lines that show a sense of base, middle, and top and that this was
previously approved.

   Mr. Kiel asked about the trees and if they would be preserved. Mr. Garris
answered that the deck is already constructed and was done to preserve the
root system of the tree. Mr. Kiel expressed concern about the water table as
well and suggested the water table should be more pronounced. Mr. Garris
answered that it mirrors the other elevations of the building that are already
constructed. Mr. Kiel suggested that the water table should be more
pronounced.

There being no comments, Mr. Cave closed public comment.

   Ms. Morrison stated she is comfortable with the water table and that it
matches the rest of the building. Mr. Cave agreed.
