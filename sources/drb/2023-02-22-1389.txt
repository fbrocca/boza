STAFF REPORT

Present:       Richard Lambert, Chair, Elissa Morrison, Timothy Kiel, Abigail Richardson,
               Richard Perez, Lee Cave.
Absent:        Steve Magoon (excused)
Staff:         Peter Stone, Chris Lubert, James Ward, John Farrell, Lynnette Lynes

      Mr. Lambert called the meeting to order at 5:00 pm.

A.   Approval of Agenda

   Ms. Morrison moved for approval of the agenda. Ms. Richardson seconded the
motion. All in favor.

B.   Approval of January Minutes

     Mr. Kiel moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Morrison seconded the motion.
All in favor with Mr. Perez abstaining.

C.   General Public Comment
D.   General Correspondence
E.   Consent Motions

All correspondence was linked to the agenda and distributed to the Board.

    There being no public comment or consent motions, Mr. Lambert continued
with the agenda.

   Ms. Morrison moved for approval to accept the additional information. Mr.

    Mr. Jose Biascoeshea, owner, reviewed the request with the Board and
stated that reason for the change to fake windows is to ensure better fire

    Mr. Kiel moved for approval with the horizontal slats and Bahama shutters
on the upper portion of the building and siding for the side of the building as
proposed. Ms. Richardson seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 5 to 1
vote with Ms. Richardson, Mr. Cave, Mr. Lambert, Mr. Perez, and Mr. Kiel in
favor; Ms. Morrison opposed.

     Mr. Bradford Egan, owner, reviewed the request with the Board.

   Ms. Morrison moved for approval to accept the addition information on
material colors. Ms. Richardson seconded the motion. All in favor.

     Mr. Bruce Koedding, 2853 Rivertowne Parkway, former Rivertowne
Property owner association member, expressed concern with traffic as
Rivertowne Parkway is the only ingress/egress for the subdivision. He
expressed concern with the neighborhood being notified of this project. He
referred to his correspondence that was submitted for the Board.

     There being no comments, Mr. Lambert closed public comment.

    Ms. Morrison moved for deferral in order to incorporate all staff comments,
reviewing east elevation symmetry, addressing rooftop equipment screening
and platform access, mechanical fence enclosure detail, consolidating
dumpster enclosures, studying the window symmetry, and including all colors.
Ms. Richardson seconded the motion.

Ms. Morrison withdrew her motion. Ms. Richardson withdrew her second.

    Ms. Morrison moved for denial to have the applicant incorporate all staff
comments, reviewing east elevation symmetry, addressing rooftop equipment
screening and platform access, mechanical fence enclosure detail,
consolidating dumpster enclosures, and including all colors. Ms. Richardson
seconded the motion. All in favor.
