   A. Ferry Wharf Master Plan, Harry Hallman Blvd., 517-00-00-247 (Tract 3-W),
      Final Approval of Site, Landscape and Architecture

      Ms. Abigail Richardson, Seamon, Whiteside, and Associates, reviewed the
   request with the Board.

   Mr. James Wilson, attorney, stated that he takes exception with the Zoning
   Administrator’s determination regarding the density and referred to a letter
   from the Zoning Administrator dated February 21, 2018. Mr. Wilson suggested
   that this is the appropriate Board to review these issues as outlined in the
   planned development (PD). He stated that a letter from the Zoning
   Administrator dated March 30, 2017 stated that the entitlement for bonus
                                                                Design Review Board

density under this PD would be handled through a “first come, first serve” basis
and the reason for coming before the Board for approval. He stated that their
position is that they are satisfying the conditions to utilize this density, paying
their share of all of the costs for the entire development, and providing a
tremendous amount of the open space required, and therefore should be
allowed the bonus density. He asked that the Board review and approve the
request.

Ms. Santilli asked for guidance from staff. Mr. Robertson stated that the Zoning
Administrator and legal counsel are available to answer any questions.

Mr. Kichanan asked if there are any other changes to the site plan other than
the trees at this time. Ms. Richardson answered that other changes were minor
in nature and could be approved by staff. Mr. Kichanan asked if the changes to
the density chart is included in the request. Mr. Wilson answered in the
affirmative. Mr. Robertson clarified that the changes would be brought to the
Board as each parcel is developed for approval. Mr. Kichanan asked if the
request is for approval for changes in density that was approved by Town
Council. Mr. Wilson answered that they are requesting use of a portion of the
approved density for their project. Mr. Robertson stated that the Zoning
Administrator’s determination differs from the applicant’s interpretation. He
stated that the determination indicates that Town Council is the proper
authority to approve those densities and not this Board.

Mr. Wilson contended that the Bridgeside apartments, for instance, did not
have to go back to Town Council for approval; it was approved through this
Board. Mr. Robertson clarified that the density and multi-family use was
approved by Town Council through the PD. Mr. Wilson stated that under the
language of the PD, the conceptual plan can be approved through this Board.

Mr. Luly reviewed staff comments with the Board (attachment 1).

Mr. Seabrook asked for clarification from Mr. Prause. Mr. Prause referred to his
letter of determination and suggested that legal advice could be provided in
executive session if desired. Mr. Seabrook stated that his understanding is that
these requests must be made to Town Council for approval.

C. Bowman Place Building A, 1136 Bowman Road, 559-13-00-033, 036, & 050,
   Preliminary Approval of Site, Landscape and Architecture

   Mr. Kyle Hoyt and Mr. Rush Dixon reviewed the request with the Board.

Mr. Kichanan asked about the facades and if the Old Georgetown façade would
be considered a front as well. Mr. Dixon answered in the affirmative and stated
that it was detailed similar on all sides and showed this on the plans for the
Board. Mr. Kichanan asked about the bump out on the right of the east
elevation. Mr. Dixon answered that it was for screening of the HVAC units and
where the fire suppression would be located.

Mr. Luly and Mr. Robertson reviewed staff comments with the Board
(attachment 1).

Mr. Seabrook asked if staff is comfortable with preliminary approval of
architecture. Mr. Robertson answered in the affirmative. Mr. Seabrook asked
about staff’s comments on the trees. Mr. Luly stated that some of the staff
comments were not read as they were not as pertinent, such as the timeline.

F. Chickfila Oakland, 3102 Ironclad Alley, 594-05-00-228, Final Approval of
   Site, Landscape and Architecture

   Mr. George Georgy, engineer, and Mr. Travis Reynolds reviewed the request
with the Board.

Mr. Kichanan asked about the addition of the canopies and how they relate to
the building. Mr. Reynolds answered that the same brick would be used for the
canopies as on the main structure. He stated that the same color palette would
be used as well.

Mr. Luly and Mr. Robertson reviewed staff comments with the Board
(attachment 1).

Mr. Seabrook asked about staff’s comment regarding a pitch roof. Mr.
Robertson answered that if a pitched roof is desired, then it would mean a larger
structure. Mr. Seabrook suggested that a canopy would be needed and
suggested that a pitch roof would not be necessary.

Ms. Santilli asked if a flat roof would be allowed. Mr. Robertson answered that
the Board has the flexibility to allow a flat roof. Ms. Santilli asked if staff
recommended approval. Mr. Robertson answered that if a pitch roof was not

needed then the plan could be approved. She stated that she would like to see
a mockup of the pitch roof.

Mr. Kichanan suggested that more preservation of trees should be considered.
He suggested that moving the dumpster enclosure farther down, study the cross
near the larger oak so that the smaller oaks do not need to be removed and
restudy of the circulation and parking for better preservation of trees. He
agreed with staff comment on the pilasters and suggested that the “rhythm of

vote with Mr. Bertin, Mr. Seabrook and Mr. Kichanan in favor; Ms. Santilli
opposed.

H. Engel and Volkers Office, 629 Johnnie Dodds Blvd., 517-04-00-105, Final
   Approval of Architecture

    Mr. John Lacasa reviewed the request with Board and stated that they would
like to paint the building white.

Mr. Seabrook asked the location of the building. Mr. Lacasa answered that it is
the former Radio Shack location. Mr. Seabrook asked if the entire building
would be painted. Mr. Lacasa answered that he would prefer to only paint the
front façade and columns, but understands that the entire building would need
to be painted. He stated that they would not paint the back of the building.

Mr. Robertson reviewed staff comments with the Board (attachment 1).

Mr. Seabrook suggested that the two facing building portions should be
considered to be painted the same color. Mr. Bertin agreed with Mr. Seabrook.
