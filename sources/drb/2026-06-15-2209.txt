Staff Report

Present:       Lee Cave, Chair, Elissa Morrison, Timothy Kiel, Abigail Richardson
Absent:        Richard Lambert, Daniel Cruz, Taylor Porter.
Staff:         Michael Robertson, Cecilia Marshall, Lynnette Lynes

      Mr. Cave called the meeting to order at 4:36 pm.

A.   Approval of agenda

   Ms. Morrison moved for approval of the agenda. Ms. Richardson seconded the
motion. All in favor.

B.   Consent motions
C.   Public comment
D.   General correspondence

There being no consent motions, public comment, or general correspondence, Mr.
Cave continued with the agenda.

E.   Business
     1. Dominion Energy Shed Expansion, 1277 Chuck Dawley Blvd, TMS # 560-
         01-00-002, Final Review of Site, Landscape, and Architecture Public
         Correspondence

           Ms. Marshall reviewed staff comments with the Board.

           Mr. Keith Clark and Mr. Zach Swalhah reviewed the request with the Board.

         Mr. Kiel asked if the seven-trunk magnolia is the main tree being removed.
     Mr. Clark answered in the affirmative.

         Ms. Morrison asked Mr. Clark to review the plantings for the Board. Mr.
     Clark indicated the new plantings on the plan for the Board. Ms. Morrison
     asked about screening for the new structure. Mr. Clark answered that there

would be more screening from the plantings along the berm. Ms. Morrison
asked the height of the berm. Mr. Clark answered that the berm is
approximately four feet higher than Chuck Dawley Blvd.

    Mr. Cave asked if any improvements would be made between the Mallard
Lakes subdivision and this site. Mr. Swalhah answered that there is a six-foot
fence along that property line, but additional plantings could be considered if
desired. Mr. Cave asked what the buffer requirements are for the site. Mr.
Clark noted that Dominion would be vacating the site within five years and
suggested that adding additional taller plantings might be a better option than
widening the buffer. Mr. Cave suggested that additional plantings should be
considered between Mallard Lakes and this site.

    Mr. Kiel asked if additional vehicles would be parked on site in the future.
Mr. Swalhah answered in the negative and stated that the expansion was
needed to house the vehicles currently parked on the site. Mr. Kiel asked when
the original structure was installed. Mr. Swalhah answered that it was built
around 1985. Mr. Kiel asked if the new construction would mean they have to
come into compliance with current ordinances. Mr. Robertson answered in
the negative; however, the Board can require additional buffer requirements
and landscaping if deemed appropriate. He stated that the ordinance would
require an E buffer, which is a natural 40-foot buffer that could be reduced to
a 25-foot buffer with a masonry wall.

    Mr. Cave suggested that the buffer should be enhanced as much as possible
for additional screening between the neighborhood. Mr. Swalhah stated that
once the shed expansion is completed, the vehicles would be housed under
the shed which would mean the vehicles would be farther away from the
neighborhood.

     Ms. Morrison asked if the structure could be moved to avoid having to
remove the magnolia tree. Mr. Clark answered that the issue is traffic pattern
around the shed. Ms. Morrison asked if there is a site plan showing the
location of the tree. Mr. Clark noted the location of the Magnolia tree on the
site plan for the Board.

   Ms. Linda Felner, 1266 Gannett Road, stated that the proposed structure
would be 18 feet high. She stated that she has kept abreast of changes to this

    site over the years. She stated that when the zoning changes were approved,
    it was noted that the trees would be preserved and no additional vehicles

    There being no further comments, Mr. Cave closed public comment and moved
    to Board deliberation.

        Ms. Morrison asked about the location of the structure. The Board
    discussed the site and if changes to the proposed plan could be accomplished
    to preserve the Magnolia tree.

        Mr. Kiel asked the height of the existing warehouse. Mr. Clark answered
    that he was unsure.

       Ms. Richardson asked about the radius of the turn drive in relation to the
    Magnolia tree. Mr. Clark and Mr. Swalhah explained the drive patterns for the
    Board and stated that the Magnolia tree would be in the middle of one of the
    main drive aisles.

        Ms. Morrison moved approval with the condition that the applicant work
    with staff to preserve the Magnolia tree and to add additional landscaping and
    plantings between the residential neighborhood and the site with additional
    plantings along the berm. Ms. Richardson seconded the motion. Motion
    passed on a 3 to 1 vote with Ms. Richardson, Ms. Morrison, and Mr. Cave in
    favor; Mr. Kiel opposed.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 5:10 pm.
