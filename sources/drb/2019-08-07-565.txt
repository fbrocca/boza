     5. Chase Bank West, 960 Houston Northcutt Blvd., TMS# 517-00-00-028,
        Preliminary Approval of Site, Landscape and Architecture

          Mr. Stone and Ms. Johnson reviewed staff comments with the Board
     (attachment 1).

     Ms. Marianne Moseley, Kimley-Horn, reviewed the request with the Board. Mr.
     Paul Gorenflo, Mr. Michael Brown, and Mr. Matt Wauben reviewed the project
     and proposed materials with the Board.

Mr. Lambert asked about the east elevation and if the roofline is cantilevered.
Mr. Brown answered in the affirmative. Mr. Lambert asked about the site plan
and if the proposed roof is accurately shown. Mr. Brown answered that there
is an error in the plans. Mr. Lambert asked about the parking fronting Johnnie
Dodds Blvd and if there is sufficient room for turnout. Mr. Brown answered in
the negative and stated that this would be adjusted. Mr. Lambert suggested
that the connection to the frontage road should be clarified.

Mr. Wurst asked if the parapet would be high enough to screen the mechanical
equipment. Mr. Brown answered in the affirmative.

Mr. O’Neal suggested that the angle of the dumpster enclosure should be
reviewed and asked how the truck would collect trash. He suggested that the
curb should have a larger radius for better truck movement. Mr. O’Neal asked
if there would be a second drive-thru lane. Mr. Brown answered that there
could be a second added in the future. Mr. O’Neal suggested that larger drive
aisles should be considered. Mr. Brown answered that the median would be
reduced with the additional drive-thru. Mr. O’Neal suggested that the building
setbacks should be verified. Mr. O’Neal suggested that the tree plantings should
be reviewed to ensure they are not impeding with the sewer lines. Ms. Moseley
answered that the tree could be moved.

There being no comments, Ms. Santilli closed public comment.

Mr. Boan suggested that the applicant be cognizant of the lighting design to
ensure there is no spill over.

Mr. Wurst suggested that the header across the double door columns could be
higher to raise the gable so it looks more “exalted”. He suggested that the
returns on the gable should not be “pork chopped”, but a straight gable. He
suggested that the pilasters should be spread out so the window trim can be
added. Mr. Wurst suggested that a window or decorative element should be
installed in the gable to differentiate it. He stated that spandrel glass is not
allowed and suggested that a framed window with a louver or shiplap could be
considered. He suggested that there should be some detail between the brick
and sill to add more detail. He suggested that the drive-thru canopy supports
could be more pilaster type to better match with the building.

Mr. O’Neal asked the reason for the parking fronting US 17. Mr. Brown
answered that Houston Northcutt was considered the front. Ms. Johnson stated
that the ordinance stipulates that Houston Northcutt and the frontage road
would be considered the front. Mr. O’Neal suggested that fronting on US17
should be considered.

   Mr. Lambert moved for preliminary with all comments being addressed. Mr.
Boan seconded the motion. All in favor.

6. Chase Bank East, 1825 N. Highway 17, TMS# 558-00-00-393, Preliminary
   Approval of Site, Landscape and Architecture

     Mr. Stone and Ms. Johnson reviewed staff comments with the Board
(attachment 1).

Mr. Lambert asked about the hatched parking area to the right of the building.
Mr. Brown answered that this is a designated walking path to the dumpster.

Mr. O’Neal asked if there is an easement for Maizy Lane. Mr. Gorenflo answered
that they are working with the owner to have the agreement in place. Mr.
O’Neal asked if the walkway to the dumpster could be included with the
sidewalk. Ms. Moseley answered that this could be reviewed.

There being no comments, Ms. Santilli closed public comment.

Mr. Lambert suggested that the turning distances for the parking should be
reviewed to ensure there is sufficient space.

Mr. Wurst suggested that a larger header could be considered. He suggested
that the rear window might need additional framing in order to meet wind
requirements. He stated that he would have the same comments regarding the
pilasters as with the Chase West building.

Mr. O’Neal asked about stormwater retention. Ms. Moseley answered that they
propose retaining the existing stormwater pipes. Mr. O’Neal asked if additional
stormwater retention could be near the pervious parking. Ms. Moseley
answered that it would impact the protected trees.
