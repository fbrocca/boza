6.    Garden Flats at South Bay, Founders Way, TMS# 560-00-00-068, Final
      Approval of Site, Landscape, and Architecture CORRESPONDENCE

     Mr. Wurst and Mr. O’Neal recused themselves due to a conflict of interest.

Mr. Stone and Ms. Johnson reviewed site staff comments with the Board
(attachment 1).

Ms. Mary Martinich, Seamon Whiteside, reviewed the application with the
Board.

Ms. Cornelia Drew, South Bay, expressed concern with the location of the
garden flats and suggested that they should be moved so they are farther from
the eagle nest and would preserve more trees. She expressed concern with
the amount of parking proposed and suggested that it might not be sufficient.

Mr. Ryan Clarey, Newkirk Environmental, stated that there are stipulations
outlined by the Fish and Wildlife Commission regarding when construction can
take place because of the eagle’s nest. He stated that the permit was issued
with those conditions.

There being no further public comment, Mr. Lambert closed public comment.

   Mr. Swails moved to defer based on a one-way and staff comments. Mr.
Duncan seconded the motion. All in favor with Mr. Wurst and Mr. O’Neal
abstaining.
