1.   Call to order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 8:30am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the July 31, 2023 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

    Mr. Stuart Whitesides, Seamon, Whitesides, and Associates, spoke regarding
the Central Mount Pleasant PD amendment. He stated that this item was deferred
from last month’s meeting. He stated that that they are requesting a three year
extension with a contribution of $25,000 for the completion of the Rifle Range Road
bike path. He stated that he is available to further discuss these issues if desired.

     Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning Street, spoke regarding the changes of seats for
the Historic District Preservation Commission. He stated that all seats are now
filled, with additional interested parties to fill any future vacancies. He suggested
that the terms should be extended to five years from the current two year terms
so that there are experienced members on the Commission. He suggested that the
seats should not be allowed to be filled by business owners within the Town.

    Mr. James Wilson, representative for Liberty Senior Living, spoke regarding the
proposed expansion of South Bay Senior Living. He stated that it is a part of the
Central Mount Pleasant PD. He stated that the proposed expansion would not
increase traffic impacts outlined in the impact assessment. He stated that as CCRC
is considered multi-family development, they are requesting to be exempted from
