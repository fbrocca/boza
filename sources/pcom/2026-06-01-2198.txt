c.    Discussion of rezoning school properties to Public Institutional (PI)
      District

    Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee. She clarified that a
church use would require a special exception, while a school use would require
a conditional use for residentially zoned properties.

   Ms. Whitaker asked the difference between PI-1 and PI-2 zoning. Ms. Reed
answered that the PI zoning is more for community facilities.

    Mr. Tinkey asked if PI-1 would be a precursor for PI-2. Ms. Reed answered
in the negative and stated that there are different allowed uses for PI-2. She
stated that if desired, a new separate zoning for schools and churches could

     be considered either during the Comprehensive Plan update or sooner. Mr.
     Tinkey suggested that a community facilities zoning that would allow
     appropriate uses within neighborhoods should be considered.

        Ms. Whitaker agreed that the residential use might not be appropriate in
     the future and rezoning to the recommended PI-1 zoning might be
     appropriate.

         Ms. Hyatt asked if a laydown yard would be allowed in PI-1. Ms. Reed
     answered in the affirmative. Ms. Reed suggested that a new zoning
     classification should be considered that would only allow those desired uses.

        Ms. Whitaker suggested that PI-1 zoning would be more appropriate as it
     would prevent the residential development in the event the church or school
     use is abandoned.

         Ms. Hyatt asked if staff reached out to the schools. Ms. Reed answered in
     the affirmative and stated that there was not much interest.

          Ms. Whitaker asked how many properties currently have underlying
     residential zoning. Ms. Reed answered that the majority of the properties
     have a residential zoning designation.

         Mr. Tinkey asked how many church uses were abandoned and then
     developed residential. Ms. Reed answered that she is not aware of any church
     uses being abandoned. Ms. Hyatt suggested that a church in the Hamlin area
     and another on Church Street was abandoned.

         Ms. Whitaker moved to recommend to Town Council approval of rezoning
     church and school properties currently zoned R-1 and R-2 to the PI zoning
     recommended in the Comprehensive Plan Future Land Use Plan. Mr. Tinkey
     seconded the motion. All in favor.

6.   Discussion of Third-Party Parking Operations at Shem Creek Park

    Ms. Hyatt stated that she has received some complaints regarding parking in
the Shem Creek area and therefore asked for this discussion to determine if any
                                                                  Planning Committee
                                                                         June 1, 2026
                                                                           Page 5 of 6

changes should be recommended. She stated that some vehicles have been
booted and asked if this is legally allowed.

    Mr. Pagliarini stated that this has been discussed over the years. He stated that
enforcement is an issue as well as how the parking fee is or is not validated and/or
reimbursed. He stated that the required parking for the businesses should not be
paid parking. He stated that another issue is determining what parking is required
for which business. He stated that some of the businesses have been in existence
for many years and trying to determine parking, property lines, etc. would take
some time. He stated that completing the title searches could cost up to $10,000-
$20,000. He stated that adequate enforcement of the parking in the area would
entail putting a detailed plan in place.

    Mr. Tinkey suggested that redevelopment is another issue, and it could mean
that paid parking is established in other adjacent areas, such as Moultrie Plaza. He
suggested that it should be carefully considered to ensure that the enforcement is
completed for all areas of the Town.

   Mr. Pagliarini stated that Shem Creek is a special area with conditions that
would not necessarily be seen in other areas.

    Ms. Hyatt expressed concern with the parking rental businesses having a
business license. Mr. DeMoura answered that all businesses operating in Mount
Pleasant are required to have a business license, so this could be reviewed and
monitored.

    Mr. Tinkey suggested that surrounding businesses might see the benefit of
having the paid parking for their business. Ms. Reed answered that a business
would have to ensure that paid parking was only for those spaces in excess of their
required parking.

    Mr. Pagliarini stated that the required parking spaces can be a complex issue.
He stated that another aspect is what parking is validated and/or reimbursed. He
stated the issue for the Shem Creek area is that the businesses are seeing an issue
where their clients are not able to park. Added to that is that many of the
businesses have been established for many years, with turnover of adjacent
properties that further complicates the matter.
