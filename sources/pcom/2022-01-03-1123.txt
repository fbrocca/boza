1.   Approval of Minutes from the December 6, 2021 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

2.   Public Comments

    Mr. Charlie Carmody, 107 New St, spoke regarding proposed limits on hotels.
He stated that one of his clients owns property on Ben Sawyer Blvd and would like
to maintain the allowed hotel use for the site so that they can move forward with
a hotel use on that site. He stated that he is in favor of other restrictions proposed
for hotel uses, but he would like to have the conditional use remain.

    Mr. Mike Bennett stated that he has the property Mr. Carmody referenced
under contract for a hotel use. He stated that he is in favor of the hotel use being
a conditional use and that it makes sense. He stated that he would like to have the
hotel use available for the property under contract so that he can move forward
with his proposed hotel. He suggested that a hotel use would be less intense than
an office use.

     Ms. Lucy Gordon, 1551 Ben Sawyer Blvd, spoke regarding hotel uses. She
suggested that the location of hotels in the Town is critical particular regarding
traffic. She suggested that with the traffic congestion currently on Coleman Blvd
and Ben Sawyer Blvd, a hotel use would not be appropriate. She suggested that
more amenities geared to serving the residents would be a more appropriate.

b.   Proposed ordinance amendment to repeal Zoning Code Section
     156.318, Boulevard Overlay District, in its entirety, along with the
     permitted uses and development standards that are subscribed to the
     properties therein. Request to relocate determined design standards
     within Zoning Code Section(s) 156.201 Bufferyards, 156.310 Commercial
     Design Review Overlay District, 156.174 Design Regulations for
     Commercial, Institutional, and Multi-Family use parking lots, Chapter
     156.102 Height and Size of Buildings and/or Structures, Chapter 156.173
     Area and Paving Requirements, Chapter 156.172, Application of Parking
     Requirements, Chapter 156.108 Curb Cuts, Visibility at Intersection, and
     Pedestrian Access, Chapter 156.303 Residential Use Districts, Chapter
     156.304 Nonresidential Use Districts. (Presentation to Planning
     Commission)

     Ms. Reed and Mr. Stone reviewed the request with the Committee.

    Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the
amendments with keeping the mixed-use component and repealing the
overlay district. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.

     Ordinances, pertaining to Boutique Hotels or Inns and Hotels / Extended
