1.   Call to order

Mr. Tinkey called the meeting to order at 10:31 am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the December 1, 2025 meeting

   Ms. Whitaker moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Crosby seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

    Mr. Daniel Brownstein, 1391 Downsbury Drive, commended the Committee on
their discussion of the Ten Mile overlay district and other settlement communities.
He stated that coordinating with Charleston County is important. He expressed
concern with the changes to the ordinances regarding accessory structures and
suggested that more public notification should be provided.

There being no further comments, Mr. Tinkey continued with the agenda.
