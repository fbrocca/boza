      Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 3:30 pm.

1. Approval of Minutes from the February 28, 2022 meeting and the February 28,
   2022 special meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Corley seconded the
motion. All in favor.

2. Public Comments

    Ms. Jenitza Laguna, 1179 Camellia Drive, asked that the number of persons
allowed for an ADU be increased to four persons when there are two bedrooms.

    Ms. Laurie Bixler, 144 3rd Ave, spoke regarding short-term rentals. She stated
that she appreciates the Town having regulations in place for short-term rentals.
She asked that the Committee continue to consider what is good for the Town and
those that currently have short-term rentals.

3. Presentation by Dominion Energy on upcoming tree trimming projects in
   Mount Pleasant

    Ms. Jennifer Hightower, Mark Branham, and Clay Chaplain, Dominion Energy,
reviewed current and future projects with the Committee. Mr. Branham stated
that they have just started project H19 section A. He stated that they do notify the
residents when they are starting the project that includes the customer service
number to contact with any concerns or questions. Mr. Chaplain reviewed the
specifications for trimming around the power lines with the Committee.

4. Budget Review FY 2023
   a. Planning, Land Use and Neighborhoods General Fund operating
      expenditures
   b. Planning, Land Use and Neighborhoods Capital Fund projects and
      maintenance expenditures

      Mr. DeMoura reviewed the PLAN Department budget with the Committee.

       The Committee discussed code enforcement and if more staff is needed.
   Ms. Reed stated that a livability task force has been established that would allow
   for better coordination between the various departments and better address
   enforcement issues.

       Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to the Budget Committee approval of
   the Planning, Land Use, and Neighborhoods departmental budget for FY 2023.
   Ms. Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.

5. Discussion and possible action related to amendments to the Town’s parking
   requirements

   Ms. Reed and Mr. Pagliarini reviewed the request with the Committee.

   Mr. Chapman moved for businesses to delineate parking for customers only.
Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. Motion failed on a 2 to 2 vote with Mr. Chapman
and Ms. Hyatt in favor; Ms. Corley and Ms. Whitley opposed.

9. Discussion and Possible action regarding the Town’s maximum occupancy for
   an Accessory Dwelling Unit

    Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee. Mr. Iacofano stated that
changing the number to 2 per bedroom to allow for more affordable housing within
the Town.

    Ms. Corley moved to recommend to Town Council amending the number of
persons allowed for an ADU to up to four. Ms. Whitley seconded the motion.
Motion failed on a 2 to 2 vote with Ms. Corley and Ms. Whitley in favor; Mr.
Chapman and Ms. Hyatt opposed.

10. Discussion and Possible action regarding the Town’s Short Term Rental
    Program

   Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

     The Committee discussed whether a deadline date for resubmittal of
applications for a permit should be considered.

   Mr. Chapman moved to refer this issue to the Planning Commission for a public
hearing. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.

reduced.
