   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Corley seconded the
motion. All in favor.

2. Public Comments

    Mr. Ben Traywick, president of Bayview Acres Civic Club, spoke re: R-37-20. He
suggested that there was not sufficient reason for an additional deferral. He stated
that there have been many meetings over the past several years regarding the
proposed development without a satisfactory resolution. He expressed concern
that this request would increase density, traffic congestion, height, and would not
be in character with the neighborhood. He expressed concern that it would
exacerbate the parking deficiency and congestion. He expressed concern with child
safety as well. He asked that this request be denied.

    Mr. Brett Woodward, representative for Queens Court, stated that they have
met several times with the neighborhood on this issue. He stated that they were
asked to provide some deed restrictions for the proposed development, which has
been accomplished. He stated that they are asking for another deferral to reach
out again to the neighborhood to receive feedback and try to resolve all issues.

     Ms. Laurie Bixler, 144 3rd Ave, spoke regarding short-term rentals. She
suggested that enforcement is a concern and further suggested that the majority
are in favor of enforcement. She distributed a copy of an ordinance from another
municipality for the Committee to review and suggested that it could be used as a
template for Mount Pleasant.

    Mr. Drew Burris, representative for Queens Court stated that they have worked
to resolve issues voiced by the neighborhood and accommodate their requests. He
stated that based on conversation with members of Town Council, they were urged
to reach out again to the neighborhood to try to come to a resolution and was the
main reason for the request for a deferral.

    Mr. Fred Smalls, 2959 Elijah Smalls Road, spoke regarding annexation of
Covington. He stated that the community is opposed to the request. He suggested
that this could mean that the zoning, density, and lot size would be changed to
something that would be out of character with the Phillips Community. He
expressed concern that these requests would affect traffic, density, and the
character of the neighborhood and asked that all these issues be considered before
approving annexation.

      Mr. Richard Habersham, 2938 Bennett Charles Road, spoke regarding
annexation and zoning requests for Covington. He stated that this is within the
Phillips Community and suggested that the request would affect character of the
Phillips Community. He stated that they have requested historic settlement
designation and if these requests are approved it could affect the historic
designation. He suggested that the request is not in character with the
neighborhood, particularly the land pattern.

     Mr. George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Rd, expressed concern with the quality of
life within the settlement communities. He suggested that many of the recent
requests within the settlement communities have been in conflict with the
character of the neighborhood and maintaining open space. He asked that the
annexation request be denied.

    Ms. Cashion Drolet, Historic Charleston Foundation, stated that she is opposed
to the annexation and rezoning requests for Covington. She stated that the
property is located in a national register historic district eligible area. She
suggested that these requests are out of character with the neighborhood and
inconsistent with the neighborhood patterns for the area. She suggested that these
requests are inconsistent with the settlement pattern and would result in density
and use that are inconsistent with the current land use pattern within the Phillips
Community. She stated that the Comprehensive Plan calls for settlement
community land use in this area.

    Ms. Betsy La Force, Coastal Conservation League, stated that she is opposed to
the annexation and rezoning requests for Covington. She stated that the Planning
Commission recommended denial and asked that the Committee uphold this
recommendation as well as the desire of the Phillips Community. She suggested
that the requests are in conflict with the character of the neighborhood and in
conflict with the Comprehensive Plan. She suggested that it would also be in
conflict with the historic designation request. She asked that if the annexation is
approved, the Committee consider zoning the property to be consistent with the
Charleston County zoning designation of S-3, that would match the density and lot
size that is typical with settlement community land uses and the Phillips
Community character.
