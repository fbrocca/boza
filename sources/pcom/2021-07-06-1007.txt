2. Public Comments

    Mr. Chris Colman, 118 Pitt St, spoke concerning nonconformities and reviewed
a handout he distributed to the Committee. He suggested that the nonconformity
ordinance should be reviewed and some changes considered. He also suggested
that changes under nonconformities should have public notice.

    Mr. Robert Hervey, Executive Director for East Cooper Habitat for Humanity,
stated that he is available for any questions on the item 4E.

    Mr. Edward Jefferson, 1145 McKnight Road, expressed concern regarding item
6A and what type of business is being proposed. Mr. Chapman stated that more
information would be provided when the item is discussed.

   Mr. George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Rd, expressed concern with changes to the
SB-OD and the proximity of businesses to the road. He also suggested that AB
zoning was not allowed in the overlay district, and the developer should contact
the community to review the project so the community is more comfortable with
the request. He expressed concern with possible changes to the allowed buffer.

    Ms. Pat Sullivan, 1002 Plantation Court, stated that one of the goals in the
Comprehensive Plan is to protect the integrity of the settlement communities by
limiting development encroachment and ensuring that any development is
compatible with the community. She reviewed maps with the Committee showing
the historical Philips Community. She suggested that the proposed development
would be out of character with the community.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley continued with the agenda.
