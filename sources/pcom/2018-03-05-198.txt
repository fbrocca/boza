    BACKGROUND
• I’On =10 phases with a maximum of 762 units. As of December 31, 2017,
  752 of these units were either built or under construction.
• This request does not change the number of units or alter the density of the
  development; it pertains only to text & explanatory graphics, and is intended
  to standardize the requirements applicable to the remaining undeveloped
  lots.
• More importantly, it will clearly present the requirements for all future
  modifications, additions, alterations, or other improvements to existing
  residences.
    BACKGROUND
• When originally adopted, three (3) different “Building Types” were
  established, with very similar requirements.
• Administering these was problematic, and it was determined that only one
  would be applied.
• That interpretation [reinforced with a letter] in 1999 was done to provide
  clarification to all parties when designing and reviewing plans for individual
  homes on lots.
• However, there was never an ordinance change adopted by Town Council to
  enact this change.
• This amendment seeks to remedy that situation.
EXISTING STANDARDS
NEIGHBORHOOD STANDARDS
Yard
1. Buildings shall be set on the lot relative to the build to zones and setbacks specified for each
   building type.
2. Stoops, chimneys, balconies and bay windows may encroach within the setback or build-to zone.
3. Street walls shall be built on frontage lines as shown.
Porches
1. Shopfront buildings shall have an arcade that extends along 100% of the street façade.
2. Side-yard and All-yard buildings shall have a covered porch a minimum of 8 feet in depth.
3. The porch shall extend a minimum of 50% of the length of the home in the case of Side-yard
   buildings and a minimum of 40% of the street façade in the case of All-yard buildings.
4. The street façade of All-yard buildings shall extend along the front yard a minimum of 40% of the
   lot width.
Outbuildings
1. Outbuildings shall have a maximum footprint of 625 square feet and building height of 18 feet.
2. The walls of the outbuildings at interior property lines shall be left windowless and shall be two-hour
   fire rated.
3. Outbuildings may have less than 3’ setbacks from adjoining property line.
     IMPACTS
• Homeowners are more frequently requesting updates and/or additions to their
  homes. During the site plan review process, staff is finding that while their home was
  constructed according to one building type, they are officially classified as another.
• This often not only prohibits them from making said changes without receiving a
  variance through the Board of Zoning Appeals, but it’s also making what currently
  exists non-compliant.
• With the intent to rectify this situation and at the direction of the Zoning Administrator,
  the I’On Company proposes the text be amended to match what has been
  constructed.
• This will not change anything in the neighborhood, but will merely align what has
  been built with the adopted text and, ultimately bring much of the development into
  compliance.

152, FLOOD DAMAGE PREVENTION: SPECIAL
STORMWATER MANAGEMENT AREA
Cause – Resulting from the information provided in the recent stormwater study conducted inside the Old
Village area, existing stormwater infrastructure was deemed inadequate and sub-standard. Residents and
Town officials became concerned with permitting home additions, footprint expansions, and grade altering
activity.

Intent – Town staff proposes to formalize unique stormwater standards based upon a specific watershed
area. This area is consistent with the Old Village Watershed Study Phase One produced in 2017. Proposal
is to ensure residents have information which allows them the opportunity to improve their property without
adversely impacting the existing system or adjacent neighbors, and remain within the 40% impervious surface
coverage.

Purpose – Address residential permitting challenges by managing additional runoff to our infrastructure and
safeguarding our citizens in an equitable and responsible manner.
