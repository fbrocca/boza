                   TOWN OF MOUNT PLEASANT, SOUTH CAROLINA
                            PLANNING COMMITTEE
                              FEBRUARY 3, 2025
                                  MINUTES

                       Municipal Complex, Council Chambers


Present:    G.M. Whitley, Chair, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman, Jake Rambo.
Staff:      Eric DeMoura, Michele Reed, Peter Stone

1. Call to order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 8:30 am.

2. Approval of Minutes from the January 6, 2025 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3. Public Comments

    Ms. Michele Stress, 545 Veranda View, spoke regarding the Patriots Annex
request. She suggested that Committee consider a hybrid plan that establishes the
PD, but keeps many of the requirements within the Waterfront Gateway zoning
that would provide better environmental protection. She suggested that some
incentives for the residents should be considered.

    Mr. James Wilson, attorney for the Patriots Annex request, stated that he is
available to answer any questions.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley continued with the agenda.

4. Employee years of service

Ms. Reed stated that Ms. Susan Bettelli was not able to attend the meeting, but
would be recognized for 20 years of service at the Department’s next staff meeting.

5. Patriots Annex Development Agreement and Planned Development. First is to
   request a five-year extension of the Patriots Annex Development Agreement.
   Second is a request to establish a new PD, Planned Development District

   Ordinance, for an approximately 30.32-acre portion of a larger 162.86-acre
   tract of land located off Patriots Point Road. Property is currently zoned WG-
   C, Cooper River Waterfront Gateway District. The newly established district is
   proposed to be called Patriots Annex PD, Planned Development. Planning
   Commission recommended Approval with Conditions.

   Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

    Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the PD request
with the following conditions:
   ➢ Entire development shall have a minimum 14% open space requirement;
   ➢ The number of residential units shall be restricted to a total of 80 units for the
      entire development;
   ➢ no flat roofs allowed;
   ➢ number of trees per acre shall be reduced from the 160 trees per acre to 120
      trees per acre.
Mr. Chapman seconded the motion.

    Mr. Chapman moved to amend the motion to include the heights previously
approved to be included with the PD document. Motion failed due to the lack of a
second.

    Mr. Rambo moved to amend the motion for the property indicated as Parcel 2
on the approved DRB conceptual plan and Patriots Fountain on the proposed PD
plan have a maximum 35 feet height. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion.

    Ms. Whitley called for a vote on the motion as amended. Motion passed on a 3
to 1 vote with Ms. Whitley, Mr. Rambo, and Ms. Hyatt in favor; Mr. Chapman
opposed.

6. Adjourn

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 9:19 am.
