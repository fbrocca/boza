3.   Public Comments

    Mr. Steven Brock, 34 Hopetown Road, spoke regarding the special Town Council
meeting held December 2, 2024, on the Patriots Point development. He suggested
that it then went to Planning Commission for a public hearing that was out of
procedure as the Planning Commission should hold a public hearing prior to going
to Town Council. He suggested that this is disdain for the public. He spoke about
the proposed tax increase at the special Town Council meeting, that it was also not
done according to procedure. He also expressed concern with the number of
special meetings that were held over the past year and that these should only be
done for matters of urgency between regular meetings. He suggested that these
special meetings are often scheduled during times when the public would have
difficulty attending. He suggested that this should be considered in the future.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley closed public comment and
continued with the agenda.

      Amendment. Request to amend the Pepper Tract PD Ordinance to

      Commission recommended approval with conditions.
