      Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 2:49 pm.

1. Approval of Minutes from the August 4, 2020 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

2. Public Comments

    Mr. Jonathan Wocher, spoke regarding items 5A and 5B stated that he was
available for questions.

    Ms. Jessica Miller, Waterway Arms, spoke regarding short term rentals. She
expressed concern that approximately 15% of the dwelling units in their
neighborhood are short term rentals and that the short term rentals are destroying
the character of the neighborhood. She expressed concern with child safety. She
stated that their covenants do not allow businesses and suggested that short term
rentals are businesses and suggested that they should not be allowed in the
neighborhood.

    Mr. Owen Meislin, 703 Center St, spoke regarding short term rentals in
Waterway Arms. He stated that he is against having short term rentals in the
neighborhood and in general as they deteriorate the character of the
neighborhoods where they are located. He stated that Waterway Arms covenants
do not allow businesses and suggested that this should apply to short term rentals
since a business license is required to operate.

    Ms. Leanna Meir, Waterway Arms, spoke regarding short term rentals. She
suggested that short term rentals should not be allowed when covenants restrict
businesses. She suggested that the short term rental units are not monitored. She

    Mr. Richard Horn, Oak Park subdivision, spoke regarding item 7A, rezoning on
Longpoint Road. He stated that his understanding is that this item was withdrawn.
He stated that many of the residents are in opposition of the request. He suggested
that this is not an appropriate location for a gas station as proposed.

     Mr. John Nelson, 623 Leisure Lane and HOA vice-president of Hidden Cove,
spoke in opposition of the proposed gas station on Longpoint Road. He suggested
that it is not the appropriate location for the gas station. He expressed concern
with traffic in the area and suggested that the proposed use would exacerbate the
traffic in the area.

    Mr. George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Rd and representative for CAGE, stated
that they are in opposition of the request for 1240 Six Mile Road and opposed to
the request being deferred for another month. He suggested that this property
and neighborhood currently provides affordable housing for the Town and
suggested that it should remain with the affordable housing.

    Mr. John Wright, 145 Rose Lane, spoke regarding the 1240 Six Mile Road
request. He stated that he is opposed to the rezoning request. He suggested that
the SB-OD should be upheld and further amended to include a legal restriction of
any changes to property within the overlay district being allowed.
