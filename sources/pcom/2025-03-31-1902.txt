3.   Public Comments

     Ms. Lisa Schniptke, 185 Mossy Oak Way, asked that the zoning change for
Patriots Point should not be approved and the Waterfront Gateway should be kept.
She expressed concern with the planned development ordinance that could allow
trees to be more easily removed, the buffers decreased, sidewalk width reduced to
five feet, and a fee in lieu of planting for up to 40% of the tree mitigation required
could be paid. She suggested that additionally plan changes could be made without
adequate public input, which would add to the public’s concern regarding
transparency. She suggested that these changes do not reflect the Town’s vision
or Comprehensive Plan. She suggested that the existing zoning should be
strengthened by increasing the tree protection per acre with no exceptions, expand
buffers to a minimum of 50 feet, guarantee public waterfront access, and increase
the open space required to 20%, and implement oversight for any plan changes.
She stated that protection of the waterfront is paramount and deserves special
consideration to preserve these areas for future generations.
