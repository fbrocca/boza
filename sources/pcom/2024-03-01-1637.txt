3.   Old Business
      A. Review recommendations from the Special Planning Committee
         Meeting held on Thursday, February 15, 2024 regarding subchapters A,
         B and C

           Ms. Reed reviewed the recommendations from the previous meeting.

        The Committee discussed the proposed mixed neighborhood floating zone
        and if it should remain with changes to the requirements or if the current
        workforce housing planned development should be kept in place with
        amendments. The Committee agreed that this should be further discussed
        with Town Council as a whole.

        The Committee discussed the proposal to raise building height to 40 feet and
        if this was appropriate.

           Mr. Rambo moved to recommend keeping the current residential building
        height of 35 feet and 40 feet if in a flood zone. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
