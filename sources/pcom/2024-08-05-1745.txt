1.   Call to order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 8:46 am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the July 1, 2024 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

    Ms. Morgan Bentivegna, The Beach Company, spoke regarding the Wilson PD
amendment. She expressed concern that if the amendment is not approved, it
could significantly impact the redevelopment of Towne Centre due to closures of
stores. She stated that they are asking for eight units per acre with six units at
market value and two units for attainable housing in order to provide needed
attainable housing for the Town. She asked that the request be approved.

    Mr. Dan Schafer, LS3P, spoke regarding the Wilson PD amendment. He stated
that the new proposed step back height would provide the height needed for
redevelopment. He stated that the step back height design is what is included in
the Comprehensive Plan.

   Ms. Jodi Manti, 675 Kent, spoke regarding the text amendment to expand the
uses allowed for home-based businesses. She expressed concern with restrictions
recommended for swim lessons on the number of people allowed and the time of

     Meeting
     a. Text Amendment. Proposal to amend Section 156.110 of Chapter 156,
         Zoning Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances, pertaining to
         Accessory Uses. Proposed is to amend the ordinance to expand Home-
         Based Businesses to allow certain outdoor instructional activities,
         including but not limited to swimming lessons

          Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

6.   R-06-24. Towne Centre. Request to amend the Wilson Tract Planned
     Development District ordinance (Ord. No. 95041) as it pertains to an
     approximately 41.74-acre portion of an approximately 45.83-acre tract of
     land known as Towne Centre, located between Hungryneck Boulevard and
     U.S Highway 17 North and identified by TMS Nos. 558-00-00-096, 558-00-00-
     098, 558-00-00-100, 558-00-00-102, 558-00-00-340, 558-00-00-387, and 558-
     00-00-399. Proposed Amendments specifically pertain to expansion of
     master plan onto 558-00-00-102, and governing development standards,
     including an increase in height, density, and signage allowances. Review of
     Impact Assessment and Conceptual Plan to be conducted as well.

     Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

    Mr. Alan McMahon, The Beach Company, reviewed the number of residential
units and the number of bedrooms with the Committee.

   Ms. Whitley suggested that there should be a study conducted to look at the
density and attainable housing for the Town and if it is viable for developers.

    Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council to pursue a consultant to
conduct a study on density as it relates to attainable housing. Ms. Hyatt seconded
the motion. All in favor.

     Ms. Whitley moved to forward the PD amendment to Town Council for
discussion with possible conditions on the proposed amendment to allow the
proposed additional height only after the failure of Belk and Regal Cinemas;
increased height for the upper right building that surrounds the open event space
upon approval of the amendment; and not allow the additional building that will be
placed on the existing parking area on the upper left of the site. Mr. Chapman
seconded the motion. All in favor.

7.   Review outstanding elements of the Zoning Code Draft Subchapters
