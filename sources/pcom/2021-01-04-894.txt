   12/21/20 Rezoning Case R-37-20: Request to rezone from R-3, Medium
   Density Residential District, to TH, Townhouse District, an approximately
   2.90- acre tract of land comprised of eleven (11) parcels and improved
   with 22 duplexes. Also included is a request to incorporate all parcels into
   the Coleman Boulevard Overlay District (CB-OD). Enclave at QC LLC.
   Queens Court (Bayview Acres Subdivision).

   Mr. Chapman moved for deferral as requested by the applicant. Ms. Corley
seconded the motion. All in favor.

c. Zoning Code Text Amendment: Proposal to amend Section 156.325 of
   Chapter 156, Zoning Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances,
   pertaining to the Principal Use Table. Proposed is to add language under
   the Utilities category to permit a lay-down yard, maintenance facility with
   open lot storage of materials, vehicles, and equipment in PI-2, Public
   Institutional-2, and LI, Light Industrial Districts.

   Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

   Ms. Corley moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the text
amendment. Mr. Chapman seconded the motion. All in favor.
