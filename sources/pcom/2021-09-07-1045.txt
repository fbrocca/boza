2. Public Comments

     Mr. Ross Appel, attorney for CKC Properties, LLC, expressed concern with
possible action against their project for a boutique hotel on Shem Creek. He asked
that the Town’s procedures and standards for zoning and amendments be
followed, particularly with the public hearing process. He also suggested that the
new ordinance should include an effective date so that it applies only to new
projects.

    Mr. Chris Colman, 118 Pitt St, spoke regarding item 6A, changes to the Shem
Creek district. He suggested that restaurants should be curtailed. He expressed
concern with parking in the Shem Creek area and that the nonconforming
ordinance would come into play and the new parking requirements would not
apply to existing uses.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley continued with the agenda.

6. Discussion and action as well as a review of Planning Commission
   recommendations regarding the Town’s definition for hotels, parking
   requirements, and their appropriateness in the NC, Neighborhood
   Commercial Zoning District.

   Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

    Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the following
changes:
   1. Definitions:
       ➢ HOTEL (includes MOTEL and EXTENDED STAY). A building or group of
           attached or detached buildings containing more than 25 total GUEST
           ROOMS intended for rental or lease to transients by the day, week or
           month, whether individually or combined in GUEST SUITES. A HOTEL
           shall have at least one full-time employee on site during daytime hours.
           A HOTEL typically provides services and amenities such as daily maid
           service, restaurant, meeting rooms, conference rooms and/or
           recreation facilities;
       ➢ BOUTIQUE HOTEL – A building or group of attached or detached
           buildings containing up to 25 total GUEST ROOMS intended for rental
                                                                  Planning Committee

             or lease to transients by the day, week or month, whether individually
             or combined in GUEST SUITES. A BOUTIQUE HOTEL shall have at least
             one full-time employee on site every day. A BOUTIQUE HOTEL also may
             provide services and amenities such as daily maid service and/or
             recreation facilities;
       ➢ INN – A building or group of attached or detached buildings containing
             up to 10 GUEST ROOMS intended for rental or lease to transients by
             the day, week or month, whether individually or combined in GUEST
             SUITES. An INN shall have at least one full-time employee on site every
             day. An INN also may provide services and amenities such as daily
             maid service and/or recreation facilities;
       ➢ GUEST ROOM – Any room in an INN, HOTEL or BOUTIQUE HOTEL
             containing one or more beds (excluding pull-out beds) provided for
             transient guests;
       ➢ GUEST SUITE - Two or more GUEST ROOMS grouped together within a
             single rentable or leasable unit in any INN, HOTEL or BOUTIQUE HOTEL.
       ➢ EXTENDED STAY HOTEL – Any HOTEL in which some or all of its GUEST
             ROOMS or GUEST SUITES include a stove, range or cooktop. No
             EXTENDED STAY shall be considered an INN or BOUTIQUE HOTEL,
             regardless of the number of GUEST ROOMS or GUEST SUITES;
  2. To amend the Neighborhood Commercial zoning designation to remove
       boutique hotels as a permitted use;
  3. To add Inns as a permitted use in the NC district;
  4. Boutique Hotels are planned to be in Community Commercial as defined
       and implemented at a future time consistent with the Town
       Comprehensive Plan;
  5. Hotels and Extended Stay Hotels shall be in designated areas known as
       Accommodation Zones or other designation consistent with the Town
       Comprehensive Plan;
  6. Parking - OFF-STREET PARKING SPACE REQUIREMENTS FOR LODGING:
       ➢ 1 per GUEST ROOM or 2 per GUEST SUITE
       ➢ up to four GUEST ROOMS in a GUEST SUITE.
       ➢ One additional parking space shall be required for every two GUEST
             ROOMS over four in a GUEST SUITE.
       ➢ Employee Spaces: 1 for INN, 2 for BOUTIQUE HOTEL, and 3 for HOTEL
             and EXTENDED STAY HOTELS.
Ms. Corley seconded the motion.
