PowerPoint Presentation

Present:    G. M. Whitley, Chair, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman, Brenda Corley.
Staff:      Eric DeMoura, Michele Reed, DeVay Dandy, David Pagliarini

1. Call to Order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 11:04 am.

2. Approval of Minutes from the September 7, 2021

     Mr. Chapman moved to approve the minutes. Ms. Corley seconded the motion.
All in favor.

3. Public Comments

   Ms. Abigail Richardson, Seamon Whiteside and Associates, stated that she and
the owner were available to answer any questions regarding the Oyster Park
development.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley continued with the agenda.

4. Old Business
   a. Oyster Park Impact Assessment and Planned Development Ordinance.
      Planning Commission Recommended Amendments. Includes two (2)
      separate requests related the 13.57-acre Mixed-Use Development known
      as Oyster Park, located off Ben Sawyer Boulevard between Rifle Range
      Road and New Parish Way. First is to rezone the entire development from
      AB, Areawide Business District, to PD, Planned Development District.
      Second is to approve an Impact Assessment and associated Conceptual
      Plan.

       Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee. She stated that this
   was deferred from last month. She stated that the plan has been revised and
   reviewed these revisions with the Committee.

      Ms. Corley moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the rezoning
   from AB to PD. Mr. Chapman seconded the motion. All in favor.

        Ms. Corley moved to recommend to Town Council that the number of
   residential units not exceed 269 units as currently exists. Mr. Chapman seconded
   the motion. All in favor.

      Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the impact
   assessment as presented. Ms. Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.

5. Review of Planning Commission recommendations from the September 22,
    2021 meeting
   a. Proposal to amend Chapter 156, Zoning Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code
      of Ordinances, by adding new sections Chapter 156.306, 156.307, 156.308,
      156.304(E)(3), 156.110(F), establishing new Shem Creek Waterfront District
      and design standards. Amendments pertain to provision of permitted uses
      and development standards for this District. Affected code sections include
      156.007, 156.325, 156.326, 156.310, 156.318, 156.314, 156.110, 156.304.

      Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

   Ms. Corley moved to recommend to Town Council approval. Mr. Chapman
   seconded the motion. All in favor.

   b. Proposal to amend Chapter 153.050, Stormwater Management and Water
      Quality Regulations, specifically the Stormwater Facility Maintenance
      Agreement, proposed is to include Clemson Certified Post-Construction
      BMP inspectors as qualified professionals to perform required inspections.

      Ms. Dandy reviewed the request with the Committee.

       Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval with the
   addition of “professional engineer” added to the engineer’s title as referenced in
   the state statutes. Ms. Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.

6. Update on Comprehensive Plan Implementation

   Ms. Reed updated the Committee on the implementation of the
Comprehensive Plan recommendations.

The Committee agreed that there should be regular updates on the progress of the
Comprehensive Plan implementation and the zoning code rewrite.

7. Discussion/Update on paid parking and parking requirements

   Mr. Pagliarini reviewed the request with the Committee.

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval to move forward with updating the parking
requirements and bring this back to the Committee. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

8. Discussion and possible action to remove residential uses as a permitted use
   in the Boulevard and Chuck Dawley Overlay District

     Ms. Whitley stated that Mr. Rambo asked for this discussion. Ms. Reed
reviewed the request with the Committee.

The Committee received this as information.

9. Discussion and possible action regarding accommodations zones in town as
   our Comprehensive Plan recommends

   Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

    Ms. Hyatt moved to send this to the Planning Commission for consideration of
establishing special area plans for accommodation zones throughout the Town.
Ms. Whitley seconded the motion. All in favor.
