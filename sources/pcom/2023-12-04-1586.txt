                 TOWN OF MOUNT PLEASANT, SOUTH CAROLINA
                          PLANNING COMMITTEE
                            DECEMBER 4, 2023
                                MINUTES

                       Municipal Complex, Council Chambers


PowerPoint Presentation

Present:     G.M. Whitley, Chair, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman, Jake Rambo.
Staff:       Eric DeMoura, Michele Canon, Brad Morrison

1.   Call to order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 8:46 am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the November 6, 2023 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

    Mr. Carl Ritchie, 1485 Endicot Way, spoke regarding the recommendation to
rename Fire Station 1 for Chief Tetor. He reviewed the request and showed a
depiction of the plaque to be installed at the Fire Station if approved. He stated
that he would be available for questions.

    Mr. Jason Milner spoke regarding the Market at Oakland request and stated he
would be available for questions. He stated that the impact assessment has been
reviewed by staff.

    Mr. Scott Schmitz, 1193 Sharpestowne Court, spoke regarding the proposal for
a full intersection at Bella Oaks Drive and Porcher Bluff that will impact the Phillips
Community. He suggested that there is an alternative that would serve the
residents more directly.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley closed public comment.

4.   Discussion and recommendation to rename Fire Station 1 in honor of the late
     Fire Chief Fred Tetor

    Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of renaming Fire
Station 1 in honor of Chief Fred Tetor. Mr. Rambo seconded the motion. All in favor.

5.   Review of Planning Commission recommendations from October 18, 2023,
     meeting
     A. Market at Oakland Impact Assessment and Conceptual Plan. Review of
         Impact Assessment and Conceptual Plan for a commercial development
         exceeding 40,000 square feet, proposed on a 6.0-acre parcel within the
         existing Market at Oakland development. Subject parcel is located on
         the corner of South Morgans Point and Porcher’s Bluff, further
         identified by TMS# 600-00-00-055. Planning Commission recommended
         approval. Deferred from Council back to Planning Committee

        Ms. Reed and Mr. Morrison reviewed the request with the Commission.

         Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the
     request. Mr. Rambo seconded the motion. All in favor.

6.   Adjourn

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 8:58 am.
