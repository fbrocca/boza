1.   Call to order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 1:31 pm.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the August 1, 2022 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Corley seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

   Mr. Trey Sedalik, Mr. Gregg Minton, and Mr. Mike Johnson, stated that they are
here for any questions on the Holliday Farms Impact Assessment request.

    Ms. Laurie Bixler, 144 3rd Ave, spoke regarding short-term rentals and suggested
that there are several items she would like to bring to the Committee’s attention
for consideration:
   ➢ Limit each parcel to one permit
   ➢ Change permit type to either owner on-site and owner not on-site, with the
       owner on-site being charged $250 and an owner not on-site being charged
       $500.
   ➢ Consider regulations similar to what Atlanta, GA has implemented where a
       permit is only issued for a homeowner’s primary residence.
   ➢ Form an advisory committee made up of STR operators

    Ms. Michelle Whitbeck, 712 Hibbens Grant Blvd, spoke regarding short-term
rentals. She asked that the Committee consider the following items:
   ➢ Change verbiage regarding 6% property tax to only require documentation
      for up to three years

     ➢ Delinquency of any taxes or fees would be considered a violation. This
       should be clarified as to when a violation would be issued, such as if one

4.    Annexations
      A. Request to annex five parcels located on Rifle Range Road and Willow
         Pond Road, identified by TMS Nos. 578-00-00-057, -060, -061, -318, and
         -319, and depicted on a plat recorded by the Charleston County ROD
         Office in Plat Book CF, Page 99 and Plat Book S, Page 130.

         Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the
      annexation request. Ms. Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.

5.    Review of Planning Commission recommendations from August 24, 2022
      A. R-17-22. 1619 Highway 41. Request to rezone from R-1, Low Density
          Residential District, to CC, Community Conservation District, an
          approximately 1.0-acre tract of land located at 1619 Highway 41,
          identified by TMS No. 583-00-00-109, and depicted on a plat recorded
          by the Charleston County ROD Office in Plat Book AB, Page 043.
          Planning Commission recommended approval

           Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

         Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the request.
      Ms. Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.

 C.     Holliday Farms Impact Assessment/Conceptual Plan. Request to
        approve Impact Assessment and associated Conceptual Plan for the
        properties located at 2233 and 2237 North Highway 17 (Between Home
        Depot and Christ Church Episcopal.) Proposed is to develop an
        approximately 8.48-acre shopping center and gas station. Property is
        further Identified by TMS Nos. 558-00-00-146 and 558-00-00-927. All
        parcels are zoned AB-2, Areawide Business-2 District. Planning
        Commission recommended approval

       Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

     Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the
 impact assessment and conceptual plan as recommended by the Planning
 Commission with the following addition:
   ➢ Similar buffer as required by Christ Church and Home Depot with a
       masonry wall and plantings on the Primus tract side of the wall.
 Ms. Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.

         Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend to Town Council changing the density for
     residential uses in commercial districts to 4 units per acre with an additional 4
     units per acre allowed if those additional units are workforce housing. Ms.
     Corley seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 3 to 1 vote with Ms. Corley,
     Ms. Whitley, and M. Hyatt in favor; Mr. Chapman opposed.

7.    Discussion regarding Workforce Housing Terms of Affordability and possible
      incentives

    Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee. She stated that the
change in the number of years the workforce housing must remain in place would
go to the September Planning Commission as a public hearing.

The Committee received this as information.

8.    Update on Zoning Code Rewrite

     Ms. Reed provided an update on the zoning code rewrite.

The Committee received this as information and agreed that a special Planning
Committee meeting should be held in November to review the draft zoning code.

9.    Comprehensive Plan Implementation - Public Input Matters

     Ms. Reed updated the Committee on the upcoming PIM meetings.

The Committee received this as information.
