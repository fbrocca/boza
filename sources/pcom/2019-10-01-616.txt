2. Public Comments

    Ms. Laura Fox, 1507 Plover Avenue, spoke regarding item 5c, the rezoning of
property for use by the school. She stated that they are in favor of the school using
the additional property for extending the drop off lanes, but expressed concern
with additional traffic. She stated that there should not be any additional vehicular
traffic on Hale Road or an additional curb cut on Hale Road. She asked that if
approved, it be stipulated that there would not be additional traffic on Hale Road
and that there be a buffer maintained along Hale Road as well.

Mr. Eddie Twilly, HOA representative for Back Bay Village, spoke regarding item 5c,
the rezoning of property on Hale Road. He expressed concern with the potential
for additional traffic. He asked that there be no new accesses to Hale Road as this
could be a hazard for pedestrians, bike riders, and children. He also would like it
stipulated that the property would revert back to its previous residential zoning if
not utilized by the school.

Mr. Lee Gastley, Seamon, Whiteside, and Associates, stated that they met with
residents from surrounding community regarding the school rezoning request. He
stated that they have included a golf cart access that would keep the golf carts off
the road. Additionally, they would include additional pedestrian access from Rifle

3. Annexations
   a. A-12-19: 4586 Cape Island Drive. Request to annex an approximately 0.68
      acre tract of land located at 4586 Cape Island Drive, identified by TMS No.
      617-14-00-021 and depicted on a plat as Lot 46, Block C, Phase I recorded
      by Charleston County ROD Office in Plat Book EA, Page 699.

      Mr. Ulma reviewed the request with the Committee.

   The Committee discussed the impact of annexing one property and if there
   should be discussion with the entire neighborhood on annexing into the Town.

      Mr. O’Rourke moved to recommend to Town Council denial of the request.
   Motion failed due to the lack of a second.

   The Committee agreed that there should be further discussion on the Town’s
   annexation policy at their next meeting.

       Mr. O’Rourke moved to recommend to Town Council deferral of the request
   for up to 60 days. Ms. Whitley seconded the motion. All in favor.

      Village Historic District, by modifying the boundaries of the district to do
      the following: i) adding a map to graphically depict the boundaries of the

      Rights for previous approvals; ix) change and clarify the requirements for
