1. Approval of Minutes from the October 5, 2020 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Corley seconded the
motion.

Mr. Chapman moved to amend the motion to include the following changes:
  ➢ Clarification that his opposition to the motion for changes to the
      Comprehensive Plan was specific to the revision pertaining to the number of
      residential units in the districts.
Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.

   Ms. Whitley called for a vote on the amended motion. All in favor.

(Upon review of the October 5, 2020 minutes and recording, it was determined that
the minutes could not be amended to reflect the requested change as there was no
clarification stated at the meeting when the motion was made or when the vote
was taken; however, the clarification is noted as part of the these minutes. )

2. Public Comments

   Ms. Sarah Rogers, 3420 Henrietta Hartford, spoke regarding rezoning of the
church property and asked that the request be approved

    Ms. Gerry Lepsen, 2061 Arundel Place, spoke regarding the cell tower on LPR
and stated that the tower is greatly needed to improve service in the area. She
stated that the tower would be located in an inconspicuous area.

   Mr. Peter Wertimer, 2072 Rose Hill Lane, spoke regarding the request for cell
tower. He suggested that the tower is needed to improve reception in the area.
He asked that the request be approved.

    Mr. Matt Everson, 324 Wild Horse Lane, spoke regarding the request for a cell
tower. He stated that the service is consistently poor. He distributed a copy of a
petition with approximately 25 signatures of neighbors in the area. He also
distributed a copy of a letter from Mr. Nick Collins in favor of the request.

    Ms. Alicia Mendicino, 2072 Rose Hill Lane, spoke regarding the request for cell
tower at LPR. She stated that the tower is needed in the area to provide better
service. She stated that the design will be aesthetically pleasing and will not be a
detriment to the neighborhood. She stated that she is in favor of the request.

    Mr. Jonathan Yates, representative for the applicant, stated that he is available
to answer any questions regarding cell tower.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley continued with the agenda.
