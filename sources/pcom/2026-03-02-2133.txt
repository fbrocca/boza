3.   Public Comments

    Ms. Denise Fugo, Housing for All Mount Pleasant, thanked the Committee for
rehearing the request to amend the Dunes West PD for Center Park. She stated
that the developer has agreed to require 10% of the units as affordable housing.

    Ms. Jenny Palmer, Seamon, Whiteside, and Associates, provided information
on the request to amend the Dunes West PD regarding additional uses for the
Center Park property such as a daycare. She stated that in researching this
possibility, it should be noted that a daycare would generate more trips than the
proposed residential. She stated that they have agreed to require that 10% of the
units be affordable housing. She stated that they are also working with Publix to
have a connection to their site.

    Ms. Debra Hulett, 1 Darlington Lane, stated that she is in favor of the
amendment to the Dunes West PD for the Center Park property. She stated that
the housing would be used for their church staff as well as having the 10%
affordable housing. She asked the Committee to consider approval of the request.

4.    Continued discussion of an Ordinance to amend the Dunes West PD, Planned
      Development District Ordinance by incorporating a 7.05-acre parcel into the
      Zoning Map, as depicted on a plat recorded by the Charleston County ROD
      Office in Plat Book L15, Page 0096, requesting to be designated Multi-Family,
      proposing 43 Multi-Plex units, within the development commonly known as
      Park West. (Ord. No. 26018). Planning Commission made no
      recommendation; Planning Committee recommended approval

     Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

    The Committee discussed inclusion of the required affordable housing, whether
the development should be part of the HOA, the addition of a bike/pedestrian path
at the rear of the property, access to the Publix property, and a larger buffer.

    Ms. Whitaker moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the request
with the condition that the property joins the Dunes West HOA and that five units
are designated for affordable housing. Ms. Crosby seconded the motion.

    Mr. Tinkey moved to amend the motion to also include a pedestrian/bike access
to All-American Boulevard. Ms. Whitaker seconded the motion. All in favor.

     Ms. Hyatt called for a vote on the motion as amended. All in favor.
