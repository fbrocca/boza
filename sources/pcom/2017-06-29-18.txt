Mr. DeMoura agreed that it should be a resident-driven forum. He stated that
there have been occasions where there was not a large participation of residents
during the review process because of utilizing stakeholders. He stated that in the
past, this has resulted in having input that more heavily favored businesses, utilities
or other interests. He suggested that having residents more involved with the
