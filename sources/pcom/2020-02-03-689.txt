2.   Public Comments

     Ms. Lucy Gordon, 1551 Ben Sawyer, spoke regarding non-conforming
properties. She suggested that the ordinance states that the property must come
into compliance if building is more than 50%. She stated that surrounding
municipalities exempt height, building structures, etc. She suggested that the
ordinance should be changed.

Mr. David Quick, 1502 Village Square, suggested the amount of tree canopy loss
should be reviewed and more greener ways to develop should be considered. He
suggested that more staff for the Planning Department should be considered as
well as taking better advantage of the Tree Bank Fund to provide more trees within
the Town. He also suggested that a Resilience and Sustainability Committee should
be considered to review these types of issues. He suggested that there should be
more focus on these issues.

Mr. Eddie Ferra, 950 Key Colony Court, spoke regarding the rezonings of Key Colony
Court. He stated that this is proposed to allow the homes to be rebuilt to the
current standards in the event of damage due to a storm event. He suggested that
the homeowner should be allowed to rebuild and the request should be approved.

Mr. Moe Jamali, 976 Key Colony Court, spoke regarding the rezoning of Key Colony
Court and expressed concern with not being able to rebuild in the event of damage
to a storm event. He suggested that the homes should be allowed to rebuild. He
stated that this is an issue that affects a number of residents and should be
approved.

Ms. Pat Sullivan, 1002 Plantation Court, spoke regarding the issue of preserving
scenic highways. She expressed concern with tractor-trailers and buses allowed to
drive down scenic highways as it can damage limbs on the trees.

Mr. David Basto, Sullivans Point, spoke regarding the rezonings of Key Colony Court
and suggested that this should be approved so that they can rebuild their homes if
damaged during a storm event. He stated that residents have made an investment
in their homes and should be able to rebuild.

Mr. Roy Olson, Sullivans Point, spoke regarding rezoning of Key Colony Court, and
suggested that the rezoning should be approved so that they can be rebuilt if
damaged during a storm event.

     d. Proposal to amend various provisions in sections 156.318 (Boulevard
        Overlay District) and 156.320 (Waterfront Gateway District) of Chapter
        156, Zoning Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances pertaining
        to the reduction of existing building height limits in portions of the
        Boulevard Overlay District and the Waterfront Gateway District. Affected
        areas are (1) near the base of the Ravenel Bridge (in the vicinity of
        Johnnie Dodds Boulevard, Wingo Way, Magrath Darby Boulevard, Mathis
        Ferry Road and Houston Northcutt Boulevard) and (2) near the US
        Highway 17/I-526 interchange (Johnnie Dodds Boulevard, Bowman Road,
        and Stuart Engals Boulevard). Proposed approximate new building
        heights include the following:
            80’ height for a portion of the “Medical Zone” at East Cooper Medical
            Center;
            65’ for portions of Hospitality and Medical Zones;
            55’ for McGrath Darby/Houston Northcutt area;
            45’ for Bowman/Stuart Engals area;
            40’ near Chuck Dawley and Bowman Road; and
            55’ for remainder of Johnnie Dodds Boulevard corridor.
        Also includes a request to amend the official Building Height Plan Map to
        correspond with the proposed height reductions.

        Ms. Canon reviewed the request with the Committee (attachment 1).

         Ms. Corley moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the request.
     Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 3 to 1 vote with Ms.
     Corley, Ms. Whitley and Ms. Hyatt in favor; Mr. Chapman opposed.
