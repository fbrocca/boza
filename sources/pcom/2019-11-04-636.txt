1. Approval of Minutes from the October 1, 2019 meeting

    Ms. Whitley moved for approval. Mr. O’Rourke seconded the motion. All in
favor.

2. Public Comments

    Ms. Pam Ireland, 820 Fiddler’s Point Lane, spoke regarding building heights.
She distributed information and a map to the Committee for review. She stated
that there is property in the Mathis Ferry Road/Wingo Way area that is for sale and
suggested that the building height should be reviewed in this area prior to this
property being sold. She suggested that the building heights should be lowered to
55 feet.

Ms. Lucy Gordon, 1551 Ben Sawyer, spoke regarding building heights. She
suggested that the building heights should be lowered to 45 and 55 feet, including
the areas in the overlay district.

3. Employee years of service recognition

   Mr. Ulma recognized Jane Yager-Baumrind for five years of service and Eddie
Bernard for 20 years of service.

9. Continued discussion of building heights in the Johnnie Dodds and Chuck

   Mr. Ulma reviewed the request with the Committee.
