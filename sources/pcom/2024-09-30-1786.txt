All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

    Ms. Lisa Schniptke, 185 Mossy Oak Way, spoke regarding the proposed housing
at Towne Centre. She suggested that having housing at Towne Centre should be
considered as it would be central to all amenities. She suggested that there should
be more affordable housing near amenities to allow for a more walkable
community.

    Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning, spoke regarding the Towne Centre project. He
suggested that there should be a mixed-use development that envisions the
vibrant, sustainable development with multi-family residences outlined in the
Comprehensive Plan. He suggested that the current plan should be denied to allow
the developer to rethink the proposal and come back with a better plan. He
suggested that there should be an equal amount of affordable housing units and
market rate housing units. He suggested that the concessions should benefit the
Town as much as it does the developer. He suggested that more green space
should be considered.

4.   R-06-24. Towne Centre. Request to amend the Wilson Tract Planned
     Development District ordinance (Ord. No. 95041) as it pertains to an
     approximately 41.74-acre portion of an approximately 45.83-acre tract of
     land known as Towne Centre, located between Hungryneck Boulevard and
     U.S Highway 17 North and identified by TMS Nos. 558-00-00-096, 558-00-00-
     098, 558-00-00-100, 558-00-00-102, 558-00-00-340, 558-00-00-387, and 558-
     00-00-399. Proposed Amendments specifically pertain to expansion of
     master plan onto 558-00-00-102, and governing development standards,
     including an increase in height, density, and signage allowances. Review of
     Impact Assessment and Conceptual Plan to be conducted as well.

     Ms. Reed and Mr. Stone reviewed the request with the Committee.

    Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council denial of the request and to
recommend approval of a waiver to the one-year waiting period to allow the
developer to restudy the project. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.
