2.Public Comments

  Ms. Pat Sullivan, 1002 Plantation Court, spoke regarding item 5E and asked
that this request be denied. She suggested that it should remain as currently
zoned and recommended in Comprehensive Plan.

Ms. LuAnn Rosenzweig, 365 Sugar Cane Way, expressed concern about traffic
and pedestrian safety. She also stated that there is a need in the Town for
affordable housing and suggested that there should be more walkable, mixed-
use hubs. She suggested that the Comprehensive Plan should be supported
and approved.

Ms. Jessica Wolsten, 126 IOnsborough, spoke regarding pedestrian access and
suggested that the Town should be more walkable. She expressed concern
with personal safety and the importance of community. She asked that the
Committee consider having a more walkable community.

Mr. Pat Gillespie, 2182 Annie Laura Lane, spoke regarding item 5E. He
suggested that the applicant is looking to remain in Mount Pleasant while
providing a home for themselves and their daughter. He stated that he and his
neighbors support the request and he asked that it be approved. He has a
signed petition of the surrounding neighbors.

Ms. Julie Hussey, 329 North Shelmore, suggested that there is a need for
affordable housing there is near work areas. She suggested that the Town
should be a mixed-use community that is more walkable. She expressed
concern with tax revenue and suggested that there should be more hubs and
mixed-use buildings. She suggested that there should be more hubs and the
hubs should be planned to better support the community. She suggested that
having hubs is a good planning technique to provide the necessary mixed-use
and planning for the Towns future investment.

Mr. Rodley Millet, 1296 Wisteria Wall Drive, suggested that a different
approach should be taken in planning for the future of the Town. He suggested
that the Comprehensive Plan and the creation of hubs as noted within the
Comprehensive Plan are an important aspect and a good solution to providing
more affordable housing through mixed-uses and providing better financial
diversity for the Town. He asked that careful consideration should be taken
when discussing these hubs and how the Town should be developed in the
future.

Ms. Wendy Bauerschmidt, 1320 Woodlake Court, spoke regarding hubs and
suggested that they are important in supporting the character of the Town and
meeting the needs of the community while reducing traffic and improving
stormwater management and open space. She suggested that the hubs would
also provide for more affordable housing for seniors, young families, and the
workforce needed to sustain the businesses within the Town. She stated that
the Comprehensive Plan calls for the development of special area plans for the
different hubs. She asked that the positive elements of the hubs are in
alignment with the goals of the Town in providing open space, affordable
housing, and reducing traffic.
