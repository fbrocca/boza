                TOWN OF MOUNT PLEASANT, SOUTH CAROLINA
                  PLANNING & DEVELOPMENT COMMITTEE
                           SEPTEMBER 5, 2017
                               MINUTES

                 Municipal Complex, Committee Meeting Room


Present:    Bob Brimmer, Chair, Mark Smith, Joe Bustos, Elton Carrier.
Staff:      Eric DeMoura, Christiane Farrell, Michele Canon, Liz Boyles
Also:       Jack Little

      Mr. Brimmer called the meeting to order at 2:03 pm.

1. Approval of Minutes from the July 31, 2017 meeting

Mr. Carrier moved for approval of the minutes. Mr. Smith seconded the motion. All
in favor.

2. Public Comments

There being no comments, Mr. Brimmer continued with the agenda.

3. Review of Planning Commission recommendations from the August 23, 2017
   meeting
   a. Request to rezone from R-2, Low Density Residential District, to R-3,
      Medium Density Residential District, an approximately 0.17 acre tract of
      land located at 425 Bank Street, identified by TMS No. 532-05-00-128, and
      depicted as Lot 3 on a plat recorded by the Charleston County RMC Office
      in Plat Book E, Page 168. Property to remain in the SR2-OD, Special R-2
      Overlay District.

   Ms. Canon reviewed the request with the Committee (attachment 1). She
   stated that the Planning Commission recommended denial.

   Mr. Carrier expressed concern with this request and having non-conforming
   structures. He stated that this is a recovering neighborhood and expressed
   concern with duplexes continuing to be allowed.

   Mr. Carrier moved to recommend to Town Council denial of the request. Mr.
   Bustos seconded the motion. All in favor.

b. Request to annex an approximately 0.13 acre tract of land located at 1378
   Old Georgetown Road, identified by TMS No. 559-13-00-030, and depicted
   on a plat recorded by the Charleston County RMC Office in Plat Book P,
   Page 033

Ms. Canon reviewed the request with the Committee (attachment 1).

Mr. Smith moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the annexation
request. Mr. Carrier seconded the motion. All in favor.

c. Request to zone AB, Areawide Business District, and UC-OD, Urban Corridor
   Overlay District, an approximately 0.13 acre tract of land located at 1378
   Old Georgetown Road, identified by TMS No. 559-13-00-030, and depicted
   on a plat recorded by the Charleston County RMC Office in Plat Book P,
   Page 033.

Ms. Canon reviewed the request with the Committee (attachment 1).

Mr. Carrier asked about connectivity and if access would be from the frontage
road along Johnnie Dodds Boulevard. Ms. Canon answered in the affirmative.
Mr. Kyle Hoyt stated that the roadway was re-configured to a cul-de-sac with
the only access being to the frontage road to Chuck Dawley Boulevard.

Mr. Bustos asked what the county zoning is. Ms. Canon answered that it is
zoned commercial.

Mr. Hoyt reviewed the request with the Committee.

Mr. Smith asked what would be the proposed uses. Mr. Hoyt answered that it
would be similar uses as with the existing shopping center.

Mr. Carrier asked about the corner property. Mr. Hoyt answered that this parcel
would be redeveloped as part of the entire project.

Mr. Smith answered that his business was previously located on one of the
properties in the area and expressed appreciation with the area being
redeveloped.

Mr. Smith moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the zoning request.
Mr. Carrier seconded the motion. All in favor.

d. Request to rezone from NC, Neighborhood Commercial District, and OP,
   Office Professional District, to AB, Areawide Business District, and UC-OD,
   Urban Corridor Overlay District, an approximately 2.66 acre tract of land
   comprised of four parcels and described as follows: (i) an approximately
   0.38 acre parcel located at 1374 Old Georgetown Road, identified by TMS
   No. 559-13-00-031, and depicted on a plat recorded by the Charleston
   County RMC Office in Plat Book P, Page 033; (ii) an approximately 1.29 acre
   parcel located at 1356 Old Georgetown Road, identified by TMS No. 559-
   13-00-032, and depicted on a plat recorded by the Charleston County RMC
   Office in Plat Book V, Page 055; (iii) an approximately 0.67 acre parcel
   located at 1344 Old Georgetown Road, identified by TMS No. 559-13-00-
   033, and depicted as Lot 7B on a plat recorded by the Charleston County
   RMC Office in Plat Book E66, Page 562; and (iv) an approximately 0.32 acre
   parcel located at Old Georgetown Road, identified by TMS No. 559-13-00-
   050, and depicted as Lot 7A on a plat recorded by the Charleston County
   RMC Office in Plat Book EL, Page 126.

Ms. Canon reviewed the request with the Committee (attachment 1).

Mr. Smith moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the rezoning
request. Mr. Bustos seconded the motion. All in favor.

e. Proposal to amend Zoning Code Section 156.007 of Chapter 156 of the
   Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances adding a definition for “Story”.

Ms. Farrell reviewed the request with the Committee (attachment 1). She
stated that the Planning Commission recommended approval.

Mr. Smith asked if this was reviewed by legal staff. Mr. DeMoura answered in
the affirmative.

Mr. Smith moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the text
amendment. Mr. Bustos seconded the motion. All in favor.

6. Staff Update on the Affordable Housing Committee

Ms. Farrell updated the Committee on the progress of the Affordable Housing
Committee. She stated that the first meeting is scheduled for September 12th.

Mr. Jack Little introduced himself to the Committee. He thanked the Town for the
opportunity to work on this important issue.

Mr. Smith thanked Mr. Little for accepting this task. He stated that he looks
forward to updates on the work being accomplished to provide more workforce
housing within the Town.

Mr. Kevin Cunnane, 3032 River Vista Way, suggested that wages for the Town
employees should be reviewed as part of the update to the Comprehensive Plan.
He suggested that financial aspects such as salaries should be reviewed in
conjunction with finding opportunities to provide more affordable housing in the
Town.

Mr. Brimmer stated that there is a clear work plan that has been defined by Town
Council on this issue. He stated that he looks forward to hearing more from Mr.
Little and the Affordable Housing Committee.

7. Staff update on the 2018 Comprehensive Plan process

Ms. Boyles updated the Committee on the progress made to the Comprehensive
Plan process. She stated that there have been several meetings with the
consultants to ensure there is a clear understanding of what needs to be
accomplished. She stated that the Comprehensive Plan forum has been selected
and is scheduled to meet on September 19th. She stated that there is a separate
page on the website to help keep the public up to date on work being accomplished
and to provide input during the process.

Mr. Smith stated that appreciation for the work staff has done on this issue was
noted in a previous Committee meeting. He stated that he wanted to make sure
that staff was aware of the appreciation previously noted and commended staff as
well for their work.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 2:29 pm.
