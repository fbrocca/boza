   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Corley seconded the
motion.

minutes:
   ➢ Page 3, vote on motion to deny zoning request for 1137 Louise Terrace—vote

4.    Review of Planning Commission recommendations from the February 16,
      2022, Meeting
      A. A-5-22 1137 Louise Terrace. Request to Annex an approximately 0.58-
          acre tract of land located at 1137 Louise Terrace. Property is identified
          by TMS Nos. 559-14-00-013.

         Ms. Corley moved to recommended to Town Council approval of the
      annexation. Mr. Chapman seconded the motion. All in favor.

      B.   R-04-22. Request to Zone TH, Townhouse District, 1137 Louise Terrace.
           Property is currently zoned R-4, Low Density Residential District, in the
           County. Planning Commission recommended denial

           Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

          Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend to Town Council denial of the zoning
      request. Ms. Corley seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 3 to 1 vote with
      Ms. Corley, Ms. Whitley, and Ms. Hyatt in favor; Mr. Chapman opposed.

      C.   R-05-22. Request to amend Coaxum Tract PD, Planned Development
           District Ordinance. Proposed is to update development guidelines as
           they relate to homes abutting open space in order to remain consistent
           with previously approved Conceptual Plan. Development consists of
           20.45 acres and identified by TMS nos. 578-00-00-128, 129, 167, 178,
           217. Planning Commission recommended approval

           Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

         Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the PD
      amendment. Mr. Chapman seconded the motion. All in favor.
