3.   Public Comments

    Mr. Eugene White, Assistant Manager of the Park West Master Association,
spoke regarding item 7, the request to amend the Dunes West Planned
Development (PD). He expressed concern with the project and asked that the
request be conditioned to include that the property is included in the Dunes West
HOA and subject to the master association governing documents, covenants, and
restrictions. He suggested that this will ensure that the architectural integrity is
maintained and protect the long-term stability and maintenance of the community.

    Mr. Aaron Schmidt, Seamon Whiteside, spoke regarding item 7 as well. He
stated that increased buffers were suggested, but this would significantly impact
the nearby wetlands and preservation of many historic trees. He suggested that
supplementing and enhancing the buffers in areas could be considered. He also
noted that there is a stormwater easement between the development property
and Life Park Church in addition to the buffer.

     Mr. Turner Rose, Center Park developer (item 7), corrected some
misinformation regarding their negotiations with Life Park Church. He stated that
