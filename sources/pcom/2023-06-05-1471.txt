     Request to Rezone five parcels, addressed as 1329, 1287, 1303 and 1307
     Porcher’s Bluff Road, all under the ownership of St. Peter’s Church, from
     R-1, Low Density Residential District to CC, Community Conservation
     District. Parcels are located off Porcher’s Bluff and indicated by TMS
     Nos. 578-00-00-198, 578-00-00-181, 578-00-00-182, 578-00-00-184, 578-
