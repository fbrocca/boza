       Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 12:42 pm.

1.    Approval of Minutes from the January 4, 2021 meeting

     Mr. Chapman moved for approval. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.

2.    Public Comments

   Mr. John Wright, 145 Rose Lane, spoke regarding 5a and 5c. He asked that the
community’s desires be upheld.

     Ms. Sandy Watts stated that she is available to any questions.

   Mr. Nathaniel Hutchinson, owner of 1065 Theodore Road, asked that the
annexation and zoning be approved.

     Mr. George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Rd, suggested that the SB-OD
requirements be upheld for the Watts property and that the request be denied as
it would not meet the 12,500 sf lot requirement. He also suggested that this would
set a precedent within the community for future rezonings and smaller lot sizes
that could affect density for the community.

    Mr. Chris Donato, Engineer for Watts family, stated that this is a unique
development. He stated that they would develop the property with 32 lots and
almost 30% open space. He stated that impact fees would also be paid. He stated
that they would preserve the historic trees on the site. He stated that they would

3.   Old Business
     a. Rezoning Case R-37-20: Request to rezone from R-3, Medium Density
        Residential District, to TH, Townhouse District, an approximately 2.90-
        acre tract of land comprised of eleven (11) parcels and improved with 22
        duplexes. Also included is a request to incorporate all parcels into the
        Coleman Boulevard Overlay District (CB-OD). Enclave at QC LLC. Queens
        Court (Bayview Acres Subdivision).

        Ms. Reed stated that the applicant has asked for deferral to the March
     meeting.

        Mr. Chapman moved for deferral of this item as requested by the applicant.
     Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.

     b. Zoning Code Text Amendment: Proposal to amend Sections 156.140 -
        156.142 of Chapter 156, Zoning Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code of
        Ordinances, pertaining to Nonconforming Lots, Structures and Uses.
        Proposed is to amend the entire ordinance to differentiate treatment
        based on damage or destruction due to neglect, natural and/or man-
        made hazards, and to make special distinctions between utilization of
        property and the physical characteristics.

         Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

         Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the
     request. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.
