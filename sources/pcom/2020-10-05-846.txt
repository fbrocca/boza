      Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 3:56 pm.

1.   Approval of Minutes from the August 31, 2020 meeting

     Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Corley seconded the motion.
All in favor.

2.   Public Comments

   Mr. George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Rd, spoke regarding the rezoning of property
on Six Mile Road. He stated that he is against the request and asked that the Six Mile
Community and overlay district be maintained.

    Mr. George Bixler, 144 4th Ave, spoke regarding short-term rentals. He
encouraged the Committee to continue with the ordinance as originally approved. He
suggested that there is no need for any amendments.

     Ms. Cindy Watts spoke regarding 1240 Six Mile Road and stated that this request
is to help provide affordable housing and additional income for the family. She stated
that they have provided sufficient notice to the residents that the mobile home park
would be closing in Feb 2021. She asked that the request be approved.

    Mr. John Betch spoke in favor of short-term rentals. He stated that they are
downsizing and would like to have the ability to utilize short-term rentals as a source
of additional income. He stated that they have had success with short-term rentals,
with the exception of disputes with a neighbor. He suggested that the issue of
violations should be reviewed so that the owners and renters are not harassed by
neighbors.

    Mr. Chris Donato, representative of Ms. Watts, spoke regarding the rezoning on
Six Mile Road. He suggested that this is a redevelopment project. He stated that
approving the annexation and rezoning would allow the property to be redeveloped
with 30% open space throughout the development. He stated that the lots would be
smaller, but it would be a smarter development. He stated that this would also allow
for conversion from septic tanks.
