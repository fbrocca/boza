Present:     G.M. Whitley, Chair, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman.

1.   Call to order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 9:00 am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the September 5, 2023 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

    Mr. George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Rd, spoke regarding the Liberty Hill
Development Agreement extension. He stated that the original plans included a
farm and asked if this had been removed from the development as this was an
integral part of the development.

    Mr. Daniel Garvey, Mount Pleasant Waterworks, spoke regarding the rezoning
at Charleston National. He stated that he is available for questions.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley closed public comment and
continued with the agenda.

4.   Continued discussion on Liberty Hill Farms Development Agreement (DA)
     Amendment. Request to amend the current Development Agreement for
     Liberty Hill Farms to extend the DA for five years and to amend the
     development schedule.

     Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

    Mr. Steven Baker, developer, spoke regarding the community open space that

     2023, meeting

6.   Adjourn.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 9:25 am.
