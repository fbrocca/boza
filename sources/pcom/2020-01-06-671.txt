2. Public Comments

   Mr. George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Road, expressed concern with item 3A and
3B. He stated that the Sweetgrass Basket Overlay District (SB-OD) was established
to have coordination with Charleston County to help preserve the area. He
suggested that the SB-OD should be maintained.

    Mr. Robert Timmerman, 2142 Gulf Drive, spoke regarding item 3A and 3B. He
stated that the older community has not been able to be maintained and has been
blighted for a number of years. He stated that over the past few years, the area
has been redeveloped and improved. He stated that there is now a connection to
Gulf Drive, sidewalks, fire hydrants, etc. He expressed concern with what the SB-
OD is preserving now and that it should be countered with what is being proposed.
He suggested that the request would be an improvement to the area and should
be approved.

    Mr. Gary Santos, 1690 Grey Stone Blvd, stated that he voted for
implementation of the SB-OD, but the area has been redeveloped contrary to the
SB-OD. He suggested that the integrity of the SB-OD should be maintained.

c. Rezoning Case: R-29-19, Request to amend the Gregorie Ferry Landing PD-

d. Rezoning Case: R-30-19, Request to rezone two parcels from R-1, Low

Ms. Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.

e. Zoning Code Text Amendment: Proposal to amend Section 156.201(I)(2)(c),
   of Chapter 156, Zoning Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances
   pertaining to the vision corridor provision of the Critical Line Buffer.
   Proposed changes include language clarifying that the vision corridor
   provision applies to property utilized for both residential and commercial
   uses; clearing can potentially occur for the benefit of an adjoining property
   owner, given the property owner provides consent; the 33 percent clearing
   requirement can potentially be incremental if consistent with defined
   guidelines; and how the Critical Line Buffer and Vision Corridor is
   measured.

   Ms. Canon reviewed the request with the Committee.
