2.   Public Comments

    Ms. Pam Ireland, 820 Coopers Pointe Lane, spoke regarding the zoning code
rewrite in response to the Comprehensive Plan and stated that when major
changes are needed, they can be fast-tracked. She stated that this is the case for
the Shem Creek area and boutique hotels. She suggested that boutique hotels
should not be allowed in this district and is in conflict with the Comprehensive Plan.
She also suggested that the parking requirements for a boutique hotel should be
addressed as well. She suggested that these changes should be made as soon as
possible.

    Ms. Lucy Gordon, 1551 Ben Sawyer, agreed with Ms. Ireland’s comments. She
suggested that the term guest room should be better clarified. She also suggested
that the parking requirements should be determined according to the number of
bedrooms as opposed to each room or unit. She stated that often the room or unit
can have multiple bedrooms along with a living room, kitchen, and laundry rooms.
She suggested that clarifying what a room or unit entails should be considered so
