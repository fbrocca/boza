1.   Call to order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 8:45 am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the September 30, 2024 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

   Ms. Michele Strauss, 545 Veranda View, spoke regarding tree protection. She
suggested that better tree protection and penalties for illegal removal of a tree
should be considered. She suggested that more trees should be planted if a tree is
removed. She also suggested that stricter requirements to allow tree removal
should be considered.

    Ms. Lisa Schniptke, 185 Mossy Oak Way, spoke regarding the draft zoning code,
specifically regarding murals. She suggested that murals should be allowed to be
placed to be seen from main streets. She also suggested that stricter tree protection
ordinances should be considered. She read a statement from Diedre Kennedy
regarding having stronger tree protection ordinances and to establish a tree task
force to review tree removals.

    Mr. David Quick, 1502 Village Square, spoke regarding tree protection and
suggested that more study is needed. He stated that he agrees that a tree task force
should be established and data regarding the number of trees within the town
determined. He suggested that there is a significant number of illegal tree removals

    Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning, spoke regarding tree protection and suggested
