b. R-20-2020. 1242 White Flat Road. (Applicant Request to Withdraw
   One (1) Parcel (1236 White Flat Road) from the Request) Request to
   rezone two parcels approximately 0.47 acres located at the
   intersection of Bowman Road and White Flat Road. One parcel is
   zoned R-1, Low Density Residential District, and is improved with
   an office that was converted from a single family residence. The
   other parcel is zoned PD, Planned Development District, and is
   vacant except for an off-site sign for the Watermark Development.
   The applicant wishes to rezone both parcels to OP, Office
   Professional District.

   Ms. Canon reviewed the request with the Committee.

  Mr. Chapman asked if the buffers meet Office Professional without taking
away the porches.

Ms. Cannon stated the buffers are based on the use and not the zoning, and
so they would look at this use against an adjacent use, and without the
porches it does not meet the buffer. If the porches were removed, it would
meet the buffer. Those are the two encroachments at this point.

Committee discussion.

Mr. Chapman asked if this were to be approved, can it be approved with
stipulations about the porches and about the parking being permeable
surface.

Ms. Cannon replied in the negative.

Mr. Chapman stated that this is a better idea than we had before, and that
the building improves the area, but he is concerned about getting closer to
compliance by the owner. Mr. Chapman asked if the owner was present,
and he asked Corporation Counsel if that was something they could ask the
owner if they would be willing to do.

Mr. Pagliarini stated it can be requested but it cannot be legally conditioned.

Mr. Chapman stated that he is requesting it.

Mr. Trevor Cangelosi stated he is owner of the property along with his
partner Jeff Ross, and he provided some background information and
comments. Mr. Cangelosi stated that they are more than willing to comply
with whatever the Town needs in terms of bringing this property into
compliance with whatever is needed in terms of any variations that are
needed, the porches. He said that they have been in communication with
Corporation Counsel and with the Deputy Director of Planning about
whatever they need them to do, and they are sorry about the
miscommunication about this because this was absolutely never their
intention.

Mr. Chapman asked about permeable surface on the parking lot, not gravel.

Mr. Cangelosi stated that the entire parking lot that they own now is on the
corner and it is entirely grass. He said they are happy to do whatever the
Town wants in terms of making that a proper parking lot.

Mr. Chapman reiterated that they cannot require them to do so; he is asking
about some type of a nice permeable surface other than gravel.

Mr. Cangelosi stated that he is happy to.

Ms. Whitley reiterated that this is not something that we can legally require
of the property owner.

Mr. Chapman stated that he understands that.

     Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the
request. Ms. Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.
