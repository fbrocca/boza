                TOWN OF MOUNT PLEASANT, SOUTH CAROLINA
               SPECIAL PLANNING & DEVELOPMENT COMMITTEE
                             OCTOBER 19, 2021
                                MINUTES

                 Municipal Complex, Committee Meeting Room


PowerPoint Presentation

Present:    Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman, Brenda Corley.
Absent:     Guang Ming Whitley, Chair.
Staff:      Eric DeMoura, Michele Reed, David Pagliarini, Michael Robertson

      Mr. Chapman called the meeting to order at 3:00 pm.

1. Public Comments

   Mr. Charlie Carmody, 107 New Street, representative for Simmons, Herring, and
Armstrong families that own property on Ben Sawyer and Center Streets. He stated
that they are approved for development of a hotel and would like to proceed with
that project. He stated that his understanding is that possible changes to allowing
hotels is being discussed.

    Mr. Michael Bennett stated that he is the developer of the proposed hotel that
Mr. Carmody mentioned. He stated that he would like to be able to continue with
the project on the property.

   There being no further comments, Mr. Chapman continued with the agenda.

2. Discussion and possible action regarding repealing of the Boulevard Overlay
   District

   Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

   The Committee discussed possible negative consequences to repealing the
Boulevard Overlay District.

   Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend to Town Council approval of repealing the
Boulevard Overlay District. Ms. Corley seconded the motion.

   Ms. Hyatt withdrew her motion. Ms. Corley withdrew her second.

The Commission agreed that this should move forward to the Planning Commission
for a public hearing.

3. Discussion and possible action regarding proposed ordinance amendments
   that effectuate the Accommodation Zones/uses as outlined in the 2019
   Comprehensive Plan

   Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

    The Committee discussed possible amendments regarding accommodation
uses.

The Committee agreed that this should be included on the November Committee
agenda for further discussion.


There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:44 pm.
