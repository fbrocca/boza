1.   Approval of Minutes from the March 2, 2020 meeting

    Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Corley seconded
the motion. All in favor.

2.   Public Comments

    Mr. Freddy Jenkins, 435 Hattie Street, expressed concern with the
proposed cell tower on Longpoint road. He expressed concern that he was
not notified of the request and is opposed to the request. He suggested that
there is already concern with the transmission line already there and
suggested that the addition of a cell tower in that area should not be allowed.

   Mr. Gary Santos, 1690 Greystone, expressed concern with the cell tower
proposed to be located at the recreation center. He expressed similar
concern as Mr. Jenkins. He suggested that a more industrial location should
be considered.

c.   Zoning Ordinance Text Amendment: Proposal to amend
     Sections 156.106, Setbacks and Yard Requirements, and
     156.201, Bufferyards, of Chapter 156, Zoning Code, of the
     Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances, pertaining to lot line
     adjustments of existing lots with a designated critical line.
     Proposed amendments would include an exemption for the
     establishment of or alteration to the critical line buffer and
     impervious surface setback from the critical line provided no
     additional buildable lot is created as a result of any lot line
     adjustment.

     Mr. Chapman moved for approval. Ms. Corley seconded the motion.
All in favor.
