                 TOWN OF MOUNT PLEASANT, SOUTH CAROLINA
                          PLANNING COMMITTEE
                              JULY 31, 2023
                                MINUTES

                        Mount Pleasant Municipal Complex

PowerPoint Presentation

Present:    G.M. Whitley, Chair, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman, Brenda Corley.
Staff:      Eric DeMoura, Michele Reed

     Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 8:30 am.

1.   Approval of Minutes from the June 5, 2023 meeting

     Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Corley seconded the motion.
All in favor.

2.   Public Comments

    Mr. Stuart Whiteside, Seamon, Whiteside, and Associates, spoke regarding the
Liberty Hill Farms development agreement amendment. He stated that due to COVID
and supply chain issues, they have not been able to complete the project. He stated
that the development includes dedicated open space, sidewalks and extensive trail
system, roundabout and other roadway improvements, traffic signal improvements,
and a contribution for future extension of Longpoint Road (Vaughn Ed Kee Parkway).

    Mr. Mike Tinkey. 208 Venning, spoke regarding the proposed changes to the
Historic District Preservation Commission membership requirements. He asked that
more candidates living in the Historic District is preferred and should have priority.
He suggested that an open house or orientation to provide information on the
requirements of being a member on the Commission should be considered as well.

Ms. Whitley closed public comment.

3.   A-06-23. Request to annex a parcel located at 2129 Gulf Drive, identified by
     TMS No. 577-05-00-020 and depicted on a plat recorded by the Charleston
     County ROD Office in Plat Book L, Page 73.

   Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the annexation
request. Ms. Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.

4.   Liberty Hill Farms Development Agreement Amendment. Request to amend
     the current Development Agreement for Liberty Hill Farms to extend the DA for
     five years, and to amend the development schedule due to unexpected delays.

     Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

    Ms. Corley moved to recommend to Town Council to negotiate with the developer
for any possible additional infrastructure improvements that might be necessary. Ms.
Hyatt seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 3 to 1 vote with Ms. Corley, Ms.
Whitley, and Ms. Hyatt in favor; Mr. Chapman opposed.

5.   Discussion of Historic District Preservation Commission membership
     requirements

     Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

The Commission agreed that there should be 2 at-large resident seats. They also
agreed that more advertising such as flyers, open house, social media blasts, etc.
should be considered to better advertise the open positions.

The Committee suggested that an open house for all of the Boards and Commissions
could be beneficial in educating the public on the membership requirements.

6.   Public Input Matters Final Report

     Ms. Reed reviewed the final report with the Committee.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 9:12 am.
