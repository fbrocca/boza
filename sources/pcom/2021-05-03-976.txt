      Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 2:00 pm.

1. Approval of Minutes from the April 5, 2021 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

2. Public Comments

    Mr. Chris Colman, 118 Pitt St, spoke regarding Neighborhood Commercial
zoning and parking standards. He suggested that parking requirements should be
reviewed according to each use and not only how the property is zoned.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley continued with the agenda.
