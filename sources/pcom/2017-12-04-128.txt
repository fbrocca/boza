2. Public Comments

Ms. Sally Jacob, 1152 Village Creek Lane, referenced the recent newspaper article
announcing the formation of a 501C3 for affordable housing.

Mr. John Wright, 145 Rose Lane, referred to agenda item 3A, and president of
African-American Historical Commission, expressed concern with the request and
asked that it be denied. He stated that the owner is not willing to meet with the
community and does not have the community’s best interest in mind with the
request.

Mr. Ed Lee, president of East Cooper Civic Club, stated that in regard to item 3A, he
agrees with Mr. Wright. He stated that the property has had several previous
requests that were not approved. He stated that the Civic Club is not opposed to
subdividing the property, but does not want the use of a bed and breakfast. He
stated a bed and breakfast would be an allowable use under the proposed zoning
and asked that the request be denied.

Ms. Pat Sullivan, 1002 Plantation Court, expressed concern that the future land use
recommends a different zoning than what is proposed. She suggested that the
Comprehensive Plan recommendations should be upheld.

Ms. Thomasena Stokes-Marshall, 481 Lillie and Rebecca Lane, referred to item 3A
and agrees with the comments of both Mr. Wright and Mr. Lee.

6. Review and possible action related to hotel development in Mount Pleasant

Mr. Ulma and Ms. Amy Livingston reviewed the hotel information with the
Committee (attachment 1).

Mr. Bustos suggested that location is a vital consideration when approving hotels
and that they should be located in a hotel district. Having the hotel districts would
provide direction on where it would be appropriate for hotels throughout the
Town.

Mr. Ulma reviewed policy tools and options with the Committee such as adding
hotel districts or making amendments to the existing zoning classifications to
determine where hotels would be appropriate.

Mr. O’Rourke agreed that there should be hotel districts considered and that it
should be discussed during the Comprehensive Plan process. He suggested that
the districts should be spread throughout the entire planning area and located so
that supporting services such as restaurants can locate there as well.

Mr. Brimmer asked if the proposed additions for the Ferry Wharf area were
included. Ms. Livingston answered in the negative. Mr. Brimmer suggested that
another consideration would be to better clarify what uses are allowed within the
different zoning classifications, particularly with Areawide Business (AB) and
Areawide Business-2 (AB-2). He agreed that the Comprehensive Plan process
would be the appropriate venue for this discussion and recommendations. He also
suggested that traffic should be a consideration as well.

Mr. Bustos suggested that more hotels are needed in the northern part of Town to
help accommodate guests visiting residents. He suggested that there should be
consideration of lowering the threshold of trips required for an impact assessment.
He suggested that staff review this and bring back to the Committee.

Mr. O’Rourke moved to direct staff to continue to deliberate on options for hotel
districts and bring back to Committee. Mr. Brimmer seconded the motion. All in
favor.

requested to meet with staff and asked if this was accomplished. Mr. Ulma
answered in the affirmative and stated that staff would like the opportunity to
provide some options through the draft ordinance changes. He stated that the

the affirmative.

residential developments. Ms. Canon answered that the recommendation was to
keep it at 16 inches as it would also negatively affect the individual homeowner.
Mr. Brimmer suggested that 8 inches be considered for new residential
developments.

Ms. Canon continued with review of the Planning Commission recommendations.

Mr. Brimmer asked if Category IV species could be removed with no mitigation.
Ms. Canon answered in the affirmative and stated that they are considered not
protected or exempt trees. Mr. Brimmer suggested that some mitigation should
be required for Category IV species.
