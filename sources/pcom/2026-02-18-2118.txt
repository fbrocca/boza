1.   Call to order

Ms. Hyatt called the meeting to order at 10:00 am.

2.   Public Comments

    Mr. Rome Vanderhorst, 1039 Funeral Road, spoke regarding the overlay district
and providing protection and preservation of their community. He suggested that
this would provide a cohesive approval for future construction between those
properties in the County and those in the Town.

    Mr. Edward Pinkney, 3768 Robbie Glover Road, passed out information to the
Committee and spoke regarding the proposed overlay district. He suggested that
the overlay district is needed in order to preserve the character of the historic Ten
Mile Community. He suggested that the overlay district would be a framework to
address cumulative impacts such as traffic, stormwater runoff, flooding, and
density. He suggested that the overlay district would provide growth management
in historic settlement patterns.

There being no further comments, Ms. Hyatt closed public comment.

3.   Continued discussion and review of the Ten Mile Overlay Zoning District
     Draft Ordinance.

     Ms. Reed and Ms. Boyles reviewed the draft ordinance with the Committee.

The Committee discussed with Charleston County and Ten Mile representatives on
how to best move forward with providing better protection and preservation of the
historic districts.

     The Committee discussed the expansion of Historic District Preservation
Commission (HDPC) to include representatives from each of the three communities
that have attained historic designation and determine a set of guidelines by which
those properties would be reviewed by the expanded HDPC.

The Committee agreed to continue discussion on the draft ordinance at the March
Committee meeting.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:26 am.
