                 Municipal Complex, Committee Meeting Room


Present:    Bob Brimmer, Chair, Mark Smith, Joe Bustos, Elton Carrier.
Staff:      Eric DeMoura, Christiane Farrell, Michele Canon, Liz Boyles,
            Austin Rutherford

      Mr. Brimmer called the meeting to order at 1:12 pm. Mr. Brimmer noted the
recent shooting in Las Vegas and loss of life associated with that tragedy.

1. Approval of Minutes from the September 5, 2017 meeting

     Mr. Smith moved for approval of the minutes. Mr. Carrier seconded the motion.
All in favor.

2. Public Comments

     Ms. GM Whitley, 809 Post Oak Drive, expressed concern with item 3A. She
stated that there was mention at the Planning Commission meeting that there
would be a possible hotel use for the property, but it was stated at the previous
Town Council meeting that there would not be a hotel use. She asked that this
information be taken into consideration when considering disposition of the
request.

3. Review of Planning Commission recommendations from the September 20,
   2017 meeting
   a. Request to rezone from NC, Neighborhood Commercial District, to AB,
      Areawide Business District, an approximately 0.39 acre tract of land located
      at 1375 Old Georgetown Road, identified by TMS No. 559-13-00-027, and
      known as a portion of Lot 8, as depicted on a plat recorded by the
      Charleston County RMC Office in Plat Book G, Page 049. Property to remain
      in the UC-OD, Urban Corridor Overlay District.

       Ms. Canon reviewed the request with the Committee (attachment 1).
                                                                 Planning Committee
                                                                     October 2, 2017
                                                                          Page 2 of 3

       Mr. Smith moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the request.
   Mr. Carrier seconded the motion.

   Mr. Bustos asked what was the maximum height allowed. Ms. Canon answered
   that the maximum height allowed would be 80 feet.

   Mr. Carrier asked if the adjacent property behind the requested parcel was
   recently aggregated. Ms. Canon answered that she was not sure and reviewed
   the zoning for the surrounding properties. Mr. Carrier asked if there are plans
   for future aggregation of property. Ms. Canon answered that she is not sure but
   presumes the purpose of changing the underlying zoning is to make it consistent
   to adjacent properties for lot line adjustment for future redevelopment.

   Mr. Brimmer asked if a hotel use would be allowed. Ms. Canon answered that
   the property is located in the UC-OD and therefore, a hotel use is allowed under
   the current zoning.

   Mr. Kevin Berry, Earthsource Engineering, stated that he was available to
   answer any questions.

        Mr. Brimmer called for a vote on the motion. Motion carried on a 3 to 1
   vote with Mr. Brimmer, Mr. Carrier, and Mr. Smith in favor; Mr. Bustos opposed.

4. Request to annex an approximately 1.15 acre tract of land located at 485
   Commonwealth Road, identified by TMS No. 596-12-00-011, and depicted on
   a plat recorded by the Charleston County RMC Office in Plat Book BO, Page
   117.

    Ms. Canon reviewed the request with the Committee (attachment 1).

    Mr. Carrier moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the annexation.
Mr. Smith seconded the motion. All in favor.

    Mr. Rutherford provided an update for the Committee (attachment 1). He

6. Staff update on the Comprehensive Plan process

   Ms. Boyles updated the Committee on the Comprehensive Plan process and
what was discussed at the first meeting (attachment 1). She stated that the next

Mr. Brimmer stated that he had an opportunity to attend the first Comprehensive
Plan Forum meeting and it was a well-planned meeting and well attended. He
noted that those not selected as a forum member or any citizen interested, could
attend these public meetings and have an opportunity to comment on issues of
concern.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 1:33 pm.
