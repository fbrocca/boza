Present:     G.M. Whitley, Chair, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman, Jake Rambo.

1.   Call to order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 9:33 am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the August 5, 2024 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

    Mr. Will Taylor, 309 Bennett St, spoke regarding the definition of half story. He
suggested that there is relevance of the definition within the historic district but
suggested that the definition should be amended to mimic the national definition.
He stated that they have a detached garage that they would like to add to the
building to add a true half story as defined by the national definition.

    Ms. Linda Balzac, 422 Hobcaw Dr, spoke regarding the definition of half story.
She suggested that the standard definition of half story includes dormers and knee
walls. She suggested that the Town’s definition should be in line with the standard
definition.

    Ms. Alison Taylor, 309 Bennett St, spoke regarding the definition of half story
and suggested that it is incorrect as currently defined in the Town’s ordinances.
She suggested that she has spoken with the Historic District Preservation
Commission members, and they are in favor of correcting the definition. She stated
that many of the residents in the historic district are also in favor of the change of
the definition.

    Ms. Jodi Manti, 675 Kent St, spoke regarding the expansion of the uses for a
home business. She asked that the restriction of hours of four hours to provide
swim lessons be removed and suggested that the use would already be limited due
to only being possible during warmer months. She suggested that the current
home-based business ordinance and noise ordinance would provide sufficient
regulation of this additional use.

    Ms. JoAnn Gorham, 683 Kent St, stated that she is in favor of swim lessons for
young adults, but there should be consideration of the level of noise within a
residential neighborhood. She suggested that the residents should not be
subjected to the amount of noise that occurs during swimming lessons. She
suggested that the rights of the neighbors should be considered as well.

    Ms. Shannon Smalls, Kent St, suggested that with regard to swim lessons, the
regulations for residential homes should be included for everyone including the
parking regulations. She suggested that commercial businesses should not be
allowed in a residential neighborhood.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley closed public comment and
continued with the agenda.

4.    Employee years of service

     Ms. Reed recognized Jane Yager-Baumrind for ten years of service.

5.    Continued discussion of Planning Commission recommendations from the
      July 24, 2024 Meeting
      a. Text Amendment. Proposal to amend Section 156.110 of Chapter 156,
           Zoning Code, of the Mount Pleasant Code of Ordinances, pertaining to
           Accessory Uses. Proposed is to amend the ordinance to expand Home-
           Based Businesses to allow certain outdoor instructional activities,
           including but not limited to swimming lessons.

         Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

         Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend to Town Council to limit the expansion of
     allowed home-based business uses to only swim lessons. Mr. Chapman
     seconded the motion. All in favor.

        Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council to eliminate the four-
     hour time restriction and to amend the time to 8 am to 7 pm. Motion failed
     due to the lack of a second.

6.   Discussion of the definition of a half-story

     Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

     Mr. Chapman moved to recommend amending the definition of a half story to
the same as the City of Charleston’s definition. Mr. Rambo seconded the motion.
All in favor.

    Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend removal of the provision that the height of an
accessory structure can be no higher than the height of the principal structure in the
draft zoning code update. Mr. Chapman seconded the motion. All in favor.

7.   Adjourn

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 10:15 am.
