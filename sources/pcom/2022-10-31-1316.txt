1.   Call to order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 8:30 am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the October 3, 2022 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

    Ms. Mari Ricozzi, 176 Heritage Circle, spoke regarding short-term rentals. She
expressed concern with the verbiage regarding the number of permit and re-
applicants being “exempt” from the cap. She suggested that this should be
reviewed to clarify that this would not mean more than the 400 permits would be
issued.

    Mr. Brandon Bruce, 264 Alexandria Drive, spoke regarding short-term rentals
and suggested that the cost of administering the STR program should be evenly
divided between the number of permits being issued.

    Ms. Michele Whitbeck, 712 Hibbens Grants Blvd, spoke regarding STR and
suggested that the permit should be allowed to transfer to a family member upon
the death of the homeowner. She agreed that the language for the number of
permits issued should be reviewed to ensure it reads as intended.

    Mr. Bill Macchio, STRAMP, suggested that permits should be allowed to be
transferred to family members upon the death of the homeowner. He suggested
that the Town is the leader for short-term rentals.

6.    Continued discussion and possible action on outstanding short-term rental
      amendments

     Ms. Reed and Mr. Pagliarini reviewed the request with the Committee.

    The Committee agreed that off-street parking should be included as well as the
proposal regarding the transfer of permits to family members that was reviewed by
staff. The Committee agreed that staff should continue to monitor the growing
issue in surrounding municipalities regarding LLC ownership and bring back any
concerns for further discussion.

    The Committee discussed increase of permit application fees and asked staff to
bring back information from other municipalities on how they handle their permit
fees and having them paid in conjunction with the business license fees. They also
agreed to discuss limiting the number of permits an individual can have at the next
meeting.

7.    SC Building Codes Amendment. Proposal to amend Town of Mount Pleasant
      Code Sections 92.01 and 150.01 to update the South Carolina Fire Code
      reference from the 2018 edition to the 2021 edition, per SC Building Codes
      Council.

     Ms. Reed and Mr. Horne reviewed the request with the Committee.

   Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the
amendment. Ms. Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.
