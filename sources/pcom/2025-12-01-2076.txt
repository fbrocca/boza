3.   Public Comments

    Mr. Larry Kobrovsky, Charleston County Council member for District 2 (East
Cooper area), spoke regarding the zoning issue for settlement communities and
changing the number of dwelling units allowed to one dwelling unit per three acres.
He stated that the decision of Charleston County on this issue will affect the entire
county and have a profound impact in the future. He suggested that the change of
density would help to alleviate additional traffic for East Cooper. He stated that
final reading should take place at the December Charleston County Council
meeting.

    Ms. Wendy Lockhart, Chandler Road, spoke regarding the recently annexed and
zoned property within the Ten Mile Community and that this would be a critical
and potentially damaging precedent for the community. She suggested that the
owner requesting the three units per acre for his property is effectively trying to
bypass the existing density requirements. She stated that there are currently 19
acres on Chandler for sale with the possibility of an additional 12 acres for sale. She
stated that if allowed to develop this acreage at three units per acre would
significantly change the character of this historic community. She asked that any

4.   Overview of the Asian Longhorned Beetle impact and update on quarantine
     efforts

    Ms. Reed introduced Mr. Jacob Beach with Clemson Extension to the
Committee and stated that he would provide an update on the Asian Longhorned
Beetle eradication efforts.

     Mr. Jacob Beach, Program Manager for the Asian Longhorned Beetle
Cooperative Eradication Program reviewed their efforts with the Committee. He
stated that it is a joint effort with the USDA, Clemson Extension, Customs and
Border Patrol (CBP), Forestry Commission, and various communities since August
2025 to identify areas of infestation, limit the spread of infestation, and educate
the public and local businesses of the infestation and proper disposal of trees
within the quarantined areas.

The Committee received this as information.
