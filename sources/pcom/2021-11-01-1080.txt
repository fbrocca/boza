                  PLANNING & DEVELOPMENT COMMITTEE

                      Municipal Complex, Council Chambers


PowerPoint Presentation


Present: G. M. Whitley, Chair, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman, Brenda Corley.
Staff: Eric DeMoura, Michele Reed, David Pagliarini, Liz Boyles

      Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 10:30 am.

1. Approval of Minutes from the October 4, 2021 regular and October 19, 2021
   special meetings

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Corley seconded the
motion. All in favor.

2. Public Comments

     Ms. Savannah Brennan, Director of Charleston Moves, spoke regarding the
Boulevard Overlay District and would like some elements incorporated in the
guidelines with any changes considered. She suggested that consideration for
fostering active pedestrian-oriented and bicycle-friendly development that
facilitates multi-modal access for pedestrian, bicycle, and mass transit types of
transportation. She asked that where appropriate eight-foot sidewalks, reduced
curb cuts, discouragement of drive-thru facilities, shared parking, recessed
entryways for more pedestrian space, incorporation of Mount Pleasant Way as
identified in the trail plan, and retaining activity zone elements to enhance the
pedestrian experience such as trees, planters, and benches.

    Mr. David Seay, 515 Coleman, spoke regarding the Overlay District. He stated
that one reason the overlay district was put in place to help provide better safety.
As a proponent of safety, he suggested that community involvement is important
as well. He also suggested that proper decorum should be upheld for all public
meetings. He suggested that overlay district has many aspects that should remain
in place.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley continued with the agenda.

3. Review of Planning Commission recommendations from the October 20, 2021,
   meeting
   a. An ordinance to amend sections 156.007, 156.171, 156.304, 156.318,
      156.325 of the Town of Mount Pleasant Zoning Code related to Hotels, Inns,
      Boutique Hotels, and Parking Requirements for the same.

      Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

       Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the
   amendments to the ordinances with the recommendations of
   the Planning Commission as delineated in the staff summary. Ms.
   Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.

4. Discussion and possible action regarding repealing of the Boulevard Overlay
   District, as discussed at the October 19th Special Planning Committee Meeting
   and at the October 20th Planning Commission Meeting.

   Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

   Ms. Corley moved to recommend to Town Council repeal of the Boulevard
Overlay District. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion.

    Mr. Chapman moved to amend the motion to include the three items
recommended by the Planning Commission to remain as part of the design
standards—wider sidewalks/activity zone, street trees instead of buffers, and open
space. Ms. Hyatt seconded the amendment. All in favor.

   Ms. Whitley called for a vote on the amended motion. All in favor.

The Commission agreed that additional design standards to remain or included in
the guidelines would be reviewed and discussed at a future meeting with the zoning
code rewrite.

   that effectuate the accommodation zones/uses as outlined in the 2019

6. Discussion and possible action related to amendments to the Town’s parking
   requirements. Specifically, the issue of whether to amend the Town’s Code of

The Committee agreed that the draft ordinance should be brought back to the
Committee in November.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:45 am.
