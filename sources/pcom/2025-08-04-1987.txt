2.   Approval of Minutes from the June 30, 2025 meeting

There being no quorum, approval of the minutes was deferred to the next meeting.

3.   Public Comments

   Ms. Sarah Eck, Belks Store Manager, spoke regarding amendment to the Towne
Centre planned development (PD). She stated that the Belk store is an anchor
tenant for Towne Centre. She suggested that they were surprised to see that the
proposal showed Belk not remaining at the center. She stated that the proposed
plan would be in direct conflict with current leases and agreements. She also
expressed concern with the affordable housing units proposed with the
amendment.

    Ms. Janet Smith, Senior vice-president for Belk, stated that as a tenant they are
in compliance with all the obligations of their lease that does not expire until 2043
and 2053. She suggested that the proposed plan directly conflicts with the current
leases in that new construction is only allowed in the current building areas and not
permitted within parking lots, sidewalks, drive lanes, and green space. She stated
that the lease also provides maintaining certain surface parking ratios and
structured parking decks other than the hotel parking deck is not allowed. She
suggested that the proposal is hypothetical and may never come to fruition.

    Mr. Rob Nickel, Regional Manager of Belk, suggested that the Towne Centre
proposal could affect and possibly accelerate retail decline and vacancies. He
expressed concern that the loss of surface parking, taller building height, increased
traffic and reduced circulation. He suggested that these changes to the area could
affect additional tenants and the livelihood of the center. He asked that no changes
be made that are not in line with the current leases and the proposed plan be
denied.

    Mr. Jay Claypoole, legal counsel for Continental Realty, suggested that the
proposal is not a means of “pushing out” any current tenants. He stated that it is
proposed as a future plan addressing possible contingencies to maintain viability.
He stated that the owners are willing to work and meet with any tenants.

   Mr. John Darby, 1500 Three Gates, CEO of Beach Company, suggested that
Towne Centre is the core of the Town. He stated that his family’s partnership with
Continental and Towne Centre was to help maintain the viability of the center.

     Mr. Alan McMahon, Beach Company, granted his time to Mr. Darby.

Mr. Darby continued that they have been working with Town staff to have a plan
that will maintain the viability and success of Towne Centre. He suggested that the
proposal is integral to the continued success of Towne Centre.

   Mr. Daniel Brownstein, 1391 Downsbury Drive, spoke regarding the annexation
request between Goodwater Drive and Tupelo Forest Drive. He expressed concern
with the proposed zoning that would increase the density in the area as it allows
more units per acre than currently allowed in the County. He suggested that RC
zoning would be more appropriate for the area.

There being no further comments, Mr. Chapman continued with the agenda.
