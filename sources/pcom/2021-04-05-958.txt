1. Approval of Minutes from the March 1, 2021 meeting

     Ms. Corley moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion.
All in favor.

2. Public Comments

    Ms. Pat Sullivan, 1002 Plantation Court, spoke regarding the rezoning within
Covington. She suggested that approval of the rezoning could affect the request
for historic designation of the Phillips Community.

    Mr. Ben Traywick, President of Bayview Acres Civic Club, spoke regarding the
Queens Court rezoning and stated that the Civic Club is in opposition of the request.
He suggested that the developers have not been upfront with the residents on their
development plans. He stated that he has a petition of approximately 82 residents
in opposition of the request. He stated that the Planning Commission
recommended denial and asked that the request be recommended for denial by
the Committee.

    Mr. Brett Woodward, developer for Queens Court, stated that they have met
with the Bayview Acres Civic Club and have made changes to the plans as requested
by the Civic Club to try to come to a resolution. He suggested that in their meetings
with the neighborhood, some residents were in favor of the development of the
site. He stated that they currently do not have architectural plans as they needed

      recorded by Charleston County ROD Office in Plat Book M47, Page 283.
   b. Request for Deferral Rezoning Case: R-06-21 Dunes West PD Amendment.
      Application to amend the Dunes West PD, Planned Development District
      ordinance, to incorporate into the PD an approximately 9.05-acre tract of
      land comprised of two parcels located off Bessemer Road in Covington at
      Park West at the terminus of Hopkins Road. Proposed is to designate the
