3.   Public Comments

    Mr. James Wilson, attorney for Patriots Annex, stated that this proposal is not
related to the property with the conservation easement. He stated that this is to
rezone property from the Waterfront Gateway District (WG) to a Planned
Development (PD). He stated that the key components of the previous WG zoning
would be carried over to the PD. He stated that they are available to answer any
questions.

    Mr. Tim Cook, spoke regarding the Pepper Plantation Impact Assessment and
conceptual plan request. He stated that the PD currently allows certain uses, with
the addition of some commercial uses. He stated that the impact assessment and
conceptual plan is for a commercial use that would be at the front of the
neighborhood. He stated that this includes changes to the buffer area for an eight-
foot wall with existing vegetation behind it.

    Mr. David Quick, 1502 Village Square, spoke regarding the Patriots Annex
request. He suggested that the public trails and rights-of-way should have special
consideration to ensure that the amenities can be accessed without additional

vehicle traffic. He also suggested that the new low impact development (LID)
standards should be required for this project as well. He suggested that there is an
opportunity to extend the existing trail system to provide access from this property.

    Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning, regarding Patriots Annex, suggested that the
grand lawn originally proposed on the waterfront should remain as this is the only
public access. He suggested that there should be a cohesive plan for the waterfront
public accesses. He also suggested that any changes to the public access design
elements should be required to be reviewed and approved by the Town, along with
implementation of the required LID guidelines, specifying high quality and native
vegetation to support better stormwater management, water quality, and wildlife,
and a list of any changes from the WG to the PD be provided.

   Ms. Katie Zimmerman, Exec. Director of Charleston Moves, spoke on Patriots
Annex, and suggested that pedestrian, transit, and bicycle access should be
considered and required to provide better connectivity. She suggested that
connection of the trail systems should be a requirement as well.

B.   R-15-24. Pepper Tract PD-CD Amendment and Updated Impact
     Assessment. Request to amend the Pepper Tract PD-CD, Planned
     Development-Conservation Design District, pertaining to the
     modification of development standards and permitted commercial
     uses, within the Rural Commercial zoning classification within the
     Pepper Tract PD-CD Ordinance (Ord. No. 11048.) Properties are
     identified by TMS Nos. 615-00-00-044, 615-00-00-375, 615-00-00-082,
     615-00-00-370 and 615-00-00-369. Second is a request for review of an
     Updated Impact Assessment and Conceptual Plan which depicts an
     approximately 140,000 Square Feet of commercial development on the
     subject parcels fronting US Highway 17 North, as well as a proposed
     access management plan. Planning Commission recommended Denial.

   Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee. She stated that the
developer has proposed a change to move the flex/warehouse storage facility
from the Rural Commercial zoning area to the Institutional zoning, which
would mean a significant change and therefore, should have another public
hearing.

   Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council denial of the request.
Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.
