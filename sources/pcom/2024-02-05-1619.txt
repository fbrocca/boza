6.   Discussion and next steps of Zoning Code Rewrite

    Ms. Whitley stated that in speaking with staff, she suggested that the draft
zoning code should be split into sections.

    The Committee discussed having special meetings scheduled to review the draft
Zoning Code. The Committee agreed that they would like weekly meetings
scheduled to review the draft and have any recommended changes available to
review at the next special meeting.

    Ms. Reed suggested that the final draft could be reviewed by Town Council prior
to the Planning Commission holding the public hearing. The Committee agreed.
