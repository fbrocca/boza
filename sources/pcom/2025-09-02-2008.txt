1.   Call to order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 11:04 am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the June 30, 2025 and August 4, 2025 meetings

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

    Ms. Barbara Smith, 410 Royall Ave., expressed concern with the property rights
of homeowners being infringed upon in regard to restrictions on heights, accessory
structures, demolitions, and rebuilds. She expressed concern with accessory
dwelling units (ADUs) and suggested that they should be farther away from the
property line with no windows or steps facing the adjoining property.

    Ms. Jackie Gore, 308 6th Ave, stated that Scanlonville does not have an HOA and
expressed concern with preserving the historical character of the neighborhood.
She suggested that the original character should be preserved, and the safety of
the neighborhood preserved.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley continued with the agenda.

5.   Continued discussion and possible action regarding the heights of detached
     accessory structures

     Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

    Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the Planning
Commission recommendation with the addition that the accessory structure cannot
be taller than the principal structure. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. Motion failed
on a 2 to 2 vote with Mr. Chapman and Ms. Hyatt in favor; Ms. Whitley and Mr.
Rambo opposed.

     Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the Planning
Commission recommendation with the removal of the requirement that the
accessory structure cannot be larger than 40% of the principal structure. Ms. Hyatt
seconded the motion. Motion failed on a 2 to 2 vote with Ms. Rambo and Ms. Hyatt
in favor; Ms. Whitley and Mr. Chapman opposed.

Ms. Whitley stated that the item would be forwarded to Town Council with no
recommendation from the Committee.
