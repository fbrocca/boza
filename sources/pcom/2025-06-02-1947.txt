                                MINUTES

Members Present:       Guang Ming Whitley, Chair; Laura Hyatt, Howard
                       Chapman, Jake Rambo.
Staff Present:         Eric DeMoura, Michele Reed, David Pagliarini, Kate
                       Miller
Also Present:          Joel Evans, Charleston County Planning Director

1. Call to order
Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 12:23 p.m.
2. Approval of Minutes from the May 5, 2025 meeting
Mr. Chapman moved to approve the minutes. Mr. Rambo seconded the
motion. All in favor, motion carried.
3. Public Comment
Mr. Daniel Brownstein, 1341 Downsbury, spoke regarding the height of
accessory structures. He suggested that the setback for the accessory
structures should be the same for the main structure. He also suggested
that the Building Area Ration (BAR) should be considered for older
neighborhoods to reduce the size of main structures. He also spoke about
the historic settlement communities and that the same moratorium as
Charleston County has should be implemented.


Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning, agreed having the BAR can reduce the size
of main structures. He suggested that this should be applicable for the older
neighborhoods as well.
Mr. Will Haynie, 316 Mallard Lakes, asked that the memorial for the Town
Hall Gym be named for Debbie Antonelli and to name Alhambra Park as
requested as well. He stated the legacy neighborhoods do not have
homeowner’s associations or covenants and restrictions and the Town
government is the only body who can preserve them and keep the character.
Mr. James Wilson, attorney for Patriots Annex, stated that he is available for
any questions and that the vested right extension is allowed by state law.
4. Continued discussion and possible action to create a process
   parallel to the County’s efforts to preserve historic settlement
   communities.
Ms. Reed reviewed the item with the Committee and stated that Mr. Joel
Evans, Charleston County Planning Director, is present as well to further
explain what they have done to preserve the historic settlement
communities.
Mr. Joel Evans, Charleston County Planning Director reviewed their historic
district designation with the Committee.
The Committee discussed the settlement communities within the Town and
how these can be preserved going forward. They discussed how a
moratorium could be established and structured.
Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council approval of a moratorium
similar to Charleston County for historic settlement communities. Ms. Hyatt
seconded the motion. All in favor.

6. Patriots Annex Extension of Vested Rights. Request for first one-
   year extension of vested rights period under Patriots Annex
   Development Impact Assessment.
Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.
Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to the Town Council the approval of the
vested rights extension. Mr. Chapman seconded the motion. All in favor.
7. Continued discussion and possible action regarding the heights of
   detached accessory structures.
Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.
The Committee discussed if the setbacks should be increased and/or height
decreased for accessory structures.
Mr. Rambo moved to have the Planning Commission review this issue and
make a recommendation for consideration. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion.
All in favor.
8. Review, discussion, and possible action regarding regulations for
   demolitions and rebuilds/major additions in older neighborhoods.
Ms. Reed reviewed the item with the Committee.
The Committee discussed whether any changes to the current regulations
should be considered.
Mr. Chapman moved to refer this matter to Town Council for discussion with
no recommendation. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.
9. Discussion of Waterfront Gateway – Cooper River District
Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.
The Committee discussed whether any changes should be considered. The
Committee agreed that no additional changes should be considered.
10. Request to approve a Monument and Memorial Application.
   This is a proposal to name a new inclusive playground planned at
   Alhambra Hall after three-year old James Henson, who passed away
   December 20, 2022. The renovated playground will be accessible
   as possible to all children of all ability levels.
Kate Miller reviewed this item with the Committee.
Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the request.
Mr. Chapman seconded the motion. All in favor.
11. Request to approve a Monument and Memorial Application. This is a
    request from Mayor Haynie to name Town Hall Gymnasium in honor
    of Debbie Antonelli, recognizing her outstanding career in
    basketball broadcasting and her tireless advocacy for inclusive
    sports.
Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the
request. Mr. Rambo seconded the motion. All in favor.
12. Five Year Comprehensive Plan Update
Ms. Miller reviewed the progress of the Five-year Comprehensive Plan
update including the upcoming public meetings with the Committee.
13. Adjourn
There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 1.46 p.m.
