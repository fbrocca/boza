  2.   Public Comments

      Mr. Trenholm Walker, attorney for the Miller family expressed concern with the
  issue of changing building heights. He asked that the building height for the Miller
  property on Mathis Ferry Road not be changed in order to allow the site to be
  redeveloped.

  Ms. Claudia Miller DeBrux, representative for the Miller family, distributed photos
  of the groundbreaking for Miller Cadillac in March 1978. She expressed concern
  with the issue of changing building heights. She stated that there has been
  difficulty in redeveloping the site and suggested that lowering the building height
  for this site would adversely affect redevelopment.

  Ms. Pam Ireland, 820 Fiddler’s Point Lane, expressed concern with the issue of
  building height and changing of building height for the Miller property. She stated
  that adjacent neighborhoods are greatly affected by this site and the potential for
  increased traffic. She suggested that allowing the existing 80-foot building height
  would be burdensome for the area. She suggested that the current height allowed
  should not be relied on because the update for the Comprehensive Plan has not yet
  been approved.

Mr. Cody Cuttino, 895 Farm Quarter Road, spoke regarding the possible change in
building height for the Miller property. He suggested that the size of the site would
support the existing 80-foot building height. He stated that the property is currently
in the Gateway district and having the current height would be appropriate.

Ms. Hattie asked if discussion would be allowed from the public on each item. Ms.
Whitley answered in the negative.

Mr. Don Slowick, representative for AT&T, stated that a letter was sent to Mayor
Haynie regarding the Mount Pleasant Waterworks (MPW) request to remove the
water tank and put a cell tower on the site. He stated that the cell tower would be
a concealed monopole. He stated that having this pole would allow access for
FirstNet which provides cell access for first responders.

Ms. Grant expressed concern regarding the Settlement Task Force. She asked that
if implemented, the members of the task force should be comprised of members of
the settlement community.

Mr. Michael German, 1707 Snowden Road, expressed concern regarding the
settlement task force and asked that the members should be from the settlement
communities. He also asked that better communication with the settlement
communities is provided for future meetings. He suggested that there should be an
opportunity for a dialogue with the public and members of the communities.

Mr. Charlie Carmody, representative for the Miller family, stated that lowering the
building height would not be appropriate. He stated that the property is located in
a hub area and that this property should remain as currently allowed.

6.    Discussion of possible reduction of existing 80-foot building height limits to 65
      feet in the Boulevard Overlay District and the Waterfront Gateway District;

      Johnnie Dodds Blvd, Wingo Way, Magrath Darby Blvd, Mathis Ferry Rd and
      Houston Northcutt Blvd) and near the US17/I-526 interchange (Johnnie Dodds
      Blvd, Bowman Road, and Stuart Engals Blvd)

     Mr. Ulma reviewed the request with the Committee.

    Mr. Chapman moved for deferral of this issue for it to be considered with
implementation of the Comprehensive Plan. Motion failed due to the lack of a
second.

The Committee discussed how to accomplish lowering the building height in areas
near the Ravenel Bridge and I-526 so that there is a better transition of building
heights.

Ms. Whitley suggested that the areas south of Johnnie Dodds Boulevard toward
Chuck Dawley Boulevard should have a maximum building height of 45 feet. Ms.
Corley suggested that having the building heights step back from 45 feet to 55 feet
could be considered. Ms. Hyatt suggested that the area below Bowman Road should
have a maximum building height of 45 feet.

Ms. Whitley suggested that the building height on Johnnie Dodds Boulevard closest
to the Ravenel Bridge should have a maximum building height of 65 feet and then
transition to 55 feet.

    Ms. Corley moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the changes to the
building heights as discussed with staff to provide an updated map for Town Council.
Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 3 to 1 vote with Ms. Corley, Ms.
Whitley and Ms. Hyatt in favor; Mr. Chapman opposed.

9.   Comprehensive Plan discussion

     Ms. Boyles reviewed this issue with the Committee.

The Committee discussed how to proceed with review and approval of the
Comprehensive Plan.

The Committee agreed that an overview should be provided in December at a special
meeting if possible or during the January 2020 Planning Committee.

The Committee also agreed that a special Town Council should be scheduled in
January during the Committees of Council meeting schedule to review specific issues
of concern within. Mr. DeMoura suggested that the Comprehensive Plan could also
be discussed during the Town Council retreat in January 2020.

The Committee agreed that the goal should be to have the Comprehensive Plan
approved by April 2020.

10. Presentation from Housing For All – Mount Pleasant

  Ms. Wendy Bauerschmidt gave a presentation on the efforts of Housing for All—
Mount Pleasant for the Committee.

    Mr. Chapman moved to recommend that the Finance Committee discuss as
possible Town Council action to remove the funding match requirement for Housing
For All—Mount Pleasant. Ms. Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 12:19 pm.
