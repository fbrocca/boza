                           PLANNING COMMITTEE
                             NOVEMBER 3, 2025
                                MINUTES

                       Municipal Complex, Council Chambers

PowerPoint Presentation

Present:      Howard Chapman, Laura Hyatt, Jake Rambo
Absent:       G.M. Whitley
Staff:        Eric DeMoura, Michele Reed, Peter Stone

1.   Call to order

Mr. Chapman called the meeting to order at 9:47 am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the September 2, 2025 meeting

     Ms. Hyatt moved for approval of the minutes. Mr. Rambo seconded the motion.
All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

   Ms. Myra Richardson, 2755 Earl Johnson Lane, thanked Mr. Tinkey for his help
with a deceased animal in the roadway in their neighborhood. She expressed
concern with tree removal and that this could mean more displaced animals that
could be injured or killed.

There being no further comments, Mr. Chapman continued with the agenda.

4.   Review of Planning Commission recommendations from the October 22,
     2025 meeting
     a. R-22-25. Chandler Road. Request to annex and zone RC-2, Rural
         Conservation-2 District, portions of land forming an aggregate total of
         approximately 6.5-acres located on Chandler Road

           Mr. Stone reviewed the request with the Committee.

         Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the
     annexation and zoning requests. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.

     b.   Zoning Code Text Amendment. Amendments to the Mount Pleasant
          Zoning Code, modifying development standards for detached accessory
          structures and the Neighborhood Preservation Overlay District

         Ms. Reed reviewed the request to modify the standards for detached
     accessory structures with the Committee.

         Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the changes
     for detached accessory structures with the historic district allowed a six-foot
     setback for one story accessory structures as with the remainder of the Town.
     Motion failed due to the lack of a second.

         Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the changes
     for detached accessory structures as presented. Mr. Chapman seconded the
     motion. All in favor.

         Ms. Reed reviewed the request to modify the development standards for
     the Neighborhood Preservation Overlay District with the Committee.

         Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the changes
     to the Neighborhood Preservation Overlay District as recommended by the
     Planning Commission. Mr. Rambo seconded the motion. All in favor.

5.   Discussion and possible action pertaining to the rezoning of five parcels
     recently annexed off Highway 17 North, and text amendments to the zoning
     code pertaining to the properties located outside of the Urban Growth
     Boundary and density of Rural Residential Zoning District

   Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee. She stated that a public
hearing held by the Planning Commission is scheduled for Monday, November 17th.

    Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council to move forward with the
recommendations as presented. Mr. Rambo withdrew his motion.

   The Committee agreed to defer action on the recommendations to their next
meeting.

     at the Market at Oakland Development Impact Assessment

     Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

7.   Adjourn

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 10:33 am.
