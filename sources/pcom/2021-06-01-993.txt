 1.   Approval of Minutes from the May 3, 2021 meeting

      Ms. Corley moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion.
 All in favor.

 2.   Public Comments

     Mr. George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Rd, referred to a map distributed to the
 Committee regarding Hwy 17. He expressed concern with the widening and
 overpass of Hwy 17. He stated that the map shows an alternative to the proposed
 widening by providing an overpass using Billy Swails Blvd. He suggested that this
 option should be considered.

     Mr. Chris Colman, 118 Pitt St, spoke regarding nonconformities in the Historic
 District, particularly parking requirements. He suggested that the parking
 requirements should be more restrictive as a means to keep more intensive
 development within the Old Village Historic District.

    Mr. Jason Crowley, Coastal Conservation League, expressed concern with the
 Covington annexation and rezoning requests as it would undermine the historic
 designation for this area that is currently being considered by Charleston County.
 He suggested that if annexed, it should match the same zoning as proposed with
 Charleston County.

    Ms. Louisa Montgomery, 404 Church St, expressed concern with the noise and
traffic from businesses along Shem Creek. She also stated that she concurs with
Mr. Freeman and Mr. Colman’s comments.

   Mr. Richard Habersham, Phillips Community, spoke regarding the annexation
and zoning of Covington. He suggested that if annexed it could impact the historic
designation for the area. He also expressed concern with the lot sizes not being
similar to the existing lots within the historic community. He suggested that the
property should be annexed with the same zoning requirements.

     Ms. Pat Sullivan, 1002 Plantation Court, spoke regarding the Phillips
Community and that it is a unique historic area. She suggested that the land
pattern should be preserved. She suggested that the annexation and zoning of
Covington could negatively affect the historic designation and would impact the
historic character of the area. She suggested that this should be a consideration
when deciding on whether to annex and zone this property.

    Mr. Hamilton Smith, 757 Longpoint Road, spoke on behalf of the Phillips
Community. He expressed concern with the annexation and zoning of Covington
and that it would impact the historic character of the area. He suggested that the
historic culture and character of the area should be preserved. He suggested that
the annexation and rezoning would change the area and asked that this be
considered.
