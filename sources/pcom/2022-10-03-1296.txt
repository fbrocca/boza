1.   Call to Order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 8:30 am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the September 6, 2022 meeting

   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Corley seconded the
motion. All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

    Ms. Michelle Whitbeck, 712 Hibbens Grant, spoke regarding proposed changes
to the short-term rental ordinance. She questioned how the permit cap would be
met if current permit holders would be exempt from the cap. She suggested that
the permit should transfer to family members at the time of retirement or death.
She suggested that there should not be a minimum number of days of use.

    Mr. Bill Macchio, 1319 Lake Mallard, stated that there is a STR association
comprised of STR operators. He stated that the association has not met with staff
to discuss the changes to the ordinance and to discuss the issues of concern. He
suggested that the permit should be allowed to transfer at the time of death to
family members.

    Mr. Brandon Bruce, 264 Alexandra, questioned why the permit fee was being
increased. He suggested that there should be clear and precise penalties and fines
for those STR illegally operating. He suggested that the parking restrictions should
not be included as a business is not subject to the same parking restrictions. He
suggested that the STR permit should be allowed to be transferred.

    Mr. Eric Benson, 242 6th Avenue, agreed with Mr. Macchio that the STR
association should meet with staff. He suggested that the minimum number of
days should be reviewed. He suggested that there should be only one permit for
the entire parcel, regardless of whether renting the whole house, rooms, or an
accessory dwelling unit. He suggested that the permit should be allowed to be
transferred. He stated that the permit fee increase and the parking restrictions
should be reviewed, and the penalties associated with failure to meet the parking
requirements.
