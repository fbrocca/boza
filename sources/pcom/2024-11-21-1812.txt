1.    Call to Order

     Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 9:03 am.

2.    Public Comment

    Ms. Lisa Schniptke, 185 Mossy Oak Way, expressed concern with murals and
tree protection. She suggested that mural should be located on facades that can
be seen from the street. She also suggested that pine trees should be preserved.
She suggested that removal of trees affects more than just the property owner
where the tree is located. She suggested that more mitigation should be required
and those mitigation trees protected regardless of size. She suggested that
stronger enforcement efforts should be taken to ensure the Town’s tree canopy is
not only preserved, but trees are planted to replace trees that are allowed to be
removed.

    Ms. Sandy Nettles, 1412 Madison Court, expressed concern with the critical
buffer ordinance section. She suggested that there should be stronger
enforcement of the critical line buffer. She suggested that the ordinance should
provide more “tools” within the ordinance to protect the critical buffers. She also
expressed concern that the ordinance does not adequately address re-construction
of homes following a major storm event. She suggested that stronger ordinances
are needed to ensure that when an existing home is damaged, it can be repaired
or re-built.

   Mr. Kevin Andrus, 3593 Deer Creek Rd, commended the Town for the work
completed on the draft ordinance for tree protection. He suggested that signage

    Ms. Michelle Stress, 545 Veranda View, suggested that the trees within the
Town should be protected and preserved, including the pine trees as they are an
important part of the ecosystem. She suggested that the Community Forest Master
Plan (CFMP) should be utilized. She suggested that in addition to having permits
posted near the street, there could be a hotline to report concerns and could
enhance enforcement efforts. She also suggested that having more involvement
of experts through a task force would be beneficial.

    Mr. David Quick, 1502 Village Square, expressed concern with tree removal and
suggested that the Town is in a crisis and losing significant trees. He suggested that
stricter restrictions are needed for tree removals, especially when tree removals
are requested for new construction or renovations. He suggested that a task force
should be established to review the tree protection ordinances and make
recommendations for better tree protection.

    Ms. Allison Williams, 729 Hibbens Grant Blvd, suggested that all staff reviewing
tree removals should be ISA and/or TRAQ certified. She suggested that stricter
regulations and replacement should be considered including increasing the
minimum size of replacement trees. She suggested that there should be stronger
penalties for illegal tree removals. She suggested there should be dedicated
resources for public tree maintenance and a stronger monitoring and inspection
program. She suggested that more public participation and tree conservation
efforts through education, volunteer opportunities, and community driven

     Mr. Mike Tinkey, 208 Venning Street, suggested that the ordinance should be

3.   Zoning Code Draft. Review of Chapter 156, Zoning Code of the Town of
     Mount Pleasant, Draft Subchapters. Specifically, to review the
     recommendations from the Planning Commission Public Hearing

     Ms. Reed and Ms. Cousino reviewed the Planning Commission
recommendations with the Committee.

     Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the Planning
Commission recommendation to increase the open space requirement in the
Redevelopment Center Overlay District to 10%. Mr. Chapman seconded the motion.
All in favor.

   Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the Planning
Commission recommendation to clarify that the building setback requirement
should be from the fourth story. Mr. Chapman seconded the motion. All in favor.

    Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council approval to allow accessory
structures within the Old Village Historic District to have a maximum height of 25
feet with the condition that the accessory structure is not taller than the main
structure, whichever is shorter. Mr. Chapman seconded the motion. Motion passed
on a 3 to 1 vote with Ms. Whitley, Mr. Chapman, and Mr. Rambo in favor; Ms. Hyatt
opposed.

    Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council denial of the Planning
Commission recommendation to increase the street frontage or lot width for
properties within the Neighborhood Character Preservation Overlay District. Mr.
Chapman seconded the motion. All in favor.

    Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council denial of the Planning
Commission recommendation to not count garage space as part of the parking
requirement. Mr. Chapman seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 3 to 1 vote
with Ms. Whitley, Mr. Chapman, and Mr. Rambo in favor; Ms. Hyatt opposed.

   Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council denial of the Planning
Commission recommendation to not allow any murals within the Town. Mr.
Chapman seconded the motion. All in favor.

    Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the Planning
Commission recommendation to require planting groups of trees in parking islands
as recommended by the CFMP, flush mounted standing curbs where applicable, and
specifying canopy and understory tree requirements. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

   Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the Planning
Commission recommendation to establish a task force for trees. Ms. Hyatt
seconded the motion. All in favor.

The Commission agreed that the draft changes to the tree ordinance should move
forward with the task force on trees making any additional recommendations or
changes.

   Ms. Reed reviewed the CFMP recommendations that have been included in the
draft zoning code with the Committee.

    The Committee discussed whether a sign should be posted on the property
when a tree removal permit is issued. The Committee was divided on whether the
signage should be required.

   The Committee discussed illegal tree removals and how penalties are assessed.

    Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council approval of protecting pine
trees 24 inches or greater with the allowance for removal if the pine tree is located
within 20 feet of a residence. Ms. Whitley seconded the motion.

     Mr. Rambo withdrew his motion. Ms. Whitley withdrew her second.

    Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council to have staff recommend

     The Committee discussed cottage courts.

    Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend to Town Council to eliminate cottage courts
from the zoning code. Ms. Whitley seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 3 to

4.   Next Steps

Ms. Reed stated that the zoning code would be forwarded to Town Council with
the recommendations for consideration of first reading at the December meeting.

5.   Adjourn

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 10:41 am.
