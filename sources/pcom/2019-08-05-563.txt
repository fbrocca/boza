2. Public Comments

    Mr. Larry Murray, Executive Director for Patriots Point Development Authority,
spoke regarding items regarding Patriots Annex project. He suggested that this
project is needed for Patriots Point to bring in additional revenue. He asked that
the request be approved.

Mr. James Wilson, representative for Patriots Annex, referred to the items
regarding the Patriots Annex project. He stated that this project would provide
access for the public to the water. He stated that in regard to the requested height,
they have amended their request in response to the Planning Commission
concerns. He stated that this project would create a destination for the citizens
and visitors and would generate revenue for the Town.

Mr. Scott Shellhaas, applicant for 1228 Melvin Bennett, stated that he would like
to withdraw the request to be included in the overlay district. He asked that the
request be approved for him to have his business office on the property.

Mr. Michael Carmody, 614 Palisades Drive, and representative for Walt Miller
estate, stated that the site has been marketed as having 80 foot height and would
like to keep the height for the property at 80 feet.

Ms. Pat Sullivan, 1002 Plantation Court, spoke regarding item 6C (Halls Pond Road).

outdoor storage, which is a violation of county ordinances. She asked that this
request be denied.

Ms. Pam Ireland, 820 Fiddlers Point Lane, spoke regarding the Miller Cadillac site.
She stated that she is opposed to 80 foot height within the Town, particularly near

Ms. Jane Direline, 1018 Lakeview drive, spoke regarding the Patriots Annex project
and stated that she is opposed to the rezoning request and inclusion in the Gateway

opposed to the height request of 80 feet as well. She expressed concern that this
project would increase traffic on Coleman Blvd.

Ms. Mary Ricozzi, 615 Bay Tree Court, spoke regarding short-term rentals and the

with Mr. Brimmer, Ms. Whitley, and Mr. Bustos in favor; Mr. O’Rourke opposed.

6. Referred from Town Council back to Planning Committee
   a. An Ordinance to amend Chapter 156, Zoning Code, of the Mount Pleasant
      Code of Ordinances by adding a new section entitled Short Term Rentals.
      The purpose of this new section is to define and regulate the use of
      residential units as short term rentals in order to protect the integrity and
      character of the Town’s existing neighborhood, as well as the health and
      safety of occupants of Short Term Rental Units. (Ord. No. 19048)

       Ms. Canon reviewed the request with the Committee. She stated that it was
   referred back to the Committee in order to review some of the procedures, fees,
   and parking requirements. She reviewed staff’s recommendations with the
   Committee.

   The Committee agreed that the draft ordinance should remain as approved at
   first reading with the following changes:
       ➢ Include a tentative November 1st effective date
       ➢ Application fee should be reduced to $250
       ➢ Short-term rental permit will not transfer in the event of a change in home
           ownership.

7. Continued discussion of building heights in the Johnnie Dodds and Chuck
   Dawley portions of the Boulevard Overlay District

    Mr. Bustos stated that he requested this discussion. He suggested that the
height should be amended to be 45 feet at curb with a 55 foot step back for all
properties in Boulevard Overlay District. The Committee discussed whether 65 feet
might be more appropriate as opposed to moving from 80 feet to 55 feet.

The Committee agreed that the height should be transitioned from 80 feet to 65
feet and then 55 feet with staff bringing back a recommendation at next month’s
meeting.
