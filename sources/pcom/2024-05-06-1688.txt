   Mr. Rambo moved for approval of the minutes. Mr. Chapman seconded the

3.   Public Comments

     There being no comments, Ms. Whitley closed public comment

4.   Review of Planning Commission recommendations from the April 17, 2024,
     meeting
     a. R-05-24. Request to Rezone one vacant parcel comprised of 0.20-acres,
         on the corner of Simmons Street and Rose Lane. Proposal to rezone the
         parcel from its current designation of AB, Areawide Business District, in
         the Special R-2 Overlay District to R-2, Low Density Residential, and
         remain in the overlay district. The subject parcel may be further
         identified by TMS No. 532-01-00-178, property is also indicated on a plat
         recorded by the Charleston County ROD Office in Plat Book X, Page 133.
         Planning Commission recommended denial.

           Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

        Mr. Rambo moved to recommend to Town Council denial of the request.
     Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. Motion passed on a 3 to 1 vote with Ms.

     b.   Low Impact Development Text Amendments. Proposed amendments to
          the Environmental Guidelines for Land Development (LID) Ordinance to
          improve the program. Changes include clarifying ordinance language,
          additional specifications and criteria, and additional LID techniques for
          developers to utilize. Green Commission and Planning Commission
          recommended approval.

          Mr. Mitchell and Ms. Wright reviewed the request with the Committee.

        Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the
     amendments as presented. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.
