Present:     G.M. Whitley, Chair, Laura Hyatt, Howard Chapman, Brenda Corley.

1.   Call to order

Ms. Whitley called the meeting to order at 9:45 am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the October 2, 2023 meeting

     Mr. Chapman moved to approve; Ms. Hyatt seconded. All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

    Mr. Scott Schmitz, 1193 Sharpstowne Court, spoke regarding the conceptual
plan for the Market at Oakland. He expressed concern with the traffic counts
included in the impact assessment and that there are some errors, particularly with
the counts not included for Billy Swails Blvd, that were included in the Billy Swails
Blvd plans.

    Jason Milner, Civil Site Environmental, Engineer for the Market at Oakland
project stated that the improvements included in the impact assessment were
reviewed by the Town’s engineers. He stated that he is available to answer any
questions.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley closed public comment and
continued with the agenda.

          Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

        Mr. Chapman moved to recommend to Town Council approval. Ms. Hyatt
     seconded this motion. All in favor.

5.   Discuss Building Permit Allocation System

    Ms. Reed stated that Ms. Whitley asked this be placed on the agenda for
discussion and reviewed the building permit allocation system with the Committee.

    Ms. Corley moved to recommend to Town Council to extend the Building Permit
Allocation System for five years, with any permits not allocated would expire, with
the new allocation system beginning with the original allocation and any permits
not allocated after January 2024 would expire and not be rolled over. Ms. Hyatt
seconded the motion. All in favor.
