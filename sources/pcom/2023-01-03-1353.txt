3. Public Comments

     Mr. George Gates, 312 Cooper River Drive, and member of the Planning
Commission, spoke regarding his December email to Town Council on suggested
changes to of the bylaws for boards and commissions and the transcription of
minutes. He asked that the Committee provide feedback on his suggestion to
change the way minutes are transcribed. He suggested that there should be a more
thorough transcription of the meetings for a better understanding of what
transpired during those meetings, particularly inclusion of the discussion of the
board or commission members. He suggested that the state statute 30-4-80(A)
requires the minutes in written form meaning that the written minutes should
include the board or commission discussion instead of only having the recording
available for that portion of the meeting. He asked that the Committee provide
feedback on how the Committee and Town Council would like to proceed with the
transcription of minutes in the future.

     Ms. Denise Fugo, Mount Pleasant Housing for All Executive Director, spoke
regarding the proposed density changes in commercial districts. She suggested
that there are residents that are losing their homes and there are more homeless
citizens than originally realized. She stated that her organization is working on
increasing the amount of attainable housing and asked that no further restrictions
on density for attainable housing in commercial districts be considered.

   Ms. Mari Ricozzi, 136 Heritage Circle, spoke regarding short-term rentals and
code enforcement for illegal operation of STRs. She suggested that those legally
operating should not have to cover the cost of code enforcement for those illegally
operating a STR.

     Mr. Bill Maccio, 1319 Lake Mallard Blvd, spoke regarding STRs. He thanked that
Committee and the Town for their efforts regarding regulation of STRs. He stated
that those legally operating STRs are appreciative of all of the Town’s efforts and
stated that STRs does provide housing for those that might not otherwise be able
to live in Mount Pleasant.

There being no further comments, Ms. Whitley continued with the agenda.

C.   Text Amendment – Conditions on Residential Density. Proposal to
     amend Section 156.326, of Chapter 156, Zoning Code, of the Mount
     Pleasant Code of Ordinances, pertaining to Principal Use Conditions.
     Proposed is to amend the condition associated with density for
     residential uses in commercial districts, specifically to lower the
     maximum density from 12 units per acre to 4 units per acre but allow
     up to 8 units per acre if the additional 4 units are dedicated to attainable
     housing. Planning Commission recommended approval with a
     modifications

     Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

    Ms. Hyatt moved to recommend to Town Council denial of the Planning
Commission recommendation and to keep the original recommendation of
having density for residential uses in commercial districts lowered to 4 units

6.   Continued discussion and possible action regarding outstanding short-term
     rental ordinance amendments

     Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

The Committee discussed including the following changes to the short-term rental
ordinance:
   ➢ A minimum use of 24 nights rental and a minimum number of 4 bookings for
      each operating STR in order to be eligible for renewal of the permit
   ➢ Increase of application review fees for a whole-house rental to $500, while
      keeping the application review fees for rental of bedrooms and ADUs to $250
      for bedroom and ADU
   ➢ Restricting the number of permits allowed per owner/entity
