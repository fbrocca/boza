   Mr. Chapman moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Corley seconded the
motion. All in favor.

2.   Public Comments

     Ms. Antony Masedo, 1240 Six Mile Road, expressed concern with the
annexation and rezoning of the Watts Park property. She expressed concern with
not financially being able to move their mobile home. She suggested that this is
one of the few areas in MP that has affordable housing. She suggested that they
should have the opportunity to purchase the property and remain where they are
located.

    Mr. George Freeman, 1450 Bowman Rd, spoke in opposition of the rezoning of
the Watts Park property. He suggested that this is a historic settlement area and
the neighborhood should be preserved.

    Mr. Charlie Carmody, representative of Miller family, spoke regarding the issue
of building heights. He asked that the property owners have an opportunity to
meet with Town Council prior to a decision being made.

   Ms. Paula Baldwin, 1240 Six Mile Road, spoke regarding the rezoning of the
Watts Park property. She suggested that this is one of the few areas where there
