                        PLANNING COMMITTEE
                         Tuesday, July 5, 2022
                              Minutes

                     Municipal Complex, Council Chambers


PowerPoint Presentation

Present:Howard Chapman, acting chair, Brenda Corley, Howard Chapman,
             Laura Hyatt.
Absent:Guang Ming Whitley, Chair.

Mr. Chapman called the meeting to order at 10:30 am.

1.Approval of Minutes from the June 6, 2022 meeting

  Ms. Corley moved for approval of the minutes. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
motion. All in favor.

2.Public Comments

   Ms. Laurie Bixler, 144 3rd Ave, spoke regarding short-term rentals. She
expressed concern with the number of permits exceeding the cap of 400
permits. She asked that an advisory committee or commission should be
established to review the procedures and determine a program that would be
beneficial for all.

   Ms. Mari Ricozzi, 136 Heritage Circle, spoke regarding short-term rentals.
She stated that short-term rental operation is her business. She stated that the
business does not provide a huge profit but does provide income. She
suggested that the professionals should have input on the procedures. She
asked that minimal changes should be considered to keep the program working
smoothly.

  Mr. Robbie Whitsell, 312 Fiddlers Point Lane, member of Lifepark Church
spoke regarding the proposed Highway 17 North Retail Village development.
He stated that the proposed pet daycare business is too close to the church. He
suggested that it would produce noise to the point of being an annoyance and
nuisance. He asked that the request be denied, the location of the pet daycare
be moved farther from the church property, or the outdoor activities of the
business restricted so the church activities are not disturbed.

3.Review of Planning Commission Recommendations from June 22, 2022,
   meeting
   A. R-15-22. Request to amend the Central Mt. Pleasant PD, Planned
        Development District Ordinance. Proposed is to incorporate three
        abutting parcels into the PD, increasing the acreage by 2.23- acres
        within the Mixed-Use Center Tract. The three parcels requested to be
        incorporated are currently zoned AB, Areawide Business in the HNB-
        VR-OD, Hungryneck—Venning Road Overlay District.

     Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

     Mr. Morrison and Mr. Aton updated the Committee on several
  transportation issues including the signal at Rifle Range Road, internal
  roadways, and access for the subject parcels.

     The Committee recommended that staff consider a turn-around or
  additional egress for Fortier Drive, and there should not be access from
  Hungryneck Boulevard or Midtown Avenue, but only from the Gather site.
  Mr. Morrison and Ms. Reed noted that Fortier Drive would be reviewed
  during the impact assessment approval process and that the Central Mount
  Pleasant PD and the Overlay District management system addressed internal
  accesses and roadways.

    Ms. Corley moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the
  amendment to the Central Mount Pleasant PD. Ms. Hyatt seconded the
  motion.

    Mr. Chapman moved to amend the motion to include the requirement of a
  Type E buffer as recommended by staff and the Planning Commission. Ms.
  Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.

  Mr. Chapman called for a vote on the amended motion. All in favor.

  B. Highway 17 North Retail Village. Request to approve an Impact
       Assessment and Conceptual Plan for the properties located 3236
       North Highway 17. Proposed Mixed-Use Development consists of
       approximately 14.6 acres comprised of eight parcels located on
       Highway 17 North (across from Lowes at Oakland). All parcels are
       zoned AB, Areawide Business District.

      Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

     Ms. Corley moved to recommend to Town Council approval of the impact
   assessment and conceptual plan including all Planning Commission
   recommendations. Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion.

     Mr. Chapman moved to amend the motion to include that the developer
  be required to construct the portion of All-American Boulevard to connect
  with Lifepark Church access and for the developer to continue to look at
  other locations for the pet daycare so it is further away from Lifepark Church.
  Ms. Hyatt seconded the motion. All in favor.

     Mr. Chapman called for a vote on the motion. All in favor.

4.Continued discussion and possible action regarding proposed amendments
    to the Towns Short Term Rental Ordinance.

  Ms. Reed reviewed the request with the Committee.

   Ms. Hyatt moved to send this issue back to Planning Commission for a public
hearing including all the Planning Commission recommendations and moving to
a two-tiered permitting system. Ms. Corley seconded the motion. All in favor.

5.Discuss Historic Preservation in Settlement Communities

   Ms. Boyles updated the Commission on staffs efforts regarding historic
preservation in settlement communities.

   The Committee thanked staff for their work and agreed with staffs direction
regarding this issue.


6.Update on Zoning Code Rewrite and Livability Division efforts

  Ms. Reed updated the Committee on these projects.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:53 am.
