3.   Public Comments

    Ms. Barbara Smith, 410 Royall Ave, spoke regarding the draft ordinance on
accessory structures. She suggested that in the future there should be a
presentation and an opportunity to provide comments. She stated that the
accessory dwelling units (ADU) were allowed to provide aging in place, and she
suggested that if the accessory structure is not allowed to be taller than the primary
structure, it could mean that one story ranch style homes would be demolished
and taller homes built that would be out of character of the neighborhood. She
expressed concern about the building area ration (BAR). She suggested that the
changes to the height of accessory structures and implementation of the BAR
would have a negative impact. She agreed that the setbacks need to be changed.

    Ms. Susie Smith, 1317 Erkmann Dr, suggested that in order to preserve the
character of Old Mount Pleasant, more restrictions are necessary such as smaller
homes, preservation of existing trees and greenspace, and architecture
requirements. She suggested that the 50% lot coverage is too much and suggested
that it should be kept at 40% lot coverage. She suggested that tree removal should
be taken out of the Engineering and Development Services Department should be
