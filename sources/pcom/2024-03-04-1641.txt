Present:     Howard Chapman, Jake Rambo Laura Hyatt.

1.   Call to order

Mr. Chapman called the meeting to order at 8:46 am.

2.   Approval of Minutes from the February 5, 2024 meeting

     Ms. Hyatt moved for approval of the minutes. Mr. Rambo seconded the motion.
All in favor.

3.   Public Comments

    Mr. Tony Berry, Prosperity Builders, spoke regarding the amendment to
Carolina Park PD. He stated that he is available to answer any questions.

    Ms. Wendy Bauerschmidt, 1320 Woodlake Court, spoke regarding the Carolina
Park PD amendment. She stated that she had to make the difficult decision to
relocate out of Mount Pleasant because her employees could not afford to live in
Mount Pleasant. She suggested that this project is needed to provide more
affordable housing in Mount Pleasant. She suggested that it is a good project.

There being no further comments, Mr. Chapman continued with the agenda.

     2024 meeting
